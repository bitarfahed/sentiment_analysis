a nightmare on elm street : the dream child this is a bad movie . there is no escaping it . i love the series and i think freddy is probably the best character ever in horror movies . but even being a fan i cannot help but see this movie is mediocre . there is not even an effort to build an interesting story and strong characters . by now they had just given up . they do not even try . they are the production of course , hoping for a few more easy dollars . the story does not grab your attention . its so simple it is almost absent . alice , a survivor from part 4 is now pregnant . freddy is coming back through the baby is dreams . at the beginning we learn that to stop him alice must find his mother . and that is it . the story advances slowly since there is so little plot meaning it turns pretty boring after a few minutes of bad dialogues and awful acting . in fact that is the only scary thing , the acting , since the deaths are not even slightly cool . the characters are so uninteresting we could not care less for them . the girls are as unattractive as possible . the whole cast reminded me of a bad amateur theatre group . i have seen better actors in school plays . it is embarrassing really . lisa wilcox still manages to bring some class to this . she is beautiful , sexy and has some talent . but the material just did not let her shine . what a pity . stephen hopkins work is nowhere close to renny harlin s brilliant direction in dream master . he tries some imaginative shots near the end but that tension harlin and craven created so well in the previous movies is no where to be found . the dream child is just another unimaginative sequel . it is the kind of movie that give horror series a bad name . the end for the once scary freddy . it is too sad to see him now . a clown that is not funny . a bad joke . goodbye freddy .
