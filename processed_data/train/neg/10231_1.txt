frankly i am rather incensed that on the basis of the dazzling reviews attributed to steven smith i wasted nearly two hours on his debut offering . have they all been written by his pals ? the action clunks along , the music is irritating and over used , the script is simply dire and the actors with the exception of the gardener mediocre at best . i do think we should support the efforts of a young filmmaker but saying it is brilliant when it is not will surely only encourage him to make the same mistakes again i . e . continuing to write his own scripts and using the same actors for another venture . yes , it is his first film , low budget etc . i get it , but it is also out there for members of the public to purchase and it is just not up to scratch .
