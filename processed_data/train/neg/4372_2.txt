i love don knotts , let me just say that up front ! he is an enormous talent and the best at what he does , which is portray a nervous , lovably befuddled loser thrown into a position of authority . he is fabulous in this role as roy fleming , the reluctant astronaut , but the film is pretty dull , really , even though as a kid my brother and i delighted in watching this and his other films . it is still worth watching but really it is a film that is best enjoyed by children . i would categorize it as 100 family friendly and something you could sit down and watch with your kids on a family night . as with all of knotts films , there is a great cast of beloved character actors and you cannot help but smile when knotts gives one of his shaky , open mouthed stares , no matter how old and jaded you are . from an adult perspective , one thing i think that is great about this film is how it captures nasa in the 1960s all the new modern buildings , the hope , the optimism , the future ! and i was surprised at how suave and studly leslie neilsen was back then . only complaint about the story is roy is love interest , a rather threadbare , unlikeable woman who cannot give him the time of day until he becomes a big shot if you are like me , you will be hoping that he gives her the shove off at the end . beware you will be whistling the theme tune for days after watching , it is that catchy .
