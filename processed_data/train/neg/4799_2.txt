i myself am a big fan of low budget 80 is horror films . this is not the worst but still not to spectacular . the plot line is decent but drags out way too long . you are through half the movie before you even get to see any zombie action . the kills are not very creative and the zombies are not too crafty . i truly think this movie would have been better if they left out the zombies and just made it into some mafia flick . it is watchable but i feel that this film did steal at least an hour of my life . i will give the film credit for being somewhat original . if you are really into b horror movies it is worth a viewing but if you are not , do not bother . but you do not have to take my word for it .
