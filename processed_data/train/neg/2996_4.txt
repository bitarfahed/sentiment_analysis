born again is a sub standard episode from season one . it deals with the subject of reincarnation and just does not fly . i have never been big on reincarnation and that could be part of my apathy toward this episode . it does reference the tooms case which is some nice continuation from the previous episode . but the positives end there . which is unfortunate because that takes place at the beginning of the episode . i think it is ludicrous that a dead guy would chose to reincarnate in the body of a completely unrelated girl . and he waits until the girl turns eight to start exacting revenge . there is even a serious lack of witty mulder scully dialogue to keep the episode afloat . if you are into reincarnation , maybe this episode is up your alley . if you are not , then at least you can learn what bradycardia is .
