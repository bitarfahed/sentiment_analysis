before i start , i love eddie izzard . i think he is one of the funniest stand ups around today . possibly that means i am going into this with too high expectations , but i just did not find eddie funny in this outing . i think the main problem is eddie is trying too hard to be eddie . everyone knows him as a completely irrelevant comic , and we all love him for it . but in circle , he appears to be going more for irrelevant than funny , and completely lost me in places . many of the topics he covers he has covered before i even think i recognised a few recycled jokes in there . if you buy the dvd you will find a behind the scenes look at eddie is tour interesting in places , but not very funny , and a french language version of one of his shows . die hards will enjoy seeing eddie in a different language , but subtitled comedy is not very funny . if you are a fan of eddie you have either got this already or you are going to buy it whatever i say . if you are just passing through , buy glorious or dressed to kill you will not be disappointed . with circle , you probably will .
