saw this movie in my english class this afternoon and was surprised by how bad this version was . do not get me wrong , george c . scott was terrific as scrooge , but the rest of the cast fails so very badly . sometimes i could not stop laughing at the stupid acting and the repeated line : merry christmas to everyone ! other times i almost fell asleep . the movie is based on a charles dickens short story about a rich guy , who do not think christmas is nothing but humbug . after 30 minutes , the rich guy is visited by three ghosts , who persuade him to celebrate christmas after all . i can not understand how this movie , with a script so bad it must have been written in five minutes , can be so well rated . instead of this piece of garbage , i recommend to you , the bill murray comedy scrooged . that at least , was funny . . .
