johnny and jeremy are vampires of sorts . minus the fangs , of course . they are dark , bitter creatures with nothing better to do than to spread their own misery . through their charms namely a sharp tongue and a fat wallet , respectively they seduce desperate souls , who they proceed to torment and victimize . that is more or less the basis of this black comedy , as i understand it . it is not a blend of black humor that i can easily subscribe to , partly because it bothers me to imagine the audience rooting for the sleazy , main character . i did enjoy , however , the sound and the melody of the rapid fire and supposedly very witty remarks . i was very impressed by the cast is strong acting , particularly david thelis is ; only the character of jeremy seemed too bi dimensional . the photography and the music , both dramatic and somber , work very well together . what really turns me off about naked and the main reason i would never recommend it to anyone is the way it repeatedly seems to present misogyny as a valid way to vent one is angst . in other words , in a world that sucks so bad , what difference does it make if one inflicts some pain on girls , right ? to suggest as some have on this website that johnny is not so unkind a person because he is not as rough on girls as jeremy , seems completely absurd to me . they are both terrible , nasty people . and they are particularly keen on hurting women every single time they get a chance . one could argue that johnny eventually gets what he deserves , as if his bad karma suddenly swung straight back and bit him in the ass . but still , his and jeremy is sadistic behavior are treated to a certain degree as a laughing matter . and i could be wrong , but i am guessing that most people who absolutely love this movie also find that aspect of the film darkly comical .
