before stan laurel became the smaller half of the all time greatest comedy team , he laboured under contract to broncho billy anderson in a series of cheapies , many of which were parodies of major hollywood features . following a dispute with anderson , laurel continued the informal series of parodies at joe rock is smaller and more indigent production company . most of laurel is parody films were only mildly funny at the time , and even less funny for modern audiences who have not seen the original movie which laurel is parodying . west of hot dog is a fairly generic parody of cowboy shoot em ups . it is marginally a specific parody of west of the pecos , an oater released two years earlier with no major actors . since west of the pecos was never a huge success , it is difficult to see why stan is film unit chose this particular movie as a target for their lampoonery , much less why they waited so long after its release to parody it . and where did they get that title west of hot dog ? possibly it is down to the fact that hot dog ! was a sexual interjection favoured by american lechers in the 1920s . as in the opening scene of the stage play amachinal . west of hot dog was produced and co directed by joe rock . among his many other achievements , rock introduced laurel to lois neilson , and he was subsequently best man at their wedding . full disclosure : in the last years of his life , i had the great privilege of befriending joe rock and interviewing him . nearly ninety years old at the time , rock is memory was impressively clear . . . but he remembered nothing at all about west of hot dog , and i cannot blame him . this movie is eminently forgettable . the leading lady is character is named little mustard : if that is meant to be a parody of something in west of the pecos , i do not get it . there are a couple of impossible gags here , including laurel is method for mounting a horse . for just one moment in this movie , stan laurel reminded me of the great buster keaton when he suddenly broke into a run . west of hot dog is vaguely similar in subject matter and tone to keaton is short comedy the frozen north , but keaton is version is much funnier . the plot of this film somewhat anticipates a situation in keaton is feature our hospitality but again suffers by comparison : here , two tough varmint brothers expect to inherit the last chance saloon , but the previous owner has bequeathed it to weakling tenderfoot stan . however , the brothers will become the legal heirs if stan dies . hmmm . . . seriously , though : is such a bequest legal ? as soon as stan takes possession of the property , surely any further questions of ownership or inheritance become his decision , not the previous owner is decision . i had plenty of time to consider such points of law while watching this dull comedy . at one point , a gag involves some crude animation drawn directly onto the film stock . it looks cheap and is not funny . this hot dog is no weiner , and no winner : it is just a whiner . my rating : one point out of 10 . hang on , stan : in a few more years you will be one half of a comedy legend .
