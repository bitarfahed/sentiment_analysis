stanley kramer directs an action thriller and leaves out two key things : action and thrills . the domino principle features gene hackman as a convict sprung from prison in order to perform some mysterious task . richard widmark , edward albert , and eli wallach are his operatives they presumably work for the government , but that , like most of the movie is plot line , is never made clear . hackman asks a lot of questions that never get answered so the film goes absolutely nowhere . while it strives to be like night moves and the parallax view , the domino principle mixes up ambiguity and mystery with confusion and boredom . the film is extremely well photographed but even that works against it . kramer is direction is devoid of any style . it is a very sunny movie ! the acting is fine with hackman proving he is pretty much incapable of being bad . widmark and wallach are suitably nasty and albert is well cast as widmark is cruel lackey . even the usually obnoxious mickey rooney is pretty good as hackman is sidekick . one oddity however is the casting of candice bergen as hackman is wife . we are told she is done time in prison and she seems to be trying to put on some sort of southern twang . kramer is idea of making her appear to be trailer trash is to have her wear an ugly brown wig . it is a role better suited for the likes of valerie perrine or susan tyrell .
