when i had first heard of solar crisis then got a load of the cast , i wondered why i had never heard of a movie with such a big cast before . then i saw it . now i know . for a movie that encompasses outer space , the sun , vast deserts and sprawling metropolises , this is an awfully cramped and claustrophobic feature ; it feels like everyone is hunkered close together so the camera will not have to pull too far back . and the effects , while good , are pretty underwhelming ; we are talking about the imminent destruction of the planet earth if a team of scientists and soldiers cannot deflect a deadly solar flare . but other than shouting , sweating and a red glow about everything , there is no real feel of emergency . do not get me started about the cast . what heston , palance , matheson , boyle , et al are doing in this movie without even bothering to act with any feel for the material is anyone is guess . makes you wonder who else is condos are not paid for in hollywood . . . . and as far as the end goes . . . . well , let is just say it is tense and intriguing but it is too little too late in an effort like this . if it had kept up that kind of pace all through the film , maybe i would have heard of solar crisis sooner . two stars . mostly for lost opportunities and bad career moves . i wonder how alan smithee keeps his job doing junk like this ?
