this romantic comedy is not too bad . there are some funny things happening here and there , and there are some rather memorable characters in it . the acting , however , is amateurish with the exception of the banker . while some scenes are great fun , others are simply embarrassing . in particular , i found the romantic part of the story poor . all in all , i guess it is worth seeing if you like football and romantic comedies . it is not really a bad movie , and the ending did feel quite good . just do not expect anything out of the ordinary . fair enough if you have an hour and a quarter to kill .
