this movie is worth watching if you enjoy marvelling over special effects . there are some interesting visuals . aside from that , it is typical nineties aughties hollywood fare of dazzle without substance . true to the title . it is not worth picking apart the story . that is like performing brain surgery on a dinosaur . there is not much there to begin with . it is nothing original and not very special . so do not go in for the story at all . just look at the effects . as has been mentioned , it got a little flashy at the end , diluting the purity of great fx treatment of an invisible and at times half invisible man . however if you ignore the standard pyrotechnics , it is a sight to behold or not to behold . all in all , it is a decent fx film worth seeing for that purpose and that alone .
