i had recently been watching johnny test in an attempt to find humor in it . i failed , horribly . cartoon network usually has a tendency to make their shows enjoyable by all audiences , but johnny test is entertainment in it is lowest form . the writing is incredibly predictable , and the running gags are not much gags at all . kids will love it , and that is about it . now , this is not to say that it is all bad . the original opening theme was actually pretty catchy , but for some reason they took the skeleton of it and figuratively smashed it with a figurative aluminum bat . it is a shame , because that was really one of the best things it had going for it . some of the characters could be very interesting , in theory . with a little work , the characters could work well together , but they are too one dimensional . then again , this makes it easy for the kids to follow . the pace is a bit too fast as well . the episodes are too busy , leaving little time for clever writing . this is a real shame , because there are so many interesting concepts that the show brings forth . on the upside , however , the fast pace will stop the kids from losing interest , and that is really the entirety of the target audience . overall , the show looks very good on paper , but just does not succeed in being funny or interesting . this is a show i want to like , but i am incapable of it . there is just so much potential that is not realized . kids will enjoy it , but that is about it .
