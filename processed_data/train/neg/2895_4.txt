the premise of this movie is revealed on the dvd box . a textile worker develops a miracle fabric that does not degrade . but the movie fails to get on with it . instead it pads for 45 minutes , noodling around a preamble before he makes the big discovery . since audiences do not benefit much from seeing a whiz kid figuring things out , it is a strange choice : the movie has successfully been prevented from engaging any topic . once the fabric is discovered , the movie too rapidly establishes that both industry bigwigs , and blue collar co workers want the invention squelched , leaving the movie with just two flimsy movements ; inventing the chemical , and running from oppressors . i cannot understand why anyone would describe this as comedy . the tone is not funny or comical . it is more like serious social criticism of the day : that capitalism warps both supply chains and production . which in turn prevents innovation from reaching and improving the world . yes , that is probably true , but without some toying with an attitude towards that fact , the movie is simply an earnest argument . you will need an extremely broad definition of comedy to find any here . this is more like a british meet john doe meet nigel doe ? .
