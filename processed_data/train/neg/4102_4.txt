wouldead letter office is a low budget film about a couple of employees of the australian postal service , struggling to rebuild their damaged lives . unfortunately , the acting is poor and the links between the characters past misfortunes and present mindsets are clumsily and over schematically represented . what is most disappointing of all , however , is the portrayal is life in the office of the film is title : there is no mechanisation whatsoever , and it is quite impossible to ascertain what any of the staff really do for a living . granted , part of the plot is that the office is threatened with closure , but this sort of office surely closed in the 1930s , if it ever truly existed . it is a shame , as the film is overall tone is poignant and wry , and there is some promise in the scenario : but few of the details convince . overall , it feels the work of someone who has not actually experienced much of real life ; a student film , with a concept and an outline , but sadly little else .
