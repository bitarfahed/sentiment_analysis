although i have enjoyed bing crosby in other movies , i find this movie to be particularly grating . maybe because i am from a different era and a different country , but i found crosby is continual references to the good old usa pleasant at first , trite after a while and then finally annoying . do not get me wrong i am not anti american whatsoever but it seemed that the english could do no right and or needed this brave , oh so smart american visitor to show them the way . it is a fish out of water story , but unlike most movies of this sort , this time it is the fish who has the upper hand . to be fair to both myself and the movie , i have watched it a few times spaced over a few years and get the same impression each time . i watched another crosby movie last night the emperor is waltz and that , too , produced the same reaction in me . and to my surprise even my wife who for what is it is worth is american found the in your face attitude of american crosby to be irritating . one too many references to teddy roosevelt , as she put it . as for the premise of the movie , it is unique enough for its day and the supporting cast is of course very good . the scenery and the music is also good , as are the great costumes although i agree with a previous reviewer that the wig on william bendix looks horrid picture moe of the three stooges . all in all for me this would be a much more enjoyable picture without the attitude of bing crosby but because he is in virtually every shot it is pretty hard to sit through this movie .
