i might have given this movie a higher rating before peter jackson is trilogy came out , but seeing the two of them side by side there is simply no comparison . the pace of this movie is rushed , many important scenes from the book are left out , and there is little character development . the animation is a strange mixture of traditional cartoon drawings and live action scenes that were painted over , which i found distracting . and the most disappointing thing about this movie is that it breaks off in the middle of the story and was never finished . there are some good points the battle scenes are exciting to watch , and the dialogue follows the book pretty much to the letter . watch this one if you are in a hurry and cannot spend 10 hours watching the new trilogy . but if you have not read the book you will probably be confused , because there is a lot missing from this version . 4 out of 10 .
