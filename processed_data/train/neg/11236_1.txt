as a former kalamazoo resident with a fondness for the town i was looking forward to seeing this movie . but , what a disappointment ! although the acting and the production values are not bad , the script is awful , the plot is unrealistic , and the theme is disturbing . the main message of this film is that women are nothing without husbands and children . i can hardly believe how regressive it is in it is view of women . has the writer been living under a rock ? although i enjoyed seeing my beloved city on the big screen , i would not suggest this movie to anyone . it is terrible . it is an embarrassment to the city it is named after .
