well , i do not think the picture is as bad as most of the reviews make it out to be . . . but there is no denying that it is got problems . mostly , the problems are in the script . there is a plot but not much story , and certainly not one that anybody could call plausible ; it trots out any number of self consciously strange and or stereotypical characters , lines , moments , what have you and , by the end , it just has not added up to much in this department . sorry , but i could not care less about whatever social ill farnsworth might be trying to address ; there will always be a sector of the population willing to do just about anything to shred their brains , even if it requires running around corn fields trying to steal ammonia , or whatever it is those morons do . so , as a film , you will not find me calling iowa important . but , at a stylistic level , the picture is more than interesting and some of farnsworth is choices in depicting a meth head is wigged out state are beautiful , hilarious , disturbing and yes , i am going to say it inspired . the acting is uneven , but that just may be a casualty of the afore praised stylistic reaching . look , rosanna arquette is a fine actress but she is not very good here , so a discriminating audience member does have to ask , what happened ? it is weird that diane foster manages a simplicity and grace that so few of the other actors can come anywhere near . for example , i might seriously consider whatever explanation farnsworth could provide for michael t . weiss is over the top turn as a probation officer , but i doubt i would ever buy it ; it just does not work . then again , it is the most alive and in the moment that i have seen john savage appear in years . so go figure . this is the sort of work that tantalizes , but does not promise and that is okay ; neither farnsworth nor anyone else is required to make movies . so , whether or not farnsworth has another film in him remains to be seen , but if he does , it seems pretty likely that it will not be bland pap . in an age when people are planning their lives around the latest installment of american idol , perhaps we could allow , not scorn , farnsworth is legitimate and undeniably flawed film . what is more , perhaps we could welcome , not berate , his energetic and sometimes blessedly idiosyncratic imagination .
