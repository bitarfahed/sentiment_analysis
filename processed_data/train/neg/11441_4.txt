after a promising first 25 minutes that makes you feel all warm inside , you are pretty convinced that this will be a great romantic comedy . then the movie takes a turn for the worse . the warm feeling might still be there , but as others has said : the plot becomes so unbelievable and artificial that it is almost unbearable to watch . the movie gets sped up , and you get the impression that you are either fast forwarding through it , or that the producers decided to fit it in less than 1h40m and had to cut a lot of scenes out . realism is not a goal onto itself , but as a viewer , i am pretty convinced that this comedy is not intentionally unrealistic , it just happens to be . on the plus side , this movie has a couple of nice interiors , and despite the bad script , i think that the actors performances are mainly good . if i could rate the first 25 minutes only , i would probably give it an eight . as it is now , it gets a four . . . . and that is being nice ! if you are a sucker for romantic comedies you will probably have a great time anyways . if not , i would recommend that you watch something else .
