t . rama rao made some extremely beautiful films in the 1980s , but he seems to be a filmmaker who cannot mature with the changing times , styles and fashions . he is like stuck with the same old fashioned film making style . actors are not bad , not good either . anil kapoor generally acts convincingly his two roles of a father and his son , but the flawed script often makes him look funny and pathetic . rekha is good , but then she is always good , and here she is nothing more than such . she makes the best of what she is given , but she always does that . in conclusion , nothing great at all . raveena is ok , which means ordinary , not bad , not good , nothing . this film is melodramatic , occasionally stupid . maybe it is a delayed film ? well , even then it still would be below standard . the script is terrible , the film is overdone , and the story goes nowhere . it feels like a film made in the early 1990s , but the script makes it look even older , the style is like from the 1950s . do not recommend , unless you are a big fan one of the starring actors .
