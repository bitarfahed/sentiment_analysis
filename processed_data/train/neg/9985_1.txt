have i ever seen a film more shockingly inept ? i can think of plenty that equal this one , but none which manage to outdo it . the cast are all horrible stereotypes lumbered with flat dialogue . i am ashamed for all of the people involved in making this . each one wears an expression of fear not generated by the plot , but by the realisation that this project could easily nix their career . even the many charms of ms . diaz do not provide an adequate reason to subject yourself to this . avoid , it is obviously a style of film that americans have not really got a grasp of . watch the final result if you must , and you will see what i am talking about , but do not say i did not warn you . . .
