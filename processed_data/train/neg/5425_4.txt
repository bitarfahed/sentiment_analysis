i got mirror mirror mainly because yvonne de carlo was in it i thought she was great in american gothic but sadly she did not have a very big role in this film . it starts off ok and the pace moves along nicely . . . but by the end it starts getting a bit tedious and dull . that is not to say that this is a boring film , but it is just very average and nothing spectacular . i did not like the posession side of it and there were no decent gore scenes . plus the amain story was very confusing and the ending does not make much sense at all . i did however like the story surrounding the gothic girl and how she got revenge on her tormentors . i would not particularly recommend mirror mirror to horror fans it is nothing to wet yourself over .
