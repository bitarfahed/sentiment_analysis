otto preminger directs this light as a feather story . bohemian jean seberg and her equally bohemian widower father david niven holiday in the south of france with nutty myl ne demongeot . things are fine until family friend deborah kerr shows up . nivens , a degenerate womanizer , finds the conquest of kerr too hard to resist . that is fine with seberg , as long as niven loves her and leaves her as he is done with all the women in his past . . . including demongeot . when it appears as though she is becoming second banana in niven is life , seberg exact revenge on kerr . preminger tells the story in flashbacks from seberg is perspective and cleverly combines black and white with sunnier color scenes . the cinematography by georges p rinal is stunning . the film features some of preminger is least heavy handed direction , although he rarely allows any close ups , which makes it difficult to make out what the actors are really feeling . arthur laurents wrote the script and it is full of acidic dialog and funny scenes mostly involving bird brained demongeot . seberg acquits herself fairly well , but niven is at his least appealing . . . and he shows no chemistry with either seberg or kerr . preminger really mis steps with that casting . it is a role that seems tailor made for someone closer to charles boyer . with geoffrey horne as seberg is would be suitor and martita hunt as his daffy mother . juliette gr co , playing herself , sings the title song in a paris nightclub . the great titles are by preminger regular saul bass .
