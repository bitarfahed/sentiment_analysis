generally , i have found that if you do not hear about a movie prior to seeing it on dvd , there is probably a good reason for it . i had not heard about this movie at all until i was in a blockbuster the other day and saw it on a shelf . since all the good movies had already been rented out the ones i wanted to see , anyway , i figured i would give this one a shot . it is really not much different than other movies in the genre , such as the singles ward or the r . m . if you are into those type movies , you will probably enjoy this . however , if you are not a mormon , this movie probably will not appeal to you . there is no way to avoid the overtly religious mormon message contained within , and at times it comes across as sappy and cheesy . ultimately , if you do not fall within the mormon demographic , you are probably better off watching something else . admittedly , there were some very funny moments in the film , but i did not think that it was enough to salvage the movie overall .
