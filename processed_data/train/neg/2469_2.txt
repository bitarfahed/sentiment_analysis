i suppose if you like endless dialogue that does not forward the story and flashy camera effects like the scene transitions in the television show angel , you will enjoy the film . me ? all i wanted was a nice , tight little story , and it was not there . the pacing was practically backward , plot points were buried under a sea of unneeded dialogue , and there was absolutely no sense of dread , or tension , or anything . is it the redneck ? is it the wendigo ? no , it is a cameraman on speed . that is not scary . it does not generate a single note of tension or atmosphere unless you are scared by mtv . like those reviewers before me , i too noticed that by the end the movie invokes derisive laughter from the audience . terrible film .
