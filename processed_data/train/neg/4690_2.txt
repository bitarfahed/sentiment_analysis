this british film is truly awful , and it is hard to believe that glenn ford is in it , although he pretty much sleepwalks through it . the idea of a bomb on a train sounds good . . . but it turns out this train ends up parked for the majority of the film ! no action , no movement , just a static train . the area where the train is parked is evacuated , so it is not like there is any danger to anyone either . in fact , this film could be used in a film class to show how not to make a suspense film . true suspense is generated by letting the audience know things that the characters do not , a fact apparently unknown to the director . spoiler : the train actually has two bombs on it , but we are led to believe there is only one . after the first bomb is defused , it feels as if there is no longer a reason to watch the film any more . but at the last minute , the villain , who has no apparent motivation for his actions , reveals there are two . nor are we certain when the bombs will go off , so we do not even have a classic ticking bomb tension sequence . a good 10 minutes or more are spent watching glenn ford is french wife thinking about leaving him , and then wondering where he is . she is such an annoying character that we do not care whether she reconciles with him , so when she does , there is nothing emotional about it . most of the other characters are fairly devoid of personality , and none have any problems or issues . it is only 72 minutes , but it feels long because it is tedious and dull . do not waste your time .
