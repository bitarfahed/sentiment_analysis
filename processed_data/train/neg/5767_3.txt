as said before , the visual effects are stunning . they are breathtaking . i personally use blender and graphics like that are not easy at all . but that is all this movie is . not only is the plot confusing , but the overall conflict is not clear . for example , in the first scene , proog and emo are trying to run away from who knows what . the conflict seems to be between man and nature here . later , when they enter the room of the bottomless pit , proog explains that one step out of place and you are dead . here , there is a more precise conflict between the careless man and nature . as the movie progresses , it is clear that a conflict exists between man and nature . but suddenly , a conflict exists between man and man when proog , out of nowhere , murders emo . proog immediately changes from being a caring guardian looking after a lost child to being a sick man . he betrays us . not only is this depressing , but we do not care because the conflict between the character is thoughts and actions is not developed . it is not a story about someone , through struggle , emerging stronger . it is depressing and has not point because there is no great truth about the human soul or about the world brought to light like a great drama does . in my opinion , the movie is severely underdeveloped in all aspects . however , the graphics are stunning , but a movie is so much more than mere eye candy . there is no truth , no struggle and a bad surprise ending . in conclusion , an underdeveloped movie without a point . . . . but the graphics are good .
