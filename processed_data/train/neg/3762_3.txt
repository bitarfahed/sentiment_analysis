i got this movie out of blockbuster in one of those racks were you can get like 5 movies for 20 bucks . i would have to say i got my money is worth on this one . i had expected horrible dialogue , crappy monsters , and shaky cameras . well , as meatloaf said , two outta three ai not bad . the acting is bad , though not as bad as some movies i have seen . or maybe i have watched so many low budget movies recently i have lost perspective . there are some bits were the acting is downright terrible , but for the most part it is of at least high school play level . the cg for the sasquatch in this movie is probably the second worst part . the first thing i thought when i saw it and i noticed another reviewer agreed with me was that a man in an ape suit would have been better . clunky stop motion animation would have looked better . so you may be asking why i call the cg the second worst part . that is because the very worst part of the movie is the sound effects . they are loud , annoying , and constant . i have been camping , i know what insects sound like in the woods at night , and while they can be loud , they are not deafening like the cacophony in this movie . usually when the background sounds drown out the movie is dialogue , it is a bad thing , but from what i caught of the dialogue of this film , i was not missing much . the action was infrequent and boring . the tension was non existent , as was any sense of empathy with the characters . speaking of the characters , they were all cookie cutter and bland . the only mildly engaging byplay was between . . . actually , i cannot think of anything . there was a line or two that made me crack a wan smile , but that was about it . the cinematography was decent , a step or two above what you would normally see in a movie like this . however , it still had that home movie quality to it that you get with movies made on pocket change and a prayer . if you are like me and get a kick out of shoestring budget genre flicks , and you see this one in the dollar bin , think about grabbing it . otherwise , stay away at all costs .
