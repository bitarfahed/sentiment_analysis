an expedition led by hunky captain storm mark dana travels to the valley of the kings in cairo to find out what happened to an earlier expedition . they meet beautiful mysterious simira ziva rodann who joins them . they soon find themselves faced with a blood drinking mummy . . . and only simira seems to know what is going on . a real snoozer . i caught this on late night tv when i was about 10 . it put me to sleep ! seeing it again all these years later i can see why . it is slow moving , the mummy does not even show up until 40 minutes in and this is only 66 minutes long ! , the acting ranges from bad dana to real bad george n . neise and there is no violence or blood to be found . this movie concentrates more on second rate dramatics involving a silly love triangle than horror . this rates three stars because it actually looks pretty good , everyone plays it straight , there is some good acting from diane brewster , it is short and the mummy attack scenes all three of them are not bad . they are not scary just mildly creepy . still , this movie is pretty bad . a sure fire cure for insomnia .
