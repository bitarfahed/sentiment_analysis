i have read all the rave reviews here and am impressed with the imagination of those who loved this film . i cannot say that i found much to recommend it . the leonard cohen sound track is not only excessively heavy handed but dreary beyond measure . the film looks authentic enough , but something is got to happen for it to work , and nothing much does : a cursory plot not a real problem for me , not much character development , nothing thematically . it just slogs along . flawed as it is , cimino is heaven is gate has some moments of genuine wonder and is a film i would sooner watch again . for a brilliant reconception of the west , hbo is deadwood is much superior to mccabe .
