the most positive thing i can say for this dull witted local comedy production is that it is inoffensive . in fact it is so astonishingly bland that one wonders how many dozens of re writes by committee it went through to have such a complete removal of personality . it is not witty , it is not entertaining , it is not insightful , and it is not charming . it is just a staid , laughless , progression of four losers who must change their ways and their attitudes towards women to be allowed to attend their best friend is wedding . with acting that would be sub par for the local amateur dramatics society , a plot line so tired it would make a forty third season of allo allo look fresh , and jokes about as humorous as watching decaying vegetables , sione is wedding nonetheless scored ten yes 10 nominations in the nz film awards recently . fortunately , somebody saw sense and it did not win any .
