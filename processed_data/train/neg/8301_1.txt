really bad . why anyone thinks this is a good film let alone funny is a true mystery . i like comedies as much as the next man and i loved a christmas story . the fact that it has the same director and was based on the same writer is memoirs has me completely puzzled as to why this film is such a complete failure on every level . charles grodin is woefully miscast as the father for starters . for another it does not seem to have the same pacing it just does not flow well . everything seems tired and forced . the joy of life that permeated the first film is completely absent here you just want the movie to end . i would not even recommend this movie for curiosity seekers who enjoyed a christmas story . it is that bad . 1 10 .
