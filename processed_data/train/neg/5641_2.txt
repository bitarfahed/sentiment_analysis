predictable , told a thousand times story with the usual drama in between , a couple of pretty raunchy sex scenes intermingled with some character paranoia , 70 is style incidental violin horror music that is comical at times , i could not help chuckling to myself . i usually like defoe , and it has to be said that the acting is not all that bad , the plot develops at a reasonable pace and does keep you guessing from time to time . its just that it is all too predictable , i felt like i was watching a made for tv drama instead of a new movie . maybe thats the style the directors wanted , but it has to be said that the review i read on here before i saw the movie could only have been written by someone involved in its production . do not expect too much , and if i could wind the clock back i would not have gone to see it at the cinema . i would wait for the bargain bins at your video shop , i am sure it wont take long .
