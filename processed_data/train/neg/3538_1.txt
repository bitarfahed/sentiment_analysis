i will tell you what . he would have told him to f k off , blown his head away , and watch with utter disdain as his equally inept partner waddles away as fast as his chubby little legs would carry him . i mean what are we supposed to believe is going through jake is head when they offer him their solution to his problem ? they are con men , therefore they must obviously also have the skill to cure incurable blood diseases ! i mean ffs . does not he start to wonder why his symptoms are not getting worse ? does not the penny drop on the third day what is happening instead of richie subjecting the audience to a painfully patronising phone call from avi to jake to let him know he is been conned . anyway , i can add a small positive note to the film by moving on to the dry humour if provides , thankfully of a similar standard to his previous films . bulls t ! this film does not try anything as smart as redeeming itself through some well timed amusing lines , oh no . it somehow managed to be so disastrously unfunny i genuinely did not hear so much as a titter from a completely packed cinema and anyone who knows the ugc in sheffield knows how full a main screen can get , and not 1 person so much as smiled . maybe he never wanted the film to be funny , and fair enough you can still make good gangster films without comedy , but what was he planning on hanging this film on may i ask ?
