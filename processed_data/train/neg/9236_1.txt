i am among millions who consider themselves cary grant fans , but i cannot think of a single reason to recommend this movie . i do not understand the casting of betsy drake and it appears no one else did , if we are to judge from the small number of films in which she played afterwards . most fans will agree that katharine hepburn was superb at chasing and catching cary grant in bringing up baby . here the director or writers try to rehash the idea , but it fails miserably . i have read comments about how creepy drake was , but i thought that was far too mild a description . franchot tone walked through this one as if he were hungover . a casting disaster is one thing . this film is a total disaster . this one does not deserve 10 lines of comments and i do not know why that is a requirement . too bad this one was preserved when so many worthwhile films lie rotting in vaults . unless you want to torture someone , give this one a wide berth .
