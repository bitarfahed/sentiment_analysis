this is one really bad movie . i have racked my brain and i cannot come up with one positive comment to make . the acting is atrocious . i have seen more believable performances on cable access . the plot is ridiculous . stolen diamonds , secret recordings of the president , and a shark that attacks anything that gets near it should have made for cheesy fun at the worst . night of the sharks is not even so bad it is good . the dialogue sounds and is delivered as if it were written seconds before it is filmed . and to top it off , night of the sharks has the worst soundtrack i have ever heard . i am surprised my ears did not start bleeding from the 80s techno synthesized sounds that someone actually bothered to record . from everything i have read , the italian film industry was dead by 1987 . night of the sharks is like a final nail in the coffin .
