this was quite possibly the worst film i have ever seen . the plot did not make a whole lot of sense and the acting was awful . i am a big fan of amber benson , i think she is usually a wonderful actress , i cannot imagine why she decided to do this film . her character , piper , is drunk for almost the whole film , with the exception of the opening scene . on the plus side , there was several points in the film where the acting was so bad , i actually laughed out loud . but despite that , i would not recommend this film to anyone . it is only 80 minutes long , but that is 80 minutes of your life that you will have completely wasted .
