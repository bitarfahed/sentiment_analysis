ghost town starts as kate barrett catherine hickland drives along an isolated desert road , her car suddenly breaks down she hears horses hoofs approaching . . . deputy sheriff langley frank luz of riverton county is called in to investigate kate is disappearance after her father reports her missing . he finds her broken down car drives off looking for her , unfortunately his car breaks down too he has to walk . langley ends up at at a deserted rundown ghost town , much to his shock langley soon discovers that it is quite literally a ghost town as it is populated by the ghosts of it is former residents is run by the evil devlin jimmie f . skaggs who has kidnapped kate for reasons never explained it is up to langley to rescue her end the towns curse . . . the one only directorial effort of richard governor this odd film did not really do much for me i did not like it all that much . the script by duke sandefur tries to mix the horror western genres which it does not do to any great effect . have you ever wondered why there are not more horror western hybrid films out there ? well , neither have i but if i were to ask myself such a question i would find all the answers in ghost town because it is not very good . the two genres just do not mix that well . there are plenty of clich s , on the western side of things there is the innocent townsfolk who are to scared to stand up to a gang of thugs who are terrorising them , the shoot outs in the main street , saloon bars with swing doors prostitutes upstairs horror wise there is plenty of cobwebs , some ghosts , an ancient curse , talking corpses a few violent kills . i was just very underwhelmed by it , i suppose there is nothing terribly wrong with it other than it is just dull the two genres do not sit together that well . there are a few holes in the plot too , why did devlin kidnap kate ? i know she resembled his previous girlfriend but how did he know that what was he going to do with her anyway ? we never know why this ghost town is full of ghosts either , i mean what is keeping them there what caused them to come back as ghosts ? then there is the bit at the end where devlin after being shot says he cannot be killed only for langley to kill him a few seconds later , i mean why did not the bullets work in the first place ? director governor does alright , there is a nice horror film atmosphere with some well lit cobweb strewn sets the standard hollywood western town is represented here with a central street with wooden buildings lining either side of it . i would not say it is scary because it is not , there is not much tension either the film drags in places despite being only just over 80 odd minutes in length . forget about any gore , there a few bloody gunshot wounds , an after the fact shot of two people with their throats slit someone is impaled with a metal pole that is it . i would have imagined the budget was pretty small here , it is reasonably well made is competent if nothing else . credit where credit is due the period costumes sets are pretty good actually . the acting is alright but no ones going to win any awards . ghost town is a strange film , i am not really sure who it is meant to appeal to it certainly did not appeal to me . anyone looking for a western will be annoyed with the dumb horror elements while anyone looking for a horror film will be bored by the western elements . it is something a bit different but that does not mean it is any good , worth a watch if your desperate but do not bust a gut to see it .
