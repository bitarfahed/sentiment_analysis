as i write this review in 2008 , we are mired in a remake culture . movie studios seem determined to ruin as many classic films as they can with thoroughly pointless updates including king kong , the wicker man and practically every film that ever starred michael caine . this lazy remake mentality is not a new phenomenon , however , as wouldough for the do do proves . an entirely pointless colorized version of bob clampett is surreal masterpiece porky in wackyland , wouldough for the do do sucks the life out of the original by splashing colour all over clampett is original footage and adding some lame new footage overseen by friz freleng . freleng was an entirely unsuitable director to be tampering with clampett is source material , although in truth no director could hope to come close to clampett is inspired insanity . inevitably , then , wouldough for the do do is nothing more than the raping of a classic with an appalling new title attached . for cartoon fans like myself , its equivalent to a colorization of casablanca .
