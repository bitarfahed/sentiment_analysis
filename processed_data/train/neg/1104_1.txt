this is one of the worst written movies i have ever had to sit through . the story is nothing new but it is a cartoon , so who cares , as long as it is pretty and fun ? i am not going to go as deep as the characterisations , or i will be here all day except to say that there are not any ; the characters change personality whenever it is convenient to the plot , but whoever wrote the script and visual direction should be forbidden access to so much as pencil and paper . thumbs down ? i would vote to cut their thumbs off . narrow in on an object prop . cut back to character close up . character gives a knowing look , which the audience will not even remotely understand . repeat that several times , with different objects props . make the characters pay no attention at all the huge lumps of rock are floating around , crashing into each other , generally raining destruction all over , and which could kill them all at any moment but make them stop and gasp in fear when they see a harmless looking , almost pastoral green rock in the distance . the whole thing is a long succession of events , actions , and behaviour that are only there for the convenience of the writer , to save him having to think or make any effort at all to write the story properly . this is the plan 9 of cg cartoons , except that it does not have ed wood groan factor to make it fun to watch . do yourselves a favour : spend your cartoon budget on pixar movies .
