this is not art , it is inner urban , politically correct propaganda ! jindabyne is political intolerance is beyond unforgivable . . . it does not see people as individuals , but rather , as members of categories . this is the most patronisingly offensive australian movie i can recall ever and it is up against some pretty stiff competition ! . a message movie , every tired theme beloved of the trendy left is there : aborigines are victims ; white men are violent or alcoholics ; white women are not that bad particularly if they are lesbians but they are most likely of a depressive nature . four men who go away fishing , find the body of a murdered woman aboriginal , naturally and leave her in the river for several days while they catch trout . it is a strange decision taken with almost no discussion , as if the men are animals . the one man who briefly demurs is the goodie . . . we know this because he is living with a bisexual woman he likes to hold his baby a lot and eventually moves to a more fashionable costal location away from all these beastly bush dwellers . this is a film made by those trendy urbanites who live in fear of the australian landscape and those evil rednecks who reside within . it is ignorance of country life is almost as shocking as its contempt . the film is shot through with long distance views of the bush backed by foreboding , mysterious music . it is made very clear by the end that aboriginal people are the only ones at home in this landscape . it concludes with an excruciatingly implausible scene of black white reconciliation . in jindabyne , country life is reduced to little more than a backdrop for a story that by implication proclaims the superiority of the values of enlightened leftist urban dwellers over those of other australians . this film was not made by people with real jobs but funded by the government is film finance corporation . it is a product of the artsy set , that soulless void populated by the beautiful people for whom lavish government funding sustains these patronisingly offensive projects which are as detached from real life as possible , as opposed to actually making popular films people want to see . it does not matter if the film is a stinker , they still get paid . spare yourself from wasting time , avoid it like the plague . more jaded social commentary than actual entertainment , this film deserves to pan !
