i had to write a review of this film after reading another comment saying that this is sidney poitier is best movie . poitier had just returned from over a decade is break in film acting and he is clearly creaky here . 11 of his films are mentioned in wikipedia and they do not include this . 5 of his films are on the afi is list of top 100 inspiring movies , again , not including this . berenger and poitier , rube and city slicker set out to hunt down a dangerous psychopath before he crosses the border to canada . some of the attempts at comedy in this film clearly fail and berenger and poitier is bonding was cringeworthy and awkward not helped by a completely bland script . kirstie alley as the hostage was underused , and almost entirely ignored when she was on screen . some attempt at suspense is made , for example when you are meant to try and guess which of 5 men on a fishing trip is the murderer all of them are type cast villains . i understand that this is the entire appeal to most fans out there . i guessed who it was and i was not really trying hard . if you are a berenger fan , watch the sniper 1993 , you even get to see billy zane strutting his stuff . it is much better . all in all i would give shoot to kill 3 10 . it is not daring , and it is just too straightforward for me .
