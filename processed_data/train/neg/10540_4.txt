patrick channing jeff kober is a disciple of satan serial killer who possesses the first power : even after being captured by detective russell logan lou diamond phillips and executed in the gas chamber , he is able to move his spirit from body to body and continue to murder at will . with the help of attractive psychic tess seaton tracy griffith , melanie g . is half sister he attempts to stop channing . this concept probably had some possibilities , i think , but ultimately the first power suffers from routine scripting and film making . this is nothing we have not seen before , sometimes done better . there is nothing about this movie to distinguish it from other supernatural horror thrillers . more to the point , it is not very thrilling and it certainly is not scary . phillips is a hard sell as a tough as nails , cynical cop stereotype , and griffith does not seem to be trying very hard ; best cast member is probably the distinctively featured kober , doing his best to be supremely creepy . the climax is rather silly and the ending very weak . not really even acceptable enough to rate as an average film of its kind , therefore : 4 10
