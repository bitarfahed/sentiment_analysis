i am a huge charlton heston fan . he is without a doubt one of the greatest actors of all time , but what was he thinking when he made this movie . normally if he made a bad movie i could blame it on the screenwriter or director , but in this case it is all him . the suckiness of this movie is all his fault . it proves that not even heston can make a shakespeare story interesting . i wasted 2 and a half hours of my life on this snooze fest and i will never get that time back . this is by far the worst heston movie that i have ever seen . if you are a shakespeare fan maybe you will find this movie entertaining , but if you are not do not waste your time , you will regret it in the long run .
