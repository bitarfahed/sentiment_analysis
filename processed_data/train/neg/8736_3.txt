the movie had a good concept , but the execution just did not live up to it . what is this concept ? well , story wise , it is dirty harry meets m . a child killer has begun terrorizing a city . the lead detectives dennis hopper and frederic forest have never dealt with a serial killer before . the mayor and the police chief , in desperation , secretly hire the local mob to speed things up . . . to go places and do things that the police would not be able to in order to bring an end to this mess as soon as possible . to be fair , this film does genuinely have some good things to offer . besides the concept , i liked the look of the killer is hideout . norman bates has his basement . this guy has an eerie sewer . in some of the shots , the light bounces off the water and creates rippling reflections on the walls ; often giving these scenes a creepy , dreamlike quality . the acting was good too . dennis hopper is one of those actors who gets better with age . once you get past that , however , it more or less goes downhill . the film is paced way too fast . the actual investigation process from both teams feels very rushed as opposed to feeling intricate and fascinating . this could have been fixed in two ways : either make the film longer or cut out some of the many subplots . either of these would have allowed the crew to devote more time to the actual mystery . for an example of how bad this is , one of the crucial clues that helps them zero in on just the right suspect is this : at one point in his life , the suspect went to a pet shop . . . that is right . . . i am being totally serious here . it is like they went from point a the first clue to point z the suspect and skipped over all the in between steps . then there is the characters . the only ones i actually liked were two pick pockets you meet about half way through the movie . considering that they are minor characters , i would call that a bad sign . finally , there is the mob angle . this is the one that gets me the most because this is why i coughed up the 3 to buy the dvd in the first place . i mean , what a hook ! there is been an absolute glut of serial killer flicks in the last 10 15 years . the mob angle was a gimmick that could have helped it rise above the rest . . . , but it did not . i figured the gangsters is methods would be brutal , but fun and thrilling at the same time ; kind of like a vigilante movie or something . . . maybe they would even throw in some heist movie elements too . we are talking about criminals , after all . instead , we are given some of the most repulsive protagonists committed to celluloid . the detectives question witnesses . what does the mob do ? they interrogate and kill them . it is not even like these witnesses are really even that bad either . i actually found the criminals less likable than the killer they are hunting . unless the good points i mentioned are enough to get your interest , i would say give this one a miss . maybe some day , they will reuse the same story idea and do it right . i hope so . i hate to see such a good concept go to waste .
