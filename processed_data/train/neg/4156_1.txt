yes , this movie is bad . what is worse is that it takes no advantage whatsoever of its own title ! ! in the entire movie , zombies and vampires fight each other once or twice . on top of that , we are never really sure if the main character in the movie is definitely a vampire . one might argue they were trying to tone it down or make it realistic , but it ends up just boring . more than half of this movie takes place in a car . the scenes that take place anywhere else are not much to brag about , either . also , there is no clear antagonist , and in the end you have no idea what really happened for the last 30 minutes of the movie . however , i will say that for a film this low in production value , the soundtrack was surprisingly appropriate and instrumented with either an origonal score or sampled music from elsewhere . i am all for independent films , but it does not look like this was ever intended for a mass audience if any . worse than scarecrow slayer .
