a family looking for some old roadside attractions to include in the father is coffee table book come across an ancient , decrepit old freak show run by an eccentric one eyed man . when their family van breaks down upon leaving the sideshow , they are forced to stay at a nearly abandoned fishing camp that was the site of a prison break decades prior . there have been many films in the freak subgenre of horror , ranging from tod browning is beloved freaks 1932 to alex winter is hilarious freaked 1993 . those are both classics or soon to be with freaked . iside sho , however , never will be . and if it ever does reach classic status . . . well , it will be an obvious clue to the sad state of our genre . from the ridiculously bad opening song to the 17 year old daughter that is obviously older than her natural mother , this film did not have much going for it . the writing was subpar , but not completely awful . . . just boring . the direction was poor , and the rare freak effects were pretty horrendous and unbelievable . the acting was abysmal and the casting was even worse . anyone who would believe the ages of these two camp age teenagers must not have met a teenager in a long , long time . there was far from enough gore violence to make up for the lack of any other quality . . . and when there was a bit of violence , it was not well done at all . and , i cannot forget to mention the ending fight scenes which were , with all honesty , some of the worst i have ever , ever seen in a film . overall , this is an easily forgettable and poorly made horror film that deserves to be left alone at the bottom of the dollar bin . final verdict : 2 . 5 10 .
