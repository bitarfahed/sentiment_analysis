this is an extremely boring film . if you grew up during the vietnam was , as i did , then you have seen and heard all of the film footage and arguments here a hundred times by now . but what really makes this film boring is the narration by director carlton sherwood . the majority of the film is shot with sherwood talking directly into the camera about his opinion of this conflicting time in the history of our nation . if you are old enough to remember vietnam , then you will not find anything new here . there is no new evidence to condemn john kerry or new evidence worthy of another documentary on vietnam . younger viewers who are interested in the subject should see george butler is excellent film going upriver ! stolen honor was clearly meant to be a hatchet job on john kerry , but it fails miserably at accomplishing that goal .
