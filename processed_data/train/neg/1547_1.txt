i could name plenty of funny movies . there are comedies that set out to be funny , and are . some movies , like a gymkata for example , try to be serious but end up funny . the ladies man is a film that is desperately trying to be funny , but could not be less funny if it was about a guy who got a lot of chicks in the middle of the wreckage of a nuclear holocaust . it is anti funny . i do not think i laughed harder than a chuckle at anything in this movie . it is simply unfunny . it is boring , stupid , inane , annoying , mind bogglingly bad , but not funny . i do not particularly care for tim meadows , or this character from snl , but i expected better than this . the movie is completely lacking logic or common sense . it is like the script writer had a bag over his head while he was typing and he could not see which keys he was hitting . they tell the origin of the ladies man , but fail to include a motivation for his bizarre fascination with acting like it is still the seventies . the movie tries to get humor out of a man who appears to pleasuring himself to porn , shortly after he tried to hang himself . this is comedy ? i like to consider myself having a pretty keen sense of humor spending a lot of time writing comedy as i do , but maybe i am just not quite bright enough for this film . lee evans , so funny as tucker in there is something about mary , is outrageously bad here . i was pleading with him in my head to shut up . by the end i was pounding on my chair , muttering under my breath , and had the film gone on any longer , would probably have attempted suicide . this film might not be as bad as battlefield earth , but it is the first movie i have seen that is come close .
