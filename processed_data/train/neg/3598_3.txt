do you know when you look at your collection of old , videotaped movies , and realize that there are some that you have only seen once or twice , and you cannot remember if they are worth the time it takes to see them ? the alibi is was one of those films ; i found it , not long ago , and decided i might as well give it a chance . i am not entirely sure if i am happy with my decision . . . on one hand , the film is really , really bad , on the other , now i have another free tape . . . yeah , you get it . the plot is predictable and not in any way original . the pacing is bad . the acting is bad , but that is not really surprising , seeing as the two leads are former soap opera stars . . . they are used to overact . the characters are poorly written clich s . the film even manages to screw up the easiest damn way to impress me through film : court scenes . even those do not elicit one single emotion for or against any of the cardboard thin characters . the film just has no real redeeming qualities whatsoever . . . even the dialog is bad . the thing is , it is so full of clich s that it is laughable . and that is the one thing that lifts this above a rating of 1 10 : the albeit unintentionally so comic relief of the many clich s and stereotypes . i did not pay very much attention to the film , but just about every time i looked at the screen , there was something to laugh at . one final note : i considered using the line tori spelling cannot act as a one line summary , but i guess everyone knows that , so i opted for the current one , seeing as it is more informative . all in all , a thoroughly bad film , but not the worst if you have got nothing else to do and if it is on tv . good for a few laughs , if you can sit through it . 3 10
