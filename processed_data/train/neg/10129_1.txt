i can barely find the words to express how utterly utterly awful this film is . i was sold on the promise of action , with segal and stealth aircraft , which normally make for an entertaining action movie . i can honestly say i would rather gouge my eyes out with a cocktail stick than have to see this film again . the acting was so awful that it was almost funny . the story was insanely weak , with plot holes so cavernously wide you could fly an f117 through them . the script was so poor , if i found out that a 10 year old wrote it i should not be in the least surprised . the direction and production is so amateur , i would not even hire these people to shoot my worst enemies wedding video . utter utter drivel . those responsible for making this movie have cheapened the art , and they should be ashamed of themselves . steven segal should never ever show his face in public again , i cannot imagine what made him agree to star in this , the worst film i have seen in my entire life .
