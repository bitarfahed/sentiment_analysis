it is interesting to see what the director tried to do with this film . but the problem is that it is not very good . there was nothing really original in the film and while the plot was well presented , the main characters were all a bit to shallow and you did not bother for any of them . rather bland and sometimes downright bad photo leaves a bit to be desired but i guess you cannot expect to much from people who are just doing a low budget film for the heck of it . it is unfair to review the film and compare it to other high budget films . but alas , that is what one must do . on its own , it is not very good . and compared to others , it is still not very good . but it is not without its good points ! i liked the plot . it was built up rather nicely and tied together well at the end . sometimes in the really dark scenes , it managed to build up a creepy feeling as well . however in the end the film fails to impress . the characters are pretty much non existent and we do not care for any of them . any of them might die , but it is possible to pinpoint the final survivor from very early on .
