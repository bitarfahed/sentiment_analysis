mona the vagabond lives on the fringes of french society , in a life without meaning , purpose or direction . i watched this because of all the stellar reviews , but i am afraid i must have missed something . the character of mona has little or no personality while drifting through life being rude to people , getting high and contributing nothing to anyone is life . she is not interesting or exciting . she is just useless . i have seen and known enough people like that : there is no secret meaning to what they are doing . they are just lazy bums . i would not want mona anywhere near me , as she tends to steal anything that is not nailed down and leave her friends in the lurch . sure she is enigmatic because there is not anything to her . lots of junkies , winos and bums i have seen are enigmatic ; i would not want to see a film about them either . possibly there is something there that i totally missed . otherwise i am assuming that all the reviews are from people who assume anything done by a french female director is high art .
