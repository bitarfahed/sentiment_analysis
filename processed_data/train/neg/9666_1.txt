having seen triumph of the will , i can only say this movie is ghastly , even measured against the historically low standards of the time . naturally it is all totally fabricated and prejudicial . this is what one would expect of 1930 is german propaganda . unfortunately , the quality of the presentation , itself , is hackneyed and cheap . it is also so blatantly ridiculous that even contemporary germans must have left the theater holding their noses . in a genre renowned for its base appeal , lack of originality and unapologetic wrong headedness , this film does not even qualify as bad . it would have to improve significantly to attain that status !
