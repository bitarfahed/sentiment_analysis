this movie was long and boring . surprising that it was selected for cannes , although they tend to like pretentiousness . point is that contrary to other dutch stars of the arty genre like kerkhof and kruishoop , guernsey feels utterly empty . even more so it has no cinematic quality whatsoever . a long opening shot does not mean cinematic depth , it is just a long boring shot . the story was not interesting and the characters had problems i could not identify with at all . the actors did not shine under her direction and seemed lost at times . leopold tried , but she is not the talent i hoped she would be . where are kerkhof and kruishoop ? they really made some waves in dutch cinema . leopold just made another attempt .
