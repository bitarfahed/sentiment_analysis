the hills eyes ii , one of the most pointless and blatantly stupid sequels to come around in some time , is 90 minutes of incompetent film making at its finest . or worst , however you choose to look at it . while 2006 is hills remake was one of the year is best , and truly frightening , horror films , this sequel takes every spark out of what made that such an accomplishment . part 2 never gets off the ground , and neither does its mind numbing dialogue . worst of all , it is not that scary . 2006 is remake followed a family who find themselves in the middle of the new mexico desert , deserted , and one by one being picked off by deranged and sadistic hill people . people who , as a result of the military testing the atomic bomb on their land years ago , have become who they are . surviving off travelers who wander into the region . the sequel puts audiences in the same desert , now occupied by the military as they covertly investigate the hills and what might have happened to that poor family . when a group of military trainees are brought to the campsite , they find it deserted with no signs of life . a grim reality soon befalls them , as they come to the realization that they are not alone . and the bloody fate that was handed to many before them will soon become their destiny . it does not take a genius to realize that hills has no legitimate reason to exist . but because last year is remake was received well both at the box office and by critics , it came to no surprise that a sequel would be rushed into production while there is still money to be earned . there is no rhyme or reason to it this time around , just an unbelievable and ridiculous set up to pave the way for thoughtless characters , unoriginal killings , a non existent story , and slipping interest . originally , director alexander aja made craven is cult classic into a remake that was a unique and thoroughly disturbing experience . one that gruesomely crossed the line on more than one occasions . its frank display of violence , sadistic torture , well rounded characterization , and white knuckled suspense were all effectively used to shock and repulse audiences . the second time around , it is rehashed hand me downs . there is no style , no grit . it tries to build up tension by dismembering bodies , when all it really does is make for a been there , done that kind film , where even the gore seems tame compared to more recent bloodbaths . it is a sad state of affairs when deformed mutants who capture women for breeding purposes fails to keep your attention . it is a bore , nothing more . hills has no bite . despite a jump or two here and there , there is nothing very scary about this by the numbers horror flick . it feels like something you would see on the sci fi channel , only with some f bombs , a blood splatter here and there , a rape , and a graphic birth scene that is more gross than shocking . it is cheap . and with hills , you reap what you sew . with no effort given , you cannot expect anything in return . replacing aja with martin weisz as director was the film is first big mistake , all he does is drain the film of any sort of emotional resonance . but even more shocking is the uncharacteristically bad script penned by wes craven and his son , jonathan craven . you ask , how bad could it possibly be ? this is the kind of dialogue that makes any comparison look like shakespeare . craven has had his fair share of clunkers in the past , but i would never expect something like this from him . it is so unintentionally funny , you have to wonder , is craven playing a joke on this ? or did he dump this one on his son after the studio payed him off ? the film is characters are one dimensional talking heads with no emotions or common sense . the acting is just as bad . the only character who may win you over is napoleon napoli , the scrawny kid who does not fit in with the others . even the deranged and instinct driven villains , who we might have found some favor with in the deepest of our thoughts a year ago , are met with indifferent . you do not hate them , you do not like them . you honestly could not care less . just like this movie . even if you were giddy with fear during the hills have eyes , as i was , you will have a tough time finding anything to enjoy in this piece of garbage . it is as generic as generic gets , and there is absolutely nothing here we have not seen done many times already . i cannot express this enough , avoid the hills have eyes ii like the plague . it is frightless , unoriginal , frantic , and a bore . stick to the remake or craven is original vision . because if you do not walk out after the first thirty minutes , do not say i did not warn you .
