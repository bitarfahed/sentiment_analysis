you may say to yourself , don johnson as elvis ? can that work ? is it possible ? seems like an terrible choice to me , but perhaps i should have an open mind . maybe i will be surprised . maybe he can pull it off . not ! don johnson is not a bad actor . but he is an awful elvis . he is too short , too weak voiced , too sharply featured . . . well you have already imagined how bad he would be . add to that a hokey black wig and heavy handed eye liner and mascara and it is a big fat embarrassing mess . the best i can say is that since johnson is acting is decent and since his impersonation is so far off , after a while you do not even think of him as elvis anymore . you see him as some other crazed pop star instead . then , on that level , the movie becomes watchable . stephanie zimbalist is also not ideally cast as the tall , beauty queen , linda thompson . but she is attractive in her own right and plays the part with the honesty , elegance and intelligence we have come to expect from all her roles . there may be too much intelligence in her performance . you have to be kind of a dope to stick with a dope abusing dope . there is nothing new to this story ; we have heard it many times before . if you have looking for new info or insight , you will not find it . it is told as a love story an unrequited one : linda for elvis and elvis for drugs .
