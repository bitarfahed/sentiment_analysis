if the crew behind zombie chronicles ever read this , here is some advice guys : 1 . in a twist ending type movie , it is not a good idea to insert close ups of every death in the movie in the opening credits . that tends to spoil the twists , y'know . . . ? 2 . i know you produced this on a shoestring and to be fair you worked miracles with your budget but please , hire people who can actually act . or at least , walk , talk and gesture at the same time . joe haggerty , i am looking at you . . . 3 . if you are going to set a part of your movie in the past , only do this if you have the props and costumes of the time . 4 . twist endings are supposed to be a surprise . sure , we do not want twists that make no sense , but signposting the reveal as soon as you introduce a character ? that is not a great idea . kudos to the guys for trying , but in all honesty , i would rather they had not . . . only for zombie completists .
