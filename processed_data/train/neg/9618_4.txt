i have to say although this movie was formulaic throughout with a plot stolen from films like friday the 13th i know what you did last summer , this movie was not that bad . in fact it was not as bad as most of the horror films hollywood has released recently . the killings although at times a little too imaginative were in most instances just that , original . the cast was mediocre which is to be expected from low budget features but much better than what that much bigger studio artisan lions gate has been offering . my only real complaint that was not due to the film is budget , which must have been small , was the contrived twist ending . i am sorry but this is what put this film in the bad category for me . the ending was just stupid and tacked on . before that i was a little bored , but actually enjoying it . 4 10
