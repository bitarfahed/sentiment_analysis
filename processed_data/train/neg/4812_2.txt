i figured that any horror film with orson welles in it would be weird . necromancy sure was but it was a little too weird for it is own good . the film does indeed have a creepy feel as it deals with a coven of satanists witches in a small town and a young woman is attempt to escape them . the director though seems to be deliberately trying to confuse the audience by using flashbacks and dream sequences . by the finale , there are too many unanswered questions . what is worse , as the story is so confusing , it is pretty hard to root for any of the characters . it seems odd that welles would agree to headline this film especially since he does not have that much to do . maybe someday they will put out a tape of the outtakes and bloopers from this movie . now that would really be fun !
