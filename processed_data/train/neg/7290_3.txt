what a long , drawn out , pointless movie . i am sure that historically this film is delightful but as entertainment goes it just does not make the grade . ralph fiennes has been in some fantastic movies , the english patient , schindler is list , but this one was such a let down . it did not seem to be going anywhere , his character at the beginning was so shallow and uptight it amazes me that his sister would ever have been interested at all . do not bother paying to rent this movie , buy yourself a copy of the english patient instead .
