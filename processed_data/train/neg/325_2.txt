having decided some time ago to collect the films of billy bob thornton on the strength of class movies like sling blade , a simple plan and the man who was not there amongst others , it was inevitable that there would be the odd turkey in there . what i did not realise however , was that there could be one this bad . i will give you an idea how incredibly poor this film is the funniest dialogue in it goes like this : knock knock , who is there ? , the big stinking man , the big stinking man who ? , the big stinking man is you ! . yes folks , it really is that bad . billy bob is only in it for about two minutes i guess he needed the work at that time in his career , and the rest of the movie is painful . for some reason though , although it is undeniably awful , i do not hate it . that is probably because i save my ire for any high budget , special effects laden junk like the fast and the furious and not a no budget flick like this one . 2 10 at a push .
