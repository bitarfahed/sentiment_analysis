this review is based on the dubbed shock o rama video released on an undeserving world in 2002 . how bad is it ? it is awful , which is what a 1 represents on the imdb scale but it is much worse than that . it is nice to imagine that an original german language print might improve matters the comedic english language dubbing is not funny at all but truthfully , this is one of the worst amateur films of any genre you are likely to see . the zombies in the film are as slow and clumsy as ever , and they do not seem to have the ability to speak or think about anything beyond their next meal . however , they are also intelligent enough to operate chainsaws and malicious enough to know that western taboos about genitalia will no doubt enliven their dinner table conversation . george romero is land of the dead posited a zombie nation that retained a shred of social coherence ; here , zombies are nothing more than an empty canvas for the perverse imaginings of director andreas schnaas . utterly without redeeming social value , and even worse , entirely lacking as entertainment , zombie 90 is a bad joke on anyone who wastes money on it .
