daraar got off to a pretty good start . the first scene really left me at the edge of my seat wondering what would happen next . other than that , the first half of the movie is a total bore . all the first half of the movie is about is rishi kapoor falling head over heels in love with juhi chawla . by the way , do not you think he is a little old for her ? ? ? things finally start to spice up towards the middle of the film when juhi tells us about her previous husband ; and wow what a lunatic is he ! he was an over protective , neat freak with a really hot temper ! he used to beat up poor juhi for no good reason ! one of the reasons i really do not like this movie is because i cannot stand to see juhi my favorite actress get so abused . this film in general has way too much abuse and bloodshed ; i find it so sickening ! ! ! anyway , all i am trying to say is if you are thinking about renting daraar , you should put it right back on the shelf where you found it and pick something else !
