this is a movie that demonstrates that mood and music and texture are not enough to make a good film . sure , the viewer is treated to numerous fine scenes of los angeles in the thirties i especially liked the view of the trolley approaching the tunnel , and the tram rising up the hillside but in a sense this fine cinematography is self defeating , because it creates a mood that something is going to happen and nothing does . the script too keeps feinting toward some plot or action or trauma and time after time not delivering . not even delivering the i assume theme of the movie , the characters essential misfit . the lead actors , both too pretty for their roles , did not convey any repression or agony , and the script did not expose us to any . now , donald sutherland ? that is another story . his character was so well fashioned , so perfectly played , that i wanted the camera to follow him .
