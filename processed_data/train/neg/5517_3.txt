i am only rating this film as a 3 out of pity because it attempts to be worthwhile . i love to praise a great movie and i am not biased toward male movies . legally blonde was an excellent film . georgia rule on the other hand , was a disorganized , weak , poorly written , unrealistic example of movie making at its worst . by the end of the film i did not care who was lying or if anything was resolved . the most important thing in a film is a good story . this story is weak and never develops just because the subject matter is deep , does not mean the story is good . a good story has dynamic characters . a dynamic character is one that experiences a major character change , and is primed for that change over the course of the movie . in georgia rule , the character changes were abrupt and undeveloped . secondly , there were too many attempted dynamic characters . pulling off a really good dynamic character is a tough job and takes time you have only got a couple hours in a movie . that means that too many attempted dynamic characters will get too little attention to their personal change . even if i ignore the poorly written story , and the litter of weak dynamic characters , i cannot even say i liked anyone . every character was a mess . that is fine if your are writing american beauty but not when you are attempting a dramatic comedy . georgia was a horrible mother , her daughter was a horrible mother and daughter , and lohan was a horrible excuse for a human being no i am not cutting her any slack because she was molested , crap happens to everyone and we are all responsible for our own actions . the dudley do right mormon kid should have had the guts not to compromise his religion and commitments . . . and simon , i mean seriously , what kind of guy lets a 17 year old girl who is been molested just stay over occasionally unless he is an actor or a politician . this movie is worth watching if you want to remind yourself what good movie making is not !
