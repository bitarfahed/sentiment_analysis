being a big fan of the other planet of the apes films , i rented this dvd despite my aversion to all things tim burton . once again , he does not fail to disappoint with his uninspired direction . even the ape makeup looks second rate , which is unforgivable considering the monstrous budget of this monstrosity . mark wahlberg proves once and for all that he is not an actor as if boogie nights was not proof enough . i was embarrassed for genuine talents such as tim roth and helena bonham carter . no doubt their paychecks motivated them since it could not possibly have been the cliche ridden screenplay . i rented this dvd on a special 1 night and i still feel ripped off .
