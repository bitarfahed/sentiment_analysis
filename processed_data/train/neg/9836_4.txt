john scott john wayne and partner kansas charlie eddy chandler are trail buddies who make their way to the rattlesnake gulch rodeo . scott is a pretty fair contestant , but finds that unless he is willing to accept twenty five cents on the dollar in prize money from a crooked promoter , he will have to collect his winnings at gunpoint . quite coincidentally , bandits pete al ferguson and jim paul fix decide they would like the rest of the rodeo take ; they shoot promoter farnsworth henry hall , and make it look like scott and kansas charlie are the killers . wayne and chandler use a running gag in the film where they are about to go at it with their fists over various trivialities . each time chandler takes a wild swing , wayne foot stomps him and knocks him silly . if you are very attentive , there is a neat lipton is tea ad in one of the scenes in which scott is love interest anne mary kornman appears . later on in the film , the buddies are framed once again over a stage robbery . having a change of heart and seeing the error of his ways , bad guy jim wants to come clean and confess to the sheriff , but pete shoots him down . while being patched up , jim tells his story to the doctor and his sister anne . in an unbelievable scene , anne marches right into the middle of a gunfight between the good guys and the villains to confront the sheriff . the desert trail is one of the blander john wayne westerns from lone star productions during this era . noticeably absent are george gabby hayes and yakima canutt , one or both are usually to be seen in these oaters . if you are a john wayne fan though , you will have to see it once , but that will probably be enough .
