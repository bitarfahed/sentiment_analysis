the oddly named vera ellen was to movie dancing what sonja henie was to movie ice skating : blonde , girlish , always delightful to watch , but not an especially good actress and usually lumbered with weak material . when i watch vera ellen is sexy apache dance with gene kelly in words and music , i cannot help noticing that her blouse yellow with narrow red horizontal stripes seems to be made out of the south vietnam flag . for some reason , the very american vera ellen starred in two musicals made several years apart set in edinburgh , a city not noted for its tap dancers : let is be happy and happy go lovely . in the latter , cesar romero plays an american impresario who for some reason is staging a musical in edinburgh . there is a vague attempt to link this show to the edinburgh festival , which is nonsense : the festival is not a showcase for splashy leg shows . we also see a couple of stock shots of the royal mile : apart from a few highland accents , there is absolutely no attempt to convey scottish atmosphere in this movie . the funniest gag occurs at the very beginning , when we learn that the title of romero is show is frolics to you : this is a cheeky pun that britons will get and yanks will not . vera ellen is , as usual , cute and appealing and an impressive dancer , but the very few musical numbers in this movie are boring and bad . the plot mistaken identity between magnate david niven and reporter gordon jackson is brainless , though no more so than the plots of several dozen hollywood musicals . romero is less annoying than usual here , probably because for once he is not required to convince us that he is interested in bedding the heroine . the single biggest offence of this movie is its misuse of bobby howes . the father of sally ann howes was a major star of west end stage musicals ; his wistful rendition of she is my lovely was a big hit in britain in 1937 . here , he shows up in several scenes as romero is dogsbody but never has a chance to participate in a musical number , nor even any real comedy . it is absolutely criminal that this movie with a title containing the word lovely , sure to evoke howes is greatest hit would cast a major british musical star but give him nothing to do ! the delightful character actress ambrosine phillpotts whom i worked with once shines in one restaurant sequence , and there is a glimpse of the doomed beauty kay kendall . as vera ellen is confidante , somebody named diane hart speaks in one of the most annoying voices i have ever heard : it sounds like an attempt to imitate joan greenwood and glynis johns both at the same go , but does not match either . val guest has a story credit , but this movie does not come up to the quality of his brilliant comedies . the colour photography is wretched , though i realise that postwar britain could not afford hollywood is process work . happy go lovely is at utmost best a pleasant time waster , with waster being the operative word . i will rate this movie just 4 out of 10 .
