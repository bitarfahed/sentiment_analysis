i have read some of the comments about this film and can only surmise that some people are easily entertained . this movie is nothing . it is so badly written , directed and acted that it barely makes an impression . the characters speak in cliche ridden dialogue and the situations are completely implausible . while that might make this campy and fun , it does not because everything is so lifeless the film becomes dull . it is as if lee rose decided to write a drama about a woman struggling with her sexuality but then she either was not allowed by studio execs to give the story some true to life gusto or did not have the cojones . this movie could go in the enyclopedia as the standard issue bad lifetime tv movie .
