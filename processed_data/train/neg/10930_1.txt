this movie is a total dog . i found myself straining to find anything to laugh at just so i would not feel like i would totally wasted my money and my time . the writing in this film is absolutely terrible . it is a shame it is not up to the standards of other hale storm movies . they should have saved the money on getting d list actors like fred willard and gary coleman and spent the money working the script until it was right . even gary coleman was not properly utilized for his role . this movie leaves you wondering what the point of most of the plot was including the subplots . after viewing this movie , i am left with the impression that the producers were hoping to capture some kind of napolean dynamite like humor , where it is not so much the lines as the character and the delivery . unfortunately , this movie fails to deliver the lines , the characters , the delivery or the humor . i should have gone to the dentist instead !
