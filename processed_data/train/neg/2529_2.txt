bobby is a goofy kid who smiles far too much and wants sex . so he buys a van to aid in this quest . the acting is lame , the comedy is pathetic and the script is no more than a loosely strung chain of clich s and cheap thrills . the makers of the film obviously wanted to capture some of the out there craziness of other films of the time , but fell a long way short . they even resort to bobby slipping on a banana skin , because this will supposedly add comedic value . i am struggling to find a redeeming feature of the film . if you like devito , this is another classic devito kind of role but he is only a supporting actor and there for clich value .
