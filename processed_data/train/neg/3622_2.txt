i caught this a few months ago on family channel , and having some memories of the tv show from my youth , decided to watch it along with my 4 year old daughter . i should have got some psychedelic mushrooms to go along with it , cause this is just bizarre ! not only is it a musical with annoyingly forgettable tunes , the requisite cheesy effects and cameos by stars long past their collective primes , it seems to have been produced as somebody is good acid trip . talking flutes , british children far too old for this kind of crap . . . in the words of krusty the klown uuuuuugggghhhhh ! what was i on ? if you are a huge fan of the whole sid and marty krofft oeuvre , go for it ; otherwise , unless you are willing to get looped before watching , stay far , far , far away .
