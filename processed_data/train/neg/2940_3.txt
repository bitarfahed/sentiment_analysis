i saw this movie when i was really little . it is , by far , one of the strangest movies i have ever seen . now , normally , i like weird movies , but this was just a bit too much . there is not much of a plot to the movie . if anything , it starts out like toy story , where toys come to life , and raggedy ann and andy go on an adventure to rescue their new friend , babette . from there , craziness ensues . there is the greedy , the looneys , a sea monster named gazooks , and a bunch of pirates singing show tunes , all of which just made the movie weirder . also , i cannot help but feel that babette is annoying and a bit too whiny . she definitely did not help the movie . now , even though i did not like this movie , there were a few cute parts . i liked the camel is song . even though it was a song about being lonely , it had a friendly feel to it . then , there was sir leonard . while most of the looneys were just plain nuts , sir leonard was the most interesting and probably the funniest . king koo koo was just a little dirtbag that made dr . evil look like a serious villain . also , there was raggedy andy is song , no girl is toy . it was definitely good song for little boys who wanted to act tough . but , honestly , even these things did not make the movie any better . but remember , this is just my perspective . while i personally would not recommend this movie , even i have to admit , it does have its charming moments . see it if you are interested , but only if you are in the mood for something really out of the ordinary .
