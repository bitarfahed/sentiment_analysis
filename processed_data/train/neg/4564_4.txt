disney has now made straight to video sequels to a good bunch of their many animated features . two of these were made for their 1991 classic , beauty and the beast . well , these ones are not really sequels , as they are both set in between the events of the first film . the first of these two straight to video films was beauty and the beast : the enchanted christmas , which seems to be disliked by quite a few fans of its theatrical predecessor , but i think that can usually be expected with sequels . however , this second one , belle is magical world , is definitely inferior . the film features three short stories , all of which take place while belle is in the castle , and the place is under the spell of the enchantress . the first is the perfect word , where a misunderstanding at the table between belle and the beast leads to trouble , and neither wants to be the first to apologize . the next story is fifi is folly , where fifi and lumiere is fifth anniversary is coming up , and lumiere is unprepared , so belle helps him . however , fifi sees lumiere practicing romance with belle , and thinks they are actually in love . the film ends with the broken wing . in this story , belle takes care of a bird with a broken wing , but a bird in the castle will probably mean trouble if the beast finds out , as he hates birds ! the plot description i gave is for the original vhs version of disney is third beauty and the beast movie . apparently , in the dvd version , there is another story added called mrs . potts is party , but i have only seen the original version . however , since i highly doubt that one story would stand out as a classic over the rest , i see no point in watching the special edition . anyway , the first thing i will say about belle is magical world is that the animation is very 2 dimensional compared to what we are used to from disney , which would obviously disappoint many people . i did not like beauty and the beast : the enchanted christmas that much , but you certainly cannot say the same about its animation . i am sure the stories in belle is magical world could entertain many kids mostly younger ones , i think , and each story has a moral , so they could also teach them some valuable lessons . however , for adults , the film really does not have a lot . i personally did not find any good humour in it , found that the constant conflict between belle and the beast got tiring , and the stories did not impress me too much at all in any way they are not very well written . in the perfect word , the way belle says to the beast , you are acting rude . . . and foolish ! is a bit cheesy , and i think there are quite a few other cheesy moments in these stories . by the time this straight to video movie first came out , i was around eleven or twelve years old . i do not know what i would have thought of it at the time , as i had lost interest in disney by then , and it would be years before i would gain any of that interest back . even when this movie was first released , i think i was a bit past the age group it was aimed at . i never saw beauty and the beast : the enchanted christmas until a couple months ago , but unlike that film , i never even heard of this one until recently , i think just after seeing the first sequel to disney is 1991 hit . well , as much as i like the theatrical original , i would not have been missing much if i never became aware of this film is existence . for little kids , i am sure belle is magical world can be highly entertaining , and probably somewhat educational with its morals , but i do not recommend it for adult disney fans .
