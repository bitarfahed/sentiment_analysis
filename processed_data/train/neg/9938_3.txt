jean luc godard is marxist polemic is as close to unwatchable a film as you are likely to see from an internationally respected filmmaker . bits of political theater , mind numbingly boring and interminable , are interspersed with the making of sympathy for the devil , featuring the rolling stones in the studio . the process of the song is development , from mick jagger playing a demo on acoustic guitar , to the backing vocals being recorded towards the end , is fascinating , and it is worth renting this film just to see the bits with the stones . almost half the movie is devoted to this , so thanks to the miracle of chapter stops , you can skip all the bizarre political skits and just watch the stones put a song together . when i had this on laserdisc , i valiantly attempted to watch it all , but i do not see how anyone could get through it . i finally gave up and just chapter skipped my way to the stones segments .
