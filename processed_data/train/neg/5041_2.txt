ye lou is film purple butterfly pits a secret organization purple butterfly against the japanese forces in war torn shanghai . ding hui zhang ziyi and her ex lover hidehiko itami toru nakamura find themselves on opposite sides of the conflict after a chance meeting . i agree with the reviewer from paris . the film substitutes a convoluted , semi historical conflict for a plot , without giving the audience a single reason to care about the characters or their causes . the sudden time shifting does not help matters as it appears completely unwarranted and pointless . normally i do not mind dark movies , but the absence of light , the bone jarringly shaky camera footage , and the generally bad film making techniques really make this a tough film to watch and stay interested in . i also agree with the viewer from georgia that this film has a chaotic editing style and claustrophobic cinematography , but i do not think that helps the movie . the backdrop to the film is one of the most potent events of the 20th century , and i do not believe you can do it any justice by editing it as if it were a michael bay film . the overly melodramatic moments do not add to its watchability . the actors are all suitably melancholy . zhang ziyi once again shows that she has an exceptionally limited acting range as she spends the entire movie doing what she seems to do best in all her films , brooding and looking generally annoyed . however , at least she adds some variety to this role by chainsmoking and engaging in the worst love making scene since michael biehn and linda hamilton in the terminator . all in all , a very disappointing film , especially seeing as how it comes from the director of suzhou he . 2 10
