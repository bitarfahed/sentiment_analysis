i never much liked the myra movie , tho i appreciate how it pushed the hollywood envelope at the time . certainly miss welch is costume became an iconic image , though i have to wonder if many people who recognize the image really saw the film and know what it was all about i rewatched myra on fmc a couple of years ago and did not think it had aged any better thru the years . there is a segment about it in the sexploitation cinema cartoon history comic books , where it is given proper credit for putting such big stars in what was then an outrageous production . however , imho , the movie is too bitter to be charming , too silly to be a turn on , and so busy trying to shock that it fails to inform , engage , or entertain
