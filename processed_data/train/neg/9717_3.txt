a pretty obvious thriller by numbers , in which the only possible twist turns out to be a hiding to nothing . i was watching principally for the english language performance by isabelle huppert . it was not great , but then it was a strange role . i would not be surprised if half her contribution turned out to have been left on the cutting room floor along with several last minute script re writes . the acting is the least appealing thing about this film . steve guttenburg looks like he is trying to flesh out his role with the charm that everyone is told him he has . there is a sensationally stupid sequence in which it is suggested that his sexual prowess will be able to help treat ptsd . it is an uninteresting performance . elizabeth mcgovern is more of a draw with genuine charm and character but it is small consolation . 3 10
