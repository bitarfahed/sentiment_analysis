actress patty duke wrote an insightful , funny , rough hewn book about her career as an actress , her crazy quilt love life , and her manic depressive episodes and suicide attempts which almost put her away for good . with this rich material to draw from and patty playing herself in the final act , one would think a crack tv director like gilbert cates could bring it all together on film , but call me anna is a pale shadow of duke is autobiography . for those who have not read the book , the sketchy narrative leaping forward in time is not absorbing , we are never allowed to get our bearings with what is happening , and the production seems stunted by a low budget . the actors are miscast , and the value of having duke herself finally appear does not pay off the film is phony reality is so thick at this point that patty cannot bring stability to the scenario . it appears as if the producers were sincere enough and consciousness minded to anxiously steer the film towards duke is ultimate diagnosis and mental freedom , but they left out many dramatic opportunities in the process .
