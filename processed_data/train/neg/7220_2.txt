where do they get the money to make films like this ? i mean , there is nothing redeeming about this film . none of the actors are known , the writing is terrible , the photography is blurry , the story wanders between being a bad version of repo man and a nicklodeon western and the acting is unbelievable . for someone who watches all kinds of film , good and bad , i must admit that this film is about the worst i have seen since attack of the eye people back in the 50 is . i do not really like to trash the effort of people trying to create some entertainment or , heaven forbid , art but this film would seem to appeal to no one . the story bends on a mystical contamination of a person who is a bad version of the celtic soul eater , although he functions more like a male succubus without the sexual overtones . the bad guys have to team up with the good guys in a town where they are unable to escape from . take it from there as that , alas , is the best part . my advice is that unless you are into bad mythology , amateurish writing , unconvincing acting and tedious settings , you will best be served by leaving this one on the shelf when you are out renting videos .
