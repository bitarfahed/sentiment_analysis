i had heard and read so many good things about weeds that i was looking forward to getting hooked on another great cable series like entourage , sopranos or mad men but that slowly eroded away with each episode i watched from season one . did not make it past the first six episodes the writing was unoriginal , contrived and the portrayal of blacks embarrassing . the dialog felt forced , like the writers are trying way too hard to be clever and hip . it was a rare moment when i actually emitted an audible laugh . the characters never developed enough for me to care about them , they were selfish and unappealing . i absolutely hated the addition of the brother in law who should have been hauled away on to catch a predator and the removal of the hodes daughter quinn from the cast by sending her to boarding school in mexico was so unoriginal and clich , i had to conclude the writers were testing the viewer is loyalty . episode after episode i liked the characters less and could not get past many of the technical flaws in the story line . add to that i heard that season two was not as good , so i lost all motivation to continue to watch this play out . if you are a fan of good casting and writing , i suspect this show will be a challenge for you to like , unless of course you are stoned and then all bets are off .
