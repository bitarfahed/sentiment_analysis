i did not enjoy the film , joshua , at all . perhaps it is because i saw another , much better similar film titled orphan 2 days prior but perhaps it is really just because this film was not very good . i am going with the ladder . sure , the plot of an evil child is not exactly original but that does not mean the film could not succeed . it could have been suspenseful and entertaining and chilling but instead it was slow building , boring , uneventful and really did not leave me thinking anything more than that was not very good when it was all over . at the end , joshua is motivations are revealed . i will not give that away but the reality is that he did not really accomplish his goals since despite vera farmiga as his mother , abby , disappearing about 3 4th through the movie , all arrows point to her returning home soon . she was committed to a mental institution because she was losing her mind but then joshua is father her husband was accused of tampering with her medication which tells the audience that the institution realized that she was indeed not mentally ill but rather was being dosed medically . so . . should not she be coming home soon ? will not joshua have failed ? will not his mother be living with him and his sister and possibly his father soon ? i question the father since his future is left open ended . at the end of the day , i did not care about the characters . the evil demon child joshua was not really scary . the storyline moved slowly and when it picked up it was still boring . suspense fell flat every single time . when it was over i could not believe i had sat through the whole thing . 4 10 just because the acting was good from the parents especially vera farmiga as the mother but if you want to see a movie about an evil child go see orphan . now that is a movie that took an unoriginal concept and created a brilliant movie .
