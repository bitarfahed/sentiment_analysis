honestly , i was disappointed in expiration date . super clever title and interesting premise , but i do not think it delivered . what was it about ? the main character is desire to reconnect with his native roots ? or , more likely , it was his need to overcome his fear of death . but , he was not set up as someone who has lived his life in fear it seems as if his life was going fine , but since doomsday is approaching he should now start worrying . i did not buy it . meanwhile , the supporting characters in the film did not seem to have needs that blended into an overarching story . they were all just doing their thing , running parallel to the main character . also , what was treated as a curse looked more like a coincidence . who cursed the family ? why ? when ? finally , why did not he just plan on staying in his apartment all day on his birthday ? those are my criticisms , but i did love the shots of seattle , cinematography was beautiful , acting was good in the times it was not outstanding .
