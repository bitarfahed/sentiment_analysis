first off i would like to say that if i had to honestly rate this movie from a 1 to a 10 , then i would give it a 4 . it is not that i am a tough critic , it is just that this movie is that bad . everything from the story , to the directing , to the editing is awful . the story is not even halfway decent to begin with you cannot expect much since it is based on a video game , something i was not aware of going into the movie but the directing and editing made it even worse . the movie cuts at awkward points and goes to scenes that are completely unrelated to the previous ones ; some , like a quick sex scene in the middle , do not even make sense being put into the story seeing as how the characters do not show any feelings toward each other . you could go into this movie expecting to see a pile of crap on the screen for an hour and a half and you would still be disappointed . honestly , if you pay to watch this movie then you are wasting your money , and if you do not pay anything then you are still wasting an hour and a half of your life . so do yourself a favor and do not watch it .
