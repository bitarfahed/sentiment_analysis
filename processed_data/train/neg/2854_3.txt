dreyfuss plays a mob boss who lost his mind , but now he is fixed . lane is his girl who is been messing around with his number one goldblum , who is supposed to have something going with lane is sister barkin . with what anyone could consider an all star supporting cast burt reynolds , gabriel byrne , kyle maclachlan and even an appearance by richard pryor cannot help this plot , as dreyfuss proves he is not fixed and tries to kill just about everyone in sight . you know , it is not like you did not know what was coming . the first ten minutes were nothing but guys trying to tell goldblum what was in store for him when dreyfuss gets out , but i will save you all the trouble : skip this movie . the actual reason i picked this movie is because i have been on a diane lane kick lately and have been trying to see all her movies . the real let down for me was not just the terrible plot , but also the fact that she does not show up until the last fifteen minutes of the movie although we hear about her all through the movie . even being a fan of over half of the cast cannot help me enjoy this film . the parts where the suspense was supposed to mount found me laughing at what was actually going on . 3 out of 10 stars .
