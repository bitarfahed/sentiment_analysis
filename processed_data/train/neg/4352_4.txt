if you have seen friends , the writing will feel very familiar . especially the last 3 or 4 seasons of friends often share the same comedy setups . the show is about a group of people whose connection is that they shared the same class when they were still rather young about 10 years old i think . now , they are in their mid twenties , and they meet again on a class reunion . this is where the series starts . a typical episode deals with multiple story lines at once . they are usually not connected in any way . each story line is cut up into multiple sections , which are then shown in a mixed order . the sketches is where my problems lie with this series . as in the later seasons of friends , it is often a rather silly setting with hard to believe situations . one of the main characters does something really stupid that is hard to believe . the situation is then heavily exaggerated , as if it was not silly enough . if you are into this kind of in your face humor , then maybe you will like this series . for me it is a great turn off . the reason i started watching friends is because of the first few seasons . there are interesting and especially credible story lines , with some romance in it that makes you root for the characters . the class has none of this . the characters are simply too forced and stereotypes are pushed too far . it is therefore not possible to relate to them and like them . at least with friends , it took several seasons before it ran out of steam and the character traits were all milked out . but in the class , it seems it has run out of steam before it even started .
