: ulises jordi molla goes out to sea looking for tuna , and does not come back , leaving his wife martina leonor watling and son to fend for themselves . then , in a furious six minutes of screen time hanging around , we are suddenly into a full blown melodrama , all in six minutes . i think this is called wayward narrative pacing . five years later , ulises as in the wandering superhero ulysses ;
