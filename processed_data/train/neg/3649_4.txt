this is a fair little show about the paranormal although it feels as if art bell and his ilk figured out how to carve a career out of the attitude that carl kolchak exemplified . of course there probably would not be an x files if this show had not prepped this audience for it so well . darren mcgavin is not exactly the super heroic type but he is a plausible enough guy to deliver heroic deeds . check out his work on some of those old alfred hitchcock presents . here he is the main attraction , there does not seem to be a girlfriend or wife who is a distraction . in fact there is not a whole lot of sex appeal to the show . something i am noticing as well is that the pacing is not really suspenseful in a typical way . there is a lot of throwaway humor to this show . sometimes its just pokey to get to the climax . there is a thread from this show coming all the way up to the present mad men show in terms of style . not that david chase writes mad men but the people that worked under him on the sopranos definitely have emulated and inherited his serio comic tone .
