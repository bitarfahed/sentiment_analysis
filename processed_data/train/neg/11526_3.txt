i enjoyed the previous ittenbach movie that i would seen , burning moon . but while that movie was rather grim and nasty , premutos seems to mostly play it for laughs . while its admirable how ittenbach made this movie with no money in his spare time and the dvd documentary is worthwhile to see this , i found myself constantly battling not to fast forward to the next gore scene . sure , there is gore , and if that is all you want then go ahead and enjoy . but be warned : there is an inordinate amount of lame comedy and tedious story exposition . many are comparing this to peter jackson is movies , especially braindead . but looking at what jackson did on a similar budget in bad taste , it is clear ittenbach is lacking one thing that jackson has talent . 3 10 for pretty good and plentiful gore effects , and for getting the most out of limited resources but not worth the money i paid for it
