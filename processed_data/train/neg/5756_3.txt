this should be a great film . . . meryl streep and jack nicholson co starring as two newspaper writers . mike nichols directing . uh uh . it is dull dull dull ! pointless and predictable ! slow and unfocused ! it is a cookie cutter boy meets girl , boy marries girl , boy has affair , girl leaves boy story . now theres an original concept ! after squirming through two hours was it only two ? it felt like six . i was not sure whether it was a comedy , a romance , a tragedy or a soap opera . it was done in 1986 . i am sure all of us did things sixteen years ago that we rather would forget . i hope the damage to the reputations of streep et al is beginning to heal and that the emulsion on the master is beginning to fade . it is not that it is such a bad picture . it is just that it is such an un good one .
