tim robbins is oddly benign here , cast as a garage mechanic in 1950s new jersey who falls in love with a perky blonde who turns out to be albert einstein is niece ! although he is on screen much of the time , robbins cancels out the inner workings of his intense persona and fades into the background it is easy to forget he is even in the picture ! . lumpy romantic comedy with a gimmick , that being walter matthau playing einstein who does what he can with a cartoonishly conceived character . otherwise , it is sugary and sunny , directed rather drowsily by fred schepisi , who shows heart but no wit or brains . meg ryan is her usual affable self and the chemistry between she and robbins is charming , like that of an affectionate sister and her big brother . from
