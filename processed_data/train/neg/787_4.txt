as an inspiring director myself , this movie was exciting to watch with criticism in mind . shot with low end digital camera probably with 35mm adapter for dof . the editing is good acting decent , sound effects are not too over the top . i would have give it a 7 for an indie film , but the story are not that interesting . it is more on the drama side , character developments than a horror flick . it is not for those who wants to get spooked startled frightened grossed out , or sit down with popcorn to just enjoy . honestly this movie would be good if we were still in the 50 is this movie is about a family who has a dry field , and that is just that .
