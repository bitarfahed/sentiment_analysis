while watching this movie , i came up with a script for a movie , called the making of 10 items or less : producer : i have got good news and bad news . the good news is , we can get morgan freeman ! writer : that is great ! but what is the bad news ? producer : we can only afford to hire him for one day . i guess we will have to get someone else . writer : so we hire him for one day . a movie is an hour and a half long . a work day is eight hours long . i fail to see a problem . producer : but . . . he will have to spend time getting into character . writer : so we have him play a character who is essentially himself . producer : but he will still need to understand his motivation and all that . you are not saying we have him play a big name actor that is doing a low budget movie , are you ? writer : why not ? producer : that is ridiculous ! but fine , at least we will have morgan freeman in our movie . and i guess we have to set the movie in los angeles too . writer : of course . producer : this script is a load of crap . we would better make money on this . just in case , have morgan freeman is character plug wal mart or target or one of those stores , so at least someone will want to sell the dvds . writer : sure thing ! producer : wait a second . . . what is this about a tiny bodega with a ten items or less express lane ? writer : oh , i guess that is pretty weird . but we cannot change the title now ! i doubt my script actually bears much resemblance to reality , but then neither did 10 items or less . this is a case of good acting , but bad writing , and i hate to see it happen . when watching an independent movie , you expect it to try to convey some sort of message . i think they might have been trying for the tired old do not let anything hold you back message that has been done to death in much better films . in any case , with 10 items or less , the only message i got was look ! look at morgan freeman !
