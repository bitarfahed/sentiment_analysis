imagine the big chill with a cast of twenty somethings whose characters are all unlikable , and an iguana like man lizard chasing them around and you have an idea of the foolishness herein . on the positive side , the movie does not skimp on showing the monster . there is no peek a boo shots , or nighttime scenes where you have to imagine what he looks like ; he is right out there folks . unfortunately , the design and construction are not that inspired . a little bit of mystery might have helped . mind you , i have seen far worse , but if you are going to have him out on full display for a lot of the flick , your monster better look damn good . spoiler ahead ! ! ! oh , and there is a twist ending involving the supposedly dead brother that makes absolutely no sense whatsoever . this came as no surprise given the shoddy writing of the script . as for the acting ; well let is just say it was not painfully bad , but i do not expect we will be seeing many of these kids in future cinematic outings . gore quotient : 2 out of 5 ; nudity quotient : 1 out of 5 ; intelligence quotient : negligible
