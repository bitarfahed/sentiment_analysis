the movie is basically the story of a russian prostitute is return to her home village for the funeral of a sister friend . there are a couple of other minor story lines that might actually be more interesting than the one taken , but they are not fully explored . the core of the movie is the funeral , wake , and later controversy over the future of a community of crones that make dolls and sell them to buy vodka but are now missing the artist who made their dolls marketable . apparently , the movie is unedited . the prostitute is journey from the city to the village is an excruciatingly endless train ride and tramp through the mud . maybe that is supposed to impress us with the immensity of the russian landscape . the village itself , such as it is , is inhabited by a legion of widows and one male , the consort of the dead girl . continuing the doll business is problematic for everyone involved and eventually seems impossible . most of the film is shot with a hand held camera that could induce nausea . another problem for western viewers is that subtitles do not include the songs and laments of the crones . do not go to this movie unless you are fluent in russian .
