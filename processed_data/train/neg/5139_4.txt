a lot of horror fans seem to love scarecrows , so i will not be very popular in saying that i found it to be rather boring . the idea behind it was interesting , but it seems to drag so much . i think the main problem is that it is all set in darkness . sometimes horror films set in darkness can work such as humongous , but scarecrows is in darkness for the whole film . a lot of the time it is hard to figure out what is actually happening , and although some shots of the scarecrows were creepy , most were hard to even see . if a little more lighting had been used , perhaps it could have been better . there is not many films involving killer scarecrows to my knowledge , apart from dark night of the scarecrow , which is much better . i would recommend that over scarecrows any day .
