i never saw it on tv but rented the dvd through netflix as soon as i found that it was available . i had high expectations , and was not disappointed . it is funny , and the animation is excellent . that level of quality i would only have expected from feature films , so i am surprised at some of the bad comments on it . maybe the dvd release had improved animation ? one bonus to the dvd is that it was fun to see both versions of the pilot . i am an adult , and i really appreciate that it is for adults . it is not only kids who like cute animated characters . it is nowhere near raunchy as some have claimed , but i can see where some parents would not want their young children to see it . i am very disappointed that it was cancelled . i wish they would produce more episodes . or perhaps a movie .
