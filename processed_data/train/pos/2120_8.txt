james stewart and margaret sullavan play co workers in a budapest gift shop who do not really like each other , not knowing they are really sweetheart pen pals who have yet to meet . a very charming romantic comedy , very engagingly played by it is two likable stars and a very eager to please supporting cast . the story is well written and the film has that romantic innocence do not quite know how to explain it that films today just do not have . this can obviously be compared to the recent you have got mail , and the original wins in every way . this is mandatory viewing each christmas , i cannot think of a better way to jumpstart a christmas feel than this little gem .
