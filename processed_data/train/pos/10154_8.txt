this is the first michael vartan movie i have seen i have not seen alias and i was curious to see if the guy can act . he sure can and is likable in this movie . natasha henstridge is of course gorgeous but she is usually in more physical and action roles , so i found her very good and lovable in this different sweet role of a schoolteacher . some of the negative comments i read are true , the movie is full of clich s and the story does not ring true at all . also , even though every character in the movie remarks how good they look together , i do not think there is screen chemistry there . however , i enjoyed this movie . the locales are nice , the characters are likable and goodlooking and the supporting actors are pretty good . if you are expecting to see a great romance , this is not it . but if you want to see a pleasant innocent goodlooking movie with likable characters its very good .
