i liked this film a lot . it is dark , it is not a bullet dodging , car chasing numb your brain action movie . a lot of the characters backgrounds and motivations are kinda vague , leaving the viewer to come to their own conclusions . it is nice to see a movie where the director allows the viewer to make up their own minds . in the end , motivated by love or vengeance , or a desire to repent he does what he feels is right . will god ever forgive us for what we have done ? it is not a question mortal men can answer so he does what he feels he has to do , what he is good at , what he is been trained to do . denzel washington is a great actor i honestly cannot think of one bad movie he is done and he is got a great supporting cast . i would thoroughly recommend this movie to anyone .
