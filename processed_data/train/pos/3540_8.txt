i bought this game on an impulse buy from walmart . i am glad i did . it was very entertaining listening to sean connery and playing the game . i thought the graphics were the best i have ever seen in a movie game remake . the bonus levels were very hard ! the sniper one i think was too hard , it made me so frustrated i did not play the game for a week and a half . there were too many people shooting at you with nothing to hide behind or life to handle it . the only thing i might change was the upgrade system . i did not notice any difference from un upgraded equipment to the upgraded , such as buying an armor upgrade did not seem to make the armor stronger or more filling on my life meter . i really liked the q copter . i think the developers did a good job .
