if you like rap or hip hop , watch this movie , although it is funny if you do not get the references , as a straight comedy . have not seen much of the much hyped cb4 , but what i did see did not have the heart that this little stormer has . have not heard from the people involved since , which is a surprise . the film is very similar to spinal tap , which is no bad thing , and i think a lot of the dialogue , while priceless in tap is funnier here , probably because i am more into rap than rock theses days , so my own judgment does cloud that point . the rap songs are funny as hell , and it is basically spot the reference for most of the film , not all of them are in your face , which means the physical comedy and the one liners get priority over the take offs . great fun , one to watch twice if there ever was a movie .
