jake speed 1986 was an amusing parody of indiana jones and other adventurer films that were popular during the eighties . wayne crawford stars as jake speed , an adventurer who is always battling evil doers wherever he goes . with his assistant desmond floyd dennis christopher they globe trot looking for some action and some decent story lines . the duo meet a young woman named margaret karen kopins who is sister has been kidnapped by an evil white slaver trader john hurt . can she find and convince jake and desmond to help her rescue her sibling ? a sappy and cheesy film that does not pretend to be something that it is not . i have to give this one a recommendation . that is if you enjoy movies that like to have fun and for those who do not take everything at face value . recommended .
