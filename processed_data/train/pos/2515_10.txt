moon child , starring japanese rockers hyde and gackt , was a better movie then i expected . in fact , i was very impressed and it immediately became one of my favorite movies . set in mallepa , the story follows a group of street orphans , sho , sho is brother shinji , and toshi who rob and murder to make a living . on one of robberies , sho encounters hyde is kei vampire burning in the sunlight . through the coarse of events kei is true nature is shown , yet no one shuns him away . the time passes and implies that the immortal , never changing kei has raised sho , and the two have a an extremely close bond . sho and kei then encounter son in an outrageous gun fight , and they become quick friends . both kei and hyde fall in love with son is sister , yi che . time skips ahead again and shows a grown sho , this time void of kei . it also explains that sho and son have become enemies . through tragedy after tragedy this movie dives into the reality of life and all it is hardships , focusing on friendship and love . it is a truly touching movie that is sad yet beautiful at the same time . as for the acting , i think gackt did a magnificent job . hyde did an amazing job for a first timer . the shots were beautiful , but the movie did have it is rare and short gruesome shots . all in all , i must say this movie is amazing , moving , and i highly recommend it .
