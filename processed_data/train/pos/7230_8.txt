the invisible mouse is a delightful and different tom jerry is cartoon . it features the usual cat mouse chases and battles , but in a different way this time . jerry accidentally falls in a bottle of invisible ink and is obviously very glad about this because he realizes that he can prepare lots of surprises for tom , scare him , torment him and confuse him . as much as it is weird , it is also very cool and funny to see what we cannot see : jerry invisible . it is amusing to see things lifting up in the air without seeing who is doing it we know who , right ? it is like those things had a life of their own or even almost like a matter of ghosts . it is equally amusing to see jerry eating some candies and fruits while he is invisible . i really like that instrumental music which plays when he is not visible . some of the best jokes on this short are when tom sees jerry is shadow and slams him and even when tom tries to slam him with a frying pan and jerry writes missed me . i also like when jerry drinks tom is chocolate milk , becoming visible again and with a happy look on his face . overall , this short has the basic ingredients needed for a classic cartoon : humor , entertainment , fun and some nice artwork too .
