a bug is life is like a favorite candy bar it is chock full of great little bits that add up to something really tasty . the story could not have been better ; it is clever , has heart emotion , and every character has a nice arc a growth or change . by comparison , the only characters in toy story to have an arc are buzz , who learns to love being a toy , and woody , who overcomes his resentment of buzz . there are tons of laughs and cute moments in a bug is life . all of the actors turn in great voice work , and the animation , both the motion and detail , is superb . this serious movie buff does not throw around 10 s lightly , but this movie certainly deserves the 10 i gave it .
