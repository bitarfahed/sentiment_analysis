a warm , sweet and remarkably charming film about two antagonistic workers in the same shop james stewart and margaret sullavan who are carrying on a romance via mailbox without either of them knowing it . the key to this film is success is that ernst lubitsch keeps any syrupy sentimentality absent and calls on his actors to give low key , unfussy performances . as a result , you fall in love with virtually all of them . there is a strong undercurrent of melancholy running through this film which i appreciated . loneliness is a major theme , most obviously represented in the character of the shop is owner and manager , played wonderfully by frank morgan . he discovers that he is being cuckolded by his wife , and realizes that the successful life he is created for himself is not enough to keep him from feeling lonely when he does not have a partner to share it . this makes the timid romance between stewart and sullavan all the more poignant , because they are both reaching out to this unseen other , who each thinks of as a soulmate before they have even met . of course we know everything will turn out right in the end , but the movie does not let you forget the dismal feeling either of them would feel if they found that the reality did not live up to the fantasy . lubitsch fills his movie out with a crackerjack cast that has boatloads of chemistry . the little group of shop employees refers to itself throughout the movie as a little family , and that is exactly how it feels to us as well . this is a wonderful , unsung romance . grade : a
