not sure why it does not play in peoria , apparently , but this is a very funny , clever british comedy . it is set at the end of the swinging sixties . peter sellars is fantastic as the rich , forty something serial womaniser . the perfectly delectable goldie hawn , playing a 19 year american girl in london , is , initially , sellars catch of the day . but the urbane tv food critic cannot stop himself from falling for the dizzy american blond . humour , pathos , great script , strong performances from the leads and supporting caste . it is a great film , and the best gag is the very last line . try it , you will like it .
