i saw this jolly little film at age 10 11 in 1979 when it was broadcast on cbs . i did not know it had been in a theater at all . to rate it from a kids point of view i would give it 4 out of 5 stars , because being a young boy at the time , it seemed a little girlish . the climactic scene where gazooks tickles the daylights out of everyone was a bit disturbing at the time but you outgrow that sort of thing . when i re discovered it a blockbuster video in 1995 , i had to revisit it ! i still liked it despite the fact that it looked a bit old . i do not know where the other reviewer on here got the idea that it was not on vhs . it is out there . might even be on dvd by now , at least i hope it is . i want to share it with my kids someday ! 10 stars on here , it is still a great kids film . end 09 08 2009 : finally found a vhs copy ! ! woo hoo !
