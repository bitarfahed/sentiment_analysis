like the arabian nights this film plays with storytelling conventions in order to make us feel that there is plot , plot and more plot : it opens with what appears to be the frame device of a blind man telling the story of his life , then plunges into a flashback which takes us right up to the blind man is present , where we discover that about half of the story is yet to come . it must be admitted that the second half does not quite live up to the promise of the first . like the arabian nights it tries to cram as many middle eastern folk motiffs as possible into the one work . a freed genie , a beautiful princess , a flying carpet , fantastic mechanical toys , sea voyages , a crowded marketplace , a wicked vizier , jewels . . . i do not know why it all works , but it does . everything is just so beautiful . the sets are beautiful . june duprez is beautiful . rozsa is score is especially beautiful . as usual , it sounds hungarian ; but somehow he manages to convince us that he is being hungarian in a persian way .
