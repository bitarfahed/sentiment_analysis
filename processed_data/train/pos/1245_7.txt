whenever i see most reviews it is called a misfire for eddie murphy . these critics want to take a look at some of the stuff he is doing these days , and maybe soften their stance in retrospect . . . the golden child is not highbrow entertainment , but thanks to some of the cast it breaths new life into old clich s , and gives murphy one of his best roles . i do not understand the pervading lack of love for its efforts , at all . perhaps it was released at a time when the establishment had grown weary of knockabout , thrill a minute adventures ? steven spielberg started it with indiana jones ; it is unfair to make this one a scapegoat when what is possibly its biggest sin is also utterly harmless . there is nothing necessarily wrong with trying to capitalise on trends . yes it is silly , but even an occasional observer should be able to understand that ridiculous is where hollywood is idea of mysticism begins and ends . what is more important than believability with a story like this is that the audience have entertaining tour guides on hand to show them the mysterious sights . michael ritchie and eddie murphy fit the bill for this capacity just fine . my advice to you is to buy the ticket and take the ride .
