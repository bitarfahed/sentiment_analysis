wow ! the sort of movie you could watch ten times and still delight in its nuances . absolutely incredible ! if this was visconti is debut film , i shudder to think what would happen if he got any better from film to film . the only other one of his i have seen at time of writing is death in venice which was absolutely incredible : more dazzling visually than ossessione obsession . one of the most beautiful films i have ever seen , but its story was not as involving as ossessione . if you click on miscellaneous on this page is links , there are stills from the movie on those websites . they will not really do justice to the experience of the movie : such graceful camera movements , such beautiful composition , such wonderful faces , such terrific characters , such a great story development , the first movie adapted from james m cain is the postman always rings twice . i cannot believe this was made in 43 , eight years before brando was supposed to have introduced realistic acting to the world with streetcar named desire 1951 . the actors in this may not have used the method technique , ie they may not have truly felt everything themselves i do not know anything about it but they are some of the best , most genuine and realistic performances up to this date in cinema . also , eight years before streetcar named desire brought a new sensuality to the screen , ossessione was electrifyingly sensual ! the most sensual thing since the beginning of cinema ! yes , i am being superlative , but ossessione was just that terrific . the reason ossessione did not cause the impact streetcar did was that it was made in fascist italy and banned by mussolini , and re cut in america . american audiences did not see its full glory till 59 , eight years after streetcar . i will not say any more about it just writing to tell you its one of the best , most beautiful and exciting movies i have ever seen , and tell you to go out and see it ! like another reviewer , i am going to buy it as soon as i can find it !
