panic is kind of a crime comedy drama with william h . macy , donald sutherland and neve campbell in the leading roles . alex played by macy is a guy who kills people for living . but for the moment he is facing a depression and that is why he is seeing a therapist . that is where he meets sarah neve campbell and he falls in love with her . he is also facing another problem : he wants to quit the family business the killings , but he does not now how to tell this to his father . what follows is the story of a guy during he is midlife crisis , facing the regular problems : falling in love with a pretty young girl and everything that goes with that . pretty good acting performances by the cast . macy is excellent as the depressed alex . i had high expectations for this one and it was a good movie but he does not completely deliver . the story was rather flat and clich . . 7 10
