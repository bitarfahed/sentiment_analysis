the sopranos is perhaps the most mind opening series you could possibly ever want to watch . it is smart , it is quirky , it is funny and it carries the mafia genre so well that most people cannot resist watching . the best aspect of this show is the overwhelming realism of the characters , set in the subterranean world of the new york crime families . for most of the time , you really do not know whether the wise guys will stab someone in the back , or buy them lunch . further adding to the realistic approach of the characters in this show is the depth of their personalities these are dangerous men , most of them murderers , but by god if you do not love them too . i have laughed at their wisecracks , been torn when they have made err in judgement , and felt scared at the sheer ruthlessness of a serious criminal . the suburban setting of new jersey is absolutely perfect for this show is subtext people are not always as they seem , and the stark contrast between humdrum and the actions taken by these seemingly petty criminals weigh up to even the odds . if you have not already , you most definitely should .
