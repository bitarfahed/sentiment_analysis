this film is excellently paced , you never have to wait for a belly laugh to come up for more than about a minute and there is much more going on than the initial premise of the film . throughout it there are mockeries of the traditional schmaltzy local boys done good overcoming adversity genre of which this parodies . do not let anyone tell you that they are trying to get cheap laughs just by using obscenities ; sure , there is plenty of that but it is all contextual , not gratuitous . i loved this film and it only cost me 2 . 99 on dvd , so in terms of entertainment value for money , it has been the best film i have seen this year .
