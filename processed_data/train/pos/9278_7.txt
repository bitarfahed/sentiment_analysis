in the white balloon and crimson gold , the two other films by jafar panahi that i have seen , the director mines surprising amounts of depth in subjects that seem , on the surface , slight . in offside , panahi is seriocomic tribute to iranian women standing up for their rights , i do not think he is as successful . not that what he is saying is not important , of course and it is too bad that , like pretty much every other panahi film , iranians cannot see it . but , after a while , the film feels a tad flat , and it feels long even at 90 minutes . not saying i did not like it , though . the actors are all fantastic , and the celebration at the end of the film is infectious . but it is not an important work , in my opinion .
