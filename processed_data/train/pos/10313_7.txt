china o'brien 1990 was an attempt to make cynthia rothrock a star in the united states . this golden harvet production was helmed by veteran director robert clouse . sadly he was either lucky with enter the dragon or he is lost his touch because he is not that great of a director . the only reason to watch this movie is to see the fighting skills of ms . rothrock and richard norton . if this movie was directed by corey yuen or hoi meng it could have been an action classic instead of a cheesy straight to video action flick . china o'brien returns home to help out her dad . he is having trouble with the local mob and he needs her help . so she returns home and restores order with the help of two unlikely people . but will they be enough to topple mister big and his evil cronies ? if you are a big cynthia rothrock fan then this movie is catered for you . i only enjoyed the fighting scenes , everything else is rubbish . why did not raymond chow shell out a few shekels and hired a top notch action director ? recommended .
