i have always enjoyed films that depict life as it is . life sometimes has boring patches , no real plot , and not necessarily a happy ending . a river runs through it is the perfect name for this film and norman maclean is novel . life ebbs and flows like a river , and it has it is rough spots , but it is a wonderful trip . robert redford brings a lot to the film . his narration has a friendly feel that fits the picture perfectly . as a director , he is restrained and calm , and captures some incredibly beautiful scenes . as for the acting , craig sheffer and brad pitt work surprising well as brothers . i do not know quite how to describe tom skerritt and brenda blethyn is performances , except that they truly feel real . a river runs through it is a wonderful film . 8 . 6 out of 10
