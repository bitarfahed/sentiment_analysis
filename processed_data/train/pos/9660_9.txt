only after some contemplation did i decide i liked this movie . and after reading comments from all the other posters here , and thinking about it some more , i decided that i liked it tremendously . i love american films probably because they are so narrative . they usually have a well defined beginning , middle , and end . presque rien , on the other hand , makes no such attempt . i disagree with other posters that say it is too artsy . in every way , this film is meant to evoke your sense memories . so often throughout the film you feel like you are there . . . you feel the summer sun , the breezes , the heat , the winter chill , the companionship , the loneliness , etc . , etc . in every way , the director pulls you into the lives of the characters which is why so many people feel so strongly that the movie disappointed them . after i finished watching it , i felt the same . but upon some reflection , i recognized that this is how the movie had to be : the istory is not the narrative , it is the emotions you the viewer feel . the lighting , scenery , and camera angles immerse you in the scenes they are rich , exquisite , and alive with detail and nuance . although i normally cannot countenance films without a fully developed plot after all , is not a movie isupposed to tell a story , this film is definitely one of my new favorites .
