was the script more fitting for a 30 minute sitcom ? yes , but they still make it work ! i thought the actors did a fantastic job with an otherwise bland script , especially jack black and christopher walken . most people on the board seem to really hate this film . i personally cannot see how that could be , but envy is just one of those film that you either love it or hate it . much like napoleon dynamite and every leslie neilsen movie ever made . you either think it is one of the worst movies ever made or one of the funniest . do not avoid this movie because of the reviews . watch it and see if you are one of the ones who really like it ! if you do , i guarantee it is worth your money . if you do not like it . . . well , now you know .
