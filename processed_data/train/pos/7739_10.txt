ever read jim thompson ? he is hard boiled noir with the most extreme fatalism and misanthropy i have ever encountered . there are rarely private detectives in his work just losers , psychotics and small time con artists . this film has thompson nailed if god made any real mistakes in this world , it was in giving us a will to live when we have got no excuse for it . every character in the film balances on a razor is edge between surreal and creepy realism . there is sleazy , conniving uncle bud , played by bruce dern and spookily well intentioned doc goldman played by george dickerson . jason patric gives a wonderful , often heart wrenching performance as kid collins , a none too bright , shy ex fighter who is more scared of himself than of anyone else . rachel ward is fay , the sexy femme fatale who we cannot quite figure out . . . it is not your standard film noir , nor is it intended to be . after dark my sweet , along with the grifters , are two excellent adaptations of novels by one of my favorite writers , jim thompson .
