it appears even the director does not like this film , but for me i think he is being a bit harsh on himself . sure it is not perfect , but there are some atmospheric shots , and the story is good enough to keep you interested throughout . it is shot in what appears to be quite a pretty village which adds to the atmosphere as well . if you like horror films shot in england , give it a go . i have just seen a trailer for this directors latest film the devil is chair which looks quite amazing . there are not enough english horror films for me , so any that come along deserve our attention , and this one is not as bad as you may think
