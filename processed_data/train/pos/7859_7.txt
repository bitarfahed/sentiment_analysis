had this been the original 1914 version of tess of the storm country also starring mary pickford , i probably would have rated it a lot higher , as this sort of extreme melodrama and sentimentality was pretty typical of the teens . however , by 1922 , this film was already starting to show its age . and , compared to many of ms . pickford is other films such as daddy longlegs , sparrows , my best girl and suds , tess comes up a tad short and not every pickford film merits a 10 even if she was america is sweetheart . now this is not to say that it is a bad film it certainly is not . but , i just cannot see how so many have given this film a 10 . the film has a very long and complicated plot especially because most films of the era were shorter . a rich old crank builds a mansion at the top of a hill next to the river . at the bottom of the hill are some dirty squatters who he hates but who he cannot evict . so he tries to come up with a variety of ways to get them off the land . one ends up in tragedy , when his daughter is fianc is killed in a scuffle with the po folks . the man accused of the murder is dear old mary is father , though he is innocent . to make things a lot worse , the only witness to the real murder will not talk and the dead man had gotten his fianc e pregnant ! so , at this point , we have an innocent man in prison waiting to be executed and a pregnant lady afraid to tell her sanctimonious father she is in the family way . there is a ton more to the film , such as the crank is son falling in love with mary , but it is best you just see the film for yourself . the film excels in some ways . the plot , while very complicated , is also rather interesting and the cinematography is top notch . the very final scene is also pretty cute . however , there is so much overt sentimentality you can practically cut it with a knife . mary is so good and so sweet and so plucky , at times the viewer might find it all a bit hard to take . while it worked great in 1922 making her the biggest star in the world , today it is very dated . this is not true of all her films , but this one certainly is . by the way , the image entertainment dvd is of decent quality , though a few scenes are badly degraded something that is not very surprising considering the age of the film . also , the only extras included are a brief filmography .
