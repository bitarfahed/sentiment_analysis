this is one of the shallowest episodes in that the plot really seemed like an excuse to just have fun . but , i appreciated this light hearted approach and this is truly one of the best episodes to see on a purely fun level . think about it the crew members have encounters with the white rabbit and alice from wonderland , a bengal tiger , a samurai warrior , a knight on horseback who kills mccoy , and a host of other seemingly bizarre events that just do not make any sense at all until the very end . despite all the danger , you just cannot take everything very seriously it is just too fun and the whole episode seems very surreal . so , on a purely non aesthetic level , it is great stuff .
