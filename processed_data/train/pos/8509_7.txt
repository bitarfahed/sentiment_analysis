i hate reading reviews that say something like , wouldo not waste your time , this film stinks on ice . it does to that reviewer yet for me , it may have some sort of na ve charm . if you like the other whistler series films , this one will be watchable . if you like 40s noirish films , this one will be watchable . this film is not as good , in my opinion , as any of the earlier series entries which starred richard dix as the protagonist . it is much slower , and the plot is trite . you have seen this same narrative device used in many other films , and usually better . but the acting is good , and so is the lighting , and the dialog . it is just lacking in energy and you will likely figure out exactly what is going on and how it is all going to come out in the end not more than a quarter of the way through . the whistler series is semi noir , and there character , mood , lighting , camera movement and angles are more important than the story itself . but this film is not noir . it is too light weight and hollywood innocent for that . neither richard dix is character nor those of any of his ladies in the previous films had to come to a good end . you just never knew until the end . but still , i will recommend this one for at least a single viewing . i have watched it at least twice myself , and got a reasonable amount of enjoyment out of it both times .
