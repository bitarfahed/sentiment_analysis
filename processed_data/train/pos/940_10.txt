ok . i am biased . i live near shrewsbury in england , where this wonderful movie was filmed . it still looks the same now . i remember them filming here quite vividly , and the fake snow on the streets for days on end . often , when i am walking through shrewsbury i see a street or a house and it will remind me of this film . george c . scott is scrooge is a more realistic character than many of the other screen versions . his physical appearance is not the typical miser . scott is is big and imposing . a man who finds those smaller than himself to be inferior . we all know the story and the quotes . the book is one of the most cherished works in the english language . and i do not believe there are many cynics who would say that people are not capable of change and redemption . this film version portrays all of that quite beautifully . george c . scott may be american but he plays the part of the english miser with wonderful skill . i love this movie . if you have not seen this version i would strongly urge that you do . it is usually available for a very small amount of money . . . or are you too mean ?
