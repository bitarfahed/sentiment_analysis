i picked this film up based on the plot summary and critics quotes on the back of the box . i am not big into foreign films , and did not know what to expect . i do not really care for subtitles either . but i absolutely loved it ! it has a simple , lovable quality that leaves you feeling good about life . i found myself laughing out loud repeatedly . i would recommend this picture to anyone , even those who abhor foreign films with subtitles . this one makes it worth the effort .
