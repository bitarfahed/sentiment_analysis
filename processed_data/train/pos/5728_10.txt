i went to this movie expecting an artsy scary film . what i got was scare after scare . it is a horror film at it is core . it is not dull like other horror films where a haunted house just has ghosts and gore . this film does not even show you the majority of the deaths it shows the fear of the characters . i think one of the best things about the concept where it is not just the house thats haunted its whoever goes into the house . they become haunted no matter where they are . office buildings , police stations , hotel rooms . . . etc . after reading some of the external reviews i am really surprised that critics did not like this film . i am going to see it again this week and am excited about it . i gave this film 10 stars because it did what a horror film should . it scared the s t out of me .
