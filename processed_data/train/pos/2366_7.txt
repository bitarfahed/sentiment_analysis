this movie with the alternate title martial law 3 for some reason introduced me to jeff wincott for the first time . and it was a great introduction . although i had never heard of him before , he seemed to be an excellent fighter . the action scenes in this movie are great ! there are lots of them too , by the way . the recruit fight at the peacekeepers hq is especially good . there is just something about one single guy beating the crap out of a bunch of people that is really fun . and for the rest of the cast : brigitte nielsen was a good choice for the villain . roles like this fits her but others do not . matthias hues also did a good job , as always . he is a great fighter and macho like character , and was a good rival for wincott in this movie .
