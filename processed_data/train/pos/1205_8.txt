this film is exactly what its title describes an attempt to get you to buy into what the writers have to offer . first , it is kinda fun to see the 1996 style toronto i remember with all its silly haircuts , sunglasses , clothes , and attitude . it really has not changed any ; just a nice , safe , cheap , provincial little urban backwater that makes a great meeting place for international film types ! it is also amusing to see kenny and spenny head to l . a . and find out that it is toronto all over again , only with a strange assortment of beach bums , musicians , fortune tellers , and yet more uppity film types . i do not see pitch as a film to be enjoyed ; it is not entertainment unless the viewer enjoys watching someone is aspirations being trampled . i take pitch as a warning that power and money is really held by studio execs and production houses . would be and successful writers , musicians , and actors are still mere transients even when they reach the big time . so , kenny and spenny are trying to sell you a warning . buy it or do not , but the message is still there .
