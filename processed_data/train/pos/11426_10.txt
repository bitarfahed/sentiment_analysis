i think this movie would be more enjoyable if everyone thought of it as a picture of colonial africa in the 50 is and 60 is rather than as a story . because there is no real story here . just one vignette on top of another like little points of light that do not mean much until you have enough to paint a picture . the first time i saw chocolat i did not really get it until having thought about it for a few days . then i realized there were lots of things to get , including the end of colonialism which was but around the corner , just no plot . anyway , it is one of my all time favorite movies . the scene at the airport with the brief shower and beautiful music was sheer poetry . if you like exciting movies , do not watch this you will be bored to tears . but , for some of you . . . , you can thank me later for recommending it to you .
