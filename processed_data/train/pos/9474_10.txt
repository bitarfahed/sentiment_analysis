i got to see this film at a preview and was dazzled by it . it is not the typical romantic comedy . i cannot remember laughing so hard at a film and yet being moved by it . the laughs are not gags here they are observations , laughs of recognition , little shocks of oh , my god , i thought i was the only one who felt that way ! i will not give away the plot , which is more than just guy falls in love with his brother is girlfriend . the whole family plays a part in the relationship here . probably the best blend of laughter and warmth since while you were sleeping . steve carell goes much deeper than he is gone before , and for the first time i really liked him . the cast is amazing , a list of veteran theater actors whom i have loved in other roles , but they blend to make a convincing family . dianne wiest is lovely as the mother , juliette binoche is luminous and hilarious who knew she was funny ? , and even the reviled dane cook gives a warm , quiet , touching performance . the sondre lerche soundtrack is a wonderful addition , and i will buy the cd the second it is available . do not miss this one .
