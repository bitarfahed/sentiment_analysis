zentropa is the most original movie i have seen in years . if you like unique thrillers that are influenced by film noir , then this is just the right cure for all of those hollywood summer blockbusters clogging the theaters these days . von trier is follow ups like breaking the waves have gotten more acclaim , but this is really his best work . it is flashy without being distracting and offers the perfect combination of suspense and dark humor . it is too bad he decided handheld cameras were the wave of the future . it is hard to say who talked him away from the style he exhibits here , but it is everyone is loss that he went into his heavily theoretical dogma direction instead .
