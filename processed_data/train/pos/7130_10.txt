well , when before i saw this film i really was not sure whether it would be my cup of tea . . . how wrong i was ! i thought that this was one of the best films i have watched for a very long time , a real family classic . the story of a young evacuee and his new foster dad , this film ticks all the boxes . i have not read the book maybe that is a good thing meant i enjoyed the film more but with regards to the story , i really cannot think of any bad points , hence scoring it 10 out of 10 and i hardly ever think anything warrants top marks ! . by the time william proclaimed i can ride my bike , dad ! i was sobbing my heart out anyone who is seen it will understand , i am sure . really heartwarming , and definitely recommended .
