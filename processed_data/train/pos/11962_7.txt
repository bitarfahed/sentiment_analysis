i was lucky enough to get a free pass to an advance screening of iscoop last night . full house at the theatre and when the movie ended there was spontaneous applause . i did not speak to anyone who disliked iscoop although two teenagers sitting next to me sighed and fidgeted uncomfortably for most of the film . they were the exception though because everyone else including myself really enjoyed themselves . iscoop is a quickly paced murder mystery . a young female journalism student is unwittingly maneuvered by forces beyond her control into trying to catch a serial killer on the loose . plenty of hijinks ensue as she partners up with a traveling illusionist and falls in love with a frisky and charming young nobleman . iscoop is not a bad addition to the woody allen filmography . it is not his best work but it is a very enjoyable and light hearted romp . i would say it fits quite comfortably into being an average woody allen film , right in the middle of the pack . if you are a woody allen fan you will probably enjoy yourself . if you are indifferent to his work then iscoop might be enough to get you interested in seeing more . i do not think that anyone who dislikes his style of film making and acting are going to change their mind . woody plays the same kind of neurotic character we have grown so accustomed to although it borders dangerously close to forced and over the top in this film . while potentially aggravating for some who might find themselves wishing he would hurry up and just spit out the words , woody allen fans know what to expect . very good performances all around in my opinion although i found myself missing ian mcshane who is excellent and not on camera nearly enough . hugh jackman is great as the charming nobleman and i think woody allen has found a new regular star to work with in scarlett johansson . i think that with amatch point this is their second pairing and she is just magic with the material that woody gives her . could be the beginning of a beautiful relationship ! i am glad i saw the movie and definitely recommend it . more sophisticated comedy than movies like iscary movie 4 so if your brand of comedy is the latter rather than the former , iscoop probably is not for you . if , on the other hand , you like a touch of class , sophistication and fun , iscoop is for you . probably not the woody allen film i would introduce to a newcomer but all others should give it a try .
