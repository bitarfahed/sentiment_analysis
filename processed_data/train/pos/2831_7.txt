i stumbled onto this movie when i was ebay'ing caesars palace stuff , as i am enamoured with its rich vegas history as the last of the original luxury resorts still standing in good condition unless you count bally is , the original mgm grand . in that respect , this movie delivers full force . you are given a grand tour of the caesars property , which in spite of all the renovations and additions they have done over the 40 years it is been open , looks alarmingly similar . as a film overall , the plot is somewhat difficult to follow , thanks in large part to the horrendous editing . and when i say horrendous , i am not using that word lightly . there is a lot of spliced in , second long snippets of vegas traffic , casino crowds , and even a scene where the robert drivas character is having a conversation with his father about how much he is grown up , and without any explanation , he drivas goes in those infamous snippets from being himself , to a baby , to a little boy , and then back to himself while talking back and forth with his father . that does not give away any plot details ; if anything , one can be prepared for it and maybe they will not be as flabbergasted as i was by the editing . the film has aged well otherwise , and has a good message about the inherent differences between a father and his son that most guys could relate to in some form or fashion .
