the day time ended is an incredible picture . in some ways , it is better than close encounters of the third kind . i prefer cheesy independent flicks to big budget spectaculars . the special effects are cheesy , but that is a big part of the fun . jim davis gives an excellent performance in this film . it is probably one of the best roles he ever had in a feature . the musical score is very good . the story does not make sense . but that only makes it all the more intriguing . like many of the best works of art , the day time ended is not afraid to be subtle and ambiguous . the day time ended may be a low budget indie film , but it is not too much of a stretch to compare it with the existential european films of the fifties and sixties . many of which were low budget independent productions themselves .
