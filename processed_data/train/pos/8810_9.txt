i first saw this film when it aired on the now defunct trio channel a few years ago , and recently watched it again sans commercials on sundance . i was impressed the first time , and found it even more engaging on second viewing . yes , some of the segments are far from perfect amos gitai is hysterical commentary stands out like a sore thumb but taken collectively , 11 09 01 is a total success . best of show : shohei imamura is amazing final segment , which contemporary critics such as the thick witted mick lasalle somehow misinterpreted as an attack on the terrorists , but now stands revealed as a masterful anti war polemic ; samira makhmalbaf is opening piece that manages to blend deep empathy for the victims of 9 11 with a prescient concern for the children of afghanistan ; and idrissa ouedraogo is amusing children is crusade for osama bin laden a hunt almost as serious and successful an undertaking as the one for the real osama . youssef chahine is segment is a noble if failed experiment which at least has the guts to remind the audience that bin laden and al'qaeda are basically creations of american foreign policy and the cia , and though sean penn is character study seems out of place , it is still an effectively bittersweet piece of film making . all in all , essential viewing , and a darn sight better than oliver stone is reactionary world trade center .
