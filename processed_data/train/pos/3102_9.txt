i saw the the bourne ultimatum last summer with a friend , and , wow ! i had already seen the first two films and i liked them , but ultimatum , i loved . matt damon plays jason bourne , a amnesia suffering cia agent on the run , trying to discover who he is . like i already said , i loved this movie from start to finish , no plot holes , slow scenes , everything was paced just right and it fit in well with the other films , but in all senses it was much better . best stunts , car chases , actors , and effects i have seen in an action movie all summer , surprisingly due to spider man 3 , pirates , etc . but i it was not just action in this film , jason does not just kill and run . he has a soul and the audience feels for him , so drama is included . but that does not slow it down . of all the threequels that came out last summer , this was the best .
