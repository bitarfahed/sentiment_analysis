contains slight spoilers it is interesting how anthony mann uses james stewart here . stewart is , of course , remembered by many as george bailey from frank capra is it is a wonderful life , so it is easy to find parallels between the two films . in it is a wonderful life , bailey gets to see the world as it would have been if he had never been born . in the far country , stewart is jeff webster , by not getting involved to help anyone else except himself , gets to see essentially the same thing : a world in which he for all practical matters does not exist . by not getting involved and by attempting not to care about anyone , webster is forced to see those for whom he cannot help but care get hurt , pushed around , and even killed while he stands by and does nothing . this reminds the viewer of george bailey watching a world that has turned upside down because he has also decided not to get involved by not ever having been born . both movies end with the same image a close up of a ringing bell . stewart , by turning around his philosophy of non involvement , has , it would seem , earned his wings .
