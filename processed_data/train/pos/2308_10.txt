i checked this out as an impulse when browsing through the movie store and could not have been any more pleasantly surprised ! my mom and i watched this film together , and we thoroughly enjoyed it . it is not the typical chick flick with a sappy love story and tears all the way through , but it definitely touches a nerve in the twist at the end . it is an ending where , although unexpected and tragic , the movie is overall effect is not harmed by it . i think reese witherspoon was a great actress even in this film , her debut , and this is definitely worth watching ! i did not recognize many of the supporting actors , but they all play their important supporting roles well . the man in the moon is such an believable story about a young teenager falling in love for the first time . most women can definitely relate to everything from witherspoon is words , her subtle glances , and her not so subtle emotions raging like the typical teenage girl . while she is playing a character confused about love , she does not come across as silly and immature , which was much appreciated considering many movies today .
