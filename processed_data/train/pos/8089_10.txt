with the release of peter jackson is famed lord of the rings trilogy , it is even easier to dismiss ralph bakshi is 1978 animated lord of the rings film as inferior . i agree with the majority that jackson is trilogy is the essential film adaptation of tolkien is work , but that does not prevent me from enjoying bakshi is ambitious pioneering effort . jackson has admitted that he received at least some inspiration from seeing bakshi is film and there are some clear similarities between their adaptations . the film is colorful picturesque backdrops are excellent and the score is memorable . i was for the most part satisfied by the drawings of the characters . the pairs of pippin and merry and eowyn and galadriel are mostly indistinguishable from each other visually , the balrog and treebeard were unimpressive , but these points did not bother me very much . however , the nazgul are aptly drawn and made sufficiently eerie . the only character representation i was bothered by was sam is ; he was made to look unbecomingly silly . this film is novel for its animation techniques . in addition to hand drawn characters , live actors are incorporated into the animation through rotoscoping . it is quite apparent which characters are hand drawn and which are rotoscoped , but none the less i found that the film is style was a novelty . the use of rotoscoped live actors for the battle scenes was a good decision and helped these scenes turn out well . the voice acting was generally of high quality . particularly good was john hurt , who provided an authoritative voice for aragorn . aragorn is not a favorite character of mine from the stories , but backed by john hurt is voice he was my favorite character in this adaptation . my other favorite was william squire , whose voice is appropriately strong for gandalf . the only actor who seemed inappropriate was michael scholes as sam , whose voice acting was irritating and added to sam is unfortunately silly image . the only other bothersome part of the voice acting is the mispronunciation of character and place names . particularly strange was the decision to frequently have saruman referred to as aruman . in producing this film , ralph bakshi expected to have the ability to produce two films . hence , this film contains about half the story , from the start of the fellowship of the ring to the end of the battle at helm is deep in the two towers . the obvious implication of this is that the film is story is a highly condensed version of the story from the books . i enjoy the original stories and more thorough adaptations , but the liberties taken to compress the story did not bother me , even the choice to leave arwen out of the story . enough of the key elements of the story were in this film to keep me engaged for the duration and there was even a novelty in being able to breeze through half the lord of the rings story in 132 minutes . the battle scenes were impressive and in particular the orc march to and battle at helm is deep were tremendous . ralph bakshi is version of the lord of the rings is not perfect and no doubt a number of lord of the rings readers lament the cuts to the story . however , for me the drawbacks of this film were minor compared to the thrill of seeing an effective adaptation of half of a great trilogy . my only strong lament is that i am unable to see the second part of this first great tale of the lord of the rings since bakshi was not given the budget to create a sequel .
