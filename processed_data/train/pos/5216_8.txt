erroll works for the department of public safety and his job is to check up on sex offenders . sometimes he pushes the line at his job and beats on the sex offenders . i do not blame him but his boss is ready for him to retire so along comes allison . erroll is now training her to do his job and it is like job shadowing . allison is somewhat naive about the job in the beginning but she does not realize how much danger she really is and it is all erroll is fault . he starts to go to far with his obsession of finding a missing girl when his job is not to be a police officer . it is a fairly decent movie about a crazy guy who pushes the boundaries and works outside his scope of practice . erroll did do a good job but at the sake of the safety of allison . it has some good mystery to it too and just when i had it figured out , there was more to it .
