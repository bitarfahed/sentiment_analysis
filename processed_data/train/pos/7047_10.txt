this film needs to be seen . the truest picture of what is going on in the world that i have seen since darwin is nightmare . go see it ! and if you are lucky enough to have it open in your city , be sure to see it on the big screen instead of dvd . the writing is sharp and the direction is good enough for the ideas to come through , though hardly perfect . joan cusack is amazing , and the rest of the cast is good too . it is inspiring that john cusack got this movie made , and , i believe , he had to use some of his own money to do it . it is a wild , absurd ride , obviously made without the resources it needed , but still succeeds . jon stewart , steven colbert , snl , even bill maher have not shown the guts to say what this film says .
