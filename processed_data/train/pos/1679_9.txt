i am really tempted to reward the case of the scorpion is tail with a solid 10 out of 10 rating , but that would largely be because i think italian horror cinema of the 1970 is is so much better than the cheesy crap i usually watch . but even without an extra point for nostalgia , this is still a genuine masterwork and earning a high rating for its excellently convoluted story , uncanny atmosphere , blood soaked killing sequences and superb casting choices . in my humble opinion this is actually sergio martino is finest giallo , and that has got to mean something , as the strange vice of mrs . wardh , all the colors of the dark , torso and your vice is a locked room and only i have the key are all top notch genre achievements as well . but this film is just a tad bit superior with its ultra compelling plot revolving on an insurance fraud gone madly out of control . following her husband is peculiar death in a plane explosion ! , lisa baumer promptly becomes the suspicious owner of one million dollars and she is eager to leave the country as soon as possible . due to the bizarre circumstances , the insurance company puts their best investigator peter lynch on the case and he follows her to greece . there , lisa becomes the target of many assaults and the case is mysteriousness increases when it turns out several people are hunting for the money . i am always overly anxious when briefly summarizing gialli because i do not want to risk giving away essential plot elements . in the case of the scorpion is tail , the events take an abrupt and totally unexpected turn before the story is even halfway , and i certainly do not want to ruin this for you . many red herrings follow after that , but sergio martino always succeeds to stay one step ahead of you and , even though not a 100 satisfying , the denouement is at least surprising . it is also a very stylish film , with imaginative camera work and excellent music by bruno nicolai . everyone s favorite giallo muse edwige fenech oddly did not make it to this cast she stars in no less than 3 other supreme martino gialli , but anita strindberg lizard in a woman is skin , who saw her die ? is a more than worthy replacement for her . the charismatic and hunky george hilton is reliable as always in his role of insurance investigator and duh ladies man deluxe . if you are a fan of giallo , do not wait as long as i did to watch this film !
