one of the best if not the best rock'n roll movies ever . and it is not just mindless fun . there really were a lot of clever jokes in it . of course i love the ramones . but with all the anarchy and the i hate high school themes , the film does not at all take itself too seriously , which is what is great about it . i first saw the movie in the spring of 1980 , and i saw it again recently . since i went to high school in the late 1970 is , it made me kind of nostalgic . like i said , this film does not take itself that seriously and is not pretentious like so much other teen fare of the seventies , eighties , and nineties . and to speak of , it is not really dirty or disgusting either . only pg rated . that is rare for a movie in this category . a great cult classic and a truly incredible time capsule .
