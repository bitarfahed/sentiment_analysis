i happened to catch this film at a screening in brooklyn it is difficult to describe the plot ; it has a lot of wacky characters , but let is just say i would have a hard time choosing which one made me laugh the hardest , i would not know where to begin . even the peripheral roles are well written and well acted . there are numerous small touches that make it unique and very enjoyable , it has a few devices that pop up and add another hilarious layer . it is refreshing to watch ; not some recycled stuff i would seen many times before . if this film could reach a wider audience , i am certain it would be a real crowd pleaser , the story is so original and heartfelt . there is a lot here to like , funny back stories , mishaps and misunderstandings which set up the final act and dramatic conclusion . cross eyed is a very funny movie with a ton of heart ; it is a touching story with fast paced comedy woven throughout . definitely worth seeing !
