frequent use of advertisement billboards in almost every urban scene , enormous billboards dwarfing any human form in sight . this recurrent visual element is obviously there to underline the way that evokes his characters psychological states , with more or less understated power and great visual impact . he is virtually unsurpassed in this skill . zabriskie point starred two very appealing leads
