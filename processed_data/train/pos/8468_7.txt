a surprisingly interesting meditation on the nature of regret in terms of the way it relates to paranoia . there is a lot going on in this for having a run time of only 74 minutes , but it works . the scares are very subtle , not the jump cut scares that seem to populate most recent asian horror films . the way the scares are set up is similar to the disturbing moments in the uninvited aka 4 inyong shiktak , directed by su leon lee , but this is not nearly to the same caliber . the film does a nice job of balancing a classic ghost story with something more unusual and psychological . it is not perfect , but it does what it is supposed to do . i would recommend checking it out if you get a chance .
