personally , i enjoyed cut thouroughly . it was the first time i have seen a theatrically release australian slasher flick . a genre normally restricted to the mainstream hollywood films . with all the usual cooky comedies and dramas coming out of australia i loved being able to see a homegrown horror movie that was not a rip off of anything . i did not even think it was really a spoof of other movies . it was a supernaturally theme horror like nightmare on elm street , not scream or i know what you did last summer and therefore there was more of a suspension of disbelief . i think it is about time australian films tried to get more into the mainstream genres . cut was original , scary enough and ultimately just a bit of fun . i would give it seven out of ten and would not treat it as anything serious . it did what i expected it to do , entertain and scare me enough times to be satisfying . i enjoyed it .
