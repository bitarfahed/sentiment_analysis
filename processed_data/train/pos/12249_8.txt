old jane is mannered tale seems very popular these days . i have lost count of the number of versions going around . probably the reason is that her ruts are our ruts even at this late date . this tv mini series gives it a mannered telling suitable to the novel . headstrong , opinionated emma is a pretty modern girl when you think about it , even though the ambience of jane austen is world may seem a tad artificial to us today . if you have not seem emma , i will only say that self sufficient emma does get her comeuppance . it is worth watching to find out how . the acting honours here go to the ladies : kate , prunella , lucy and samantha . they could almost have had a psychic connection to old jane !
