this was surprisingly intelligent for a tv movie , and quite true to my own experience of bulimia . it was actually well researched , and i can only assume it was written by someone who is gone through a similar experience , because it had all the little details . the characters were quite well drawn , and the performances by mare winningham and alison lohman were great . i think what i like most was that they made them specific and smart , and there was no dumbing down of the reasons for beth is bulimia it was not some diet gone out of control , caused by the pressures placed on girls by the media , pressures we are not actually going to address . . . . her mother was not completely clueless too often on television they will take an issue that everyone has some awareness of and try to tell us that their protagonists are the last remaining people on earth who do not diabetes ? what is that ? oh , my world is all askew , doctor , please explain it all to me as if i am a small child , etc . it was brilliant that her mother was a psychologist and even she did not see the signs . and the scenes where beth was throwing up were not overly melodramatic and sensationalist , and concentrated more on bulimics need for secrecy , and their out of controlness . the scene where beth tells her mother she is bulimic would have made me cry if there had not been other people in the room . okay , so i liked those bits . what did not work for me so well was the ending , which headed back to the tv movie territory we know and do not particularly love , but i guess they had to wrap it up . you , too , can cure your child is eating disorder , if you have lots and lots of money and live in america . . . and can i just say again that i really like mare winningham . she is great .
