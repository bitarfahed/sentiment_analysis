excellent warner bros effort starring errol flynn in one of his best screen performances . it is often cited as his best , and i cannot really judge fully until i have seen his dawn patrol . however , his work in they died with their boots on takes some beating . i am a big flynn fan he is my favourite actor after james mason it also helps that he is an aussie and i think he is just marvellous , a great screen presence and also a great actor . he is the centrepiece of gentleman jim as the legendary boxer with the fancy footwork , but he is also backed up by a literate , warm and funny script , and raoul walsh is direction . every walsh film i have seen never loses a beat of it is pace , he truly was a born film maker . walsh directs the ring scenes beautifully , as he does with the lighter moments and that poignant , great final scene between ward bord and flynn . add to that great production values the gay nineties never looked better ! and a lovely supporting cast and it is pretty much perfect entertainment . alan hale usually played flynn is sidekick , but here is his father , and it still all works . alexis smith is flynn is love interest . the pair are head over heels in love with fighting with each other .
