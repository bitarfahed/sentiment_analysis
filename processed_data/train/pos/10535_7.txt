the only word that sums up this movie is quirky . it is a light hearted romp through an existential concept . bouncy in more way than one and a bit nutty . i would not exactly call it grand and unforgettable cinema and it does not seem quite as memorable as the director is first movie cube but it is a good pit of fluff to watch on a sunday morning . the acting veers from respectable to annoying at times but i believe that is how it was to be written . done as a serious movie it could perhaps have been great or may very well have stepped into a state of pretension . a little like the matrix meets head meets human nature . 6 . 8 out of ten
