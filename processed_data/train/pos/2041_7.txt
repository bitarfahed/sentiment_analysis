creep is a new horror film that , without a doubt , will please many genre fans simply because it is so down to the point and unscrupulous ! it has many genuine shock moments , a whole lot of repulsive gore sequences and a rare claustrophobic tension . what it has not got is logic and a solid plot but , to tell you the truth , that did not bother me for one second . when the end credits start to roll , there are still many unanswered questions to ponder on but director writer christopher smith in his debut seemly preferred to fully focus on tension and adrenalin rushing action instead of long , soporific speeches and theories that could explain the existence of the creep in the london subway . the story revolves on the young and haughty kate , who leaves her own party in order to go and meet the famous actor george clooney who is in town to present his new film . she falls asleep in the subway , misses the last train and she finds herself trapped in the underground subway network . things really get terrifying when she encounters a mad raving lunatic who lives in the old tunnels and kills kidnaps people to experiment upon . even experienced homeless people , security guards or sewer workers cannot rescue her from this ravenous monster ! i really dug the creep character ! he is nauseating , hideous and primitive but in a strange way fascinating . christopher smith only leaves us clues and hints , and it is merely up to the viewer to guess this vile creature is origin and background . i reckon this is not very original , and i am sure many people will not appreciate the lack of content , but i forgive smith and i think it is better this way than going over the top completely , jeepers creepers style that particular film started out great as well , but as soon as the creeper is identity was clear it turned into a very mediocre horror effort . the obvious aspect to love is the outrageous gore ! there is some severe butchering going on in this film and the make up , as well as the sound effects , are very convincing . the ominous setting of the abandoned london subway during night is effectively used . there also is some acting talent present in this film , with franka potenta run lola run returning to graphic horror nearly five years after the cool german film anatomie . creep is terrific entertainment when you are in an undemanding mood and christopher smith definitely is a director i will keep an eye on . make sure you do not have to take the subway right after watching this film . . .
