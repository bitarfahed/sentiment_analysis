one of hung is better early efforts . the humor is dead on in parts as hung tries to imitate lee is moves and facial expressions , and also in a bit where hung pokes fun at jackie chan is drunken master . the action is also pretty good , especially when hung takes on a trio on martial arts experts at the end . it is not the greatest film , but pretty good for 70 is kung fu , especially if you are a fan of hung and or lee . be warned , though : most video versions of the movie have pretty shoddy quality . there is also a character in blackface which some people might find offensive . sammo hung is a rural swine herder who moves to hong kong to fight off some bad guys . sammo turns on his dead on bruce lee impersonation when fighting ! this film is often billed as a parody of bruce lee is enter the dragon , but it is not exactly that . . . sammo is a rural swine herder obsessed with bruce lee who moves to hong kong to help his relatives run a small open air restaurant . once situated at the new job , he is forced to defend the eatery from local gangsters looking for protection money . when sammo switches into fighting mode , he switches on his bruce lee impersonation , which must be seen to be believed ! this film is pretty sloppy , in many of the fight scenes sammo battles against people who are obviously actors rather than martial artists , and there is one character who is supposed to be black who is played by an asian man in heavy and preposterous makeup . but what this film lacks in budget and accuracy , it more than makes up for in atmosphere and energy . highly recommended for a good mood .
