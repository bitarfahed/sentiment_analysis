although i did not like stanley iris tremendously as a film , i did admire the acting . jane fonda and robert de niro are great in this movie . i have not always been a fan of fonda is work but here she is delicate and strong at the same time . de niro has the ability to make every role he portrays into acting gold . he gives a great performance in this film and there is a great scene where he has to take his father to a home for elderly people because he cannot care for him anymore that will break your heart . i would not really recommend this film as a great cinematic entertainment , but i will say you will not see much bette acting anywhere .
