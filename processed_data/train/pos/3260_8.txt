people call this a comedy , but when i just watched it , i laughed only once . i guess the problem is that i first saw it when i was 14 , and i was not old enough to understand that it was not meant to be taken seriously . there were quite a few scenes that were meant to be funny , but i cared too much about the characters to laugh at them . i suggest that you watch this film next time you are falling in love , and try to take it seriously . i think you will find that , despite a few silly flaws , it is one of the most moving love stories you have ever seen .
