i generally find loretta young hard to take , too concerned with her looks and too ladylike in all the wrong ways . but in this lyrical frank borzage romance , and even though she is playing a low self esteem patsy who puts up with entirely too much bullying from paramour spencer tracy , she is direct and honest and irresistible . it is an odd little movie , played mostly in a one room shack in a hooverville , unusually up front about the depression yet romantic and idealized . tracy , playing a blustery , hard to take regular guy who would be an awful chauvinist and bully by today is standards , softens his character is hard edge and almost makes him appealing . there is good supporting work from marjorie rambeau and glenda farrell who never got as far as she should have , and jo swerling is screenplay is modest and efficient . but the real heroes are borzage , who always liked to dramatize true love in lyrical close up , and young . you sort of want to slap her and tell her character to wise up , she is too good for this guy , but she is so dewy and persuasive , you contentedly watch their story play out to a satisfying conclusion .
