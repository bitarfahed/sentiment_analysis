this movie was excellent . it details the struggle between a committed detective against the dedicated ignorance of the corrupted communist regime in russia during the 80 is . i give this movie high marks for it is no holds barred look into the birth and development of forensic investigation in a globally isolated thanks to the regime community . this is a graphic movie . it presents an unsensationalized picture of violence and it is tragic remains . nothing is candy coated with overdone blood or gore to separate us from the cruel reality on the screen . this movie is based on russian serial killer andrei chikatilo . i am familiar enough with the true story to have a very deep appreciation for how real they kept the film . it is not a comedy , but for those who appreciate dry and dark humor , this movie is a must see .
