this movie is not for everyone . you are either bright enough to get it or you are not . fans of sci fi films who do not take themselves too seriously definitely will enjoy this movie . i recommend this movie for those who can appreciate spoofs and parodies . everyone i have recommended this film to has enjoyed it . if you enjoy monty python or mel brooks films , you will probably enjoy this one . the voice characterizations are done in a tongue in cheek manner and the one liners fly fast and furious .
