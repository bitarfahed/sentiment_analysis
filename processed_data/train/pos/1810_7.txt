i can understand why some people like this movie , and why some people do not . for me , though , i really like it , even if i noticed some good bits , and not so impressive bits . the animation was actually excellent , like charlie is dream . the characters were a mixed bag , the best being anne marie , voiced by the late judith barsi . i was physically ill when i read what happened to her also , carface is a very convincing villain , especially voiced by the wonderful vic tayback i particularly loved morons i am surrounded by morons and along with rasputin and warren t . rat is probably the most memorable of all the don bluth villains . charlie and itchy only just lacked the same sparkle , but i loved king gator and his song . some of the film is very haunting , like annabelle is you can never come come back , which kind of scares me still . unfortunately , there were some bits i did not like so much . the story had a tendency to become clumsy and unfocused , but disney is black cauldron suffered from the same problem . also there were some dark scenes , that young children would find upsetting , but the ending is very poignant . however the biggest flaw was the rather bland songs and the way they were sung . none of them in particular stick out , with exception of let is make music together and love survives , and burt reynolds cannot sing and dom deluise has done much better singing . all in all , a watchable movie , that could have been more , but is definitely memorable , and i would definitely watch it again . 7 10 . bethany cox
