have not seen any of the japanese grudge films , but i really enjoy this one . i rarely get scared when watching films . i can jump , if the effect and sound is startling enough , but getting scared from a movie is a rare thing for me . but i did get scared from grudge . maybe because i did not expect anything at all when i watched it . i did not expect getting scared . i did not know anything about it either . that was probably a good thing . this is a film that you , apparently , either love or hate . most people seem to compare it to the japanese grudge films , but even though i have not seen them , i believe it is not right to compare any film , actually . this film stands on it its own . the story is weak , most people say . i do not agree . the story is minimalistic , and done so on purpose . the story telling techniques used the broken time frame for instance is perfectly done . the director knows exactly what he is doing , and i believe he got his vision through as he wanted it . i gave this film 8 of 10 . it is a film you will enjoy watching , or hate . it is as simple as that .
