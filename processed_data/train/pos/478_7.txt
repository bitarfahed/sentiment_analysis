i am rather surprised that no reviewer so far has commented on the rather elementary chess blunder in luzhin is game as white against an unnamed opponent immediately before the final . despite the use of jonathan speelman as consultant chess expert , luzhin is shown winning the game with an illegal move . in between the rapid cuts away and back to the board it is not hard to spot that after luzhin is combination culminating in a queen sacrifice , his rook on d1 is still pinned by black is rook at c1 against his king in the corner at h1 . thus he is unable to play the purported mating move rd1 d8 which would be illegal but he is shown doing so to rapturous applause from the audience .
