written by the writer who penned the excellent murder rooms series which chronicled acd is adventures with doctor joseph bell , i was looking forward to this and i was not disappointed . it was quite slow moving , with a lot of emphasis on doyle is frustration at sherlock holmes which was very accurate and excellently portrayed . it was an interesting character study and very well shot on digital video , unusual for a period piece . the acting was excellent all round , particularly tim mcinnery and brian cox although the actor who portrayed acd , whose name i cannot remember impressed me no end . an excellent character study which has about the same amount of twists as any normal sherlock holmes case . do see this if you get the chance
