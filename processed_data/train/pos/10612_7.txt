it is cheesy , it is creepy , it is gross , but that is what makes it so much fun . it is got over the top melodramatic moments that are just plain laughable . this movie is great to make fun of . rent it for a good laugh . the film centers around three women newscasters , during a time way before cellphones . they go to a small town to cover a festival , but they cannot get a room to stay the night . and that is when they meet ernest keller . he is creepy in a psycho kind of way . and he offers to let them stay at his home . but he does not tell them the truth about who lives there . stephen furst is performance is so amazing as the unseen , that he really carries this film . most of the movie is kind of dull , although finding out the truth of ernest is family is kind of interesting . just seeing this cast in these scenes makes it worth a look . barbara bach and doug barr make nice eye candy . i consider the movie an old gem , hard to find and worth a look .
