when i think women in prison , my mind often goes to sleazy italian spanish productions by directors such as jess franco and bruno mattei ; and while these films are often very sleazy , they are also very samey and once you have seen one ; you might as well have seen them all . i have to admit that these types of films generally are not my favourites ; but in fact the idea of women behind bars has been done very well on several occasions outside of italy and spain ; and roger corman is new world pictures is responsible for some of the best of them . caged heat is the directorial debut of oscar winning director jonathan demme , and it is a well done little flick with plenty of entertainment value ! naturally , the film centres on the story of a girl who is caught committing crime and sent to a women is prison where she is introduced to a host of violent inmates . this prison is ruled over by the stuff wheelchair bound superintendent mcqueen ; and she takes offence to a play put on by the girls ; leading them to plot an escape . this film is much lighter on the sleaze than i am used to in a women in prison flick ; but this is more than compensated for by some great action scenes and dialogue and that is what ensures caged heat entertains throughout . it does have to be said that the plot is not particularly original or ambitious and basically follows a structure similar to many other women in prison films that came before it ; but that is not such a big problem . the film never gets boring and is peppered with standout scenes ; including an escape attempt while out working in a field and a bank robbery . the film is helped along by assured direction from the man who would go on to helm the masterpiece the silence of the lambs and a great cast with plenty of standouts ; including best of all the legendary barbara steele in the role of the head prison warden . overall , caged heat may not leave the viewer with much to think about by the end ; but it is a brilliantly entertaining little grindhouse flick and anyone that enjoys this type of film will surely want to track it down .
