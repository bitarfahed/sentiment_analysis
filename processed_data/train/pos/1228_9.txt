this 1991 nbc tv movie aired six months before john goodman is big screen version of the life of babe ruth came out . for my money , there is no comparison between the two . the tv production is not perfect but it presents the babe is story with more depth and complexity than goodman is one dimensional telling . i especially enjoyed the film is depiction of the complex love hate relationship ruth had with yankee manager miller huggins , who always understood his star player is brilliance and also kept trying to point out why ruth is own character flaws would never let him become a manager or leader of players . the tv movie rightly notes how ruth never fulfilled his dream of managing the yankees because of his flaws , while the horrible goodman version tries to push the falsehood that ruth was denied what should have been his for the taking . this film makes a great companion piece to eight men out since the story starts with ruth is arrival in new york in 1920 , one year after the black sox scandal and when his home run exploits literally saved baseball from ruin . indeed , the continuity between the two films is even accentuated with john anderson reprising his eight men out role as commissioner kenesaw mountain landis .
