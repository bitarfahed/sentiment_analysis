fever pitch is a fun enough movie . it has a lot of funny moments including a hilariously disturbing shower scene . like most romantic comedies , it has a dead zone in the middle where all the heavy , she is breaking up with me stuff happens , but other than that it continues to be funny until the end . even though the plot revolves around fanaticism towards the red sox , it is not overloaded with sports . you do not have to be a fan to enjoy this film . of course that is easy for me to say : i have been a red sox fan since i was a boy , too . 7 out of 10 . barky
