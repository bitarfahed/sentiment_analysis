this one stood out for it is originality . i am seriously tired of seeing hindi movies that are a hotch potch of a whole bunch of hollywood and brit movies . some flaws were inevitable , nonetheless , this movie is a must see . surya is portrayal of the clean cut , conscientious cop as opposed to the pot bellied , money hungry ones that we normally see was awesome . he is come a long way from his work in nerukku ner . i liked the movie so much that i had to own it . i am not usually into the mindless violence type of movies , somehow i actually felt for each character and therefore cannot really bring myself to call it amindless violence . i do not appreciate the excessive melodrama and sentimental scenes that go hand in hand with most hindi and tamil movies . i absolutely loved this movie for it is lack of the same . acp anbuselvan is reaction to loosing his wife , is not overdone , is heart wrenching and makes me want to bawl my eyes out . there are certain times when i am watching a movie when i want to hit the ff button . plenty of times i have wanted to do that at a cinema hall . never wanted to do so when watching this movie . i am really hoping that ghajini releases soon .
