icy and lethal ace hit man tony arzenta a divinely smooth and commanding performance by alain delon wants to quit the assassination business , but the dangerous mobsters he works for will not let him . after his wife and child are killed , arzenta declares open season on everyone responsible for their deaths . director duccio tessari relates the absorbing story at a constant snappy pace , maintains a properly serious and no nonsense tone throughout , stages the stirring shoot outs and exciting car chases with considerable rip snorting brio , and punctuates the narrative with jolting outbursts of explosive bloody violence . delon is suave and charismatic presence adds extra class to the already engrossing proceedings . this film further benefits from sterling acting by a bang up cast , with praiseworthy contributions by richard conte as wise mafia kingpin nick gusto , carla gravini as arzenta is supportive lady friend sandra , marc porel as arzenta is loyal pal domenico maggio , anton diffring as ruthless , calculating capo grunwald , and lino troisi as the venomous gangster rocco cutitta . silvano ippoliti is glossy cinematography boasts several graceful pans . gianni ferrio is funky score hits the get down groovy spot . erika blanc and rosalba neri pop up briefly in nifty bit parts . better still , there is no filler to speak of and we even get a decent dab of tasty gratuitous female nudity . the startling conclusion packs a mean and lingering wallop right to the gut . a solid and satisfying winner .
