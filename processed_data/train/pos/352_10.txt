i just saw valentine and i have to say that it was the best slasher movie that i have seen in years . unlike the recent trend of 90 is horror flicks , this movie is more concerned with being eerie than it is with being self mocking . for those out there that hated scream , there is not one reference to the horror rules in this movie even though the old slasher movie rules do apply here . this is the perfect blend of 80 is and 90 is horror . you get the style , cinematography , and good acting of 90 is films and the stalking slasher madness of an 80 is flick . i guess the year 2001 is going to finally give horror fans the kind of movies they were longing for . this is definitely a move in the right direction . denise richards stands out here as a fun character . you can tell she liked her role , and that makes her stand out . i loved her in this movie .
