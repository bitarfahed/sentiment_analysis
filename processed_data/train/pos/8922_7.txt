the only notable thing about this film is that it was steve mcqueen is first big starring role . mcqueen is talent is undeveloped and raw but refreshingly honest in this campy little sci fi horror piece . steve shows himself as the anti establishment , hot rod car loving actor who would become a polished icon of the film industry just five years hence . later on , mcqueen would say he hated this film and that he was the blob . but everyone has to start somewhere and the blob is cute , fresh and innocent . would that we all had stayed that way . the plot is fast paced and although predictable , still an entertaining hour or so . and it is really fun to see steve mcqueen before he became the king of kool and anita corsaut before she became andy taylor is girlfriend . a close friend sent me the dvd a while back and it is a treasured addition to my steve mcqueen film collection .
