it is only 2 episodes into a 5 part drama , but i can already state that this is one of the best things i have ever seen . that is on tv , silver screen or even in real life . as a writer , it is so good it is almost demoralising ! as a viewer it is so entertaining that i am annoyed the episodes are over a fortnight instead of monday to friday . it is clear that all these negatives are actually positives . i am a modern guy who previously turned over from tv dramas . in comparison to movies , tv dramas always seemed to be dated , quite tame , and well , generally boring ! five days has really brought tv drama into the 21st century , so for me at least , it is mind changing . go watch it .
