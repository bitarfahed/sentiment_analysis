it is hard to put your finger on this one . basically i suppose it is a comedy about an idle rich drunk who falls in love with a comparatively poor girl , whom he wants to marry at the risk of being disowned by his family . it has funny moments , romantic moments , and touching moments . dudley moore is funny and somehow makes his self centred character endearing , liza minelli is a convincing foil as the the feisty opposite he attracts , but john gielgud steals the show as arthur is wonderfully sarcastic butler . it is corny but great fun with a memorable soundtrack , and ran for nearly 3 months at our local fleapit .
