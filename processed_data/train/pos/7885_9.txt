first of all , i have to start this comment by saying i am a huge nightmare on elm street fan . i think it is the greatest horror series ever . for me , freddy is the boogeyman ! of course , freddy is dead , which tried to be the last chapter back then , is a weird movie . it does not have the same atmosphere than the previous films . freddy has a lot of screen time . some think it makes him less scary , which i do agree . and that is , in my opinion , exactly the point . this movie exists so we can know freddy a little better , who he is , who he were , how he became the man haunting our dreams . for some people , it is a bad thing , it is better if we never know because it is scarier not to know why evil is evil . obviously those people will not like rob zombie is remake of halloween . to truly enjoy this one , you have to see things differently . it is not about a strange guy hiding in the bush of your dreamland waiting to scare the hell out of you . this was the first one , and it was awesome . as the years passed by , freddy killed more and more people , and nobody could ever get rid of him for good . now it is time to learn about the nature of this evil , the psychological aspects of freddy is realm of terror . beside the story of freddy is past , i also really liked the atmosphere of the movie . no more kids in springwood , only crazy grown ups . the nightmare scenes are all great . the soundtrack is awesome , especially the opening song called i am awake now performed by goo goo dolls . in my opinion , the final nightmare is a horror masterpiece and i cannot believe it is so underrated . maybe it is misunderstood , or i have different tastes ! anyway , all freddy fans should watch it . it has a lot of scary moments as well as funny moments too , and a lot of cameos ! get yourself ready for something different and you might not be disappointed .
