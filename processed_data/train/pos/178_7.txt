protocol is a hit and miss picture starring goldie hawn as a bubbly cocktail waitress who one night saves the life of a visiting arab from an assassination attempt . the woman immediately becomes a celebrity , and gets a new job working for the u . s . government . will the corridors of power in our nation is capital ever be the same ? hawn is excellent as usual even though protocol is not as funny as her best film private benjamin . but it is still a good movie , and i did laugh alot . out of four
