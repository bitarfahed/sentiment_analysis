i saw saving grace right after it came out on video . since then it is become one of my favorites ! the plot is not particularly complex but it does not take away from the entertainment . it is chuck full of comedic moments and has a very endearing quality to it . the characters are what makes the movie so good . they each have their own quirky qualities which adds to the humor , the two old ladies played by linda kerr scott and phyllida law leaps to mind . superb acting was done by all , particularly brenda blythen . she and craig ferguson were great together in pulling off some of the funnier moments . if you are looking for a good comedy i would definately recommend this movie !
