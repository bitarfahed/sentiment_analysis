ok , let is get this clear . i am really not into sci fi , but for some reason i love stargate sg 1 . jack o'neil takes his team sg 1 through a stargate . a round device that creates a wormhole . it gives you the ability to travel to distant worlds . it might sound like your usual sci fi series , but it is not ! the plot is set today not in some distant millennium like many other sci fi series . i find that great . it gives you things , happenings and such you can relate to , and you can jump into the series at any time without having to learn many new terms and names of all the gadgets . they have some of course but thanks to o'neil who likes to keep a simple terminology , there is not many . the series has a nice blending of action , humor and drama . if you enjoy loads of special effects you are not going to find it here . they do not use many bad ones but a limited amount of well made special effects .
