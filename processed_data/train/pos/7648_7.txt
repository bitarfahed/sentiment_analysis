i admit i have a weakness for alternate history stories , from its a wonderful life to groundhog day to 12 : 01 . among those greats is this little gem . it is pretty difficult to get through mr . destiny without giving a nod of appreciation to each and every cast member , from the goodhearted james belushi to the murderous courtney cox . this movie lacks the gravitas and scale to make it a great film , but it is a fine cheer up on a rainy afternoon . it is also a great rental for an inexpensive date .
