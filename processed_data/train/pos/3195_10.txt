the beaver trilogy is , without a doubt , one of the most brilliant films ever made . i was lucky enough to catch it , along with a q a session with director trent harris , at the ny video festival a few years back and then bought a copy off of trent is website . this movie has to be seen to be believed ! i sincerely recommend searching for trent is name on the web and then buying the film from his site . he is an incredibly nice guy to boot . do not get confused : the cameraman in the fictional sections of the beaver trilogy is not trent ! after having seen the trilogy a few times , i do have to admit that i could probably do without the sean penn version . it is like a try out version for the crispin glover orkly kid section and is interesting more as a curiosity item if you are a penn fan than it being a good video . penn is pretty funny , though , and you can see the makings of a big star in this gritty b w video . this is probably also one of crispin glover is best roles and i would just love to see an updated documentary about the original groovin gary . once you see this film , you will never get gary is nervous laughter out of your head ever again .
