definitely one of the lesser of the astaire rogers musicals . it is just very poorly plotted and paced . it only runs a few minutes longer than swing time , for example , but it feels a heck of a lot longer . this is partly due to the secondary romance between randolph scott and harriet hilliard . scott is rarely ever interesting . i like hilliard . she is sweet , and i love at least one of her songs , but where are you ? get thee behind me satan , her other number , is a weak leftover from top hat , thankfully cut from that masterpiece . follow the fleet would actually be a bad film if not for at least three brilliant dance sequences between astaire and rogers . the dancing contest vies for the top spot of any of their numbers . the dance is just fantastic . i am putting all my eggs in one basket presents the two rehearsing a dance that they do not quite have perfected yet . its imperfections make it all the more perfect . and let is face the music and dance is easily one of irving berlin is best songs . so the film is well worth watching for its great moments .
