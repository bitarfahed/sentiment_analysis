as a movie this barely rates a 4 but for movie fans of the 1940s period , it is almost a must see and rates a 9 as a variety show ! i was drawn to watch this by the presence of richard captain midnight webb who plays the colonel in charge of the event . what surprised me was the stunning performance of doris day . outside of calamity jane , i have never seen her put over a song better than she does here . randolph scott is memorable as well , even if he does not see much screen time . it is been a while since i saw the movie but i was almost sure humphrey bogart put in an appearance . with so many familiar faces , it is hard to keep track . if it ever turns up on a tv station near you , be sure to catch the doris day sequence , if nothing else !
