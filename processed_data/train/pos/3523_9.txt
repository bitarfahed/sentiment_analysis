a bizarre and brilliant combination of talents between the director , robert siodmak and ella raines as a secretary trying to save her boss from the electric chair by tracking down a mysterious phantom woman , and franchot tone as a crazed and murderous sculptor . as well there are some fascinating smaller parts played by elisha cook jr as a drugged out trap drummer , as well as the other characters who make up parts of the conspiracy . if you are used to seeing raines in more wholesome parts , she is allowed to go overboard here , especially in her scenes with cook . franchot tone also plays someone very different , and though it is not a convincing part , he acts phenomenally . while the conspiracy is too contrived , the film has real atmosphere and is very interesting .
