sweet romantic drama comedy about stewart and sullavan writing love letters to each other without either one knowing who the other is . naturally , they work together and cannot stand each other . you can guess the rest . it is beautifully acted by the entire cast especially sullavan , stewart and frank morgan , has a witty , intelligent script and looks absolutely stunning . it takes place in budapest and was shot in hollywood , but i found myself believing i was seeing budapest ! everything looks so perfect and dream like . a one of a kind film . do not miss it !
