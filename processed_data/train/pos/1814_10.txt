all dogs go to heaven is , in my opinion , the best animated film ever made . i am not really a big fan of animated films , but there is something about this one that makes it better than any other animated film i have seen . the music is wonderful as is the performances of burt reynolds , dom deluise , and especially ken page as the king gator . let is make music together is perhaps one of my favorite songs from any movie musical i have seen . this is definitely a must see for people of all ages .
