ok . there are people who should not see this movie . 1 do not see it if you do not like satire or black humour . 2 do not like it if you got offended by the watchmen . 3 do not see it if you want a serious superhero movie . the rest of you , run , do not walk , to see mystery men . it is funny , it is quirky , it is a delightful sendup of every bad superhero cliche known to man . occasional forays into junior high humour do not ruin the tongue in cheek low key fun of jeanane garafalo , ben stiller , and hank azaria , as well as a couple of amusing smaller parts by geoffrey rush and greg kinnear . good to see louise lasser getting work , too . i laughed all the way through . utterly unserious , somewhat weird , but good .
