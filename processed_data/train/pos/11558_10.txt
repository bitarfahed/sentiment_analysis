the odd couple is one of those movies that far surpasses its reputation . people all know it , they hum the theme song , they complain of living with a sloppy oscar or a fastidious felix . . . but they are under selling the film without knowing it . this is not just about a neat guy living with a sloppy guy ; it is a portrait of two friends helping each other through the agony of divorce . it is also damn funny from start to finish , but it is the kind of comedy that arises from realistic , stressful , and just plain awful situations . so , some viewers have actually found the film to be a bit uncomfortable , but i think its verisimilitude is its strength . besides , matthau is bulldog face just cracks me up ! my favorite comedy , by a country mile .
