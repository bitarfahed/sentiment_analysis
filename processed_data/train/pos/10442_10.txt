this is truly truly one of the best movies about love that i have ever seen . closely followed by none other than before sunset , which technically is not another movie at all , since it is about the same two people and the same romance . this is love in the real world . ok , that is only if most people are as intelligent and eloquent as the leads in the movie . reading the other reviews , it pleases me to know how so many other folks are crazy over dialog based movies as well . and this is what makes before sunrise so good . the dialog is perfect . it is so real , so engaging and funny . it is hardly a surprise that jesse and celine fall in love , coz you fall in love with them at the very same time . my favorite scene is the one in the coffee shop , where they pretend to phone their best friends , with the other pretending to be said best friend . it is perfect . brings you back to the very moment when you fell in love for the very first time in your life . i must say that if you have a choice , do watch before sunrise before watching before sunset . if like me , you watched sunset first , it is hard to shake off the feeling of pity and sadness for the two young lovers throughout the entire show . once again , the greatest romantic movie in my books . wonderful acting , excellent script , and beautiful locations . young love , at it is best .
