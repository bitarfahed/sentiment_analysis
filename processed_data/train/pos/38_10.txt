one of the very best three stooges shorts ever . a spooky house full of evil guys and the goon challenge the alert detective agency is best men . shemp is in top form in the famous in the dark scene . emil sitka provides excellent support in his mr . goodrich role , as the target of a murder plot . before it is over , shemp is trusty little shovel is employed to great effect . this 16 minute gem moves about as fast as any stooge is short and packs twice the wallop . highly recommended .
