wonderful romance comedy drama about an italian widow cher who is planning to marry a man she is comfortable with danny aiello until she falls for his headstrong , angry brother nicholas cage . the script is sharp with plenty of great lines , the acting is wonderful , the accents i have been told are letter perfect and the cinematography is beautiful . new york has never looked so good on the screen . a must see primarily for cher and olympia dukakis they are both fantastic and richly deserved the oscars they got . a beautiful , funny film . a must see !
