i first saw this at a foreign film festival . it is a beautifully paced nail biter about a plot to relieve the estonian treasury of a billion or so in gold . it is all shot in a gritty , grainy style that hollywood rarely uses but it captures the atmosphere of the newly emancipated baltic states beautifully note : tallin was actually looking a lot less grim in 2003 when i was there . there is a lot of humor and some romance , too . i do not want to spoil a number of startling yet logical surprises , so i will just say this heist film starts from a great script , and the directing and performances are top notch . darkness in tallin is simply the fastest and most nerve racking example of its genre i would put it up against rafifi , topkapi , and it is miles ahead of the new ocean is 11 , though deliberately not as glossy . rent or buy it now .
