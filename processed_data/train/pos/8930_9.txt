to suggest that he is us , the first world versus the third , seems too simple since he feeds no less on his fellow first worlders , on all of us . denis is camera is eye when it looks at things i know goes usually where mine would , so i tend to trust her when she looks at things i do not know . snow trekking , too fast bicycling , and forest darkness i have known in small ways , but the south seas not at all , so i made better entry into l'intrus , both france and the crystalline isles of its finish , than into beau travail . l'intrus is , for me , a very comfortable discomforting film . it is a sequence of places portrayed familiarly , with a intimacy that allows us to know them whether we have seen the reality or not . a single image , trebor cycling , his massive weight on the thin racing frame , the sounds of violated air and shrieking tires , the asphalt ribbon , the dark in bright sun evergreens , cued me that the film would be linear , a road trip , a single will driven thrust . despite trebor is personal power , he is a human failure . no matter who he is with , he is alone , though apparently he has not always been . his body aborts life twice , first to need the new heart , then despite it . l'intrus is tragedy . trebor is hubris . i am navigating perilously the thread of what i remember . let is leave it at that .
