i did not approach still crazy with any real anticipation . just another rock'n roll picture , i figured . . . good nostalgia for the baby boomers . this film is partially that , but so much more . brian gibson , the director , previously helmed a biography of tina turner , and is quite successful in his style . i suppose it is fitting that this was his last film . the cast is well chosen . bill nighy is perfect in his role as the band is frontman . actor turned director bruce robinson appears as the band is washed up guitarist . he does a superb job , even though he has not appeared on film since the late 70 is . if you are looking for an touching and funny film with some great songs , you have found it . 7 . 4 out of 10
