first off , i am not some justin timberlake fangirl obsessed with making him look good , in fact i am not even a huge justin fan , but i did like this movie . i work at a video store and when i saw this movie with its huge cast that i would never even heard of i had to see what it was about . i did not find justin is acting that bad , it was clearly the worst out of the group , but it is a pretty impressive group , with cary elwes and dylan mcdermott being two names that did not even make the first credits list . the story is basic , a journalist uncovering corrupt cops , but i found it well done . l l cool j is character was clearly conflicted , but i honestly did not know what he would do in the end . morgan freeman is as always , the wise mentor figure he does so well , and as much as i love kevin spacey , he was kind of just there . his character did not have a whole lot of substance , but it is kevin spacey , he can do no wrong . surprisingly i thought dylan mcdermott gave the best performance as a homicidal cop . truly believable and really in character , he freaked me out a couple of times . i was really expecting a lot of cheesiness to be honest . horrible catchphrases , unjustified action sequences , stuff like that , but it was surprisingly well done and i did not find any of that . every shooting had a point , it was not clich d , pretty solid really . overall , amazing cast , decent story that kept me interested and just enough action to make me jump . i do not know why it did not appear in theatres , it was better than some garbage i have seen on the big screen . i would say it is worth seeing .
