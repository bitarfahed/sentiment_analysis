the golden child was eddie murphy is first film since his megahit beverly hills cop . and even though it is not as good as cop , it is a fun comic adventure . murphy stars as a finder of lost children who is assigned a most unusual case . his assignment : to find the title character , a child with mystical powers . this movie could have been titled beverly hills cop and the temple of doom since parts of this movie plays like a spielbergian adventure , kinda like an indiana jones comedy . it is got comedy with laughs , and adventure with special effects . lots of fun . out of four
