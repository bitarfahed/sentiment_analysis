this is very dated , but that is part of the charm with this 1933 movie . you can say the same for most pre code films ; they are just different , and usually in an interesting way . it was the short running time , the great acting of spencer tracy and the beautiful face and sweetness of loretta young is character which kept me watching and enjoying this stagy but intriguing film . you would be hard pressed to find a nicer girl than trinna , played by the 20 year old young who was already into making her 50th movie ! she started acting as a small child . that , and the fact they made movies quickly back in the old days . the camera , although in soft focus throughout much of the film , zoomed in on loretta is face and eyes many times and i was mesmerized by her beauty . playing a crotchety man with a cynical outlook on life , tracy is bill slowly transformed into a loving man , thanks to trinna . spencer delivered his lines here with such naturalness that you hardly knew he was acting . although they have small roles , supporting actors walter connolly , marjorie rambeau , arthur hohl and glenda farrell leave lasting impressions long after viewing this 75 minute film . i was particularly fascinated with connolly is role as the minister father figure of the camp . the story is a little far fetched but hey that is the movies . this story is about two lonely great depression victims trying to survive in a hooverville type camp and it winds up to be a very touching tale .
