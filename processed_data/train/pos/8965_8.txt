made in the same year as vertigo , this is an equally bewitching movie , though in a much lighter vein . it is set in an enchanted new york during the winter : kim novak is a witch who casts a spell over james stewart , but gets caught in it instead . the interesting sidelight is that novak is rival is played by janice rule , who originated the part of madge in picnic on broadway the part that novak would make famous on film .
