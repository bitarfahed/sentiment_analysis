i was very fond of this film . it kept me guessing till just before the very end what would happen . one of the better movies about the partition that i have seen . urmila matondkar is gorgeous too . this is one of the most personal and down to earth films i have seen on the partition . it is a little less mainstream than gadar , and is really an emotional roller coaster where you start out with one opinion of what is going on , and come out completely one the other side . this is not typical bollywood fare , but rather an art house type film . the best part of this movie is that it does not dehumanize one side of the partition conflict when focusing on the story of another . it does not blame or castigate but rather lets you draw your own conclusions about things .
