holes , the novel , was forced on me in an education course . i did not think i would like a children is novel ; plus , the other couple of books i was forced to read for the class were really bad . but , to my surprise , i absolutely loved holes . it really is one of the most perfectly written novels i have ever read . i think it has the rare quality that makes it appeal to pre teens , teenagers , and adults . everyone who reads it , i think , will walk away a better person . while i cannot quite say that for the film , i am happy to say that they got it mostly right . i do not think viewers of the film will walk away as enriched , but they will certainly be entertained , without the side effect of being stupider when they sat down . it is an intelligent story , and it is very well told . i think it moves a tad too quickly . the novel takes more time in developing the characters . and the flashbacks come in and out so quickly that they do not have too much time to register . the interracial romance in the past feels more clich and trite than it does in the novel . and the ending , which ties together all the loose threads , seems very ridiculous . it is exactly the same in the novel , but there is a sense of the absurd that does not quite exist in the film . it works a lot better . i also do not like the multitude of pop songs . i wish disney did not feel it such a necessity to sell soundtracks . the cast is across the board excellent , from the young kids to the old pros . jon voight is especially great . not quite sure why we need catwoman and the fonze , though . 9 10 .
