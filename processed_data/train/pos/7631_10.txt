kurt russell is chameleon like performance , coupled with john carpenter is flawless filmmaking , makes this one , without a doubt , one of the finest boob tube bios ever aired . it holds up , too : the emotional foundation is strong enough that it will never age ; carpenter has preserved for posterity the power and ultimate poignancy of the life of the one and only king of rock and roll . i would been a borderline elvis fan most of my life , but it was not until i saw this mind blowingly moving movie that i looked beyond the image at the man himself . it was quite a revelation . elvis remains one of the top ten made for tv movies of all time .
