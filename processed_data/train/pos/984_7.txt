i happened across bait on cable one night just as it started and thought , eh , why not ? i am glad i gave it a chance . bait ai not perfect . it suffers from unnecessarily flashy direction and occasional dumbness . but overall , this movie worked . all the elements aligned just right , and they pulled off what otherwise could have been a pretty ugly film . most of that , i think , is due to jamie foxx . i do not know who tagged foxx for the lead , but whoever it was did this movie a big favor . believable and amazingly likeable , foxx glides through the movie , smooth as butter and funnier than hell . you can tell he is working on instinct , and instinct does not fail him . the plot , while unimportant , actually ties together pretty well , and there is even a character arc through which foxx is character grows as a person . again , they could have slipped by without any of this , but it just makes things that much better . i am surprised at the low rating for this . maybe i just caught this move on the right night , or vice versa , but i would give it a 7 10 . bravo , mssr . foxx .
