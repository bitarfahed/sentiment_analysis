this is the ultimate one man show in which eddie murphy is at his very best . just forget the nutty professor and the distinguished gentlemen , this is the real eddie murphy . his imitations of mr . t . pretending he is gay , michael jackson and other artists are killers . i think it is also quite daring to make fun of artists who where really popular in that time . my favorite act is the one where he is at his annual bbq with the family and plays his drunken dad and aunt bunny who falls from the stairs . this show is the best medicine when you feel down ! if you watch the sequel raw do not be disappointed . it is quite good too but does not match wouldelirious .
