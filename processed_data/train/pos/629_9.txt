the 14 year old in me is immensely happy that they are now able to make really good looking fantasy movies , and that they are all the rage , what with lord of the rings , harry potter and the chronicles of narnia making loads of cash at the box office . this year will see and already has seen several more , most notably the golden compass , which has the most exciting trailer i have seen this year . stardust , based on a novel by neil gaiman , showed up in theaters this week with little more than a peep . i saw no previews for it , only a couple of commercials . the critical reaction is kind of blah . i would not even have seen it if not for the fact that i have to wait on a friend to see the bourne ultimatum , and that nothing else interesting opened this weekend . well , if you will forgive the horrible pun , the stars must have been rightly aligned , because i went to see stardust , and i loved it . it is not a huge movie like lord of the rings . the plot line is your very basic fantasy quest the hero sets out to look for a fallen star filled with obstacles . but within that basic outline , the story is lively and imaginative . it is simply aiming to be a lot of fun , and a charming little romance . and it succeeds wonderfully . there were a lot of big films this summer , but none of them were nearly as fun as this one . there is a lot going on , but the story is told well and is almost entirely coherent . it is not a masterpiece , but it definitely can occupy the same kind of ground that something like the princess bride has though i do not like it quite as much as the earlier film . a lot of fun to be had here if you are a fan of the genre .
