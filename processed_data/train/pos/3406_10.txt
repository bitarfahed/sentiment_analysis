in terms of visual beauty this movie is outstanding ! i had no idea that technicolor came out so early . although i did not like the ending , the entire movie is fantastic and makes me wish that i was in north africa . the cast is excellent and marlene dietrich is a big plus and of course she is so alluring and i just loved her in this flick . basil rathbone is also perfect in this movie . i could not get over the scenery and the sets . . . the hotel , the palm trees , the desert , it is all there . . . the legionnaires also bring a beau geste feeling to the film . they certainly do not make movies like these anymore . do not fail to watch this classic !
