brokedown palace is truly a one of a kind . it is an amazing story , showing two girl is plight for freedom against the thailand justice system . they soon find themselves placing faith into a system they know nothing about . alice morano claire danes and darlene davis kate beckinsale , are two best friends , strait out of high school . they suddenly change their vacation plans from hawaii to thailand , and are immediately captivated by a young man , nick parks . he flirts with them both , and suggests that the three of them go to hong kong for the weekend . when the two arrive at the airport , they are immediately searched for drugs . someone tipped off customs , and in an instant , their life is changed forever . in the mix of the confusion of settling into their new life , they learn about a highly respected lawyer , named hank green bill pullman . an american who knows the thai justice system , he fights for the girl to be free . but they soon find out , when they leave or go is all up to them . if you are looking for a great movie that will stay with you for years brokedown palace is definitely the way to go .
