moonwalker is probably not the film to watch if you are not a michael jackson fan . i am a big fan and enjoyed the majority of the film , the ending was not fantastic but the first 50 or so minutes were if you are a fan . i personally believe the first 50 minutes are re watchable many times over . the dancing in each video is breathtaking , the music fantastic to listen to and the dialogue entertaining . it includes many of his finest videos from bad and snippets from his earlier videos . it also includes some live concert footage . if you are a big fan of michael jackson this is a must , if you are not a fan do not like michael jackson , steer well clear . 9 10
