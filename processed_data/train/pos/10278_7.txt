house of games is definitely not without its flaws plot holes , stiff acting , final scenes but they do little to detract from the fun of watching a thriller that so methodically messes with your head . house of games does almost everything a good thriller is supposed to do . of course , this is not a huge feat given the fact that we are dealing with the the world of confidence men and the cons they perpetrate . so it stands to reason that we never really know what is going on , even though we think that we do . but that is what makes the film worthwhile for those who are game ; a film for which repeated viewings are indulgences instead if necessities . it has a definite hitchcock slant to it . the film draws on some similar themes found his 1964 effort marnie , considered a misfire when released but now regarded as one of the master is more thought provoking works . one could easily consider the idea of lindsay crouse is character being the same as tippi hedrin is . . . ten year later perhaps . both are strong willed loners , both with compulsive behaviors which compel them to walk too close to the shark pool . as crouse is repressed , up tight character says , what is life without adventure ? put your reality check on a low setting and enjoy swimming with the sharks !
