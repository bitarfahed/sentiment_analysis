two great comedians in a great neil simon movie based on his hit play . great combination , especially when the comedians in question are matthau and burns . small wonder why burns won an oscar for this ; he is as sharp and as funny as ever . and matthau is every bit his match , if a tad more crotchety . this is familiar simon territory : two old vaudeville partners reunite for a tv special but still cannot stand one another after all these years . it is a delight to watch these two pick at each other , their scenes together make this film an absolute delight . myself , i especially enjoyed the knock , knock , knock enter ! scene . and if you are a fan of either burns or matthau , you will enjoy it , too . in fact , you will enjoy the whole movie . ten stars . put a little sunshine in your life .
