this is the french and belgians doing what they do best . it is quirky , visually inventive , exhilarating and emotionally challenging storytelling . director jaco van dormael takes us into the world of georges , a down is syndrome sufferer and his quest for a meaningful relationship with someone , just anyone . this is not done in a patronising way but with a great sense of fun and also honesty . georges interplay with corporate management guru , harry is dazzlingly handled shifting from comedy to tragedy back to comedy again with breathtaking ease . the eighth day puts similar hollywood fare like barry levinson is oscar winning rain man or robert zemeckis is forrest gump well and truly in the shade . at times , it evokes the humour of milos forman is one flew over the cuckoo is nest with shades of dennis potter thrown in for good measure . as the emotionally blunted and desperately lonely yuppie , harry , daniel auteuil turns in yet another sublime performance . but it is matched by the brilliant pascal duquenne as georges . it is a movie with uniformly strong performances and so many , memorable set pieces the shoe shop scene , car showroom scene , george is dance to genesis is jesus he knows me , the conference scene , the fireworks scene . if you have not seen it , there is only one thing to do . just rent it or attend a screening at a retro cinema near you and see what you have been missing . better still , buy this movie . sheer genius . . . . .
