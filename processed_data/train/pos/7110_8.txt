john garfield plays a marine who is blinded by a grenade while fighting on guadalcanal and who has to learn to live with his disability . he has all the stereotypical notions about blindness , and is sure he will be a burden to everyone . the hospital staff and his fellow wounded marines cannot get through to him . neither can his girl back home played by eleanor parker . he is stubborn and blinded by his own fears , self pity , and prejudices . it is a complex role that garfield carries off memorably in a great performance that keeps one watching in spite of the ever present syrupy melodrama . the best scenes are on guadalcanal , where he is in a machine gun nest trying to fend off the advancing japanese soldiers in a hellish looking night time battle , and later a dream sequence in the hospital where he sees himself walking down a train platform with a white cane , dark glasses , and holding out a tin cup , all the while his girlfriend walks backward away from the camera .
