i went to see fever pitch with my mom , and i can say that we both loved it . it was not the typical romantic comedy where someone is pining for the other , and blah blah blah . . . you were not waiting for the climatic first kiss or for them to finally get together . it was more real , because you saw them through the relationship , rather than the whole movie be about them getting together . people could actually relate to the film , because it did not seem like extraordinary circumstances , or impossible situations . it was really funny , and i think it was jimmy fallon is best performance . all in all . . . i would definitely recommend it !
