this is undeniably the scariest game i have ever played . it is not the average shoot everything that moves kind of fps which i usually do not care much for , but the acceptable gfx , interesting weapons and magic , great surround soundeffects scryeeee , scryeeee . . and above all incredible atmosphere . i love the scrye , which enables you , at certain places in the games , to see or hear events that happened there in the past . the only game i have had to take regular breaks after a few minutes of playing just because of the intensity of the atmosphere . i am a great horror fan , escpecially of clive barker is stories and movies , and participating in a horror story like this makes me yearn for more games that emphasizes atmosphere and a more involving story . 9 10 1 because i am no fps fan , and perhaps the game was a bit short ?
