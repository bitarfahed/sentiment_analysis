i am gonna tip the scales here a bit and say i enjoyed this . however , the cartoon is really only going to appeal to those who have very absurdist tendencies . it is definitely something that most people will not get , as is the nature of absurdism . the animation is horrible , but yes , that is the point . the main character is foul mouthed , violent , and stupid . no redeeming qualities whatsoever . his wife shrieks and wails , apparently just barely capable of the most basic communication skills . most of these stories completely lack any kind of point . but again , that is the point ; if non sequiters , foul language , and complete and utter randomness are your thing , you are going to love this . it is really short , so i would probably rent instead of buying .
