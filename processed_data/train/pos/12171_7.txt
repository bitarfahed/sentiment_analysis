i was literally preparing to hate this movie , so believe me when i say this film is worth seeing . overall , the story and gags are contrived , but the film has the charm and finesse to pull them off . that gag where jason lee thinks he has crabs , and tries not to let his boss future father in law and co workers see him scratching himself is not terribly intelligent , but it sent me into a frenzy of laughter . very few of the film is gags are high brow , but they made me laugh . as i said , the film has charm and charm can go a long way . the characters are likable , too . i must say i wish i got to see more of james brolin is character , since he was a hoot in the very few scenes he was in . plus , i admire any romantic comedy that has the guts to not make the character of the wife who serves as the obstacle in the plot a total witch . the selma blair character is hardly unlikable , and there is never a scene where i thought to myself , why did he want to marry her in the first place ? the ending is hollywood ish , but it could have been much more schmaltzy . the cast is talented . i have not had a favorable view of most of jason lee is mainstream work . i just loved him so much in kevin smith is films that i could not help but feel disappointed at seeing him in these dopey roles . and he never looks comfortable in these dopey roles . even in this movie , he does not look perfectly comfortable , but he contributes his own two cents and effectively handles each scene . but i still miss his work in independent films . julia stiles proves again why she is so damn likable . of course , she is a very beautiful girl with a radiant smile that makes me want to faint , but she also possesses a unique charm and seems to have good personality . in other words , her beauty shows inside and out . i do not know the actresses name , but the woman who plays the drunk granny is hilarious . julie hagerty also has a small part , and she is always enjoyable to watch , which makes me wish she received better roles . i loved her so much in airplane and lost in america that it is a shame she does not get the same opportunities to flaunt her skills . do not be put off by the horrible trailers and even more horrible box office records . this is a funny , charming film . romantic comedies are getting so predictable nowadays that it feels like the genre itself is ready to be flushed down the toilet , so it is always to see a good one among all these bad apples . my score : 7 out of 10
