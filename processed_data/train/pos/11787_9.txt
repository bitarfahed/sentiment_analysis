it is a short movie from david lynch with just 8 minutes , but it got all the lynchian ingredients ! it is mysterious , dark , inconclusive , eerie , and strange ; and before the blond girl starts to talk it is even a bit scary ! the soundtrack is exceptional to create this odd atmosphere because it is also sinister and mysterious about the setting itself , it has not the traditional red curtains , but it has socking purple painted walls , which give it an equally effect of eeriness . the plot is about a girl who is locked in a dark room and she cries for help ; then comes another girl who starts talking to her in a mysterious way , saying she is there just because of her fault we do not know what did happen or what will happen next it ended unsolved and puzzling , as a good lynch movie must end ! it is a great short , despite some amateurish acting . the girls are professional actresses , but i think their acting could have been better in this short .
