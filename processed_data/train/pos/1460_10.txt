excellent film that reveals how people are connected to the taken for granted , ordinary beads exchanged during mardi gras . the film is much more than a commentary on globalization . in fact , it humanizes the workers in china , the owner of the factory , the bead distributor in new orleans , and even the revelers in new orleans . what stands out the most is the director is ability to tell a tricky story with complicated details in such a simple and seductive way . his amazing access to the factory is another aspect that is intriguing and i only wish i knew how he got inside . it is a beautiful story without sentimentality or guilt associated with it , and the conclusion provides hope without leaving people feeling alienated .
