i did not know what to expect from ned kelly , but absolutely loved it . it was dark , dramatic and gripping . it also felt very authentic , i felt that i had been transported back to the 1800 is . i have never been much of a heath ledger fan , having only seen him in teen type movies , however he is quite compelling in this role . ledger plays ned kelly with dignity and intensity , showing us how an highly spirited boy became australia is most notorious killer . naomi watts is great in a supporting role as kelly is society lover . highly recommended and that is from an aussie !
