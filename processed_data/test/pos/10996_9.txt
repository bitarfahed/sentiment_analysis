i would recommend this film to anyone who is searching for a relaxing , fun filled , thought provoking movie . the absence of sex , vulgarities and violence made for a most pleasant evening . i especially enjoyed the buffalo scene , but that is probably because i live a short distance from there . even so , this film could have been produced in any city ; it is the theme that is so important here . i am just grateful that manna from heaven dropped down on us . try it . . . you will like it !
