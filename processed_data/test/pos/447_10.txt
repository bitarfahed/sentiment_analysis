the silver screen gets freshly polished with this beautiful film about aging happily and enjoying life is rainbows . there is plenty of silver hair on this silver screen , but the film is namesake is more like 85 going on 25 with his energy , humor and lust for life . the story of entertainer extraordinaire uncle frank , his devoted wife aunt tillie , and the zippy residents of the local area nursing homes inspires us to live each day as if it is your last and brings a glimmer of hope to those often dreaded golden years . a great movie for young and old audiences !
