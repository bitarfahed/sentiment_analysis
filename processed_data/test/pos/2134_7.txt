this great film never showed up in my town , so actually i did not have any opportunity to watch it until the late 80'es when i caught it on german television . i was expecting something of a disaster , and found instead a well acted grand western with superb location work . the tiny tube could not really damage it and there is almost not a dull moment in this 4 hour film , so i hope to see it once again on the big screen . what a spectacle that would be ! do not miss it , if you ever have the chance . unfortunately the harsh treatment of heaven is gate at its opening ruined michael cimino is career and he moved from the passable year of the dragon to the boringly ludicrous the sicilian and the screechingly dumb desperate hours .
