i think this is one of the best tamil movies i have seen in a while . i love the fact that it does not revolve around a guy and girl falling in love and they made the movie brilliantly . the cast did a great job and i especially congratulate the litte girl . she was brilliant and really brought out the feeling of an adopted child searching for her real mother . best of all are the songs , beautiful music and moving lyrics . there are some great songs in this move ya will ! especially kannathil mutthamittal and havelai pookal . great songs , both of them . i highly recommend this movie to anyone who wants to enjoy a good cry and some great acting and superb songs ! . cheers simran .
