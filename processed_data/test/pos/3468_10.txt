i have been looking forward to seeing this film ever since i first caught the trailer , and i am so glad now that i have . it is truly a wonderful film . the actors are superb , the writing is fresh and real , the whole thing was just spot on . i love james mcavoy in this , and i cannot wait to see him in the lion , the witch , and the wardrobe movie this december . romola garai is wonderful too . be sure to check her out in i capture the castle or nicholas nickleby , two of my favorite films . overall , i think i liked this movie because it did not chicken out . it is a difficult subject matter to tell a story about , in that you are very likely to offend a lot of people or mess up and make it into some overly sentimental sugary sweet love fest . but they avoided doing that completely , and instead made a film that is real , honest , and touching , yes , but never over the top . very well done . amazingly well done . go out and see it , and you will know exactly what i mean .
