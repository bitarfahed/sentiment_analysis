if you are a long time fan of the doctor and you cringed when you heard they were making another series , rest easy it more than meets the high expectations of the original . the pacing is much quicker than the original shows , fitting more often into 50 minutes episodes rather than the average 90 minutes . the writing is excellent , the acting superb . the hardest and best thing to get used to is the production values of the new series . compared to the original , it is got some now . although i will always have fond memories of bubble wrap and hand puppet monsters . if you are not a fan , or if you tried the original and could not get a handle on it , jump in with both feet now ! everything you really need to know about the doctor , they will tell you as they go along . this series was written with minimal references to the doctor is enormous back story specifically to encourage new viewers . admittedly , i am only seeing the first new series now as it is being shown on the sci fi channel in other words , probably cut to ribbons for time constraints , but i am looking forward to future episodes either on broadcast tv or on dvd . july 4th cannot come soon enough !
