basically , this is a very good film . it is very different from seagal is other films though , which unfortunately may turn off some of his fans . unlike some of the other films , this does not feel like a iseagal vehicle . you get to see plenty of the other characters as well and all of mr . seagal is actions are not the same as we have seen before . the concept of him being an intellectual in something other than bombs and other weaponry is a nice change . although there are some unrealistic parts to the film some more obvious than others , in general you can let them slide since the film is fun to watch . it boils down to this : if you are looking for a classic seagal action film , sorry , but you are going to be disappointed . watch it anyway for the fun of it . if you are open to seeing seagal without the action , it is well worth a look . i personally believe it is some of the best work he is done in quite a while .
