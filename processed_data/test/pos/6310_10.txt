well , where do i start . . . as one of the other reviewers said , you know you are in for a real treat when you see the opening shot minutes and minutes of film time spent on a guy standing on a travelator . i will not repeat rubin is excellent summary of the story . what i would like to say , though , is that this film gripped me more than any film i can remember . i sat open mouthed , and on the edge of my seat all the way through . the camera work , sound track and fantastic performances particularly that of tony servillo draw you to the screen and will not let you look away . it is italian , so of course everyone looks fantastic , but it is by no means merely an exercise in cool style . this is a film with lots to say about luck , loss and love . go and see it .
