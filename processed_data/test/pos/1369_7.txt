my buddies and i spent the majority of a saturday afternoon watching a selection of bad movies . among the flicks we watched , the strongest contender for quality bad movie fare was easily jack o . it is ludicrous that movies such as gigli , glitter and you got served are listed in imdbs bottom 100 . while they are certainly bad movies , they do not belong in the bottom 100 . they are robbing jack o , and keeper of time , etc , of the bad movie greatness they so richly deserve . so what makes jack o so great in bad movie terms ? for starters , steve latshaw , the director , decided to cast his son , ryan latshaw , in the role of sean kelly . unfortunately for steve , ryan latshaw was dangerously close to being out acted by a block of wood . the kid , seriously , has no ability to emote whatsoever . the end result : unintentional comic gold . the kid could be listening to a joke , or just moments away from getting his head smashed asunder , and his expression is one of stony emotionlessness . the other aspect of the movie that we found awesome was the sheer number of double dreaming sequences . what is a double dream ? well , it is when a character wakes up from a nightmare , and then something equally nightmarish happens , and then the character wakes up again . basically : they wake up after dreaming about waking up from a nightmare . clever device , no ? i believe the character of sean kelly experienced no less than 3 double dreaming sequences . let is see . . . what else ? oh yeah ! this movie has a veritable cast of thousands . it is truly stunning to see how many speaking roles are introduced throughout the course of the movie . best of all : almost none of the characters have anything to do with the story . they are either killed by jack o , or they serve no purpose whatsoever . jack o himself was pretty sweet . like most other b movie monsters , jack o has the amazing ability to , seemingly , teleport over great distances . he is invariably hanging out , somewhere in the background , whenever you are dealing with a major character . what is puzzling , however , is that when he is actually chasing someone he moves at a shambling stumbling speed , and yet he is able to keep up with people who are sprinting . that is all for now . closing remarks : if you are looking for a unintentionally hilarious bad movie , you cannot go wrong by renting this beast . bad movie score : 7 10 good movie score : 3 . 5 10
