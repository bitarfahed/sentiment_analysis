i caught this movie on local t . v . at about 4 : 30 a . m . i did not have expectations for it but after the opening credits i knew i was into something good . there are situations that are left for your interpretation to find out what really happened so your attention is a key factor . the whole psychological thriller situation deals a lot with character development and the way things are executed by the main character . your sub conscience also plays an important part because through a series of events and dialogs you will be able to make a correct interpretation and generate an opinion . the technical aspects are very good . really good in fact . kelly overtone is extremely sexy and she is candy for the eye although she is most of the time in her white clothes . the fear factor is almost inexistent but there is plenty of mystery and tension to dig from . i would recommend it for those who enjoy supernatural and psychological thrillers .
