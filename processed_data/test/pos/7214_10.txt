i think it was fran ois truffaut who said that the best movies either involve the joy of making movies , or the agony of making movies . this flick is definitely of the first type . tromeo and juliet is a pleasure to watch from start to finish . the zany zeal fuelling this shakespearian shenanigan is infective . i do not think i have laughed so hard since i saw monty python and the holy grail . it is that good !
