the royal rumble has traditionally been one of my favourite events , and i have been a wrestling fan for a good few years now . the other shows may have better matches , but i have always found the actual rumble match to be full of excitement . i am not going to reveal the winners of any match as i do not see it as fair to ruin the results on a review . i will comment on the quality of them though . we have the standard 4 matches , and then the big rumble event . two from smackdown and two from raw . shawn michaels and edge open up for raw . this proves to be a good match from two talented guys . this is a match i would recommend watching . it is hard to sum up without giving away the winner . next we have the usual undertaker against some big nasty monster , be whoever it is . giant gonzales , yokozuna , kamala . . . well this time it is heidenreich . its also a casket match . typical undertaker fare . watch if you are a fan . i have to admit i am , purely for the entertainment factor . it can hardly be regarded as a classic wrestling match . the next two matches are the title matches . for once smackdown manages to upstage raw . their title match is pretty thrilling and enjoyable , but with a anti climax and let down to end it . raw is match is a pretty dull and boring affair , which is a pity as i am a fan of both guys involved . now to the main reason i love the event , the rumble . it is a pretty good one this year . coming up to the event we all had a pretty good idea of who might win , and it may not prove a big surprise , but hey , its very enjoyable . there are the usual diverse ways of people being eliminated . there is the token guy who does not make it to the ring , the entrant who is ridiculous and we all want to see vanquished , and someone gets eliminated by a previously eliminated combatant . it has its usual highs and lows , and i loved the ending , in particular the vince mcmahon entrance . i would recommend this show . not the wwe on top form , but its still good . add it to your collection .
