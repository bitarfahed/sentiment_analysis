not bad but could be loads better , also seems to follow a staple diet for crime drama . you have got sandra bullock as the cop who finds it difficult to work with others , you have got her placed with a new partner . there is a closed case but a cop who works on it anyway , and there is so many corney lines in the film it makes you want to scream . however , if you can look past the formulaic elements there is a good film hiding underneath . it is gripping and the subplots worked for me . well worth a rent but maybe not a purchase .
