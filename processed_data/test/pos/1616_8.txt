i bought this dvd from walmart for cheap , thinking it would be a typical , crap straight to video monster junk , but it turned out much better than expected . there is not really any criticism to say about it . . . it is obviously low budget , but that just adds to the cheesy old fashioned fun . it is very cool and entertaining . there is everything : horror , sex , a great plane crash and good characters . and i would say it is pretty original , cuz it really does not come off as any other movie i have seen . it has it is own unique look , which i liked very much , that is why this film deserves credit . i look forward to seeing more of these awesome movies from the scare master , brett piper , whom i have never heard before this one . the dvd menu is really creative with groovy music playing over it , so it is perfect just to keep it on when you are not yet ready to sit down and watch it . it also includes some special features , which are really interesting . but we never get to see the director or hear him in the commentary , must be shy . this comes out of edgewood studios in vermont , usa . i highly recommend it to all horror buffs , you will love it !
