like most , i thought another crocodile movie . so far we have had primeval and rogue in the last 12 months , what can they do that is new ? where both those films were about action and violence , this one is about fear and tension . the performances are not oscar worthy when there is nothing going on , but in times of distress or terror , these people suffer so much it is like torture . there are holes in the plot and maybe crocs do not really behave like this as others have pointed out , but the fear is so effective it is a stretch to say you will enjoy this movie . it will leave you feeling as uncomfortable as the passion of the christ .
