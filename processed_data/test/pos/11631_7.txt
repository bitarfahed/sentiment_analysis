i was a bit surprised to see all of the hate comments on here . sure it is not the best kid is show , but do not people stop despising barney this much after the fifth grade ? okay , everyone hates barney . okay , i think his voice and songs are annoying . okay , he is kinda creepy and strange . i am fourteen years old , so i know well enough . but here is the thing . kids ? they love this show . when i was a little kiddie of two or three , my parents spent more time chasing me around the house than they did anything else . nothing could hold my attention for more than ten minutes . face it , that is how toddlers are . even the most patient ones cannot sit still long enough to give their parents a break . there is too much to do and see and explore , too much trouble to get into . and then came barney . i do not know exactly what it is about the purple dinosaur that is so amusing to children , but they sure do love it . i know i did . i was hooked on the show , and wanted to watch it over and over . yes , the songs kind of drove my parents nuts , but to be able to watch their kids learning , and being excited over something that can really hold their attention span , it is worth it . i learned my abcs and 123 is , the magic words and brushing your teeth . i would grown out of it by five or six , of course , but by that point at least i was a little more patient , and gave my parents a break . my nieces and nephew all went through the barney stage growing up , much to their mother is delight . i know what keeps barney on the air . he entertains . of course there is big bird , ernie , and oscar , and they are great , too . but at the toddler stage , it seems that more kids prefer the big singing dinosaur . and that is enough for me .
