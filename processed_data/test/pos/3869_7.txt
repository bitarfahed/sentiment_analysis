bart the genius whilst not the first simpsons episode , bart he genius more or less is the first typical episode . there is no gimmickry or theme it is just your typical simpsons episode in set up . it always seems to me that it is an episode that grows on you . there are certain elements i do not care for , largely the blotchy animation which can be forgiven . but over time i take a liking to this story of it is uniqueness . for example , it would be very hard for a live action sit com on a standard budget to do this episode due to the various different sets that show in this episode , the computer bays in ms . melon is class , the opera and so on . my point is with that , the simpsons realises one of the biggest strengths in animation . the sheer lack of visual limitations when compared to live action . on a writing stand point it is also highly intelligent and fresh . the concept is pretty unique , and particularly the problems faced . instead of the ol fail safe that work was too hard , it was simply bart is social isolation from his classmates that failed him although the exploding science experiment may prove otherwise . . . which i also think is one of the best visual gags of the series . the ending seems a little unoriginal , largely because the bart running naked into his room to avoid homer was already done in the shorts , but still funny for marge and lisa is short back and forth if for nothing else . ultimately it is a very good episode , with lots of interesting new point in the series , though not exactly perfect . oh , and the now iconic name kwijybo was of course unleashed onto the world .
