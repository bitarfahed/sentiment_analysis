having discovered the ring trilogy , i have been greedily gobbling up all those other japanese and korean films that are either on or following the bandwagon . i do not have an easy definition of horror , but this film certainly pushed some of my buttons , even though i cannot claim that the film makes a lot of sense . i am squeamish so there were several points in the film when i just did not want to watch what was happening on screen . the film unnerved me so i became apprehensive of seeing things that i thought i was going to see . it is an imaginative film offering a great deal visually . it also provides food for thought . and plenty of material to argue about when the film is over . the characters are well defined to say the least . could they make films like this in the west ? so it does not make sense in the end , but when one has an appetite for the occult , the supernatural , the bizarre , the otherworldly , then no film is going to deliver a final all explaining pay off .
