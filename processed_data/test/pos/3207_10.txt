i am not the type of person to watch t . v . shows because the acting normally sucks or it is unrealistic or too dramatic ! but this show is perfect . everyone can act , and you can relate to the characters and their situations . everyone has their own personality and lorelai gilmore is the best for her sarcastic comments that can make any bad situation seem a little funny . rory gilmore is a good role model for all girls . she takes pride in wanting to attend harvard and boys boyfriends always come second in her book . she is a loyal friend and always the peace maker . there is subtle romance which is what i like , personally . not the mushy gushy romance that not many people get to have in their lives , but a realistic type of romance . every character eventually gets it , and they do not find their prince charming at first glance and they do not just fall in love with every guy that comes their way . it is a realistic show but when you watch it , you better brush up on your movies , pop culture , and random facts because lorelai gilmore is always making references . i fell in love with this show and if you give it a chance , so will you .
