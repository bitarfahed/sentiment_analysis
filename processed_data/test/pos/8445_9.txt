as long as you do not mind paying a little more attention than you normally might for other films , this is one of the best thrillers you will ever see . the film portrays an incident that might occur in real life ; nothing in it seems fictional at all , in fact . it also portrays how people might react in real life . in fact , it portrays these so well that is seems like real life . combine that with the lack of a soundtrack , and you have almost got the best news like movie you have ever seen . jane fonda and jack lemmon are particularly good in this late seventies masterpiece , evoking concern on her end and genuine tragic pity on his end . i highly recommend it to anyone who likes thrillers .
