like most other reviewers , i really enjoyed this tv miniseries . i did not see it when it first came out , but my grandfather a big fan of mysteries and war films happened to record it in 1989 . i remember watching it at my grandparents cabin one night and i was completely drawn into the story ! it has a very intriguing plot and a good mix of drama , romance , and espionage . i have seen lots of films that are set during wwii , but none with a twist like this ! thankfully , i was able to make a vhs copy of the film several years ago . i have watched it several times since then and i still enjoy it . i would love for this film to be released on dvd . it would be so much easier to view with chapters .
