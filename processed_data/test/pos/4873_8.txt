remember that friend in college who always insisted you rent the weirdest movie possible ? this is the movie he would have made if he would had the chance . i wish i could tell you exactly what sea of dust was about . it pretends to be the story of a doctor who gets sucked into weird goings on in the black forest . he goes there to help , but ends up being caught between two young women , both of whom he seems to have a thing for . but that is just scratching the surface . this is the kind of movie where things just randomly happen . . . and not nice thing . people are constantly being whipped and stabbed . there is a pair of creepy little girls who appear to have walked out of the shining . tom savini is some kind of imaginary religious figure who decides he does not want to be imaginary anymore . he is got a plan to take over the world by sharing jesus suffering . on some level , this is a movie about sex . it is one without nudity , which was a disappointment , but there is no mistaking the intent . on another whole level , it is a stoner is paradise . unexpected stuff happens so often that it stops being unexpected . by the time the doctor travels through his girlfriend is birth canal to be reborn , you will just chalk it up to the crazy nature of the flick . on the down side , the film is pretty wordy . some of the points are hammered home over and over . if you are watching it with a bunch of stoned friends , this might prove an asset .
