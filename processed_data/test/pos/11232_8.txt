when northfork debuted at the cannes film festival , many people did not like it because they felt it was boring and too slow . while i agree that it was slow one of the slowest movies of the year , in no way was it boring . as roger ebert said , there has never been a movie like northfork . i usually do not agree with ebert , but for once he speaks the truth . although john sayles sunshine state may have some of the same immediate themes , nothing that i have ever seen or known of can even compare to the striking originality of the polish brothers northfork . northfork is a perfect example of how many times it is better to trek an extra few minutes to go to an art house film instead of the latest jack black movie . the plot is not some hackneyed , cookie cutter plot ; it is just so strikingly original . a small town in montana named northfork has a dam nearby that is about to be taken down . therefore , the entire town must be evacuated . some people , however , just do not want to leave . in a side plot , a young orphan duel farnes is very sick and bedridden ; he is being taken care of by father harlan nick nolte . the boy imagines himself as a fallen angel , so to speak , who help him out through his time of sickness . although much of the movie is straightforward , some of it could give david lynch a run for his money . there is odd weather patterns , a weird , wooden , huge dog thing , and symbolism that would make fellini proud . it is not as overall confusing as a lynch film , but it is still quite odd . that is what makes northfork so great : it is so out of the ordinary and yet so simple and plausible . northfork has a magical feel to it : it is almost like you are watching something you are not quite sure what it is but you feel entranced by it . as i said earlier , i agreed with ebert on how this movie is unlike any other . however , i disagree when he says that it is not entertaining . he goes on to say it is just enthralling . perhaps he just thought he should give it good reviews because everyone else is , but in lieu of how slow it was , i still thought it was very entertaining , something many dramas now cannot do . northfork may not be the quickest movie or the most popular movie , but if you can get to and through it , you will be extremely surprised , as i was . my rating : 8 10 rated pg 13 for brief sexuality .
