i really loved seeing this movie . i think it is a brilliant , underrated alfred hitchcock movie . everyone is familiar with the famous statue of liberty scene , but there is a really great movie before that . robert cummings is great in what i consider to be his greatest role , and the beautiful priscilla lane shows that she has a lot of talent too . but i think norman lloyd gives the best performance . his character , fry , is so evil and devious . even though he is not in it for very long , the movie would not be the same without him . saboteur is a great movie that every hitchcock fan should see . i give it a 10 out of 10 ! !
