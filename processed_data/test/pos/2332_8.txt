starring : kelsey grammar , rob schneider , lauren holly , rip torn this movie is a classic family favorite . at least for most members of my family that is . one of us rolls our eyes at the mention of this movie and thinks , what a stupid movie . i am not that person though . i still find this movie highly amusing . i just watched it again last night with someone who had never seen it , and i laughed just as hard as the first time i watched it . it is still very funny to me . naval captain tom dodge grammar is a bit of a black sheep in the navy . he is the kind of guy who will play golf while on a sub , cracking a ball onto a golf course while they sail past it . he is the kind of guy who will get drunk , pass out , and wake up with a hangover and a tattoo on his dongle . he is not the kind of guy that everybody wants to be commanding a sub . but dodge is at the end of the line . he is put in applications to get command of his own sub several times , and if he does not get a sub this time , he is headed for a desk job , meaning that is it for naval life . the admiralty decide to give him his own boat , but they do not give him a new nuclear sub . they give him a diesel sub , an ancient relic from world war ii . his mission is to clean her up and take her out on the atlantic for a war game . dodge is obviously a little bit frustrated to have such a crappy boat given to him when he is worked so hard to get to the point that he is at , so he goes and talks to admiral winslow torn about it . winslow explains the purpose of the war game . diesel subs are still being sold out there in the real world , to countries like iran , iraq , and libya , to name a few . winslow wants to know what would happen if some renegade captain in a diesel sub came to the states and tried to smuggle a nuclear warhead into one of the bases . would they make it ? dodge and most of the admiralty do not think so , but winslow wants to know for sure , hence why he has devised this war game . dodge is mission is to take the stingray out to sea , then try to evade the u . s . nuclear navy and blow up the american naval bases . in simulated battle , of course . so , that is about as deep as the plot gets . this is not a plot driven movie . it is a stupid movie that is just a big gag . it makes fun of itself , and you are not expected to take it seriously . if you are looking for good dumb laughs on a friday night , i would urge you to check it out . bottom line : 3 out of 4 worth a look
