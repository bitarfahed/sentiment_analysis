this movie is a mystery . it is not scary , it is spooky . you will probably jump at some points and for some of it you will be scratching your head to figure it out . it unfolds its mystery through the main character eve and her psychotic college project at an insane asylum , don . he needs her to search for some clues to vindicate himself from murdering his mother , and once eve agrees to help him , she completely opens up pandora is box and unleashes some very strange things , including malachi . i will not go into malachi , but i thought the way that played out was complete genius . i know movie buffs will not regard this movie with the same prestige as other classics because it does not have anyone very famous in it angelina jolie is brother was excellent , but maybe he should have asked his sister to show her face just so this movie could get some recognition but i do think the way the malachi ending works is something to talk about . i have recommended this to my friends , definitely worth the purchase or rental price .
