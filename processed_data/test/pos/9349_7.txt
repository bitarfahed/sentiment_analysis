i have seen this movie more than once . it is not the greatest scifi flick i have every seen , but it is not a bad movie . the acting is good and the characters are more real than most in low budget sci fi . at least it is not full of dumb bimbos like so many other low budget scifi . i especially like elizabeth pena . she is a good actress and she does worried single mother as well as any and better than some . do not let the nay sayers run you off . see it for yourself and judge it for yourself .
