i gave this 9 stars out of a possible 10 . if it had had just a teensy weensy bit more plot line i would have given it 10 . nonetheless it is a highly interesting film . judith ivey , playing a likable old floozy , should have been given the oscar for her performance . emily grace portraying alice , whom i had never seen before , also does an excellent job and has the sexiest body i think i have ever seen on film . in a beat to heck old car , alice has lit out from the n . e . for florida where she has a friend or maybe it is her sister , i am not sure , and that is my fault , not the film is , and high hopes of going to college , which she and her family cannot really afford . she seems rather vulnerable out there on the road alone , and sure enough she encounters some slightly rough looking characters and shortly after that it is discovered there is a hole in one of her tires . she is at a rest stop at the time and is assisted by a woman named sandra and her husband , bill , an older couple who are traveling in an rv . they are going south , to florida , and take her under their wing , but is everyone quite the way they are presenting themselves ? flashbacks and paranoia enter the story as our young heroine learns some new lessons about life .
