i liked this western starring randolph scoot and glenn ford . it is got pretty much everything a western should have . there is gunfights , action , chases on horseback . there is a bit of a romance angle that is hinted at . there is some nice comic relief in the form of edgar buchanan , who plays uncle willie mcleod , a character who pretend to be a doddering old fool , but is actually aware of everything going on . randolph scott plays the town sheriff , and glenn ford plays cheyenne rogers bill smith , a hunted outlaw who eventually tries to change his ways . i liked the different mix of characters in the movie . i did not find it always fast paced , but i never found it boring either . even though the movie is in 1943 , it is in colour . 8 10
