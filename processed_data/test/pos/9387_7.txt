death bed : the bed that eats . judging from the title , you can guess what this movie is about . and yet there is a lot more background story to this film then one might suspect . okay , so the main plot is about a bed eating people and food , but there are also a few subplots . i will not spoil them for you , but they are a nice touch . sadly , the acting in this movie is very mediocre . the fact that most dialog is not even spoken by the actors does not really help to improve the quality of the movie as a whole . because there is a lot of voice over work . the thoughts of characters are also revealed to the watchers . sometimes it works , sometimes it does not . the effects are fine . sure , it could be a lot better if you compare it with today is movies . but you really should not ; just judge the movie as it is and do not take it too seriously . you have to admit that a killer bed is quite creative . if you are easily spooked , do not watch this movie . you might never want to sleep again . . . death bed : the bed that eats is a strange horror gem with a low budget , but i would still recommend it to fans of horror movies . in conclusion , i give this movie a 7 out of 10 stars for it is creative story and unexpected twists here and there .
