quite simply , goldeneye is the single greatest n64 game to date . the learning curve is just about perfect , and you will still be playing it with your friends months on , as the multiplayer mode is nothing short of exceptional . the system for acquiring cheats for once requires some degree of skill , rather than simply knowing which buttons to press , and the challenge of aztec on 00 agent level is astonishing . all in all it is the best game i have ever played on the n64
