i did not have very high expectations about just before dawn . i do not know why i keep buying these slasher movies when i know it is all the same every time . maybe i am a bit masochistic ? anyway , i am glad i bought this one . yes , it is all the same . no , it has nothing original . yes , it is about a little group of teenagers going in the woods to camp , drink and have sex . yes , they get killed one by one by a maniac . and you know what ? it is just magic ! it is one of the best experiences i had watching a slasher movie . the places where it is shot are so wonderful . you see mountains , waterfalls , rockies . it is just amazing . it takes a very long time except for the opening scene before the killer starts killing , but i did not care ! it was enjoyable to watch enough not to care . what i mean is , you will not be sitting there waiting for the murders to start because every thing that happens is enjoyable because of the beauty of the film itself . the ending is a bit weird , but i will not tell you anything more about it . you will know what i mean . another thing i enjoyed : you care about the characters . they are not just a bunch of jerks who spend their time bitching each other . sure , they are pure stereotypes of usual slasher characters but , there is something special about them . anyway , it is a great horror movie and i hope you have a pretty good time watching it because i really did !
