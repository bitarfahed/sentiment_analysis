thank goodness for the coen brothers . their success has brought them bigger budgets , but has not rid them of their creativity . i had planned on seeing another movie , but it was sold out so i went to this one instead . by the time it began , i had forgotten what movie i was there to see . i was surprised in more ways than one . this movie is hilarious , but they do not make any cheap jokes just to get the laughs . the writing is brilliant , and delivered with great skill by george clooney after this , nobody can say he is just a pretty face and the rest of the cast . it can be appreciated on many levels , whether you remember the odyssey or not . i cannot remember the last time i saw a movie that was this clever . i have seen others i would describe as beautiful , intriguing , funny and charming , all of which also describe oh brother , but this movie reminded me of older seinfeld episodes where all the subplots came together in the end . you can feel that their journey is building up to something , but you cannot tell what . and the coen brothers do not fail us , the end is certainly not disappointing . it is surprising , and ties up all the loose ends neatly , without wearing the story out .
