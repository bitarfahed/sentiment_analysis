the film was apparently spawned from an idea one of the writers had when he isaw one of his creations in a supermarket . the inhabitants of royston vasey head into our world to persuade the writers not to stop writing about them and thus destroy their world . if that sounds a bit too serious , do not be put off . within the first few minutes we get : bernice the vile female vicar letting rip at an unfortunate penitent during confession ; chinnery the vet who inadvertently destroys every animal he touches attempting to collect semen from a giraffe ; mickey thick beyond belief being , ah , thick ; and tubbs inbred sister wife and local shopkeeper being sweet as ever but still disgusting . some of the regular characters are missing , but a new idea by the gents introduces some 16th century characters and we have the gents themselves in the action too . if you are new to the league of gentlemen , this is an easy introduction and a lot of fun . if you are a long standing fan , this has everything you have come to expect including the joys of jeremy dyson spotting . all told , it is got the same faintly surreal humour that is the hallmark of the series , plus some moments of quite touching introspection . herr lipp , for example , maintains a gentle dignity on learning that he is regarded by his creators as a one joke character . while most of the characters stay as they are , some develop in unexpected ways that are perfectly natural when they happen . this film is a iswan song for royston vasey , but it is also a showcase for the gents who prove that gasp ! they can write other stuff and it can be very funny . but you knew that anyway .
