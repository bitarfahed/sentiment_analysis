three is company style story of mistaken identities , and mixed messages , and the cast . amanda bynes is a star ! ! even since the days of the horribly campy yet strangely entertaining the amanda earth , bubbly teen although she is twenty now and whether or not she will be able to carry her talent and style over to being an adult actor will remain to be seen . but for the purpose of this film she is perfect ! ! she actually legitimately pulls off the rather outlandish plot of her impersonating her twin brother and makes it believable . not entirely . . . but believable enough . most of the script relies on the comedy of her errors trying to be a guy but it is just hilarious , non stop laughs . channing tatum redeems himself from his deplorable performance in 2005 is havoc by plays duke . he is the jock , the captain of the soccer team , and eventually bynes object of affection , unfortunately he is also sebastian is room mate who is bynes . he is a good leading man , and the effect the rest of the cast one way or another . james kirk is great in his small role as the real sebastian . his resemblance to amanda bynes is astonishing . . . they are absolutely believable as it worth while . this is one of those films that shows so much in the trailer and yet it is not one of those films that when that part comes up it is not funny anymore . the parts in the trailer that
