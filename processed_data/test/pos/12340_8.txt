one dark night is a highly overlooked and little known film from the early 80 is that deserves an audience that i fear it will never get , and that is a damn shame . i have seen this film compared to others that have gotten a bigger name over the years , most notably phantasm , hell night and mausoleum . this is a much different film than those and i do not see the comparisons other than the mausoleum , which is a bit similar to the one in phantasm , but not enough to make any real comparisons . i am not sure how this one slipped through without a broader acceptance . maybe it is all in the marketing , i do not know . perhaps a remake would breathe new life into it , unless raymar drained all the life out of it that is . i am not too big on all the remakes that are abundant these days , but i think they do work well with lesser known films except for the awful ghost ship remake , which other than the opening scene and mudvayne is not falling blaring , was utter crap . so if a remake of one dark night would happen to fall into the right hands , i think it would make a lot of people go and watch the original . i know that is what i do if there is a remake of a film i have not seen before . so anything short of a remake , i fear , would not bring this film back to life . unless , of course , raymar got his eyes on it . anyways , one dark night is a must see for horror fans , especially 80 is horror fans cause we all know that is when the best horror movies were made . creepy setting . fairly good acting . very good story . campy . what more could you want from an early 80 is horror film ? what is that . . . nudity and gore ? well , sorry . no nudity or gore in this film , but it is still great nonetheless ! a solid 8 out of 10 . enjoy .
