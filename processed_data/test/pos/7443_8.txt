the movie is really cool , i thought . it sticks to the original game quite well , and some of the battle scenes are depicted in such high detail , it is incredible . i thought the movie could have been a little better if they would give you a little more information before the end , but trying to figure out what is going on is what hooks on you this movie . the cg is beautiful , i do not think they could have done a better job graphics wise . every little detail , the way they move , the way their swords look , everything is perfect . the way they make the characters look is almost like they are real people . the music was great , and i found it entertaining to listen to just how much of it was remixed from the original game , with new instruments and effects . all and all , i would recommend this movie to anyone that enjoys fast paced action and a good storyline . i would definitely play the game before i would watch the movie though .
