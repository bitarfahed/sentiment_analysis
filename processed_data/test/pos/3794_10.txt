the very few reviews i could find online of barnens really do not do it justice . i read them all before ordering the dvd , but for some reason i ordered it anyway . i regretted it almost immediately , but the order had already gone through so i could not cancel it . i am very glad now that i could not . it is an extraordinary movie . i will not give a synopsis of the plot , because other reviewers have already done that . but i will say that i do not understand comments that it is bleak , shocking , weird , clinical , depressing or pornographic . it is certainly very unusual , which i suppose could make it seem weird to some people , but the other criticisms must reflect the reviewers own issues , because i did not see any of that in the movie i just watched . i am not attracted to boys , so the nudity did not seem pornographic at all to me it is just a kid trying to figure out who he is with no help at all from the irresponsible adults in his life . and it is sweden , not utah , so topless women are no big deal . but what surprised me most is how positive the movie is in its depiction of this gutsy lost kid who goes on a sort of odyssey through all sorts of strange experiences , looking for and ultimately finding himself . it is fascinating , thoroughly original , and deeply satisfying . i am not at all surprised that barnens won three major guldbagge awards , for best film , direction , and actor , but i am absolutely astounded that the actor who won was not tomas fryk , the kid whose fearless performance as reine must be one of the most remarkable ever filmed , but ingvar hirdwall in the relatively insignificant role of stig , reine is mother is sleazy boyfriend . i do not understand that at all , but it does not alter the fact that this is a great movie .
