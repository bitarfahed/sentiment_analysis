of short disgusting , gross out skits strung together in an effort to make entertainment . and as much as every critical bone in my body cries out to give this movie a failing score , gosh darn it , i am going to give it a much better score . the kind of people who are going to enjoy this movie are not the kind of people who really care what critics have to say , so i am not going to give some snobby critique of jackass is quality of surreal existentialism that permeates and commentates on how our society . . . . oh forget it . what is to say ? there is no plot development , no character development no real beginning there is a semi enjoyable end that is not nearly as funny as the skit that precedes it , and everything in between reeks of improper , bathroom humor that junior high kids laugh at . it is actually pretty funny . leave at the door all your preconceptions of a good movie . jackass : number two is just gross out crap . that is really all it is . i do give the filmmakers this , though : they come up with some pretty imaginative stuff . you will probably cringe several times throughout , want to throw up a couple times , wiggle and shift uncomfortably as the people on screen do death and vomit defying stunts . vomit , crap , urine , semen . . . . virtually every bodily fluid can be seen in jackass : number two . there is nudity , sexual humor abounds , and so on and so forth . it is entertaining . if you are the kind of person who finds this stuff enjoyable , then you will probably really like jackass : number two . i laughed several times . like i said , it is pretty imaginative stuff . i never once wanted to vomit though i do have a pretty strong stomach . just accept the movie is premise : a bunch of idiotic guys do weird crap for fun . once you get past that , accepting it and going with it , you will enjoy it . the only flaw in the movie is the sheer excess of all the weird and disgusting stuff . there are several very imaginative , very disturbing skits , but it drags in many places as they do ones that are less interesting . towards the end things pick up , but in the middle of the movie the drag hurts the overall film is quality . in a half hour tv show this is not a problem , but with film length celluloid , it is inevitable that boredom will ensue at some point . after awhile the gross crap just becomes desensitizing . on the whole , though , number two is an entertaining , imaginative , and above all , disgusting comedy that will leave you with a feeling of pain , nausea , and hilarity . go see it if you must , but hey , if you know you do not like this kind of stuff , do not bother . you will just be disappointed .
