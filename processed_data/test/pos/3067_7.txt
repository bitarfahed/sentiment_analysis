mel brooks is a great writer , director and actor , but once in a while even he can have a klinker . the beginning of this starts out almost basically just meeting one character after another . you have cary elwes who is charming and talented , but he cannot do comedy . his expressions into the camera show that even he cannot believe the inanities in the script . richard lewis looks bored and distracted throughout without much to do and amy yasbeck is stuck in high school acting mode . dave chapelle shows great comedy range mugging and hamming for the camera and mark blankfield is mostly stifled in the role of the blind man , but then he does still manage to show great comedy range . eric allen kramer does not have much to do , but the few scenes he does have are binding . the best roles are those of roger rees , tracy ullman , megan cavanaugh and brooks for when he does appear . dom deluise has a funny turn as a mafia don employing a clint eastwood lookalike , but on the minus side , some of the jokes are not very funny while some of the really funny ones seem stolen from mel is other movies .
