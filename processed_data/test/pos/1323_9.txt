mishima is one of the greatest films ever made . now i think paul schrader is the greatest screenwriter of all time , but i do not really like the films he is directed of what i have seen with the exception of this and affliction , but this is an amazing , disturbing , and highly 3 dimensional character study . it follows the life of yukio mishima , japan is most celebrated writer , combining the last day of his life with flashbacks and his stories . i do not know how , but paul schrader manages to combine all of those in a very artistic way . the acting is great , so is the photography , and a perfect score by philip glass . although confusing the first viewing , this is one of the few films that becomes richer with each viewing . truly an underrated gem of a film .
