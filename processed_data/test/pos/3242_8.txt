i am a fan of independent film . dialogue driven , character study pieces are where it is at for me . some of the other posts are right : wannabes is not going to rival the godfather for best mob film ever . on the other hand , wannabes is a well written and well directed picture that has surprisingly good performances from every actor actress . my problem with one of the other reviews : conor dubin stands out as the only irishman in a cast of italians dubin is a jewish last name , and as such has a dark complexion , not a traditional ruddy one of an irishman . he does not stand out at all , rather , delivers a great performance . this did not win many awards , but it is deserving of a saturday night with a bowl of popcorn . i found it for 7 bucks at blockbuster and was pleasantly surprised to say the least ! highly recommended .
