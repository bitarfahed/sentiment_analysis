out of all the bat films , batman returns is my favorite . this beautiful , dark , and funny film is one of tim burton is best work . although it is much violent and darker , this is the batman that creator bob kane envisioned many years ago . michael keaton reprises his role as the avenger of gotham city . this time he is up against two deadly foes , danny devito is penguin and michelle pfeiffer is catwoman . with a great cast and film score by danny elfman , the movie takes us on an adventure as batman battles the evil forces that are trying to take over gotham city . christopher walken makes a great appearance as max shrek , a shrewd businessman who has an evil scheme up his sleeve . but of course , michelle pfeiffer is the one that steals the show . with all of these components , you have a film that will blow you away . this is the reason why you go to the movies . it is got everything . it is really a shame that tim burton did not get to direct the other sequels . if so i think the franchise would still be going strong today . batman returns is an awesome experience for fans that like cool movies .
