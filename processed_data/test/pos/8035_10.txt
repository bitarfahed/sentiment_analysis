this is probably one of the best thrillers i have ever seen . it has action , but not this bullet flying , good guys bad guys , van damme stallone action , but quick , realistic and nervous action , it has a plot , cause till the very end of the movie you do not know how this is gonna end , it has characters , aidan quinn , donald sutherland and ben kingsley are just perfect , and it has suspense , this movie just will not let you go away before you have seen the end of it . though there are only a few characters , i did not find it difficult to keep my attention the the story , and as for the story , it is basic not too tom clancy difficult , but simple and raw and realistic . if you are in for a movie with a good story , some action and great acting , watch this and i promise , you will not go away till you have seen the end of it . the very end of it .
