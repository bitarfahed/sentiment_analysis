i just re watched a few episodes of this series on very poor vhs tapes that i recorded the series on . i am glad i did , though . ramona is based on the children is books by beverly cleary and follows the adventures of the title eight year old sarah polley . she has fun with friends and family and gets into trouble much like many eight year olds . all ten episodes are pretty good . mystery meal kind of grosses me out as a vegan , but it is funny seeing the parents trying to get their kids to eat cow is tongue ! and any episode that mentions godzilla versus the smog monster twice cannot be all bad . i like ramona is bad day even more . although it is disheartening to see ramona going through a rough day , she does imagine herself getting revenge on her friend howie is uncle a pre psi factor barclay hope , who always teases her . the perfect day is also good . lori chodos plays beezus , ramona is sister . i swear i have seen her on something else , but imdb does not list anything besides this show . unfortunately , this series is not on dvd . why is every crappy show made these days on dvd except something i would actually like to buy ? an injustice if there ever was one !
