the bbc is blue planet is simply jaw dropping . i do not think i am exaggerating when i say it contains some of the most beautiful sequences ever captured on film . from familiar creatures on and near the surface of the ocean to some more unrecognisable and just plain bizarre ones in the murky depths , next to nothing is left out . weighing in at a hefty 8 hours , some people may want to check out the edited highlights brought to you in the form of the film deep blue but i would heartily recommend you give the series a go . i do not think it will disappoint and if your kids enjoyed the aquatic world brought to them by pixar is finding nemo i am sure they will love this too . i just wish all television was this entertaining .
