this is an extraordinary film . as a courtroom drama , it is compelling , as an indictment on the american justice system , it is frightening . for brenton butler the consequences of this system could be devastating . this film highlights the fundamental flaws of the legal process , that it is not about discovering guilt or innocence , but rather , is about who presents better in court . in truth , the implications of this case reach beyond the possibility of an innocent man being found guilty , or a guilty man being free . every citizen has a right to justice , whether a perpetrator or a victim . but do they get it ? the film is well paced , understated and one of the best courtroom documentaries i have seen .
