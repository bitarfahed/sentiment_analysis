flashes of lightning ; a sprawling cemetery ; the name of adam batman west : all pop up on screen before the opening credits are even over , and yet , despite these rather naff elements , one dark night is not as cheesy as it might first seem . meg tilly jennifer is sister plays pretty student julie , who reluctantly agrees to spend the night alone in a mausoleum as part of her initiation into exclusive high school clique the sisters . what julie does not realise is that the other isisters plan to freak her out with some ghoulish pranks or that the most recent body to be interred in the mausoleum is that of psychic vampire raymar , who feeds off the life force of scared young women . admittedly , this is not the most original of set ups , but thankfully there are enough inventive touches to help set this film apart from the competition , my favourites being the macabre sight of everyday objects embedded in the walls of raymar is apartment , and the creepy manner in which mouldy corpses float through the cold marble corridors of the mausoleum during the excellent finale . hal trussell is impressive steadicam cinematography and tom burman is wonderfully macabre special effects also add immensely to the chilling atmosphere .
