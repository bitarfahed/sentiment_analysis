i just saw this at siff , and i absolutely loved it . there were parts where i laughed so hard i could not catch my breath . the script and direction by glenn mcquaid are fantastic . i cannot wait to see more from this talented young man . the performances make the movie . larry fessenden and dominic monaghan are just delightful as the graverobber willy and his apprentice arthur . ron perlman turns in a fantastic performance as usual as a less than sympathetic jailhouse priest . bonus : angus scrimm as an unscrupulous doctor ! as always , he balances menace and humor perfectly . this is a style of horror comedy that really has not been seen since vincent price did comedy of terrors , the raven , and theater of blood . the movie is genuinely scary in places , then it will suddenly flip back into hilarious mode , keeping you totally off balance . some parts are scary and funny at the same time . i can only afford to see three movies at siff this year , but even if the other two are awful , i sell the dead was worth the price of all three . i am going to be looking for more movies from larry fessenden is glass eye pix . he is giving the horror genre a much needed kick in the butt .
