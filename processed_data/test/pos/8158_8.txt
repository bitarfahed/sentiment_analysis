latter days is a very , very independent movie . and compared to many of today is more modern films , it is lacking on many parts . the shooting seems at times quite amateurish , the dialogues can be a bit chopped up and the characters are not impressively complex . so , do not have too high hopes for this movie , because as i have said , it is very independent . but whatever it lacks in the concrete aspects of the film can be overlooked because of the story is charm ! i cannot claim that the plot is outstandingly original , but the story is still beautiful and heart warming in many ways ! it is not about being gay versus being straight , it is about faith , and how you decide to run your own life ! it is a silly story , that makes you want to both cry and smile at the same time ! so to be honest , latter days is far from perfect , but i truly loved the movie and i highly recommend it ! it is very critical towards the religious aspects of our society , and there is homosexuality involved but approach it with an open mind , and i am pretty sure that most people will enjoy it as much as myself !
