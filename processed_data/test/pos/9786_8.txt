milo is an overlooked underrated horror flick from the late 90 is but the feel and atmosphere is more that of an 80 is horror flick . seems a bunch of little girls are taken to see things at the office of milo is father , a gynecologist , and as a reward for getting to see what is there in jars yes , a gynecologist plus something else . . . one girl plays doctor with milo . hmm , his first patient and he lost her . now milo is a creepy little kid that always wears a yellow rain slicker , rain or shine , and he talks funny . . . . but that is not all that is strange about him . fast forward to the present where these girls are adults and there is a wedding planned for one of them but tragedy strikes and leaves one girl that came in from out of town with a choice of whether or not to replace her friend as a school teacher , which she does . and then strange things begin to happen again . which of course involve this little kid in a rain slicker . this is not overly violent or graphic but has a distinct creepiness that you do not find in many modern horror flicks , and it is well worth seeing . one wonders why it is so hard to find any good horror flicks in this day age but here is one that is criminally overlooked and definitely worth your time if you check it out . 8 out of 10 .
