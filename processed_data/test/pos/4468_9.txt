as far as i know i have seen most of lucio fulci is films , with the exception of some later ones that are popping up on dvd now , and i would have to say this was his best pre zombie offering . in fact it beats out his later movies hands down . this is a tale of superstition and suspicion in a small italian town out in the middle of nowhere , where young boys are dying mysteriously . it is no mystery how they died , i guess , it is more who is doing it . is it a woman that the townspeople have always considered a witch ? is it a young woman that lives in a fancy modern house on the outskirts of town , who used to do a lot of drugs ? is it the village idiot , who gets nabbed early on because of being in the wrong place at the wrong time ? you will only know if you watch , and watch you should , for this is a top notch mystery thriller , or should i say , a giallo . i have seen this before but it is been a long time and upon a second viewing it was still as powerful as the first time . for those of you that only know lucio fulci for his zombie flicks , well , open your minds to this because it is well worth seeing and while the monsters in this are human , they are as nasty as anything dredged up in his later films . 9 out of 10 stars , see it ! please . . . .
