meant to be some sort of a social commentary about the way that our lives were spinning out of control in the 1970 is , this movie plays more like something mr . schlesinger threw together after having a bad day on the freeway . a mish mash of snippets about the nuts encountered along the freeway in florida , and along the freeway of life , the cast is mostly wasted and probably ended up on the cutting room floor . the problem is that what did not end up on the cutting room floor probably should have ! now available on dvd , it is kinda fun to see a comedy that would have cost 100 million to produce had it been made in 2004 . it rolls along and is not too difficult to sit through , and , strangely enough , in 2004 we are all still pretty much just hopping in our cars and going faster and faster , heading nowhere .
