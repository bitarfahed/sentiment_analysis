if you liked lock , stock and two smoking barrels , the chances are you will like this , too . although i guess a few of the british in jokes like calling two london characters dixon and winterburn , after arsenal soccer players may be lost on some , this is still cracking entertainment , which veritably pumps with vitality . carlyle , miller and tyler are all excellent , bringing depth to their characters , and the interplay between the two protagonists is always well judged . in fact scott is direction is very assured , considering that this is his first feature film , and proves that he has his father is talent for putting us at ease in unfamiliar surroundings . hell , i have convinced myself . i am gonna see it again !
