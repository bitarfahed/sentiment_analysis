in december 1945 a train leaves the central station of stockholm for berlin . there are not much left when it arrives . not of the train and not of some passengers . this is a black comedy directed by peter dalle and acted like they used to act in the 40s and also photographed in b w like they used to during that period . the actors must have had lots of fun making it . they are not much of characters , like they were not in the 40s , but the story is well narrated and everybody has timing . a deadly black and deadly funny film . see it , if you did not think the swedes were capable of humour .
