i was not sure about getting this movie on dvd because i really do have something against people making black and white films in the 21st century , but i ended up buying it anyway . i still do not understand why it had to be black and white , but that is the only negativity i can see about the film , and it sure is a perfect example to see the definitive rise of brazilian cinema . not everyone can understand a film like this , but it is quite rewarding to those who do . unlike o homem que copiava , the surrealism fits this movie pretty well , and the acting is at least as good as that one , or other successful brazilian films such as bicho de sete cabe as , cidade de deus , brava gente brasileira , etc . . my only hope is that this gem does not get insulted and raped by a crappy hollywood remake . it is amazing to see how those guys spend zillions of dollars and still cannot make a movie that is 1 as good as this possibly low budget flick . bravo to the entire crew ! 10 10
