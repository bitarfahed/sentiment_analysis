the polish brothers are unique film artists , and they have really pushed the envelope here . a fantasy that has points in common with wings of desire , northfork tells the story of a 50s era small town in the middle of nowhere that is two days shy of being inundated and submerged thanks to the u . s . government is desire to make a reservoir on the place where the town stands . it is a wry parable about loss and remembrance , featuring angels , dreams , premonitions , and the most hilarious government reclamation functionaries since repo man . the performances are all outstanding , especially nolte and woods . i have noticed in reading down some of the comments that there are people who were offended simply by the fact that the polish twins use elliptical storytelling tactics , and i want to say , that is one of the things that makes this film so great : its willingness to embrace the mysterious as an aspect of everyday life . david mullen is cinematography is stunning . highly recommended ; if you have suffered a meaningful personal loss , such as the death of a parent , i would even call this film necessary viewing . ray
