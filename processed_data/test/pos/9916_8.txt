i rank opera as one of the better argento films . plot holes and inconsistencies ? sure , but i do not think they impair this film as much as many other reviewers seem to . a lot of elements that are in many of argento is films are kinda off the wall , but that is part of the draw of his films . . . short story : psycho stalks the opera is new leading lady . the typical argento twists and turns ensue , leading up to a decent payoff of a climax . not argento is best , but i still pull this one out from time to time . definitely worth a look if you like his other stuff just do not get this one mixed up with the abysmal phantom of the opera remake that argento did , that one is truly awful . . . 8 10
