i was very curious to see this wajda depardieu outing , plus the time period is definitely fascinating . being a wajda fan , i was disappointed , and that may be an understatement . the film never really took cinematic flight there is no foundation for the animosity between danton and robespierre , etc . basically , the script was weak adapted from the danton affair . and yet , the direction was masterful . . . it is wajda , afterall ! also , there were some amazing actors but they never really grab the audience is attention like they should . depardieu comes off as a quasi goofy , nonchalant danton . . . not exactly the image we have in mind . woijech pzsoniak is incredible , as usual , but again the script puts up limits even actors of great talent cannot break down . andrzej seweryn and bogoslaw linda pop up . . . as bourdon and saint just . . . and if you are familiar with wajda , then you would know them . overall , i was disappointed with this much lauded film . great cast , great director , but no quality foundation . bad , undynamic script . we need to get in danton walesa and robespierre is general j mindsets . . . what are their motivations ? eh . . . who knows ? one likes women , the other powders himself ? riiight . ok , so if you are looking for a great french revolution movie i highly recommend la revolution francaise . . . it is in two parts and oh so great ! excellent performances , in depth script , juicy tid bits . . . definitely a satisfying experience ! ! klaus maria brandauer is a much better danton than depardieu . . . the wonderful andrzej seweryn apparently took some notes from danton and is brilliant as robespierre . see it ! now ! as for wajda fans you are better off with man of iron marble , promised land , and the like . cheers ! !
