well , i guess i was in the mood for a movie that really grabbed me from the beginning . this movie was not it . it plodded along at a pretty slow , deliberate pace for the first 40 minutes , but there was not really anything in it that i was terribly interested in there is an intriguing and mysterious feud between jean reno is character and an old man , but more of the first 40 minutes is dominated by the wanderings of the main character , whom i did not know much about and could not really relate to at the time . he wanders around alone for the most part , he does not meet anyone ; i imagine the director was trying to depict the loneliness of the human condition in this post apocalyptic world or something , which is all good , but i still wish he would trimmed it down from 40 minutes to 15 , because it can get incredibly boring . but after those 40 minutes , things start to get very interesting . i guess i will not really say more than that because i do not want to spoil anything . so if you have seen the first 15 30 minutes of this movie and are thinking about turning it off like i was , just stick with it it gets a lot better . one of the most interesting things i found about this movie was the fact that it had no dialogue whatsoever , which really made me have to think about what was happening , how characters were feeling and what their motivations were , why things were how they were in this post apocalyptic world , all of which gives the story a lot of room for audience interpretation . and it is amazing how much more satisfying a movie is when the actors are not telling you exactly what is going on .
