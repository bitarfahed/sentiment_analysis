i myself am a physics student , and i have to say i think this is one of the best popular introductions to string theory that is out there . the elegant universe manages to make the entire topic of string although it is actually m theory accessible to a wider audience . some popular science programmes feel that the best approach is just to throw the audience in at the deep end , throwing technical jargon at them without so much as an explanation , and presenting the theory in a boring , stale style . this programme goes through concepts such as general relativity and quantum mechanics , and explains the issues that need to be resolved between the two so we have a coherent theory that can be applied to the universe on both a large and small scale . i suppose some could say it is slow and takes too long to get to the interesting stuff like extra dimensions and wormholes , but the thing is : that is against the point . explaining string theory from the start is nigh on impossible without at least mentioning the physics at its base , and the way it is explained in the elegant universe is clear and entertaining . whether you will like this program really does depend on if you are willing to perhaps be initially dumbfounded by some of the ideas that greene presents : extra dimensions and warping of spacetime are not exactly prevalent in everyday life , are they ? but , if you persevere with some of the more exotic concepts in this programme , you will find that it will give you an insight into the research that drives the world of physics today . and if you are studying physics , well , it is great entertainment as well as you will be likely able to follow this and appreciate it even more !
