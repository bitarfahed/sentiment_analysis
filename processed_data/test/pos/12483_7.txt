this film is so lovingly made you want to be part of it forever . the flics are straight but not without malice , the goods are transparent and evildoers are hardly there . even the cabaret are so naive they will make you daydream with nostalgia in comparison to anything available on tv . blier is fine , if a bit one sided . louis jouvet is perfect , you just cannot have a better copper . he has the best line : my dad cleaned other people is dirt , and i do the same . susy delair is unbearable , but i guess in part it is the songs , wardrobe and hairdo . simone renant , on the contrary , makes a great femme fatale , if a bit silent . i did not realize she may be a lesbian as imdb user dbdumonteil and others rightly suggest .
