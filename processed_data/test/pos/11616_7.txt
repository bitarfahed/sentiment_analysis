well , it is safe to say that subconscious cruelty is one weird film ! supposedly an insight into the human mind , subconscious cruelty is comprises four macabre and bizarre tales of the extreme . the first segment , entitled ovarian eyeball is really just a warm up , but it is good in that it gives the viewer an idea of what to expect from the next three segments . it simply sees a naked woman laid out on a table , while another woman cuts into her abdomen and pulls out a human eyeball ! i have got no idea what the point is , but it certainly makes for visceral viewing . the following story is the best of the bunch , and takes in the old favourite sick movie theme of incest . the segment follows a man who lives alone with his pregnant sister . he is repulsed by her pregnancy yet he wants to have sex with her anyway , and naturally he gets his way . this story stands out because of the monotonous and amatter of fact narration , as well as the ending which does not fail to deliver the shocks . this segment is well acted , well filmed and easily the highlight of subconscious cruelty . naturally , the next two sections are not as good as the second one ; so the only way from there is down , but director karim hussain still manages to pull something out of the bag before the film ends . he does not do it right away , however , as the third segment is the weakest of the film , and simply sees a lot of people have sex with the ground . it is very surreal , and therefore memorable for that same reason ; but there does not seem to be a lot of point to it , and i was in the mood for something a bit more morbid after the second section . the film ends on a high , however , as while i am not entirely sure what the point was the final segment features the film is best imagery . this segment focuses on religion , and certainly is not for anyone that values it ! director karim hussain has achieved something here as while this collection of four extreme stories does not come together as a complete whole , the film almost feels tasteful as it is shot in such an eloquent and eye catching manner . the director would seem to have been imitating the highly respected surreal director luis bu uel , albeit with gore , rape and incest ; and if you ask me , he has not done a bad job at all . not for everyone , but certainly worth a look for extreme fanatics !
