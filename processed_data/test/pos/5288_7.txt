boy named sosuke . in the process she gains a strong magic and unknowingly throws the world out of balance between land and sea , and a giant storm drowns most of the cliff top town where sosuke lives been a joy for miyazaki to work with and maybe even his entire motivation for choosing this story . among the highlights are droplets of water that fujimoto sends after ponyo that move like living is fans in every way with its imagination , and newcomers will still be smitten by his simple yet visually ambitious storytelling , but this is distinctly more of a children is movie , best for
