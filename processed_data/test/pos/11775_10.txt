talk about your classics ! ernie fossilus the foss from here on out came up with a cute and creative trailer totally spoofing star wars . this gem is so jammed packed with tributes and gags i laugh every time ! not only that , when star wars did a re issue with new special effects , hardware wars did the same ! talk about a spoof that just will not die ! there is a reason george lucas calls this his favorite parody . he was so impressed , he even hired the foss to work on return of the jedi do not believe me , check his entry in imdb ! this has to be the first , and in my opinion , the best parody ever done . i think the special edition was a bit overdone , but on reflection , i think it is perfect for the modern day re release of star wars , and goes to prove that sometimes , it is wrong to mess with perfection . yes , it is only 10 minutes , but it is well worth your time . you will laugh , you will cry , you will kiss 3 goodbye ! well , maybe 15 for the dvd , but you will be real happy you did .
