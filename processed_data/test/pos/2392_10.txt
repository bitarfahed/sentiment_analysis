i have been writing hardboiled crime fiction for a number of years now . when a writer develops a story he always has a character actor in mind to bring the story to life . last weekend i found a new one in paul vario playing uncle benny in eddie monroe . this was a slick film highlighted by vario is presence both on and off the screen as his voice over narration is also heard . i also especially liked the actress playing benny is niece and eddie is ex wife , although everyone did a fine job in this exciting movie about playing with bad guys and the double crossing that goes with it . a nice job all around . . . and mr . vario shined brightest . he is gotta be my tony gangi someday . . .
