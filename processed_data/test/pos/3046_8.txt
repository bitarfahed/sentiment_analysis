martin ritt seems to be a director who was always interested in social issues as the son of immigrants , he had every incentive to be so , especially since he was blacklisted in the 50s . conrack is based on pat conroy is novel the water is wide , about his own experience in 1969 teaching a school of impoverished black children about the outside world , much to the chagrin of the right wing superintendent hume cronyn . what added to the movie is strength was the cultural and historical context : conroy jon voight frustratedly tells another teacher how many of the children do not know about paul newman , sidney poitier , the vietnam war , or even where vietnam is . he proceeds to enlighten them about all these factors . somewhere , i read a complaint that when conroy played music for the children , he only played white music . the truth is , you cannot blame the movie for that ; it was based on conroy is real experience . either way , the movie is a real gem .
