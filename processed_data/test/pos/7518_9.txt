anybody who goes to the manhattan hospital center is taking his life in his hands . that includes the staff of the hospital . i had never seen the hospital before and i was intrigued at how similar the characters and situations of the plot were to that other paddy chayefsky masterpiece , network . there are elements in george c . scott is character that have both al schumacher is and howard beale is . he is the administrator of the hospital and he is mad as hell and not going to take it any more . he is completely estranged from his wife and kids . it takes a faye dunaway type character in the person of diana rigg to make him snap out of it . one roll in the hay with her and he is shocked back to reality and the fact he still can contribute in the world . but first he is got a real problem . someone is out killing hospital staff , four of them in a 48 hour period . and the nice part is their deaths can be attributed to in large part to the general incompetence of a medical bureaucracy . that is where the comedy comes in . there is an actual howard beale type character in the person of barnard hughes , diana rigg is father . his end is not quite as dramatic as beale is though . back in my working days it was part of my job to pay medical suppliers . some of them could be as big creeps as you will find portrayed in the hospital . the black comedy satire had some real bite to it for me . george c . scott was nominated for best actor , but having won and refused to accept the previous year is oscar for patton , he was not about to get a second chance . he lost to gene hackman for the french connection . still his handling of the role is unforgettable . try viewing the hospital back to back with network and see how many similarities you spot .
