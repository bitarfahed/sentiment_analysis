this is easily my favourite film . a tragic romance intertwined with a complex mystery whose threads are all but invisible until they all unravel at the end in one fantastic rush . sheer brilliance . i would love to see some more of gilles mimouni is work , but at least according to imdb , he has not made any other features . has the high quality of this work made producing another too daunting a task ? has he moved back into some other sphere of creative endeavour ? i certainly hope this will not be his final feature but i cannot really blame him if he decides to stop with this gem as his only contribution to the world of feature films .
