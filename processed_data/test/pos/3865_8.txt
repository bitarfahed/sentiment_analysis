no . i am not kidding with this one . he was a guest reviewer for entertainment weekly and gave this movie positive marks . and who can blame him ? this is a charming , upbeat , and rather funny disney movie . who does not love kittens ? the music in ev'rybody wants to be a cat is jamming . it makes me want to snap my fingers or something . only years later when cats do not dance came out have i seen a movie that was that musically fun . what aristocats lacks in animation and story , it makes up for in charm . plus , everything moves at a relaxed pace , and even the villain is not all that scary . it is perfect for the younger set while not being so sappy that adults cannot like it . if snoop was here , i am sure he would say the same thing . yeah . dig those crazy cats , man .
