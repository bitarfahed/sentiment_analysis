although this movie does not have the darkness of the books , it is in my opinion a great movie . it is great campy fun with the beautiful stuart townsend as lestat . he may not have the blond hair and blue eyes that are so vividly described in the book , but to be fair , he would not look good with blond hair , and lestat is most definitely about looking good . he moves like the predator i always imagined lestat would have . the visual effects are pretty good , and the soundtrack is absolutely amazing . it is not interview with the vampire , so do not try to compare the two . interview is louis story . this is a cut and paste version of lestat is . in any case , i highly recommend .
