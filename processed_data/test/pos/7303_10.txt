having watched this film years ago , it never faded from my memory . i always thought this was the finest performance by michelle pfeiffer that i have seen . but , i am astounded by the number of negative reviews that this film has received . after seeing it once more today , i still think it is powerful , moving and could not care less if it is based loosely on king lear . i now realize that this is the greatest performance by jessica lange that i have ever seen and she has had accolades for much shallower efforts . a thousand acres is complex , human , vibrant and immensely moving , but surely does not present either of the primary female leads with any touch of glamour or sexiness . i do not think this is well received in these times . perhaps one reason for this film is underwhelming response lies in the fact that the writer jane smiley , screenplay laura jones , and director jocelhyn moorehouse are all women . i know that , in my younger days , i would not have read a book written by a woman . i did not focus on this fact until years later . if you have not seen this movie or gave it a chance in the past , try watching it anew . maybe you are ready for it .
