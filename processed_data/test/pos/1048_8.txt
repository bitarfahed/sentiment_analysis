this film reminds me very much of the later rock hudson film man is favorite sport about a fishing writer who has never fished and is forced into entering a fishing tournament . in this case , barbara stanwyck is a martha stewart like writer who cannot cook and does not really have the perfect family she describes in her articles in fact , she has no family at all . well , like rock , she is maneuvered into performing in this case , creating a huge holiday dinner while vacationing in the countryside . since there is no family , she takes home a veteran and a few friends and tried desperately not to let the cat out of the bag that she cannot do any of the things she is known for doing . a cute little comedy and a welcome film to the annual holiday film lineup .
