do not torture a duckling is one of the coolest italian horror films i have ever seen , and i have seen my share . to call it a giallo is a little misleading because it is not really a typical murder mystery . it is more of a straight horror movie . complete with one of the most brutal and gory scenes ever in a movie this old , fulci is twisting and turning film oozes with a creepy ambiance and an old school italian feel . the setting is perfect : an old italian village . the music is ridiculously perfect . the finale is genuine and original . after seeing do not torture a duckling you really have to wonder how fulci is later stuff got so off beat . i like all his stuff , but he strayed . maybe he figured he already did it . . . because with this film , he hits the bullseye . i would recommend this film to anyone who thinks that dario argento owns the giallo genre . fulci beats him with this one . 10 out of 10 , kids .
