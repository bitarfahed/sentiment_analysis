before watching this movie from beginning to end , i happened to just catch the last half hour . ordinarily i do not watch a movie if i have not seen it from the beginning , but a friend had it on and once i started watching it i could not stop . i am really surprised this movie did not get a wide theatrical release . this is quite a funny movie often gallows humor , and the monster and monster truck in it are quite menacing . the monster makes leatherface look pretty , and the monster truck is a like a cross between a world war i german artillery vehicle and a giant coffin . a timid twenty five year old virgin guy is on a long drive to stop the woman he loves from getting married . his ex best friend tags along and rags on him constantly . they are menaced on the road by a vintage black hearse and the aforementioned monster truck . they also pick up a hitchhiker , played by the very sexy aimee brooks . i also watched the animated trailer with the director is commentary , and the electronic press kit and i found those both to be interesting . i would bet that the feature commentary with the director and the two male stars is pretty enjoyable too , but i have so many other movies to watch i have never seen before . . . .
