alex benedict john cassavetes is an orchestra conductor having an secret affair with his pianist . when she threatens to expose him and create a scandal if he does not leave his wife , he sees he has no choice but to murder her and make it look as if she had committed suicide . too bad for him our rumpled detective , lt . columbo peter falk , is on the case . stephen bochco has written another razor sharp episode for the series , following marvelous detail with marvelous detail . we watch the villain commit his murder and set up his alibi for about twenty five minutes , and we are completely engrossed . then when columbo appears on the scene , it gets even better . we first see columbo after the murder as per usual . this time he is at the vet with his new dog . scenes of purely comic relief are usually the weakest ones in the show think columbo is discomfort over the nude model in suitable for framing . columbo is hilarious when he is dithering or bumbling deliberately in order to trip up his quarry ; but too many writers make the mistake of showing him in a ridiculous light when he is with non suspects . bochco , by contrast , shows columbo a bit awkward with his new pet in a handful of low key scenes that are funny and artfully integrated into the story . and i am grateful he did not strain for a punchline to columbo is quest to give the dog a name . as far as we learn , the dog remains nameless . bochco gives columbo some of his best moments in this episode , and peter falk makes the most of them . he is particularly good in a monologue where he expresses disbelief that anyone could kill herself , much less this brilliant and beautiful young pianist . and he is even better in his scenes playing cat and mouse with cassavetes . that these two long time friends and collaborators would play off each other brilliantly is to be expected ; and that is what we get . notice how alex benedict cannot help but admire the deceptively dimwitted columbo , even as the brilliant detective is tightening the noose around his neck . bochco makes the most of every situation in his script . when columbo goes to question benedict is snooty mechanic , of course of course ! he asks the man to look at his own beat up heap . and when benedict finds columbo at the hollywood bowl before rehearsal , of course of course ! he is on the piano playing chopsticks . and what should our audacious lieutenant bring with him but the victim is typewriter with the phony suicide note still in the roller . columbo also has good scenes with a precocious little girl dawn frame and the lovely blythe danner , who plays cassavetes is wife . it is nice to see myrna loy show up as danner is mother , though she does little with her minor role . the director nicholas colasanto who also acted , most notably as coach in cheers does a perfectly creditable job , though he follows the lead of nearly every other columbo director by adding one or two silly flourishes . scenes end by going out of focus and begin by coming into focus . then there is that bit where cassavetes sees the carnation he dropped at the scene of the crime and we see a zoom in shot of it reflected in his sunglasses . there is also one scene with myrna loy and group of old fogies that is atrociously performed by everyone except cassavetes . these are quibbles , though . this is a splendidly entertaining episode , the kind that made me a columbo fan .
