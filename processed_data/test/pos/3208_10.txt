gilmore girls is one of the funniest , most clever , sharp witted , sarcastic and heart warming television shows i have ever watched , second only to my most favorite television show of all time , f r i e n d s . the quick pace and many pop culture references can leave some viewers confused , but once you catch on to the ways of the gilmores , you will be hooked for life . just some random comments , i recently finally began watching season 6 and wow ! it is one of my favorite seasons so far . i love the luke lorelai thing and all the drama in the various episodes is as always exhilarating . the only thing i am not so fond of is the surprising bad attitude and reckless actions rory has suddenly adopted . unfortunately i do not find this very realistic and hope it is not just a way to stir up some more drama to keep the season going strong . all in all great season though and i cannot wait for the next ! ! ! ashly a . k . a . tookie clothespin
