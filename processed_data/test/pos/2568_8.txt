i am astounded and dismayed by the number of reviewers on this site who did not get the point of black snake moan . it is not about black white relationships or old young relationships , though i think director craig brewer deliberately threw in both elements to tweak imagined taboos . it is not about sexual abuse or sex addiction , though christina ricci is character , rae , typifies those . it is not about folk religion in the black community , though religion plays a large role . it is not a love story , though there are not one , but two happy couples at the end . and it is certainly not about the south , where everything is hotter , though it is set in the south and it is undeniably hot ; holy smokes , even the tag line writers did not get the point . black snake moan is a parable about mississippi delta blues ; who feels them , who writes them , who plays them , what they are playing about , how it heals them . it is as though the film producers sat down with a blank slate and asked , ok , if we were going to help people understand what the blues are really about , what would it look like ? so they set it in the rural south . then they dream up two characters , one whose wife left him to live with his best friend , the other who goes off to war and his badly abused girl sleeps with everybody in town . then , we throw in grizzled worldliness touched just a little by folk religion they know jesus wants their lives , and though they respect him , they know they cannot give him that , some violence between men and women , and lots and lots of steamy sexual images , including ready to go over the top ? a black man in a sleeveless undershirt holding a half naked white girl captive on the end of a 40 lb chain . fill it with authentic delta blues sounds , make it about a genuine blues picker , use music as the main healing element in the plot , slap clips of blues man son house on both ends , and voila you have a modern parable about what the blues are all about . even the film is climax is not character conflict , but the whole town dancing steamy dances to hot , raunchy blues . of course , there is a bit of a dilemma here . rae ricci is being destroyed by uncontrollable lust , and is being healed by lazarus jackson is homey religion and steadfastness and do not forget the chain . but then , we are shown the restored rae dancing raunchily to blues at the end . is this an expression of a restored , healthy life force , or just more of the same trashy behavior that ruined her in the first place ? brewer wants it both ways , but blues really is about sex and violence , not to mention depression . i suppose he would say blues gives healthy expression to both sex and violence without unleashing either . i have my doubts . not for the first time , samuel l jackson plays so well that we forget we are watching samuel l jackson ; the man is unbelievably good . he even picks some of his own tunes in the film , and his playing is authentic , dirty , and hot . christina ricci is not usually this good , either . granted , half her job is done by the costume that was not there and her slinky figure , but she is a marvelous combination of cynical lust , rebellion , and vulnerability ; bravo to her , she is arrived . i was impressed by the country preacher , john cothran , jr . i had to check the database to assure myself that he is a professional actor and not a genuine country minister . parents need to be aware of what they are getting if their kids bring this one home . the language is pretty far off the charts , the first half hour is full of graphic sex , and women are violated in a dozen ways during the course of the film lazarus means well and is decent , but honestly , chaining a woman to the radiator ? plus , ricci spends half the movie dressed for sex ; if you have got teenage boys , they will be licking the screen halfway into the film . i do not recommend this for kids of any age . adults only , please . that being said , black snake moan is informative and accurate about blues , folk religion , and sexual abuse , and tells a tale that is redemptive in lots of ways . it is unorthodox , but well worth the time . and , my goodness , is the sound track hot .
