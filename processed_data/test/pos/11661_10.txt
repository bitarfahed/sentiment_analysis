i must have seen this movie about four or five times already , and it gets better with each viewing . suffice it to say : this is the best film i have ever seen . and i think i have seen a lot . but i have always wondered why this film got so shunned in some reviews or ratings . for example , take the imdb top 250 . why does it rank only at 216 as of today ? surely , the answer is not in the film itself because that is nothing but flawless , but in its reception . the film caused controversy in its portrayal of compassion for a convicted murderer and its anti death penalty attitude . and so , obviously , the more conservative minded user probably did not like the film as you can see from some of the other comments . so dead man walking gets a ranking that is nothing but ridiculous in relation to its quality . those people did not understand what the film wanted to say , and maybe they did not want to understand , being pro death penalty . so now i get it : it is all political . you are pro death penalty you do not like and therefore do not want to hear what the film has to say . i am truly sorry there are still so many people out there who simply tune out when a new perspective questions their beliefs . mr . robbins , your movie is issue split people is opinions . some reconsidered their point of view , some simply did not listen , but you made a very important point . your movie will probably never show up on any top 100 movies of all time list , but it will be remembered , long after films like braveheart or babe or apollo 13 all of which were unjustly preferred over your film at the oscars 1996 are forgotten . congratulations , mr . robbins , and thank you for this important piece of filmmaking .
