this film is a variation of a theme we have all seen over and over again the posiedan adventure in space . the characters are all stock characters feisty heroine , judas , coward , etc . etc . the only question really is who is next to snuff it . sometimes this tried and tested formula works like in this case but more often not like the truly mind numbing red planet . so why does this one work ? because it is stylish . the acting is above average . and above all it is not predictable . the conclusion is not sign posted . i really had no idea how it would all end and was surprised at how it did end . that alone deserves top marks in my book . it is an intriguing and entertaining movie . and though it was never likely to change the face of cinema as we know it , it does not disappoint .
