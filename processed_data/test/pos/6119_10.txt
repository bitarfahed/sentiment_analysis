the film that gave us john waters is one of the funniest films ever . yes it may be gross but it is filled with hysterical moments that nobody has done since . the infamous dog scene has to be one of the highlights of cinema . not a film for the easily offended it is one of those movies where you always see something new . divine is performance is worth the reputation . mink stole is hysterical . this is the film responsible for the farrely brothers and the entire gross out comedy genre . i know that sounds a bit extreme but think about it . before this comedies were very run of the mill and almost identical . after this movie comedies became strange and weird , but in a good way . i would give it 15 out of 10 if i could .
