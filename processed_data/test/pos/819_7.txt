crack house 1989 was one of the few film during the 80 is that falls into this genre . what is supposed to be an anti drug film turns out to be nothing more than some white exploitation exercise in depravity . there is nothing wrong with that however . the video presentation even has an anti drug message from one of the stars of the show richard turncoat roundtree , the movie follows two young lovers in high school . one of them is a quasi tough guy and the other is his girl . one of them get is turned out by a mutual friend whilst the other is given a trip to the slam and is later on given a chance to get back at his ex friends . jim brown appears as the movie is mister big , he is one bad dude who still can punk out anybody and is a very sadistic guy who likes to smack his hoes and beat the tar out of those who try to defy his word . luke from general hospital makes a guest star spot as well . if you like hard edge sleaze then this movie is for you . sadly , hollywood does not make these any more and when they do , it is neither exploitative nor entertaining . recommended for sleaze fans .
