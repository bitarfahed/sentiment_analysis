i must say i did not expect much about this movie , but it turned out not to be bad at all . most striking of course , was aidan quinn is performance . i would never expect to see this fine actor as an action hero . the great thing about it is that he really builds up his character annibal . i mean , it was not like mel gibson or bruce willis would do it , he was sensitive and modest . for example , he is really upset when he kills someone . i also noticed that some clich s were avoided . when annibal gets his training , you would easily expect him to be a rebel and act like any average american would do in such a situation , ask what the f is going on and refusing to cooperate . but annibal is a professional marine officer , he does not give up and he tries not to lose his courage , in which he succeeds pretty well , except for a brief break down on christmas eve , which i think was very realistic . i am glad that aidan quinn got this opportunity to show another side of him in fact two , because he plays the villain as well , even although the film was not that successful .
