i have just sat through this film again and can only wonder if we will see the likes of films like this anymore ? the timeless music , the tender voices of william holden and jennifer jones leave this grown man weeping through joyous , romantic scenes and i am not one who cries very often in life . where have our william holden is gone and will they make these moving , wonderful , movies any more ? it is sad to have to realize that they probably will not but do not think about it , just try to block that out of your mind . even so , they will not have holden in it and he will not appear on that hill just once more either . you can only enjoy this film and watch it again .
