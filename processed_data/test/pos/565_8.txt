i watched this on hbo because it won the oscar a week earlier . it compares favorably with fictional courtroom dramas . the story is of a 15 year old black kid placed on trial for the jacksonville , fl , murder of an elderly white woman based almost solely on the identification by the victim is husband and on a confession that the defense contends was coerced . about half the footage is of the trial ; it is supplemented with footage of the defense lawyers two public defenders explaining their case , interviewing witnesses , and visiting key locations . this is edited with a minimum of needless repetition , and placed in logical order . the camera work is pretty solid . and there is a mildly surprising epilogue .
