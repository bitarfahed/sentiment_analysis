based on its current imdb rating as well as several plot summaries , i did not expect much from two hands . but how wrong i was . from start to finish , you are kept deeply engrossed in a genre which has been continuously unoriginal for quite a while . even in terms of mise en scene and cinematography , the director excels and creates a film consisting of great imagination . if you are looking for a film which is not only entertaining , but also provocative , compelling , and genuinely extraordinary , then make this the next film you watch . i am hoping heath ledger is tragic death will have lead to more people picking up this film . he was an incredible talent , and his performance in this is one not to be missed . do not make the mistake of judging and discarding this amazing film before you have seen it .
