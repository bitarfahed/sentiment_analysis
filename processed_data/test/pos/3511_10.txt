i first saw this one afternoon in the 80 is on network t . v . i think i was like 9 . picture seeing a violent horror flick nowadays on regular television . anyway , i have seen it again years later and it is like i remembered , it is really good , scary flick . i think the reason why it might of gone unnoticed is cause it was not followed by a load of sequels i . e . friday the 13th . but it is one of those movies that takes the original idea and does it better . even though this is a killer in the woods flick like friday , it has more in common with the original texas chainsaw massacre . that is because the movie lays down a certain atmosphere and feeling of dread , even in broad daylight . and the killers feel more threatening than in friday . there is also a good amount of suspense . i recommend seeing it now that it is being released on dvd in late july .
