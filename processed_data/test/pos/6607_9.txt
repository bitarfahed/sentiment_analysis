take a subject i did not know much about and make it exciting , why do not you ? it so happens that back about 1979 , director robert altman said that he did not believe he had ever made a real movie and that he expected that one of these kids riding skateboards if he does not break his neck will make the first movie . well , i would not put such an expectation on stacy peralta , but he is a skateboarder who has made a good movie . of course , he was forced by the nature of the film he was making to use existing footage , and it is certainly a good thing that so much archival footage existed . peralta edited it together well with not your usual talking head shots of his erstwhile colleagues as they are today . the whole effect is post modern in the best sense , but that has been done . altman is prediction has not quite come true . what peralta has done , however , is capture enough of the energy of those heady days that we can appreciate what it must have been like when modern skateboarding was invented by the z boys . this is all good . i highly recommend dogtown .
