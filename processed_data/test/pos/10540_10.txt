this review contains a huge spoiler , but i do not know how to explain how cool it is without giving it away i saw pinoccio is revenge a while ago . now , you might think it is just a rip off of child is play . indeed there are similarities . however , chucky was a possessed doll who works independently of the kid . it is possible that pinoccio is possessed with a demon or cursed or something . however , the puppet itself is actually completely inanimate . the kid is insane , and the kid is the one killing people ! everyone , including the audience , the survivors and the kid herself thinks it is the doll . but it is the kid . the nudity is almost a pity , because otherwise i could tell everyone to see it , because it really is an interesting horror movie .
