this movie is a hidden gem . i cannot understand why this movie does not get more air time . errol and eleanor parker make for a real attractive and dashing couple . and their chemistry is impeccable . i really liked the touch of his daughters reference to him as being her robin hood . i highly recommend this to anyone who enjoys errol flynn . it is nice to see errol playing a father as well . by all accounts from his very own children , he was a very attentive and loving father in real life . also , the supporting cast is wonderful as well . you cannot go wrong with supporting players such as hattie mcdaniel and lucile watson . hattie mcdaniel makes a movie that much better from the get go . this movie has now become a holiday tradition in my home . enjoy ! ! !
