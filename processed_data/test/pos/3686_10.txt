i wonder why i have not heard of this movie before . it is truly a magnificent comedy i would say farce , but it is too well acted and i would hate to denigrate it is quality . a comedic character study about the travails of mid life crises , the inequity for gays who cannot have their long time unions recognized , and the general differences of people who cannot fit nicely into society , that pairs kathy bates and rupert everett to a wonderful effect . forget julia roberts and rupert ; kathy bates and rupert should do another movie together . the anxiety ridden , acerbic and outrageous daughter in law , maudie meredith eaton , is a truly wonderful character . if you liked the tales of the city films , you would like this quirky charmer .
