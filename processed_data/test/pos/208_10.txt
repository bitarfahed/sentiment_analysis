if you are looking for a typical war movie , this is not it , so a note to all the testosterone pumped carnage craving war buffs out there , do not bother . although the film is about russian characters in wwii , do not expect to see any nazis , cannons , blood , gore , etc . it is not a film about people who cause a war or who fight a war . it is a film about ordinary people who war happens to and the choices they make in dealing with it . acting , cinematography , writing : all perfect 10s here . you will certainly appreciate it if you are russian like me , but even if not , you will probably love it . if you speak no russian , look for the ruscico russian cinema council dvd version . it is got subtitles in about 14 different languages , but the english dubbing on this one i would say is just as good . it is of course not as good as the original russian track some stuff is lost in translation , but just as good as the english subtitles . so go check it out , especially if you are studying film in any aspect .
