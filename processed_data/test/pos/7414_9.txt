how on earth this film is not more widely regarded is beyond me . i picked it up for 1 , and i am not exaggerating when i say it is the best pound i have ever spent on a film . the thing that usually lets films about the club scene down is either the music or the actual scenes filmed in clubland . here , the music and club scenes are completely credible , using some big tunes of the time , and filmed in real clubs , with people actually looking like they want to be there . the performances from the actors are of a high standard all round , although jason donovan in particular for me stands out he was playing frank n furter in the rocky horror show in london at roughly the time this must have been filming , so his drag phase was in full flow ! , and of course tim curry who is eloquent drug land boss is convincing . simply put , if you are a fan of british film you have to see this , it matters not a jot if you are into clubbing as the film is strong enough as a story anyway . highly recommended .
