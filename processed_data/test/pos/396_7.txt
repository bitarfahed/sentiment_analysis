this is another good western , which i enjoyed . it is not an epic or anything , but it is good for what it is . it about 3 fur trappers , led by a men named jed , who is crude and uncivilized . jed and his two friends find themselves as scouts for a fort that is the only thing standing between them and and indian band , who resent the americans on their land , and want to take it back . that is the gist of the story . what follows is action , excitement , even a bit of humour , and forbidden romance . one of jed is friends , gus , reminded me a lot of the character quint played by robert shaw in the movie jaws . they both have that crusty , gruff demeanor . anyway , if you are a western fan , you should find lots to like about this particular entry . i think it deserves a 7 10
