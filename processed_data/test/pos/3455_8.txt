i watched this film during a special advanced preview in reading , berkshire . the two main characters , michael and rory strike up a friendship , when rory is introduced to the care home that michael already resides . michael is amazed to find someone who can finally understand him , as until now his cerebal palsey made people think that he could not communicate . rory introduces michael to the life that he is missing . rory is a feisty character , who does not let his condition muscular dystrophy get to him . they bond and apply for independent living , and find a lovely flat and assistant and have all the fun that they could ever dream of . do not go to see this film if you are after some entertainment . do go see this film if you are willing to see something a little different . the storyline is strong , the actors are fantastic and despite the sadness that comes with the film the mood is uplifting . i thoroughly recommend it . though if you are anything like me and prone to crying take tissues , as you will need them !
