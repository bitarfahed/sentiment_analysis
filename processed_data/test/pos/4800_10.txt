nazarin is some kind of saint , he wants to live in life exactly how christ taught man to do . but it is too late : now the catholic church is between the hands of a wealthy bourgeoisie , the bishops live in luxury and do not give a damn about the poor and the sick . that is why our hero cannot follow the way his hierarchy asks him to follow . so he divests himself of everything , and on his way to purity , he is joined by some kind of mary magdelene and a woman who is attracted by him sexually the scene between this girl and her fianc is telling . in spain it was the late fifties , they thought nazarin was a christian movie ! knowing luis bunuel , it was downright incongruous : all his work is anticlerical to a fault . comparing nazarin and his holy women to jesus is a nonsense . on nazarin is way , only brambles and couch grass grow . his attempt at helping working men on the road is a failure , he is chased out as a strike breaker . all his words amount to nothing . at the end of the journey , he is arrested and offered a pineapple by a woman bunuelian sexual symbol . thanks to nazarin , bunuel was allowed to return to spain where the censors had not got a clue and to direct viridiana .
