alexander nevsky marked director sergei eisenstein is return to film making after a period of exile , and what he produced is a bald faced propaganda film proclaiming russia is superiority over germany . there is very little plot : russians unite to battle germans ; russia wins . the film is really an extended montage of mostly battle scenes , mixed in with some moments of german brutality like a rather shocking scene that shows german soldiers throwing russian children onto a raging fire . this was eisenstein is first sound film , and it is clear that he had no idea what to do with the medium . indeed , this film really looks more like a silent film with some sound added . there are some dialogue scenes , but they sit rather lifelessly on the screen , and the pacing is all off , as if the actors were not actually on the set together at the same time when they were filming them . eisenstein mostly uses sound as an excuse to overlay a rousing prokofiev score over the proceedings , and indeed , that is one of the film is biggest assets . say what you will about eisenstein is ability or lack of to direct actors or tell his film in a more narrative format , he certainly knows how to edit images and music together for maximum effect , and alexander nevsky over all shines through its deficiencies . grade : a
