i enjoyed this series , but felt that the whole thing was let down by the sound recording mixing . for whatever reason , they have had to employ an awful lot of what is called adr , where the actors replace the original location sound with a re recording of it in a sound booth . the reasons for doing this are usually due to problems at the location , or because somebody screwed up somewhere in the sound production chain . it would not be so bad if the adr was done well , but at times it is just plain distracting . it is not just the adr that is the problem the sound mix just does not match up to the quality of performances and the pictures . i would be curious to know what went wrong .
