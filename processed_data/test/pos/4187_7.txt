sadly , this movie is relegated to curio status it seems . many people that i have asked did you know the monkees made a movie ? usually answer no . that being said , if you are one of that large number , i recommend you see it , but with the following caveat s : if you expect monkey style humor , it is in there . it is just not all over the place like the tv shows . yes , they are trying to break their tv mold a bit by poking fun at it like a bunch of no longer teenagers who have been on the short end of a lot of sticks . no , you do not have to be inebriated to enjoy it . no , you do not have to be inebriated to understand it . if you like classics , you will love the interspersed clips throughout . if you like the psychedelic era , you will love some of the cameos . if you are a monkees fan , you might recognize some of the jabs they are taking at the heavy commercializing of the band . in the nigh immortal words of one of my best friends after seeing 5 minutes of it : this is a weird movie , man . in fact it might be better if you do not try to understand it , just sit back and react to it . it is weird , it is funny , it is a bit surreal , it is experimental still . . . it is many things . overall , it is an experience .
