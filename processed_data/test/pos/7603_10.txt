this movie may superficially appear to be flaky and for teenie boppers . it seems to be the stereotypical high school movie . but in fact , it takes an honest and touching look at the high school experience . it does not propose some unrealistic ending where the geeks and the popular kids reconcile . it does not change what happened in adolescence ; it just attempts to reckon with it . this is a really great , fun , heart warming movie , good for teens and for those who can still remember being one . . .
