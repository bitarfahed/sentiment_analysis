i tend to be inclined towards movies about people who choose to cross the barriers of censorship , and express what they really want to express . eric bogosian is character of barry is like howard stern , but much more intelligent . the character itself is very fascinating . as an oliver stone film , i guess i was expecting more . the film sags a bit during the third act . plus , it is pretty obvious that talk radio is based on a play , with its long dialogue scenes . but overall , the film works . bogosian is great in the lead , and the fact that he also wrote the play from which the movie was based on probably helped him . if you want to check out one of stone is greater films , i better suggest you check out jfk or salvador . this is not his best work , but a good movie nonetheless .
