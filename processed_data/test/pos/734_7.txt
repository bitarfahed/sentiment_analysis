do not look in the basement is actually a very clever and well thought out exploitation flick that gets a bad rap because of its cheap quality and bad acting . sure , it is not a masterpiece by anyone is standards but it is a very fun little b film with a lot to offer and even a lot of creepy scenes that will stay in your head . as i said , the acting could have been a lot better but that is the case with most exploitation so i cannot really complain . the story is clever and has some great plot twists that will keep you guessing . i thought the gore was a lot of fun too . there is just something great about older gore films because they did not have cgi back then so they had to actually set it all up themselves . see this one for a good time !
