this film is completely underrated . it is a film similar to will keenan and patrick hasson is waiting , as well as adrien brody is restaurant and the classic film breaking away , which are all about young adults who are stuck and know they are stuck , with little or no chance of breaking free . death by pizza delivered is about an intelligent , free thinking , artistic young adult will , played by david strictland who is stuck and waiting , bitter at the world is hypocrisy and bitter at his own lack of direction and desire . will meets his nemesis , reed ron eldard , another intelligent young adult who is so bitter , he is chosen the path of crime . both end up helping each other to free themselves of their bitterness , which enables them to get unstuck . for these young adults , getting unstuck , or , breaking free , can mean both forging ahead into life , and plunging downward into death . will is life is filled with the trademarks of a young stuck adult : a soul sucking , sweaty , under paying job , crude customers , an ex girlfriend who left him because he was unmotivated , a partial college education with no degree , a house filled with self made art , and of course the new friend whose ungodly choices help him to save himself .
