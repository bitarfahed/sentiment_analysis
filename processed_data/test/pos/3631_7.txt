let is be fair : there are no rules for scriptwriting , so i will not say the movie should be something other than it is . i will just state my opinion . ok , i really liked the script , the way things are told and characters are introduced . however , i think when a play is adapted to the silver screen , it could maybe try and fit this other media . since this movie was directed by jo o falc o , the same man who directed the play , it is not too much of a surprise that the film turned out a bit too theatrical . it is the man is first movie , for cryin out loud ! what i am saying is , there are parts in the film like the city of nordestina , which slightly resembles the set of hoje dia de maria that feel like we are not at a movie theater . we are at the theater ! lines are spoken too formally , characters are moving choreographically , and the lightening is clearly meant for a stage . sure , maybe that was the intention , but that is been done . on stage . wonderful things have been made when adapting a play into a movie the beautiful closer , for instance . when i went to see this one , though , i did not feel like i was watching a movie . but overall , it was a good one , worth watching . it is a nice love story , funny at times and sad at others . my vote is a 7 .
