ok , when i rented this several years ago i had the worst expectations . yes , the acting is not great , and the picture itself looks dated , but as i sat there , a strange thing happened . i started to like it . the action is great and there are few scenes that make you jump . brion james , maybe one of the greatest b grade actors next to bruce campbell , is great as always . the story is not bad either . now i would not rush out and buy it , but you will not waste your time at least watching this good b grade post apocalyptic western .
