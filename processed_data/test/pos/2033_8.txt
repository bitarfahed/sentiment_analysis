i must admit i was not expecting much on this movie . i was surprised i truly enjoyed it as much as i did . the script was not oscar material , but it was not horrible either . the acting was great by mark wahlberg . jennifer aniston had a great supporting role , and looked lovely as ever . what made this movie for me was the music . if you do not like 80 is glam metal or hair bands , then you probably wont like this movie . its all about being a rockstar . some cliche is were present , but did not bring down the movie at all . i would recommend it to anyone who likes rock and roll and remember to stand up and shout ! ! ! 8 of 10 for great acting and awesome music . jason
