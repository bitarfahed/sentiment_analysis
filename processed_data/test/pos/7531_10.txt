watching this movie , i cannot help drawing the comparison between it and wild reeds , another thoughtful film about teenagers coming of age . like wild reeds , this movie is slow and the director would not be hurried . so if you want a quick resolution to things , do not watch it . this movie is like a slice of life , beginning imperfectly and ending imperfectly . there is no resolution to anything , no happily ever after for anybody , just like real life . this movie is as real as it gets . the acting is surprisingly good . the director is fond of long , really long , shots and the actors and actresses excel at showing subtlety and inner thoughts . i love this movie . i almost did not watch it but now i am very glad that i did . it is not a movie for everyone but if you are willing to let it grow on you , you will be rewarded . 10 10
