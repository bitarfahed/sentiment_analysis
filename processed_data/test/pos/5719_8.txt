i never saw any of john leguizamo is stand up before i watched freak , and after seeing it again on hbo comedy recently it was better , more enlivening and with things that , at the age of 14 , i could not understand quite before . spike lee did a wonderful job directing , keeping the visual angle up to par with leguizamo is theatrics and the style of personal storytelling ; it is substance and style merging together but unobtrusively . we get leguizamo is rants about race and sex the bits about sex are classic , but a lot of it is about family , and what sticks still fresh in my mind is about his father . even when things get dark in some of the stories , there is something fresh or crazy or random that leguizamo pulls out to get a laugh , and it works more often than not . if something does fall flat for me at any rate it might be his personality tics here and there . but overall , it is the kind of fun material that is not heady , but is so true to itself that it stings . find it if you can !
