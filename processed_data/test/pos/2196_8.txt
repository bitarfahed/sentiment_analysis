last year , i fell in love with the tim burton is version of sweeney todd so i wanted to check out the other versions of this musical and i found this one at the library . though i think burton is is best , probably because i like film a lot better than theater , this is still a great production of the story . i have not seen any of the other versions but i am trying to get my hands on them . after seeing johnny depp as todd , it is hard for me to imagine anyone else in the role , but george hearn does a fantastic job . angela lansbury is great , as always and all of the singing is fantastic . i found myself singing along . this is a play you will not want to miss , but try and see it before you see the film version so you will not have a biased view like me .
