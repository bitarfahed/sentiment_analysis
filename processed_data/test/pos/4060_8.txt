showtime is a mess , but it is an entertaining mess . the plot is forgettable , the satire is lightweight and the action sequences , though well staged , are not that exciting . but i laughed . a lot . credit goes to the inspired pairing of de niro and murphy . they have great chemistry . murphy delivers the one liners , de niro cracks us up with a look or grimace . this movie will get a rush hour like reception from the critics . do not listen to them . if you are a fan of de niro or murphy , you are going to enjoy this movie .
