this was a very gritty movie about anti semitism . however , unlike gentlemen is agreement that also deals with anti semitism , the movie has aged well and does not seem heavy handed . in other words , it deals with the topic without seeming preachy or trite by today is standards . robert ryan plays one of the most vile characters , as he beats a guy to death just because he is jewish . robert mitchum plays the investigator trying to get to the bottom of this crime . i give the movie kudos for its gritty and unflinching look at hate . it is in many ways an example of film noir even though the topic is not about the usual gangsters or robbery .
