which , i hope , i am . the first thing : it is a certain masterpiece , even of that kind of art which remains through a long long time ; the second : it is one of those rare dark movies in which childhood of soul . those who have such souls are usually branded as crazy by our society , but from another point of view , they all have the virtue all the others lost when become grown : the virtue
