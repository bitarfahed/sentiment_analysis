having read so much negative press on this movie over the years , i would always avoided it , but the advent of the cheap public domain dvd encouraged me to finally give it a viewing . unfortunately , it is been transferred from a poor copy . the modern prequel , shot on tinted stock , is blurry and the contrast , non existent . faces are occasionally difficult to make out . having said that , the actual story is entertaining and lou comes across as an accomplished actor , more so than in many of the boys movies . once the movie switches to the jack story , the film switches to colour and i use that term loosely . most hues are orange or brown . the greens look particularly bad . dorothy ford as polly , the giant is maid , was a big plus for me as i enjoyed her in an early andy hardy appearance . with so many negative comments put down to the actual quality of the print , i am still happy to give this movie a 7 . it gave me a lot of laughs and that is more than the greater majority of comedies i have watched over the decades can manage . one to watch if you get the chance .
