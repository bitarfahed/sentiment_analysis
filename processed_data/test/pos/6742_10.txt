this is without a doubt one of neil simon is best plays turned movies . it is full of great characters , and memorable dialog . johnathan silverman makes a great screen version of young eugene he was played by matthew broderick on stage . this is the first of simon is autobiographical trilogy , its followed by the wonderful biloxi blues , and closes with the tv movie broadway bound . if i had to say the movie has any flaws it would maybe be that characters sometimes usually speak in obvious dialog , but that is alright because it is great dialog . rent this little gem , you will not be sorry !
