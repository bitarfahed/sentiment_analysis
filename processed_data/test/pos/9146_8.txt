if you love vampire lore and are a fan of gothic horror , then you might want to check out soul is midnight . i did not know much about this movie before i watched it , and i was not expecting much , but i found the movie to be fun and entertaining . starring armand assante as the leader of the vampires simon , soul is separates itself from other low budget vampire flicks by weaving in the mythology of st . george and the dragon in a fun new way . i am not sure what the budget for this movie was , but i sense that if it was a little more then they might have really been able to hit home the gore and effects . if you are up late one night and you are in the mood for a fun low budget vampire flick then soul is midnight is a good choice .
