this is a movie you will either love or hate . i loved it . if you are looking for suspense , great special effects , action , sophistication , cynicism , etc . you will not find it in this movie . it is a feel good movie , sentimental , positive , uplifting . the heroes of the movie are coach jones played by ed harris , a man of strength and integrity , and radio cuba gooding , jr . a mentally retarded man who finds a way to contribute to his world . i guess i did not find this movie to be sentimental hogwash as so many did , because it felt very real to me . i know people like these . i have seen jocks who think it is fun to pick on the vulnerable . i have seen men of integrity stand up for the vulnerable . i have seen people who think high school football is serious business . i have seen people who know what really is important in life . any of these people could have been people i knew . i did laugh ; i did cry . i left the movie feeling good , remembering that there are people like coach jones and radio in my world . if there are not people like them in yours , you might not like the movie . if you do not like a movie that shows the better side of human nature , you will hate it .
