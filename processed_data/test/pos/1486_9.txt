because mr . bean almost never speaks , i heartily recommend using a dvd player with the teacher holding his finger over the pause play button . at the end of any age group is lesson , simply devote 5 minutes to pausing and playing the dvd , encouraging students to shout out the answers to what is this ? , what will happen ? , what is happening ? , what is wrong ? , or any other question that elicits responses from that lesson is new vocabulary and grammar . because everyone is looking at the tv , normally shy students become vocal . because the dvd can be started or stopped at any point , it is a perfect filler for the awkward between times while students are leaving and arriving . i tried other dvds , notably tom jerry cartoons and red skelton dvds , but no others were as good as mister bean at holding students constant attention .
