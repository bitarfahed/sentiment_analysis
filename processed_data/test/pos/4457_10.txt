the name lucio fulci congers up images of graphic death and mutilation in the minds of may fans . thanks to movies like zombi 2 , city of the living dead , the beyond and the new york ripper , fulci has a reputation for being one of the goriest directors in history . and although many of his later movies certainly justify his reputation until the release of zombi 2 in 1979 fulci is films did not contain anywhere near the amount of blood and guts he is know for , in fact they were for the most part gore free , instead relying on more traditional shocks and disturbing imagery to work . do not torture a duckling hardly contains any gore , yet ranks as his best . do not torture a duckling is set in a small sicilian town where superstition rules instead of logic . the townsfolk are very distrustful of outsiders as well as anyone different , often shunning them . after a series of child murders though many people descend upon the town , including andrea martelli thomas milan who tries to uncover the truth about the murders while they continue to happen . this is a remarkable film . it is very well made with an excellent cast filled with many favorites of italian exploitation cinema . it also contains a solid score as well as many creative camera movement courtesy or lucio fulci . but the real draw of do not torture a duckling is the disturbing nature of the movie . little kids , around twelve years old are shown mocking retarded people , visiting prostitutes and being propositioned sex by an older woman . it also contains some very biting commentary on the middle class and the catholic church . it is for reasons like this that do not torture a duckling was blacklisted throughout europe when it was first released and never was shown in the united states . still , do not torture a duckling stands as a monumental achievement in giallo cinema as well as lucio fulci is best work . i really cannot recommend this one enough , check it out .
