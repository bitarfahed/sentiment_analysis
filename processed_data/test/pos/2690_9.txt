i guess my husband and i are a little slow . we do not usually warm up to a series until they are almost at the end of their production life . in this case , we did not start watching koqs until almost the 6th season . i am not sure how it escaped our radar for so long . other than the fact that we are not big fans of appointment tv viewing . our schedules our such that we do not like to commit to watching series every time they come on and we did not have a dvr yet . so i guess it was not until tbs starting running reruns on their daily lineup in the evenings that we started watching consistently . by the time we got hooked , there were only a couple more season is left before the series was canceled . but we still watch it almost daily on tbs . i almost prefer to see series this way , because you can watch multiple episodes day after day and it helps to build continuity and what is going on with the characters without having to wait a whole week . but the episodes stand alone in the since that the stories do not carry over from week to week . but that is fine with me , because you can watch an episode , then miss weeks and still pick back up . my only criticism is the writing was not always consistent . some episodes would be outrageously hilarious , and then some would only be mildly funny . so , i am not sure it had the quality of writers that seinfeld or raymond had . but i loved the casting and the characters are all quite believable and realistic . kevin james is just plain funny to look at ! so even if the plot is not that great , james body language and expressions make the show worth watching . leah remini is great as the play it straight wife . i think its harder to play the straight character for laughs , than the comedic character , and she does a great job . she has a knack at sarcasm and insults like no one else . she is one tough cookie ! and who can forget arthur as carrie is dad , who lives with them in their basement . this a great series and i was sorry when it cancelled . but a big thanks to tbs for keeping the king alive in reruns !
