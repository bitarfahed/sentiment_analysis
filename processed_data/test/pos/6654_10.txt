how can a name , not even a real name , break your heart ? here is how . there have been few film versions of a celebrated novel that have done better justice to their source material than anthony minghella is movie of charles frazier is cold mountain . if you have read the book you will be able to feel most of the major scenes soul shakingly recreated . i personally cried numerous times while reading the novel and spent much of the evening watching the film through tears . astounding scope , beautiful words , great acting and great music . in the interview on the dvd accompanying the film minghella talks about the multiple layers of the story . all of them work . one of the best films i have seen and an invitation to one of the greatest novels of the last ten years .
