very typical almod var of the time and , in its own way , no less funny than many of his later works . and why is that ? there is nothing to be provoked or shocked about , and i guess any such effect is more coincidental than intentional . no , the great humor stems from an underlying , almost surreal , absurdity that is woven into the scenery : the characters nearly complete lack of taboo . it is the same kind of comic suspense you find in his later works , though you will find it in a more rough version here . he is building up for masterpieces to come , but is not yet there . the sole reviewer who commented on this movie before i did , claimed that it had to be a very select group of people who would find this movie hilarious . i do .
