i first saw this film as a teenager i am now in my 40 is , and have long considered it to be my favorite movie . the story is enormously moving , without being sentimental . the acting , especially by march and loy , is dead on . and the fact that dana andrews is too old for his role does not take away from the believability of his romance with theresa wright whom i believe is the only major character in the film still living . this could have turned out to be another post war melodrama , but the script and cast are simply too good for that to happen .
