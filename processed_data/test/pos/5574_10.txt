after 2 years of using this site for movie reviews , i finally registered with imdb just so i could give farscape a 10 . the show is writers , cast and crew have proven themselves the unambiguous masters of the science fiction genre . even those who do not normally appreciate sci fi should be encouraged to give this exceptional series a chance ! farscape is virtues are simply too numerous to list , but one of them stands out above all ; the quality of the writing is amazing . i have not heard dialogue this good since blake is 7 . in fact , farscape feels a lot like a blake is 7 with good special effects and a bit more romance . everyone , enjoy !
