let me say from the outset i am not a particular fan of this kind of film , but nightbreed holds a certain fascination for me with a message about perspective . back in the old days , the folks who inhabit midian would have been called zombies , the undead . and according to what clive barker has given us certain members of human kind , in this craig sheffer are born with the potential to become part of that world . psychiatrist david cronenberg at first looking like the mild mannered professional has taken unto himself a fanatical mission to rid the world of the nightbreed . he tricks the police into killing sheffer , but sheffer goes to a graveyard named midian cemetery where the nightbreed congregate and live underground . sheffer has also left a girl friend , anne bobby , who still has feelings for him even after he is been killed and is now one of the undead . she tries in her own small way to be a bridge to humankind . clive barker is creatures are a pretty gruesome looking lot and are not particularly fond of humans . but it is plain to see that if humans left them alone , the nightbreed in turn not bother with them . your sympathies are definitely with the nightbreed especially after seeing a fanatic like cronenberg and redneck police chief charles haid in action . clive barker is been an out gay man for some time now and some have suggested to me that the nightbreed is a metaphor for gay people . i can see where that would come in , especially since there are a whole lot of people who do not even think of gays as anything human because they are taught that way . granted nightbreed is pretty bloody with a lot of gratuitous violence , but it also does make you think and i do like the way clive barker does turn traditional theology on its head and makes craig sheffer a kind of messiah for the nightbreed creatures .
