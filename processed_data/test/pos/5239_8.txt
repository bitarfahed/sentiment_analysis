i really liked this film . it features john wayne in his first starring performance . even then , you can tell wayne has a real presence , although he would not really mature into the icon he is known for until stagecoach , 9 years later in 1939 . it is about settlers from all over the country heading to the new west to colonize it . wayne is character breck coleman joins up , but for his own personal reasons . most of the main actors were stage actors and had never done a film before , which makes the movie even more amazing . they managed to create believable , distinctive characters and there is quite an oddball mix here . cimarron would come out a year later , and had a very similar story , though i did not like it as much as this movie . for me , the big trail is a strong 8 10 .
