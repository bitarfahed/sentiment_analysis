just saw this film the other day at the santa fe film festival . i was delighted by its honesty and humor . it was the most thought provoking movie i have seen in a very long time i just cannot get it off of my mind for some reason . it may not be for everyone , because it makes you think . i do not know if people like to think in movies so much anymore , which is a shame . the actors who play the franklins are quite a find . i could not imagine playing these complicated people with such frankness . though i thought a couple of the supporting characters were not as good as the franklins , i can totally forgive it because this is an important movie in my opinion . i did not expect to like it so much , but as i have thought about it for a few days , it is proving to be a very relevant piece of entertainment .
