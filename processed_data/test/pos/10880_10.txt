this is my favorite game for the nintendo 64 platform . i have played many different first person shooters , and i have never really liked any of them much , but this game has a certain something that i cannot put my finger on that makes it an amazing amount of fun . maybe it is the extraordinary detail put into the game . maybe it is the fluid movement of the characters . maybe it is the gadgets and weapons . maybe it is the suave character of james bond . whatever it is , this game never seems to get old no matter how many times i have played it .
