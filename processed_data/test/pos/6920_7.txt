this movie is all about entertainment . imagine your friends that you love spending time with , the ones that you know inside out becoming a bit silly and perhaps taking on a character or two . that is what this film is about . an inventive script and brilliantly performed . it is not about pleasing the masses with this one , it is about having fun with a bunch of brilliantly talented people . which is what i am sure they all thought when they signed up for it . the above review sounds completely unfair and i think that the person who wrote that was in the wrong frame of mind when they watched the film . in a lighthearted moment , there is great dignity in it if you care to look . a job well done , i thought it was a great film . i would watch this before the north american norm any day . in a nutshell , it is not the best film you are ever going to see but it has a hell of a lot of moments . i have not laughed that long in an age .
