it takes a rare movie to get better each time you see it . o brother does that and then some . the first time i saw it , i have to admit i had never seen anything like it . then i wanted to see it again , and now i am up to double digits with this great movie , the coen brothers finest movie they have ever done , with fargo and hudsucker proxy coming in a tight second and third for me . george clooney gives the performance of his career playing ulysses everett mcgee , a fast talking know it all escaped convict who really does not know that much at all . tim blake nelson and john turturro are the perfect sidekicks for clooney , particularly nelson and his portrayal of delmar , a loyal albeit uneducated fellow escapee . john goodman is my favorite bit character as big dan , perfect . it reminds me of his part in raising arizona , he is just a perfect actor for this role . coen brothers favorites holly hunter and charles durning also provide memorable performances . joel and ethan coen are masters of their trade . it is not like they try to win academy awards every time they make a film , they just try and tell a story that they want to tell , and it is entertaining . it is a loose adaptation of the odyssey , and i mean loose for all of you homer fanatics . it is just great . the most amazing part of this movie though is the coloring . i have watched how they got the dusty feel to the movie and i am still in awe just thinking about it . the coloring does become an important part of the movie . great great movie .
