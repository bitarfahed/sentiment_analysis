ok , most of us agree that this is a weak attempt at a remake , but at the same time it is also a different movie in its own right . do not get me wrong , american werewolf in london is a superior film , but american remake in paris is a decent movie as well . first off , the only real similarities are the title , 2 american backpackers , and werewolves . other than those 3 things , american remake in paris stands apart fairly well on its own with its special blend of humor , action adventure , and horror . most of the people who say this film is better than the original are the youth of today is generation that think any movie made before 1990 is total crap . while those of us who grew up in the 80 is can appreciate older films and what they have added to the horror films of today . what a lot of people fail to realize is that without the old classic films , including the b w ones , horror would not be what it is today . now i am getting a little off the beaten path . . . an american werewolf in paris is a good attempt at re creating a classic , but it will never surpass the original , ever . with that said , this is still a very entertaining film to watch and i do recommend it . i am giving it a 7 out of 10 . maybe that is being a little generous , but i have seen much worse attempts at trying to re create a classic .
