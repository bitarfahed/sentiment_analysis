i saw this superb documentary at the santa barbara int'l film festival . it is extremely absorbing and very well crafted , drawing you into the life and career of harry nilsson , one of the most overlooked musical talents of the 1960 is and 70 is . while i was familiar with his better known compositions one is the loneliest number , without you , i learned about this man is brilliant writing and beautiful singing . if you love music , you will find plenty to draw you in to his world , which unfortunately spiraled out of control as his success increased and his past haunted him . however sad , he nonetheless was completely devoted to his family and you will find yourself so glad to have met this man . the profile is effectively told through nilsson is own words and those of his friends and colleagues a virtual who is who of rock who loved and respected him . do not miss this !
