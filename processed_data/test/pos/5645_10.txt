this is probably the greatest war film and certainly one of the greatest films . there is no sentimentality , no patriotic agenda , not even a hopeful message of universal brotherhood in this bleak glimpse of what we can only call hell . it is this quiet rigor and lack of manipulation that give the film its astounding power . there are not any attempts to make a hero out of ichikawa is protagonist tamura either . he is just a poor doomed sap trying to stay alive in a world of horror who hopes , but is not sure , he can hang on to his humanity in the process . ichikawa is fierce lack of cant and illusion make fires on the plain stand alone . ichikawa died february 2008 .
