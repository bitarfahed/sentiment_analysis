having seen other bollywood flicks with salman khan in them , i can say this is my favorite of the more recent ones . the songs are all quite fun , especially o priya o priya which seems to have a nice mix of beatles , indian music and dare i say this a bit of prince . the love stories are a bit more believable than , say , chal mere bhai . the occasional focus on prem is use of alcohol is at times troubling as it does not really seem to make sense to me , but it is played well by khan although his voice does become squeaky when he is portraying drunkenness .
