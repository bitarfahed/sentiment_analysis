a great look at the 60s through the eyes of four friends from their student days in 1960 to their reunion 10 years later a yugoslavian immigrant in love with the american dream and struggling to cope with the often violent reality ; a prematurely balding undertaker is son ; a soldier ; and the crazy hippy girl they all love . good direction and a strong cast do justice to steve tesich is brilliant script ; the dialogue is not as snappy as in breaking away , but the themes of growing up and father son conflict are dealt with just as well , and there are still a few wonderfully comic moments among the shocks and drama .
