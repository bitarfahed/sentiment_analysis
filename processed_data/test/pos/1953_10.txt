apparently this movie was based on a true story . i am not sure how accurate it is , though . but it really reminded me of how when i see that someone has been murdered on the news , it is amazing how much it does not affect me . sure , i think it is terrible , but i honestly do not care . i move on . it seems that murder is trivial now . this is what river is edge shows . nobody really seems to care about this girl and her death , not even the killer . then what is the point ? the killer in this story is john , and for the large amount of the movie he hides out with another killer named feck , played by dennis hopper . feck is older , and you can see the generational gap . he says he loved the girl that he killed . when he asks john if he loved the girl he killed , he simply replies , she was okay . the movie only seems to offer one solution : life is more important than death . a character is life is spared , people get second chances , and one hopeless case is killed . the acting is really good . after watching this movie i could only come to the conclusion that crispin glover is either a brilliant actor , or a terrible actor . i still have no idea . he was my main reason to see this movie , though . but the best performance is clearly given by dennis hopper . even though the fashion is really 80 is and characters sometimes mention then current issues , i still think river is edge is as relevant today as ever . my rating : 10 10
