this is a low budget stop motion monster movie from brett a nymphoid barbarian in dinosaur hell piper . . . and it delivers just what i would expect from such a production : light hearted though cheesy dialogue , some cute actresses and lots of stop motion critters . that is why i have given the film 10 out of 10 because it delivers what i expected it to deliver . . . and a bit more : brett does not penny pinch when it comes to putting his critters on screen . he hurls lots of bugs at his cast for the finale . and , anyway , i love stop motion monsters which , compared to cgi critters in bigger budgeted movies , just seem to be that much fun to watch .
