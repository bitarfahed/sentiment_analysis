i blubbered like a little girl during the ending of this movie and i dare anyone else to hold it together without a sob . absolutely heart wrenching stuff , yet uplifting at it is core . a great effort on the part of ann margaret who plays a terminally ill mother of ten who , knowing her arthritic steel working husband will not be able to support the family when she is gone , arranges the adoptions of her children before she shuffles off . the role really deserved an oscar . you truly feel for this poor family as the dying mother gives her all to ensure that her kids do not end up in a poorly administered state orphanage system . if you have not seen it get it if you can .
