richard abernethie , a very wealthy man , has died and his relatives have assembled for his funeral . included in the funeral party is abernathie is youngest sister cora galaccio . while none of the family has seen cora in at least 20 years , they all agree that cora was always a bit different . so when cora says something about abemethie having been murdered , most laugh it off as one of cora is eccentricities . but someone is obviously taking cora seriously . the next day , cora is found dead in her bed having been beaten violently . is there a connection between the two deaths ? it is up to hercule poirot to find a killer . after the funeral is one of the most well put together episodes of the entire poirot series . i have always been a fan of this particular agatha christie book and , from what i remember , the movie is as faithful to christie is source material as any of the poirot installments . the mystery is top notch with plenty of clues , suspects , and red herrings . and as i have written before , i always enjoy an christie story where hercule poirot gathers everyone together in a drawing room for the final reveal . it might be old fashioned , but that is the way i like it . getting beyond the plot , technically and artistically after the funeral is a winner . sets , editing , direction , and cinematography are as good as you will find in one of these movies . the acting is equally impressive . i have come to expect an enjoyable performance from david suchet as poirot and he does not disappoint here . the rest of the cast is just as strong with monica dolan giving an especially noteworthy performance . other than a minor quibble with the rapid fire way the characters are introduced , i have got no real complaints . it is a good show all the way around .
