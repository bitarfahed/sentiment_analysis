suzumiya haruhi no yuuutsu is a very high rated anime in almost every review page you will find on the web . so i really wanted to know why , and i was anything but disappointed . if you can get past the very bizarre but rather funny first episode , you will find yourself in a very entertaining and much strange world . a very well drawn , perfectly animated world , that is . i cannot tell much of the story without spoiling it , so i will just say that it is a high school comedy . . . and yet it is not . i cannot really say what it is about , really . seriously , i am a huge anime fan , and i have got around 50 full series , and i am not kidding when i say that , even though i have not finished watching haruhi suzumiya , it is actually standing in a very high rank in my personal collection . i fell in love at first sight with this one , and i assure you that , at the very least , you will not be indifferent to its irresistible charm . trust me , i do not go around giving a 10 to every thing that i watch .
