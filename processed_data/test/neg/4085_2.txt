this certainly is not a comedy i do not know what it was marketed that way . as a serious movie , it lacks any sort of substance . unless you are fresh out of sunday school or needing your noah fix , you will find yourself bored to tears . the supporting cast took away from what little of the movie was left . lauren graham plays an empty housewife with no real depth . his children do not really add anything to the movie . they seem to be around solely to brood about their absent father at the beginning . jonah hill plays a creepy internet addict that does not come off as humorous . i found the original to be a decent movie . disappointed that this one did not really go anywhere .
