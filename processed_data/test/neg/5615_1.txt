despite a great soundtrack and the presence of the ever amazing rappaport and woods , this is another one of those moronic comedies where new york throws itself at the hero in an effort by the writer and or director to show what a zany place it is . yeah there is some other stuff in the movie that sucks too , but that is what is important . the trend for new york independent filmmakers seems to be i do not need to be talented , i have new york ! okay , to be fair , the movie has its moments . the flashback bit about why the one guy is called wacky jack was pretty amusing . the script is not a story or a plot , it is a bunch of not good scenes tied to each other by featuring the same character . one of the worst things is that there is no motive behind what the characters do . uncle sam has the kid deliver the drugs , why ? if its so important why did not sam do it himself ? then the lead character lies his ass off in scene after scene with absolutely nothing to gain from lying . the guy falls in love with a flight attendant with neither of them having any reason to fall in love . the characters are a bunch of pawns for the writer to move around to see if he can get anything zany to happen . if you are easily amused or like watching bad indie movies because they make you feel smarter than watching bad mainstream movies , watch this . if you want to see what a good light hearted crime movie looks like , watch takeshi kitano is brother . kicked in the head is the perfect example of why so many people hate offbeat indie movies : a lot of them suck . and a note to the director : do not be afraid to excite , amuse , enlighten or entertain the audience now and then . being boring does not make you a better filmmaker than the ones who can interest me .
