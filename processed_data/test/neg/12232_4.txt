when i first saw the trailer for the comebacks , it looked absolutely horrible and i had no interest in seeing it , but when it came out on dvd today , i figured since there was nothing else that caught my interest , i would rent it and give it a shot . i watched it tonite and it really was not that bad . i think it was immature and stupid at times , but there were a few funny moments that made me laugh . i do not really watch many sports movies , so i wonder maybe if i saw more , maybe this movie would make more sense to me , but it is all good , i still did not mind so much watching the comebacks . i admit , these stupid spoof movies are lame , but what is the harm in a stupid joke every once in a while ? the comebacks is not really that bad if you give it a fair chance . coach fields is failing in life , family and career both , but when he is offered a chance to bring his career back to life if he can bring a looser football team into the championship . but the team is really really terrible , like beyond terrible . but with a little work and team effort they try to give it their all , even though that might turn into something more sad . the comebacks over all is not the worst film i have seen , i think it is good for a couple laughs and giggles . i know that this was stupid , but i could not help but laugh when the coach comes in the middle of a fight in the locker room and he is beating the nerd is head against the locker , just him and the nerd in general were so funny together . if you have an open mind and do not take this movie too seriously , i think you will have a fun time watching it , if you watch it expecting it to be oscar worthy material , this is not the movie for you . 4 10
