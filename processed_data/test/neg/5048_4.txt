going into teaching mrs tingle , all i wanted was a fun , enjoyable teen comedy that would entertain me for it is running time . despite a rather good first half hour , the film quickly subsides into a dull , clich d mess that is about as entertaining as pulling out your eyelashes with pliers . rusty pliers , at that . iscream writer kevin williamson wrote and directed this movie , and proved that scream may well have been a fluke . most of the elements of this movie have been seen a million times already in other films ; and while it was ok for him to steal elements from other movies in scream , due to the fact that it is meant as a slasher tribute ; here , it just looks like he is completely ran out of ideas . the plot follows the cleverest girl in school , played by katie holmes . after being caught cheating along with two of her friends ; the three decide to take the teacher that caught them hostage in her own home . however , this is not just any teacher ; it is mrs tingle , the meanest bitch in school . she is not taking being tied to the bed lying down either , as she begins to play mind games to turn her captors against each other . the plot is very similar to the 1997 flick isuicide kings , and a whole host of earlier films . it is actually not a bad idea for a movie , and if williamson could have populated the film with interesting characters ; it could have worked really well . the character of mrs tingle is the most interesting in the movie , but she is massively one dimensional , and like all the other characters in the film ; is merely a caricature . the acting is largely diabolical , with the exception of helen mirren in the title role . she is suitably evil in the role , and while she does not have a lot to get her teeth into ; she clearly enjoys herself playing the central figure . the teenage cast is not worth mentioning , with only katie holmes standing out ; and that is only really because of her star profile , not her acting talents . williamson has draped nearly every scene in dull soft rock music , which would be really annoying if the film was not absolutely terrible anyway . honestly , this movie does have a few moments that are rather good ; but basically , if you want to see a good example of the teen comedy this is not the movie that you want to see .
