in terms of quality movies , this is not one of them . it is actually the first chuck norris movie i have seen and i was left pretty underwhelmed . the fight scenes are slow and do not have a lot of variety . norris just uses a lot of roundhouse kicks on all the bad guys coming after him which makes the fights pretty boring . the movie also is quite short , but for some reason the movie does not even seem finished when it ends . it is a pretty anti climatic ending . all the same though , i have watched a lot of bad movies , and this is not one of the worst that i have seen . it is worth a watch , i am guessing especially for norris fans . there is also nothing like seeing a group of rigs hurtling down the desert which in my opinion was the highlight of the movie .
