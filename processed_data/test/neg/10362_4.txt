this movie was extremely disappointing , i thought it would be another amarijuana comedy but do not be mislead , it is not at all . there are barely any weed related jokes that come to mind , i do not even think they smoke any weed in this movie . the marijuana field is merely a plot device . my impression after seeing this film was that four friends were bored one summer and decided to write up a script full of their idea of witty dialog and make a movie . the product is bland dialog supported by mediocre acting , to say this movie has no hook is a huge understatement . do not waste your time or money .
