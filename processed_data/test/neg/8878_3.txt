this is not exactly a complicated story . it is not a mystery , or a plot you have to spend time re watching because you missed an intrinsic part of the dialog . claiming a movie has to be seen multiple times for you to get it may apply to a very few films , however this is not one of them . those type of movies have substance and are so involving it is easy to miss an important portion of dialog , or a subtle nuance to plot details you may have overlooked . with goya is ghosts this does not apply . the main flaw in this movie is an absence of detail . it jumps from one character to the next , never giving enough substance to any one set of scenes to allow the involvement of those watching . because of that you do not connect with any of the characters . you do not get to the point where you care . either the main actor is gone just as the movie seems it is going to redeem itself , or their actions are despicable and you are not inclined to anyway . goya is refusal to go very far to help ines is a good example . his refusal to commit himself further mirrors the overall tone of his part . the only characters you may momentarily feel for are ines , when she is being questioned and then tortured . after her rape by lorenzo any focus on her character is pretty much gone . following that the only other in depth involvement comes from the best scenes in the movie centered around lorenzo is invitation to supper with ines family , and her fathers all out attempt to force lorenzo into signing a bogus confession and getting his daughter back . then just as things start to be developing again , they are gone from the movie . i have read a lot of the comments here about how the movie did not know what it wanted to be , etc . i found that basically it succeeded in not being much of anything . from lorenzo is return and the coincidental viewing of ines daughter by goya , everything seemed nothing more than contrived . without much of a storyline , no effort to really examine the turbulent times they lived in took place . the essence of who goya was did not materialize , other than his actions , or lack thereof , proving him to be more self centered than anything else . as for his art , the movie seemed intent on examining a few of the paintings he did but again frittered away another opportunity in examining the reactions to this work rather than the work itself . to sum things up best , the movie had no cohesion , it grossly lacked the substance to delve into the historical environment it covered , and upon ending , left you feeling like what you would just seen was a series of aborted attempts to engage the viewer by switching from one thing to the next without adequately engaging the viewer in any of them . you cannot watch something like this several times expecting it to improve with added examination . there is just not enough to it to waste the time . it is not a case of your missing out on something , it would just be more proof that the ingredients needed to make this movie good , just are not there . as for best film ever made ? it is not even the best film i have watched today .
