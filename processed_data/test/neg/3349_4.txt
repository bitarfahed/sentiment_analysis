i saw this on a cheap dvd release with the title the entity force . since i enjoy cheesy 80 is horror films i thought i was in for a real treat . sadly the film is mostly boring and you are constantly waiting for something to happen . it does eventually get somewhat interesting , but this is in the last quarter of the film . it is a case of too little too late . when the action does happen it is not that great , what we get are dead corpses floating along the ground and chasing after the girls in the mausoleum . it is not the absolute worst i have seen , but you can do a lot better . i would only recommend this to die hard collectors of cheesy 80 is horror .
