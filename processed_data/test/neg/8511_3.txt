i was a still photographer working in europe the summer that jim salter shot the movie three . i did some swell pictures for him , one of which i was told became the poster for the movie . i did not see the film until years later . i thought it was bad . a pity , because the elements that went into it were compelling . robie porter is girlfriend was almast as beautiful as rampling herself . salter asked if i would stick around and be an extra cameraman so they would not have to shoot everything twice . i said sure , but i had to return to ny , promising i would be back . alas , i never did get back . one of life is unsung melodies . i wish i could post the pictures i made somewhere . rowland scherman
