this film was strongly recommended by a friend and , being a great fan of brando , i ordered this film from harrods and eagerly waited for it to arrive . i have seldom been so disappointed by a film . brando was good as a german officer but the rest of the film was simply unbearable . you never get to like or care for any of the characters except brando , maybe . the acting was ok . story , what story ? maybe that is the point . it is war after all , but i have seen far better war films . lawrence of arabia , apocalypse now , even star wars it was so boring i kept checking my watch . . . overall this is one of the worst films i have seen . please do not waste your time and money on this . may i also add that this is the first user comment i have written , i just had to let my feelings about this film known . by the way , my all time favourites are : casablanca , the godfather 1 2 , one flew over the cuckoo is nest , pulp fiction and amadeus . if you have not seen them yet , please spare the time to do so . and amadeus must be in widescreen 2 . 35 : 1
