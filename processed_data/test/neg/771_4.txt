someone else called this film a fable horror movie , and i think that fits pretty well . that is the concept at least . a group of teenagers , each with their own vice , catch the eye of a twisted sideshow barker . he decides to teach the teens a lesson by making them part of the freak show . a cool idea , but could have been executed better . the fate for some of the teens is shoved in your face too obviously , while other characters could have been fleshed out more . also , the ending was a serious let down . no resolution or big twist or anything . but as a low budget horror movie , it is pretty fun to watch . if you are into cheesy spookfests , you should have a laugh watching this one . i think one of it is faults though . . . that it takes itself too seriously . it is a silly movie , and if it was a little more self aware when it comes to that , i think it would have been better . but at least there is enough neat carnival themes and b movie monster makeup to keep you watching .
