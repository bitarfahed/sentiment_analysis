blame guy ritchie . the late 90s success of ritchie is clich ridden lock stock and two smoking barrels triggered a series of pitiful gangster movies from which the genre never really recovered . sadly rise of the footsoldier the true story of essex hardnut carlton leach is not likely to reverse that trend . despite a decent lead performance from hartnett , the film falls victim to all too familiar east end stereotypes . they are either busy blowing someone is brains out or shagging a scantily clad blonde . from fearsome football hooligan in the eighties to a key figure in the criminal underworld during the nineties , footsoldier charts leach is rise through the ranks of thuggery . leaving the terraces for nightclubs , leach becomes a bouncer where he is given carte blanche to kick the crap out of anyone . here he gets in with notorious gangland leaders pat tate fairbrass and tony tucker stone , and begins to realise gang warfare ai not what it is cracked up to be . there is fun to be had deconstructing writer director julian gilbey is laughable join the dots yob patois , as every sentence seems to start with an , i am gonna fackin or you fackin or , on occasion , iso then i only went an fackin , typically concluded with a mandatory caaaant ! the direction , too , smacks of sadism , especially the obvious glee gilbey gets from filming violent scenes in close up and , in the case of the bloody shotgun to the face denouement , in triplicate . director gilbey is use of the classic rise and fall gangster narrative is not what will condemn footsoldier to big screen obscurity . nor is it the fact that half way through , the film annoyingly sidelines leach in favour of the events culminating in the infamous shooting of tate and tucker . it is gilbey is sickening appetite for scatter shot violence that ruins the film ; whether it is a brick in the face or axe in the head during a vicious attack on a train , it is all unnecessarily prolonged . footsoldier does not so much pack a punch as leave you feeling violated and more importantly , robs you of two hours of your life you will not get back . . .
