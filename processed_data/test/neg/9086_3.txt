originally , the spiders was planned as a four part serial , and it shows . i dislike serials ; they are typically ridiculous , convoluted and banal . this one is no exception . in the first part of the spiders , the golden lake , a rich adventurer thwarts the plans of a gang of criminals the spiders to steal gold from incas . a silly love triangle ensues , with some cowboys and indians action . the sensationalism and exotica of it did not entertain me . one can legitimately trace themes from this two part series to lang is later , far superior work . and , the film making is better than in other serials i have seen from this time , including les vampires 1915 . in the spiders , the camera is not as static , and this film is fast paced , thankfully . the tinting and lighting are adequate , too . none of that is remarkable , though . it is nearly unseemly , however , in how closely this series resembles louis feuillade is serials ; the criminal gang dresses and behaves like those in les vampires only the names , and to a lesser extent , the situations have changed . for the further comments on the spiders series , see the web pages for it .
