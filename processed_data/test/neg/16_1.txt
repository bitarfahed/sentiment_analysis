for years , i have been a big fan of park is work and old boy is one of my all times favorite . with lots of expectation i rented this movie , only to find the worst movie i have watched in awhile . it is not a proper horror movie ; there is no suspense in it and even the light part is so lame , that i did not know whether to laugh or cry . i introduced my younger brother to chan wook park and what a disappointment he got from this . for me , an idol has fallen . if you loved movies like old boy , the mr lady vengeance or even his short films on three extremes , do not waste your time , the film is not worth it .
