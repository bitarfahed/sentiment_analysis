i wish i had something more positive to say about devil fish , but i honestly cannot seem to come up with much . i cannot even come up with many of those so bad , it is good kind of moments . devil fish is just plain old bad . the plot is completely derivative jaws , anyone ? , the acting is wooden , the characters are uninteresting , the special effects are beyond bad , and the score is annoying . add to that the seemingly inept direction of lamberto bava and you have got one stinker of a movie . i think , however , that the film is biggest sin is its lack of a budget . it does not appear that bava had much to work with . by 1984 , the italian film industry was in full decline especially as far as genre films go . the funding available to bava was most probably very meager . film is like devil fish that rely on special effects just never had a chance to be good . it is not the only italian film to suffer this fate . there are a number of italian movies made in the mid 80s whose ideas and concepts far exceeded what anyone could realistically have expected given their budgetary limitations . however , having said all that and noting the film is many weaknesses , i cannot bring myself to rate devil fish lower than a 3 10 . i have even considered rating it higher but cannot because i realize how bad a movie it is . why do not i rate it lower ?
