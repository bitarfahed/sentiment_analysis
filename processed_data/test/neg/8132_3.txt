i really wanted to like this film but it barely eked out a 3 . it is surprising that equal amounts of the votes were 10 , the other half 1 . all the characters were entertaining and even talented but as a whole the film did not pay off . sebastian hernandez is appealing ; charismatic and likable he even physically resembles marc anthony in a more approachable yet ultimately conscious sort of way . . . but even he could not save this mess of a movie . scenes dragged on far too long and points were ultimately beaten to death . the banana scene fringed on self absorption , seemingly showing how much movement was in his boxers . but in his defense it was his first movie as director that i am aware of and i appreciate his labor of love . it just left me empty despite what was trying to be said . ultimately i think the story would have been better handled by a more experienced director . from what i have seen of the extras , there is a real story behind this and , perhaps when i finish watching them , i will appreciate this attempt more .
