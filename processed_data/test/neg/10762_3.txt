though it is better than most made for tv movies , buried alive is nothing more than a run of the mill revenge tale . there are so many plot holes in this one , it makes you wonder why the screenwriters did not go through a series of re writes . the ending has a nice twist to it , but it is hardly believable . the acting by jennifer jason leigh is terrific , as always , but tim matheson hams it up with cheesy one liners that reminds one of jack nicholson in the shining . do not bother with this one .
