first of all , i would like to say that i really enjoyed this movie . however , that said , i cannot say that it was a good movie . i went into the theatre with pretty low expectations something i have learned to do . i am glad i did , because the plot and development was not stellar . the jokes were low grade humor . but , i was in the right mindset to enjoy them . i was not the only one . in fact , the entire theatre was laughing out loud . i did not hear anyone complain after the movie came to a close . i even saw one guy fall out of his chair laughing ! but again , i have to warn you , grandma is boy is not a top quality film . it is a funny albeit low brow movie , and if you go with the right mindset , you will really enjoy it . oh , and do not take kids to it .
