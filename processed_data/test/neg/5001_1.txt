i am not sure what the appeal of this movie is , but i could not find it . it is a really long , barely credible , hardly lucid conversation between three guys on one set . it does not move anywhere , the characters are just totally bizarre , the underlying plot equally so . it is lost on me , definitely a walk out movie . the one thing that keeps you from walking out is the ever unrealized possibility that it might have some kind of point or meaningful climax , and the fact that , all irritation aside at the banal personalities , they are acted quite reasonably . but you have to brace yourself for endless dialog , wishing on many an occasion that teach would just shut the up for a moment .
