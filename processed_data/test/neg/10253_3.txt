i readily admit that i watch a lot of really bad movies . but there are very few that i can think of that are quite as bad as when women had tails . it is a stinker of epic proportions . what should have been a sexy comedy about a group of cavemen discovering a woman for the first time is instead a dull , lifeless affair without a single laugh to be had . the comedy is extremely weak . i suppose if you think bashing someone in the head is funny , you might find a laugh or two . the guys in this movie make the three stooges look like high art . and there is just not enough of a plot to hold the thing together . it seems to drag on and on and on . well , you may be asking yourself , if it is as bad as you say , why have not you rated it lower than a 3 10 ? good question ! and i have got two answers . first , the movie is not without its curiosity value . i do find a bit of interest in an italian spoof of movies like one million years b . c . with raquel welch . i will give when women had tails a point for its historic value . the other two points are for the mere presence of senta berger . i know it is not much of an explanation or reasoning for a rating , but what are you going to do ? it is the best i can come up with .
