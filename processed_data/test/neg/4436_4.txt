quintet marks the only venture of both paul newman and director robert altman into the realm of science fiction . it was said of newman that he could not do comedy , but he tried until he finally scored a real success in that genre with slap shot . but the failure of this film left him gun shy and he never tried it again . this is one of the biggest downer films i have ever seen . it is a futuristic ice age , brought on by who knows what , but presumably it is a nuclear winter . even during the ice age of thousands of years ago , the equatorial parts of the earth still sustained animal and human life , but apparently not here . seals have survived and paul newman is a seal hunter on the outside . but hunters do need a little r r and newman goes to a futuristic city where things are so boring the natives have some kind of game played with six people and it is a kind of russian roulette . to win you have to kill five other participants in your game . it is a sad turn to see what man has come down to . which is one of the reasons i just could not get into this story . the atmosphere is bleak , the story is bleak , the people are bleak , it is all so bleak . no wonder this thing came up short at the box office . it is a film that just about everyone thinks is never going to be on the top ten list of paul newman films , including me . this is man is future , what a bummer .
