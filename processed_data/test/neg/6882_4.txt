i gotta admit it , i love horror films . . . especially 80s slasher films . hell , i even love cheese like sleepaway camp and night of the demons . but , i did not think much of this movie . the death scenes were not very well done , the cgi was terrible , and the acting was ho hum . worst of all was the story which did not make sense at all . i would say save your money but chances are , if you want to see this movie . . . you are going to anyway . i did not hate it . . . it is just not very good . overall , it is just another bland , lifeless horror film that lacks life it is no surprise that this one was on the shelf at dimension for over a year after it was completed .
