the plot of open graves is very simple : it is about a board game called mamba , where the players die in real life the same way they die in the game . laughable death scenes include killings via computer generated crabs and snakes . the characters are cardboard and deliberately annoying and there is not even a tiny bit of suspense . i liked eliza dushku in wrong turn , but she is completely wasted and unmemorable here . the climax with cgi witch coming from the sea is utterly laughable and stupid . the only reason to see open graves are some interesting camera angles plus sexy eliza dushku . if such movies are the future of horror then i seriously give up . give me any 70 is or 80 is low budget horror flick over this modern piece of crap . a generous 3 out of 10 .
