the tagline for this show is , he is speaking his mind . we are hiring extra lawyers . if you look back in time , any classic raunchy comedian never prided himself in being controversial . richard pryor is tagline was not , i am crude , racist and daring . that is basically how comedy central is marketing this show in your face , non pc and honest but how can a television show pride itself in being this way ? where is the humility and humbleness ? and what suddenly has made carlos mencia this huge figure for comedy central ? let is start at the beginning dave chappelle cancels his show which became unexpectedly popular and controversial and comedy central is looking around for someone new to push . they hire this guy named ned who claims to be a mexican , even though he is not . they splatter his face on a few tv ads and make it look like they are being daring by unleashing him upon the public . i have seen a lot of hateful topics on the forums for this show , and i do not agree with mencia is detractors . this is not an awful show . it had me crying in laughter a few times . when it is funny , it is very , very funny . yes , it is juvenile but so was chappelle is . the problem with carlos is that he uses a lot of the same material over and over . and he is too obvious . the overt marketing put aside , carlos has now said beaner so many times i have lost count . he is trying to make it the next famous line like i am rick james , b ! but it is way too obvious . in terms of repeating himself , carlos uses many of the same jokes over and over . for example , on one episode he said he would love it if all mexicans disappeared from america overnight . he would wake up and an american guy would be saying , room ser'vuce ! in a southern accent . he used this exact same joke verbatim when he appeared as a guest on adam carolla is talk show . it was a great deal less funny the second time around , because he seemed more desperate . is carlos funny ? i think so . there are some outrageous moments on his show . but he focuses too much on trying to be controversial rather than just going with the flow and letting his comedy naturally progress . repeating silly little catchphrases over and over again coupled with goofy faces and loud vocal screams does indeed get old quite fast . . . i just hope carlos or his writers can give a new edge to this show , because right now it is starting to dwindle in repeat hell .
