amanda bynes is an enormously talented actress , and i have really enjoyed all of her roles in the past , especially in the fantastic she is the man . for that reason alone , sydney white was a huge disappointment for me . the real reason for my dislike of this film is the poor use of characters . in a good fun teen comedy , its perfectly alright to have a cast of all fairly reality based teen characters . in sydney white , this idea is thrown out the window . amanda bynes makes a fine sydney , but she is really lacking in a lot of what usually makes her sparkle as an actress . i blame this on the script , which makes her character too bland and restrained , and on the hair makeup department , which gives her a detestable plastic look and an annoying hair style . the seven dorks that sydney eventually befriends are far worse . they are so dorky that it is impossible to like them or even respect them as characters . they are essentially repulsive gag fodder . as is the story is prince charming , who is completely unbelievable as a character and is about as cheesy as can be . perhaps the film is biggest star to be with the possible exception of amanda bynes is sara paxton , who is actually very good as the conniving rachel witchburn . it is just a shame that the script writers did not make her a bit more gray than black . the plot is basically that of snow white . what kills it is that it is too blunt and obvious a re imagining . the connections to snow white are thrown at us so much throughout the film that they are ultimately annoying and overblown . prince charming is unrealistically charming , the witch is far too despicably witchy , the seven dorks are too dorky , and sydney is just too pure of heart . now , this is not to say that the movie is all bad . though bynes is forced down by the script , she still has her moments . as does sara paxton , who brings physical comedy to the max in her scenes . sydney is room mate , named dinky is also great fun as a character . it is a somewhat funny film at parts that is ultimately just too cheesy and clich d to recommend .
