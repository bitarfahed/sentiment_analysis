i tired on several attempts to sit down and watch this program gilmore girls . it baffled me for i just could not put my finger on what this was about . was this about a young woman having a baby young in life and never growing up ? was this about the daughter being more responsible than the mother ? was this about a rebellious rich girl and her non rebellious daughter ? what the heck is this show about ? finally , i just did not care . the cast makes me want to scream . the writing is neither smart or intelligent it is syrupy and tedious . so why did i watch ? because i heard so many good things about this and i am not one to voice an opinion until i have watched . knee jerk reactions are usually wrong , so i watched a few times . the first time i watched this , i saw the mother running around like she was 12 and the daughter acting like she was 40 . maybe that is what did not attract me . i never liked any of the freaky friday films not to say this is like that , but there are some similarities . also i have a friend who watches this show every week . so i asked her , what is this show about ? a very bright young lady , usually articulate she never could give me a straight answer . so i asked others who rave about it they really do not know either . gilmore girls is turning out to be a tv program that is like an art house movie . many of us wont get it , but those that go try desperately to find a meaning where there really is not one , just to be hip . yes , i find lauren graham is lorelai annoying whine , whine , nasally whine . a whole hour of that . wow . and the rest of the cast is about as memorable as yesterday is cheese sandwich . the town is hokey , the men are wimps , the grand parents are boring , and sadly i find nothing redeeming about any of these characters or care about anything they do . it is like watching paint dry on the wall .
