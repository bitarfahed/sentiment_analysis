the power started off looking promising but soon became boring and tedious to watch . the plot is about an ancient aztec doll that takes possession of those who own it . the idea is decent enough and this film would have been fairly entertaining had it been done better . however after the first ten minutes or so it soon becomes boring ; we do not get any good death scenes and have to listen to loads of talking . at the end one of the possessed men meets his death by melting away in front of two girls , but it is not very interesting and definitely not gory . i would not recommend the power to any horror or slasher fan as there is little to be gained from it .
