eliza dushku is a very talented and beautiful actress . she manages to be the rock steady centre of tru calling but that is not enough to rescue her tv series from mediocrity . it is a real shame that a woman as attractive and talented as dushku should go from a meaty supporting role in buffy the vampire slayer to this clunker . unoriginal and desperately trying to be hip , tru calling fails to excite on any level above hormonal . the eponymous heroine spends a lot of her time running hither and yon across what must be a very small city , in order to avert the deaths of good looking corpses to be that she is already met in the mortuary where she works . despite all the running she does , she always arrives looking like she is just stepped out of a portable air conditioned dressing room . in every episode , eliza dushku and the rest of the cast struggle to breath life into the bland , characterless screenplays but it is a pointless exercise . tru calling just lies on the slab , gazing lifelessly at the ceiling .
