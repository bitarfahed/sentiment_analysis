apparently re cut episodes from the gangbusters tv show on the big screen . while this was frequently done in the 50 is and 60 is because people did not have a tv or a color tv and producers wanted an increased return on their investment big screen ticket sales or if it went to the small screen resale of a series that is not in syndication , the results were usually less then the sum of their parts . the only time i have ever seen it work were where multi part stories were put together ala rocky jones or man from uncle or in the case of horror anthology the veil and 13 demon street . here the effect is to have stories of american criminals in the 20 is and 30 is dillinger , pretty boy floyd , bonnie and clyde , etc inter cut with each other as a narrator talks about how the fbi hunted them down . its a weird concoction that does not quite work because its clear that there are things here that do not belong together . more than once i looked at the tv oddly because things did not seem right . in fairness i will not describe the cheapness of the production since this was what early tv and the series was like . its not bad , but its not very good either . to be perfectly honest the episodes of the series that i have seen work better a single episodes where we are not expecting as much . given the choice i would rent dvds of the show instead of this movie .
