rossini once described rival composer wagner is work as having some wonderful moments . . . and some awful quarter hours . inuyasha , it seems , can also be described this way . it has many great episodes , but in between them are countless filler episodes . the entire series consists of about 175 episodes , of which i would say at least 125 are filler or some sort of subplot i did not bother to count and i would not be surprised if that number were in fact higher , though . some of these filler episodes are actually quite enjoyable , though many are quite silly or dull . nonetheless , the constant digressions start to wear thin after the first few seasons as the plot ends up progressing at a labored crawl for most of the series . character development , too , slows down greatly and by the later seasons , the cast has become quite unchanging , resulting in increasingly stale jokes particularly those concerning the monk , who is ironic traits start out as mildly humorous but grow tiresome when the jokes associated with them appear repeatedly . however , all of that is not to say that inuyasha is a bad series . it just is not a great series the way neon genesis evangelion , for example , is generally considered to be . as something to watch at the end of a hard day , it is nice , but it could never be confused with high art .
