this movie is dated in so many ways , it is sensational . you can either laugh at it or shake your fists in rage . this movie deals with many problems of american history , but with the typical white male christian american paternalism : the main character is one of those i can do it all and you have got to love me for it kind of guy . he is so pompous and not to be taken serious at any time . what a horrid creature ! his wife is a weak little woman for the first part of the movie and a still very weepy , but stronger character in the end . too bad she still forgives his cocky ways after he is left her for the second time . it is just sad that the character did not really change at all . even though she is supposed to portray a strong and independent woman in the end , she is consumed by worry about her adventure seeking husband . so 2 out of 10 points from the feminists among us and those are only for the good intentions . . . ! the problem of indian suppression is dealt with quite nicely , but there is that patronizing story telling again . and the fact that all african american characters are the typical stereotypes makes the movie even more hypocritical . i was so enraged most of the time ! so one point for trying from the civil rights movement . i know , that the movie was done at a different time . i love old movies and i have a lot of patience with some of those dated point of views . but this was just disgusting ! what saves this movie are the parts without the main character . mr dix is acting is way over the top and smug . maybe that is why his character is so disagreeable . . . i liked mrs dunne is performance , even though her character enraged me at times . but i must say the famous land rush scene was incredibly done . and the way that the oklahoma settlement was portrayed made the movie worthwhile . it is just amazing how civilization rose out of the dirt and dust of the 19th century . and i do not understand the problem some have with that church scene . i thought it was quite funny and amusing . maybe it was not supposed to be , but i liked it . so 9 points for those scenes and the impressive storytelling of the development of the oklahoma state . that makes about 4 points altogether !
