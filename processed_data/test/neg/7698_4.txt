these days , ridley scott is one of the top directors and producers and can command huge sums to helm movies especially since he has films like alien , gladiator and blade runner to his credit . so from this partial list of his credits , it is obvious he is an amazing talent . however , if you watch this very early effort that he made while in film school , you would probably have a hard time telling that he was destined for greatness . that is because although it has some nice camera work and style , the film is hopelessly dull and uninvolving . however , considering that it was not meant for general release and it was only a training ground , then i am disposed to looking at it charitably hence the score of 4 . by the way , this film is part of the cinema 16 : european shorts dvd . on this dvd are 16 shorts . most are not great , though because it contains the man without a head , copy shop , rabbit and wasp , it is an amazing dvd for lovers of short films and well worth buying .
