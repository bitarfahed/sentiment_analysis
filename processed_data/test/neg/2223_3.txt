compared to director kevin connor is later arabian adventure , this is a masterpiece . however , that is not saying much . in fact , at the earth is core is a silly fantasy adventure in which peter cushing who appears to be on something strong and some other actor whom i do not know use a giant digging machine called the iron mole dig their way down to the earth is core only to find that the inside of the earth is pink and populated by ape like creatures who have enslaved the humans . there is also a giant bird that controls the apes by means of telepathy , and we get to see it blink its eyes in closeup throughout the film . most importantly , cushing and what is his name also encounter the lovely caroline munro in the subterranean caves . and here is what i have really got against this little flick : why oh why is it that whenever munro is in a movie , she only gets approx . 5 minutes of screen time ? in the spy who loved me , maniac , dracula ad 1972 , golden voyage of sinbad always she is in the background ! how can kevin connor possibly think that we would rather listen to peter cushing is fake accent and look at some ridiculous ape man whose voice sounds like a scratched cd than gaze at the beautiful munro ? i do not get it . please , somebody direct a movie where this english brunette is on screen all the time !
