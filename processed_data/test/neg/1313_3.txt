flood is a prime example of how throwing good actors and cgi at a film will do little to compensate for a rubbish script . the basic premise is fine : what if a freak storm threatened to send the sea straight over the thames flood barriers and engulf london so fast that most of the inhabitants would probably never get out in time ? it is basically the new york segment of the day after tomorrow , but that should not make it any less of a film . however , the script just is not there . it is merely functional , flat , and lacking in depth . great british talents like robert carlysle and david suchet to name but two do their level best with what they have got , but their characters are two dimensional cyphers , like something out of an old marvel comic . and it would be frankly easier to turn back the tide . not that every actor gets let off the hook tom courtenay seemed capable of only one emotion throughout the film , but then he was not given much of a challenge . i applaud any opportunity to see some non hollywood disaster flicks for a change , and i do not expect zillions of dollars spent on rendering ultra realistic graphics . however there is no excuse for shonky writing especially from a country that has produced some of the best science fiction ever made on next to no budget at all . this is the kind of half hearted b grade fluff the sci fi channel produces , and that is hardly a target to aim for . if like me you are such a fan of disaster films you are still tempted , do yourself a favour and watch it with some friends . better still ; do not bother .
