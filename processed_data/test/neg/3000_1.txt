this is the most boring , pretentious , and stupid film i have seen in a long time . i saw it at the academy in beverly hills , and there were quite a few people in the lobby who had left the screening and were seeking refuge there . all were solemnly shaking their heads and looking as though they had been to a funeral . what a waste of time and money . even worse are the critics who gave this pretentious blimp good reviews . what is with them ? are they just afraid they will not be considered hip ? were they bribed ? this film is staggeringly bad . do not take a date to it expecting to have an in depth chat at the cheesecake factory afterwards . if going to see this film was your idea , she will browbeat you and hold it over you the rest of your life .
