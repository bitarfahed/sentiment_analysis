return of the seven has a few good action scenes , and elmer bernstein is score is as rousing as ever . nevertheless , it is a boring film , because it simply fails to involve us emotionally . mcqueen is absence makes a really bad impression , and the fact that his character here is played by a different little known actor is odd in a bad way . the characters are not developed , so we do not connect with them and we hardly care when some of them die . this sequel is a passable but poor imitation of the original .
