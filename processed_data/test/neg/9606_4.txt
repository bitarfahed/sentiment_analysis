i tried to give this show a chance , but it really does not sit well with me . although the performances are good , the writing is not . the two oldest step siblings , derek and casey , are equally annoying ; i get the impression that we are supposed to side with casey , given that she is the protagonist , but i do not find her at all likable . the parents are continually portrayed as utterly clueless . the three younger children are the most watchable things on the show ; lizzie and edwin are sweet the way they team up , and marti is adorable . the plot lines are a bit far fetched and the whole premise is mostly hard to swallow blended families are common , yes , but this family is not really trying all that hard to blend . on the whole , i think it grades thumbs down .
