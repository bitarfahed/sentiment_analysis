i am trying to understand what people liked about mirrormask . i am an avid film viewer and hobbyist film maker . as i was telling friends during my lunch hour , mirrormask may well be my biggest movie disappointment of the year . just like the short moongirl , the film missed its marks . several times during the movie it made attempts at humor . it sets you up for the laugh . instead of making you laugh , it leaves you feeling empty . the jokes reminded one of the recent star wars films . they were not funny unless you were five . and the acting felt similarly terrible . i have seen actors actually act in front of a blue screen . and i have believed it . but not in this film . not for a second . this film takes a formula and tries to apply it with pretty artwork and though the script is totally workable and the special effects quite beautiful , it has no heart to it and fails miserably . i left the film shaking my head and considered leaving the theater . i felt hallow and miserable and still have not gotten the sour taste out of my mouth from it . i love independent film . i encourage people to view independent films to support them . but not this film . this film should not have been made . at least , not like this . why did the director miss the marks so clearly ? they were clearly setup not just the humor . but the emotions . the drama . even the lines were poorly timed and delivered . it was like the film walked on three legs instead of four . its steps are awkward and miss timed . and it could fall over with the slightest push do not see this film . i do not care who you are . it is not worth your time .
