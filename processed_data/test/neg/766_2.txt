tom and jerry are transporting goods via airplane to africa . but being white men , they are worried they will not be safe , so they put on blackface to fit in . once they are wearing it , they adopt black dialect and fully inhabit their new characters . they crash into the ocean and use the wing of the plane as a raft . before reaching land , they suffer the advances of an over affectionate octopus and more serious danger from sharks , a swordfish and a whale . once on land , they are frightened by fantastic creatures , and duck into a cave . inside it is even worse when they encounter living skeletons in blackface . and upon exiting the cave , things are even worse than that when they are discovered by cannibals . plane dumb is an especially sloppy effort from van beuren studios . one example : a lion , unknown to tom and jerry , enters the cave before they do . but the animators must have forgotten about it , because the lion never appears again . another example is the ending that is not an ending : it is just an arbitrary stop . according to a youtube poster , the cartoon was originally intended to feature the voices and caricatures of a popular negro comedy team known as miller lyles . but aubrey lyles died of tuberculosis before the recording session was completed , and co directors john foster george rufle were forced to rework the animation into a tom jerry story . they should not have bothered . the crude animation and poorly executed gags make the film a loser from beginning to end .
