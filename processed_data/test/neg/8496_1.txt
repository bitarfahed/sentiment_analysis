mr . kennedy should stop experimenting with bad movie scripts . what was he thinking ? this is a movie that should not have passed the hey , i have got an idea , let is make a sequel stage of inception . if there was a zero rating , i would give it , but i guess i will settle for a generous 1 . it seems these days that if there is a buck to be made , movie execs will dig up an old hit and run it by a set of writers and see what turns up . hey , i said hit and run ! kinda describes how i felt when this movie ended ! how this piece of trash ever saw the light of day is beyond me . it is filled with unpleasant humor , strange animation and jokes that do not quite take you anywhere besides a state of confusion . if you are being dragged to this movie , and someone is paying for you . . . . fine . . . . but its still going to be more painful than a brick in the forehead . however , if you are planning on paying your own hard earned money , search out a better alternative .
