i want to say i liked this film , i really do , but when it boils down to it it was just far too boring . it starts off looking promising , and even once they get inside the mines the creepy atmosphere and mood is great . the problem is the monster , or lack of it . as people have said it is a stop motion monster , but that is not the problem stop motion can look great if done properly . the problem is that we do not see enough of it and we do not get to see any deaths . this is meant to be a horror film so a lack of monster and a lack of deaths equals boredom . there were some scenes i liked , such as when one girl is using her camera in the dark and the monster is coming towards her . there are also a few other creepy scenes but they are far too few to hold your interest and definitely not worth watching the film for . it is a shame , because i can see that had it been handled correctly it would have been an effectively creepy horror flick . the saddest thing is that films like this will never be made again , because film makers over polish their films these days and rely on crappy cgi effects .
