mr . moto is gamble has a fairly straight forward plot when a boxer is murdered in the ring with a mysterious poison , it is up to the even more mysterious mr . moto to solve the case . i am shocked at the number of positive reviews for mr . moto is gamble on imdb . because to me . . . well , i found it extremely disappointing . i enjoy mr . moto and i enjoy charlie chan , but i cannot say i cared for this mish mash of the two . for those unfamiliar with the story behind mr . moto is gamble , it was originally intended to be a charlie chan film . but when warner oland backed out , some of the scenes and action were rewritten for peter lorre and mr . moto . as i indicated , the end result left me underwhelmed . mr . moto is not chan . he is more mysterious , he is more athletic , and he is more exotic . so trying to put moto in a chan film is like trying to fit a square peg into a round hole it does not work . and listening to lorre moto try to deliver one of chan is trademark euphemisms just ends up sounding silly . add to that the fact that almost 10 minutes of the already brief 72 minute runtime is made up of boxing scenes something that i never seem to enjoy and you end up with a movie that i could not help but dislike . if i have to say something positive i would point to the performance of keye luke . but even he is not near enough to save mr . moto is gamble in my eyes . sorry , but a 4 10 is about the best i can give this one .
