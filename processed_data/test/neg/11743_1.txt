a horrendous film , ill conceived and crude . the acting of anne heche and vince vaughan is so inferior to that of perkins and leigh in the original version they have to be seen to be believed . there was no reason to make this picture , which only highlights how accomplished and brilliant hitchcock was , and how inimitable . also , there is a creeping , pervasive insensitivity in the film that is not there in the first film . hitchcock is psycho was scary and shocking , but one could genuinely feel for all concerned , even the pitiful norman bates . there were moments of pathos , irony and fey humor the remake does not have . one of the best things about hitchcock is film was its incredible and intuitive depth and sense of nuance , of when to cut away and when to show something , on whether to use a close up or long shot , on whether to make an actor sympathetic and when to make him frightening , and so forth . the remake has none of these qualities and does not even try for them . it is an idiotic exercise that i am amazed even got released .
