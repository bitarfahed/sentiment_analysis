so it is a space movie . but it is low budget . you ask , what about the effects ? the effects are at times good , and at times really , really bad . i mean bad . and notice i started with the effects . there is a story here , but it is told in what i think is the wrong order . i do not mean a tarantino style wrong order . i mean , it is told in a completely nonsensical arrangement . most of it is about a mother in the future , because you know , it is sci fi as told by her daughter , which is mostly exposition done in narrative from the daughter is perspective . only once you are through the first hour and hear paul darrow is voice as a computer do you realize how much more tolerable the constant narrative would have been if he would read it . this narrative is so constant and inclusive , that the actors on screen hardly say a word for the first hour . there is also a lesson here for you up and coming filmmakers : if you are not doing 2001 and want to have some action this one does , then please hire a good fight choreographer . otherwise , your fights will look like , well , what is in battlespace . and notice the title has the word battle in it . ugh . i think this might be the classic scenario of trying to make a movie based on nothing more than a concept . and some effects . my biggest surprise is seeing the imdb listing this film as costing 1 . 8 million . when you compare it to something like primer , which did better with a budget of a few thousand , you realize in low budget film making , it is all about the story . i was not expecting much but i was still disappointed . two out of ten stars .
