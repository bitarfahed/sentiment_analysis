i did not expect too much from this movie , but i was still disappointed . it is supposed to be a comedy , but there are only four or five scenes where i actually laughed , and i think that is rather poor . there is no real plot either , i always had the feeling that most of the scenes could have been put anywhere in the movie because there is no connection between them . but the worst thing was the acting of kim franke . he has one facial expression during the whole movie , as if he was supposed to play some retarded guy , but i believe that is not the case . all in all i rate it four out of ten , i have seen worse movies than this , but i would not spend money on this one again .
