here is a spoof that is guaranteed to entertain folks in the iq range of homer simpson . it is a cheap shot at every great superhero , notably spiderman and batman . but it does not end there ; it gets progressively worse until it disintegrates into a pathetic ensemble of slapstick trollop by the truckload . for those interested in the plot , you have only to watch spiderman while under the influence of some heavy narcotic . what you get is dragonfly man a boy wonder , who loves the girl next door , suddenly inheriting some stupendous super powers . the rest of the script is as predictable as waking up in the morning and brushing your teeth , but do not take my word for it ! for kids , this is somewhat amusing ; for adults it is a great film to drop the kids off for .
