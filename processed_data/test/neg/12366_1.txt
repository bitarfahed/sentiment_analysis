i hope this is not a portent of things to come . high definition camcorders are getting cheaper all the time although i would not swear that is what was used here , so it is open season for all the wannabe scorseses and tarantinos . there is no hiding the cheapness of this stinker , and calling it a film would be doing the industry a big disservice . the photography is of a standard you would expect on a family outing to the zoo . i could build me a new house with all the wooden acting . what is remarkable about that is that nobody stands out as the worst . they are all equally terrible . like a whole bunch of ben afflecks . or steven seagals . what hooked me was the title . i am a sucker for this sort of thing , like frankenhooker , or monsturd . frankenhooker was pretty bad , too , but at least i got some laughs out of it , and the acting was merely bad , not awful . i cannot comment on monsturd as i have yet to get hold of a copy of it . anyway , i hope the people who made this did not make any money from it . else they might be encouraged to try it on again . please , guys , pawn the camcorder and go back to your regular job .
