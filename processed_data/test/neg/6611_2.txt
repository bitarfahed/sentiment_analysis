when just days away before the film is premiere , its screenwriter backs out of the project , and demands his name to be removed from the credits , you know that this does not bode well . the books , on which wiedzmin is based on , have great potential for a vivacious fantasy film geralt de rivia is a witcher ; his sole purpose is to destroy the monsters that plague the world . but not everything monstrous looking is evil , and not everything fair is good but all that did not matter , when a polish film crew , with a low budget , and no , or little appreciation for sapkowski is work , decided to make a 13 part mini series out of it . the two hour film is a by product of their actions , to maximize the profits . it is not hard to point out in this case , what makes people label this film as bad . plot incoherent , thanks to cramming it with too many stories , acting below average , with exception of zebrowski , dialogues bland , editing choppy , special effects unbearable and choreography poor , add up to the film is overall bad experience . only the soundtrack , done by grzegorz ciechowski , brings out the beauty of the world of wiedzmin , which was never brought to the screen . even poland is grandiose flora and fauna where part of the chronicles of narnia : the lion , the witch and the wardrobe was shot , a crucial element in almost any fantasy film , were not used up to their full potential . the more i think about this dead loss , the more i am running out of words . at least , it is a comfort to know , that marek brodzki , the director of wiedzmin , has directed only one film ever since in addition , in germany . for now , we are left with top notch fantasy stories and a fantastic computer rpg released in 2007 referring to the wiedzmin books . but i am sure that one day , justice will be done for the wiedzmin saga , and we will be treated with an equally good film adaptation soon enough .
