grande ecole is not an artful exploration of mixed sexuality but , if you are in need of it , a movie for an x rated channel . although i suspect there is nothing in this movie to spoil for a willing viewer , the plot is simply an excuse for male to female and male to male couplings set in the unconvincing context of a competition between a parisian school for future ceos and a major school for those seeking higher degrees in the liberal arts . there is likewise a frisson of cultural clash between high status and lower status french youth , plus a societal conflict involving native frenchmen and arab immigrants from north africa . all that is missing is a female to female coupling , which could easily have been arranged with no more than a slight twist in the plot . the acting is at a somewhat higher level than in the usual pornographic movie but grande ecole is , to be blunt about it , no more than pornography with artistic aspirations . i am not offended by the sex . it is just repetitive and , before long , boring . where is the hays office when you really need it ?
