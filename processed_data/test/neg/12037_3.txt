some movies you watch and you say , well , that made no sense . and you do not really mean it . you are just saying things were overly complicated or slightly nonsensical . bread and circus makes no sense at all . and i mean it . and that is not because it is surreal . from the start , it is pretty clear it is a feeble excuse to do splatter special effects . there is no script . there is no plot . there is no story of any kind . one event does not lead to the next that is how fundamental the bad writing is here . so what ? i mean , there are tons of movies out there that fall into that category . they want to show you gore , they give you gore . why even talk about it ? because , in this case , bread and circus gave me hope . okay , there are some surreal elements . vaginas , in the ground people crawling out of them . the earth , in space , two legs on either side . these sorts of images are wonderful , fun , odd , crazy . but the movie does not do anything with them . stuff happens , the movie ends , and it is all very unsatisfying . i suspect the script was made up on the fly . too bad . if there had been a story of any kind at all , it would have made for a much more entertaining film . the film is very much like the beautiful car you would love to own . then you lift up the hood and there is no engine . just a small man peddling a bicycle . grr !
