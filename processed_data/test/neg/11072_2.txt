what a shocker . for starters , i could not stand the constant screaming and noisy panicking all the time . it did not make me scared , horrified , or make me sympathetic towards the characters ; it was simply annoying . the jerky camera movements were also annoying . the plot was the same as pretty much every other cheap horror . there was a few pathetic attempts to give the characters some depth , but it did not really work into the rest of the plot . and then there is the ending . i am still not really sure what to make of it . i guess it was supposed to be clever twist , then shed some light on the situation , but it was just stupid . the case had a couple of those little award winner nominations symbols on it , so i figured it could not be too bad . i was wrong . if you see it , you should probably just leave it on the shelf .
