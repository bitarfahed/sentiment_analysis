the adventures of sebastian cole is about a boy named sebastian adrian grenier who fancies himself becoming a writer at some point , given he actually puts effort into it . this movie is presumably the years where he gets his material for writing , the adventure years , hence the title and the previous quote . in it we experience the very typical coming of age stories and warnings of loves , drugs , and sex . . . changes . yeah , there is a slight twist here that is very interesting , and that is that sebastian is step dad clark gregg very early on makes a rough decision to get a sex change that has a huge impact on sebastian is family and his relationship with his step dad . clark gregg plays hank henrietta , sebastian is step father and is very good in the part , very believable without being over the top , which is a route this film could have taken rather easily . thankfully they did not . adrien grenier , who i am only familiar with from entourage , is also very good in his part as sebastian , and together , he and gregg have a great relationship on screen . it is always quite engaging to watch these guys ? relationship as it develops and is genuinely heartbreaking at times . and that is the best of what this film has to offer . unfortunately , it brings with it some mediocre camera work , direction , and cinematography . it is not bad , but it is a far cry from being good , or memorable in the slightest . the characters are also thinly written , and it is clear from the get go how most of the arcs will pan out . the only truly fascinating character through and through is clark gregg is hank henrietta . i have already said grenier did well acting wise , but the character of sebastian is not only not engaging , but is completely unlikeable . i do not honestly see why anyone in the audience would route for his character in anything he does . he mopes , whines , cheats , lies , and lacks any aspirations other than to be a complete slacker . it would be different if he was maybe a side character or comic relief , but to have him as the main focus and to be asked to take the character seriously ? come on . and i do not really hold it against this film , but i just want to say . . . pick a different song in all these films , hollywood ! no more where is my mind by the pixies , we all know it is a good song , stop using it in every other film ! i feel like i could just keep tearing more and more of this film apart , but in all honesty i did not hate it . i just did not really care for it , and i would not recommend it to anyone . the adventures of sebastian cole is not a bad or boring film , it is just not a very good or engaging one either . it is very uneven and the script could have used quite a bit of work . i guess the point of the film is to be a loose sort of look at the life of a writer before he made it , and it worked . . . if that writer put out pieces of fiction that i would not want to read .
