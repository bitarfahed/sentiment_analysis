the dune miniseries opens with a flashforward montage of action sequences . the realisation quickly dawns that these are the best scenes out of the 265 minute running time , and they are not good . not good at all . oh dear . but let us not judge a book by its cover even though that is exactly what we are being invited to do . let is give dune a chance to redeem itself . well , here is the expected watery opening on caladan . but who is this petulant , strangely ugly man ? paul atreides ? this is paul atreides ? this generic plastic puppet ? and why does he look so old ? what is that ? the actor is only 25 ? well , he does not look it , and that is way too old anyway . but at least he has charisma , right ? wrong . alec newman is a stumbling , mumbling buffoon . i am picturing him being discovered sitting in the dark in a remedial acting class because nobody liked him enough to tell him the class was over , and he is just too dumb to realise it . when your paul atreides has all the screen prescence of soggy toast , and an acting range from petulant to blank your production of dune is doomed from the start . the other actors take pity on poor alec though , and give uniformly insipid and incomprehensible performances so that he does not look too bad by comparison . at least , i assume that is what they are doing . because i am charitable , you see . to be fair , they are clearly being given no direction at all . random gestures , blank or inconsistent deliveries , missing their marks , it is all here . this is like a master class in how not to do it . and sure , there are more elements of the book in this miniseries than there are in the 1984 movie , but there are not twice as many , because of all the . pauses . to fill . time . but we can forgive all this because of the small budget of 20 million , or only 5 million per hour . nobody could be expected make quality science fiction on that sort of budget . except perhaps stargate sg 1 which makes do with 1 . 4 million per 50 minute episode , or farscape at 2 million . and frankly i would rather watch four episodes of either of those while being punched in the kidneys , than have to sit though the travesty that is dune the miniseries again .
