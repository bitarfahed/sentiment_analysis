early talkie feature based on a popular stage play . a murder has been committed and a bunch of people hire a medium to conduct a s ance to see who the murderer is . while the lights are out there is another murder . . . so it is someone in that room . what follows is an obvious , dull and creaky murder mystery . most of the cast overacts to a ridiculous degree . they act like they are on stage where you have to overdo things and it looks silly on screen . most embarrassing is bela lugosi two years before dracula who really overdoes it as a police inspector . static direction by tod browning who was always overrated does not help . for browning and lugosi completists only . i give it a 3 .
