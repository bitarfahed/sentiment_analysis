throughout this movie i kept thinking why on earth did they make this as a documentary , yet not include real footage of the people who were interviewed ? sure , it would have been just like any other documentary , but then it would have been up to the film makers to find the meaning for the movie to deliver . using a host of well known movie stars many of whom apparently asked to be in it to portray real people gave me the feeling that there was a pre determined message to be delivered , and the director was so intent on it that real people could not be trusted so actors and rehearsed scenes were used . yes , i know this was also a play , but a documentary should be a documentary . i really found myself getting put off by the various stars , and kept expecting one of them to drop character for a moment and say i am a good person because i am in this . this movie could have had a much more powerful social commentary had it been more objective or let us see matthew shepard and his murderers as people rather than symbols . the much superior boys do not cry had an unflinching view of those involved good and bad . instead the laramie project gives an almost relentless lecture that someone is sexuality should be accepted regardless , and little else .
