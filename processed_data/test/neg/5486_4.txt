i have now seen quite a few films by pedro almod var , but this would have to be the most disappointing so far . this film seemed to lack the zaniness that is usually everywhere in his films , and the story just never got me interested . many almod var regulars appear in this film , so it is not like there was a lack of on screen talent , but this film just seemed more serious than his other films . if there was a comedic edge to this movie , i certainly could not find it , and it made for one surprisingly weak movie .
