your ability to enjoy the ashes of time may depend on our expectations before stepping into the theater . even its most strident supporters seem to agree that audiences can be split right up the middle in their appreciation of this unique film . unlike most hk actioners , the battle scenes are curiously kept at a distance . when they do happen , they are rendered in a jerky style in which it is difficult to make out exactly what is occurring on screen . the dramatic scenes can be extravagantly beautiful , with the of maggie cheung , brigitte lin , and a roll call of hk is top acting talent chewing up the scenery . as with some of wong kar wai is early work , the dialog could be more precise . in short , the ashes of time requires a forgiving attitude . released around the same time as wong kar wai is spectacularly successful chungking express , it is clear that the director is not as confident working with the elements of the martial arts film . anyone looking for tense action is likely to be disappointed . but those intrigued by the director is aesthetic will likely find this a unique experience at the very least .
