graduation day is a result of the success of friday the 13th . both of those films are about creative , bloody murders , rather than suspense . if you enjoy that type of film , i would recommend graduation day . if not , i would not . there is nothing new here , just the same old killings . even though i have given the film a 4 out of 10 , i will say that it is not a repulsive film . it is watchable if your curious about it , just not creative .
