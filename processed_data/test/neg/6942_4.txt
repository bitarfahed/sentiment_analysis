marlene dietrich is magnificent as concha perez , the temptress who drives all men to despair in josef von sternberg is the devil is a woman . her performance has nothing to do with acting in any conventional sense but she is incandescent , a true star in full command of her material . but she is the only good thing about the film , she is so good she keeps you watching this dross ; i doubt if any other actress could have done the same . the story is an appallingly lame melodrama , surely pierre louys novel was better , in which dietrich is amoral concha ruins the lives of two friends played by lionel atwill and cesar romero with a stiffness bordering on petrification . and for von sternberg it is an ugly looking movie , maybe in keeping with its fairly ugly subject matter . bunuel , on the other hand , thought enough of it to remake it as that obscure object of desire with a greater emphasis on the surrealist aspects of it all , down to casting two different actresses in the same role . neither film is either director is finest hour and while the bunuel version may be the better film , it is dietrich is performance you remember .
