being a history buff , i rented this movie because of the subject matter . the idea of the ellis island experience at the turn of the century focusing on one small group is intriguing . unfortunately , the movie falls flat . much of the story is simply boring ; nothing much happens for long stretches . the director uses goofy imagery offered up in the form of daydream sequences in an apparent attempt to break up the glacial pacing , but instead , it clashes with the authentic look and feel of the movie . the characters are also poorly drawn . in the end , we do not really care as much about them as we should . it is a shame that this was not what it could have been . i would still like to see a good movie about the american immigrant experience , but this one is not it .
