i have watched this film a few times and i never really liked it . i am not a fan so termed nu horror , so i cannot be dismissed on that account . i found it a little sleazy , i think the thing that irks me about hammers is the sexploitation aspect , not sex mind you , but sexploitation . i am surprised at how many people have rated this film so highly , so i will have another look at it . but for me , it was not creepy , it had no atmosphere , just a bit of omigod , look at those bad stupid london prostitute women and that little innocent anna about to get raped by yet another nasty man , ooherh ! i suppose ultimately for me , the film had no depth whatsoever , just a bunch of nasty priggish men and women only there for the titillation value . this no doubt was to some degree what victorian england was like , but the sets are even too clunky and dull impart a sense of victorian menace . roger corman is horrors on the other hand , now that is style , atmosphere and elegant horror . for me , this was just exploitative , flat footed trash .
