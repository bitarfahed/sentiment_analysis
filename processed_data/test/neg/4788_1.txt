with the plethora of repetitive and derivative sitcoms jamming fall , summer , winter and spring line ups , it is nice to see a show that sets itself from the lot in more than one area . earl takes an unusual approach . it is not about the daily musings of an eccentric family zzzz . . nor about the other boring stuff you see everywhere in sitcoms . the show is about this small time white trash thief earl who scratches off a lottery card and scores big time . right at that moment , karma took it away from him . overtime , he learns that that unusual incident was probably because of all the bad things he is been doing , so he sets off on a mission to right every wrong he ever did and he is got all his deeds on a paper . this is a brilliant premise for a sitcom . thankfully , it landed in the right hands . the execution of the show produces extremely satisfactory results : you get an innovative comedy that is genuinely funny and really touching at many times . you cannot help but fall in love with earl is sincerity and steadfastness , randy is simple mind , good heart and observations on life , joy is wild , flamboyant personality and darnell is mellow , chillin demeanor that really endears him to you very easily . when you combine the show is innovation with its genuine humor , good heart , interesting characters and well written dialogue , you really have a keeper . with shows like this and the incomparable the office , nbc is obviously on to something . did they finally free a cubicle or two for quality assurance ? let is hope so . and let is hope for more quality shows like these will occupy the line ups ; shows that will make both us tv viewers and nbc executives stop crying over the long gone days of nbc is golden days frasier , seinfeld , friends
