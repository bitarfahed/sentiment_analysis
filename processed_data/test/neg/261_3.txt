when i read the reviews of kahin pyaar na ho jaaye , i thought , huh ? . it was that confusing . to be sure , i went to watch the film and what do you know ? it is a remake of the wedding singer . several scenes have been changed to suit the whole essence of indianness , but the rest of it is a direct lift from the 1998 hollywood hit . bollywood is no stranger to remakes , but this is one so poor that it pains me just to watch it . i groaned so much watching this and i realized i was not the only one doing so ! one guy actually walked out of the theater and never came back ! salman khan should seriously stop doing comedy roles . he shrieks and whines too much . why cannot he just take it easy ? he does not do justice to the role originally acted out by adam sandler . he does not have sandler is sense of comic timing . rani is a wonderful actress and one of my favorites , but she is no drew barrymore either . the scene where she stands in front of a mirror practicing to say her new surname hi , i am mrs pugalia does not match up to barrymore is version hi , i am mrs julia gulia . i felt embarrassed watching that scene , even though i had loved the original . the music is not too bad . it is probably the only saving grace of this otherwise horrible film ! avoid this at all cost !
