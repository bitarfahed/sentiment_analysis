problem child was an okay movie , but did it really merit a sequel ? i do not think it did . the original movie is only redeeming asset was gilbert gottfried , and he was not even good in this sequel . i cannot really put my finger on why this movie was bad . for starters , it just was not funny . even when i saw this as a nine year old , i did not sympathize with junior michael oliver at all . his character came off to me as whiny , self loathing , and perhaps most importantly , a rebel without a clue . he appeared to hate every woman that his father ben john ritter dated for the sole sake of hating them . it also does not send a good message to kids with divorced parents who constitute over half children in the u . s . these days when the one woman ben decides to almost marry is a southern aristocrat who is vindictive and who happens to hate children as it is . and as cool as i thought it would have been to see original snl cast mate laraine newman come back to the big screen , she could not even save this movie . i also found it strange that she was a white southern d butante whose name was lawanda . that sounds more like an african american woman is name . but of course , that has nothing to do with why i disliked this movie . i think the movie did not work because you had antagonists you were supposed to hate , along with protagonists you were not supposed to hate . john ritter is character was supposed to be a good parent who tried desperately to teach his child right from wrong without conforming to authoritative parenting . instead , he came off not only as a wimpy parent , but also one who was desperate to find a wife in a matter of days , regardless of how well he knew the woman . did i mention this sends a bad message to children of divorced parents ? in a nutshell , the rest of the things that went wrong with this movie included amy yasbeck unnecessary and unexplained return to play an entirely different character , that young girl who was even more obnoxious than junior , completely uncalled for toilet humor , and even more outrageous and outdated homophobic humor involving the dog catchers . the movie was just a mess , and really does not deserve a dvd release if it has not been given one already . it should just rot on vhs along with all the other bad , forgettable 90 is comedies .
