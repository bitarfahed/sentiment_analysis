i think the bone snatcher should go after anyone associated with this movie . watching this will seem like the longest 90 minutes or so of your entire life . the plot is boring and stupid . there were no scenes that were horrifying , even remotely . if you manage to endure this fine piece of cinema art all the way to the end , you are either going to be highly disappointed or die laughing hysterically . i bought this movie based on some other reviews i would read . i wish i had my money back . what a skunker . if you are looking for a horror movie that will hold your interest , watch u turn . it may be based on a kooky plot , but it is full of those creepy scenes that keep you jumping from beginning to end .
