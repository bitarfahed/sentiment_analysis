there is a certain irony in a parody of the gothic genre being turned into a mess of clich s by filmmakers who either had no idea what the story is purpose was , or just did not care . all of the hallmarks of your average family film are present rambunctious younger siblings , a grumpy teenager who does not want to move , unsympathetic parents who are unable to see the apparition , and of course a romantic subplot . the movie has very little in common with wilde is original story , which was largely written to poke fun at the melodramatic gothic novellas that were all the rage at the time . if wilde saw this version , he would probably laugh and then of course , write a parody . one can only hope that the children who watched this bland , mass produced pap eventually discovered the wit and sparkle of the original version .
