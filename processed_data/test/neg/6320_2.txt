in the opening sequence of where is poppa ? , george segal rises from his bed one morning , shaves , showers , puts on a gorilla suit and goes into his mother is bedroom , we realize later , to give her a massive heart attack that will kill her and get her out of his life forever . this is about the level of humor one can expect from most of this picture : insanity , blended with what might be taken as morbid daring . segal plays a new york attorney who lives with his supposedly senile mother ruth gordon , whose life is further complicated when , while hiring a nurse to care for the old bag , meets the girl of his dreams , the pleasantly prim trish van devere , decked out like florence nightingale . his dilemma : how to integrate the lovely nurse into his and his pesky mother is life . segal is performance is about the only thing holding the picture together . his frustrations , his reactions , his comic timing is almost peerless whatever happened to that guy ? ; where the film fails is in other areas . ruth gordon is characterization is dreadful as the mother . at the beginning , you cannot figure out if her character is senile or just being deliberately vague to keep her son from moving out . by the end , it is clear she is just nuts . when segal brings van devere home to meet her , gordon is eyebrows furrow and she gets a mean , sinister look . she wants the intruder in her son is life removed ; she is calculating . this is not the mode of a senile person . you are not getting a consistent performance throughout the picture , which is probably the director is carl reiner fault as much as gordon is . ruth gordon is old lady in rosemary baby is much more successful because with the kind of ingratiating , cloying person that ruth gordon generally plays , the audience responds to her as annoying . but when mia farrow is too timid to fight back , gordon becomes more cloying , her fangs dig deeper and deeper and we are frightened for farrow ; this kind of imposition is genuinely terrifying . here , we are being asked to laugh at what we would normally find annoying , and if gordon played it as helplessly nutty all the way , we might . but she is selfish and mean as well , and it dampens what little humor there is . there are a few good laughs , though . a courtroom scene with barnard hughes as a military officer and rob reiner as a counterculture punk is fairly hilarious , and vincent gardenia does a nice turn as a lombardiesque football coach . there is also an inspired bit where segal is brother ron liebman , having been stripped naked by muggers on his way to segal is place , asks him for something for to wear home and he gives him the gorilla suit . but of a lot of the script is poorly conceived and simply does not make sense . why is a new york lawyer with his own practice even living at home in the first place ? why does segal , if his mother is senile , try to reason with her logically : if you spoil this for me , i will punch your f ing heart out . why does liebman keep cutting through the park if he knows he is going to get mugged ? why does he take a taxi after leaving segal is with gorilla suit ? why was not he taking taxis all along ? a funnier bit would have been liebman , as the gorilla , terrorizing the muggers . why does van devere keep coming back after her first husband was a kook , why does she want to get involved with this bunch ? i suppose if i put this to carl reiner , he would say , these are crazy people , they do not have to make sense . which is a convenient way to excuse a lousy script that is full of holes . the characters moment to moment behavior may not have to make sense , but their motivations do , and that is where where is poppa ? falls apart ; the situations are created just to have the gag , and the gags are mostly one shots , they do not build to anything . carl reiner is the most guilty in this whole fiasco . how he has acquired this vaunted reputation as a pillar of comedy puzzles me ; basically , his career has been to hold a microphone in front of sid caesar and mel brooks while they talk in funny voices . his son rob has ten times the skill and intelligence as a director . in show biz terms , reiner pushes buttons ; a monkey could do his job . and that is most apparent in his framing of the action . why is all of new york shot in tight and in close ups , but the scenes in the country are all distant and panoramic ? that is the mentality of carl reiner is direction , claustrophobic for the city , spatial for the country . in the final lunatic scene at the old folks home , the camera is so far off , you cannot even make out what is going on . the abrupt ending suggests a resolution that segal could have easily arrived at ninety minutes ago ; it also suggests reiner could not figure out how to end the picture . so he just cut it , as another example of craziness . reiner seems to think dumbness equals craziness , and craziness without logic is always funny . it is not , and the creators of where is poppa are as demented as ruth gordon putting pepsi in her fruit loops .
