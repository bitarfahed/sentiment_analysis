i caught 2 : 37 at the afi fest in los angeles . it is a very well shot first film though the dv format begins to show itself in outside scenes , and i am sure it has good intentions of showing us the dark side of high school in other words every side of high school . but the filmmaker does not have the talent to write or direct up to the premise is promise . there are several characters , but none of them are any more than what the plot requires them to be . there is no depth to these caricatures beyond the machinations of i am troubled teen x , i have y problem . the perceived roles of men and women in this story are phenomenally troublesome . let is start with the men . you have the stoner kid who is gay , the jock who is also gay , the boy who rapes his sister , and mr . peepants . as the stereotype requires , all gay men must be sexually unfulfilled and violent toward women and themselves . naturally or unnaturally as the stereotype assumes , the two gay male characters beat up women , peepants , and themselves . i would be perfectly fine with these characterizations if the stereotypes were turned on their heads , or if the characters somehow transcended them . yet neither took place , and that is all there is to these characters stories . next , the ladies . one young woman wants to be a bulimic housewife , another is the pregnant rapee of the sister raping brother , and there is the girl who kills herself i will get to that later . again , i do not think there is a requirement of political correctness for filmmakers i would be out of a job were that the case , but i do think that it is only justified if there is more to that character or story . if that archetype were being used to reveal something about character other than i am a teenager and life sucks , i would be happy as a clam . but nothing new is revealed ! nothing is subverted , or changed , or sublimated . finally , the girl who kills herself . this is blunt and highly sloppy storytelling . we are supposed to sit through 5 minutes of a girl violently killing herself who we have seen for maybe 30 seconds through the whole film ? we have followed all these other stories for an hour and a half , and now we are invited to torture ourselves for a character that is not part of the story ? it is cheap , exploitative , and sloppy . despite the millions of crappy indie films that came before this , you have to earn something like that . you cannot simply purchase it on credit . so this suicide happens , we get wrap ups from the characters that go similarly nowhere but down , and the film ends . what have i learned ? i already knew high school sucked been there , done that . i already knew people have stereotypical views of gay men and young women . i already knew that kids with disabilities are mocked . what else is there , then ? smoke , mirrors , and some really nice views of leaves . oh , and the nastiest deus ex machina i have seen in a while .
