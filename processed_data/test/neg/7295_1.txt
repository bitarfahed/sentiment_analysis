if you have seen atlantis 1 , then you would know that what made that film truly great was brilliant animation and a good script . this movie was so sloppily drawn and animated . the story is also dopey . i was so disappointed in this half baked drivel that i could not make it past the first hour , and man did i try ! the one thing this film had was that it expanded the mole character , making him both more sympathetic and three dimensional . take it from me , judge this junk from the cover on the video box . the cover is poorly drawn disney schlock , clearly grabbing for an easy buck from an unsuspecting parent . if this was a stand alone flick , it would not be so bad , but riding on the coattails of a brilliant piece like atlantis makes it utterly inexcuseable .
