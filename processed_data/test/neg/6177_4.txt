well , killshot is not awful , but it comes close . production values are decent and the main actors do a pretty good job except for rosario dawson in a wasted role , but the story is just pathetic . i do not know if the elmore leonard book had such dumb characters , since i have not read it , but i am guessing that the book was supposed to be at least slightly humorous . the movie has no detectable humor . after the first twenty minutes , you will be yelling at the screen , oh , come on ! nobody is that stupid ! ! in a nutshell , and without any spoilers , everybody acts in a manner convenient to the plot , which makes no sense anyway . a very frustrating and unrealistic movie , which may account for it sitting on the shelf for as long as it did .
