i think rosemary is baby is the most overrated horror film out there . not scary , interesting or much of anything . it is reasonably well directed and ruth gordon was wonderful but that is about it . but this sequel makes it look like gone with the wind ! i caught this on tv back when i was 14 . unfortunately , i still remember it . from what i remember rosemary is baby andrew is now grown up and the coven wants him to start taking over the world that is . but there are forces trying to stop it . . . and andrew is having doubts himself . . . i am giving this a 2 for a few reasons : gordon is in this and still very good ; stephen mchattie was actually pretty good as andrew and there is one spooky moment at the beginning with rosemary patty duke being kidnapped by a bus . . . with no driver . other than that it is dull , silly and needless . the original did not need a sequel ! ira levin is book followup in 1999 was not much better . tune in for the beginning with duke . . . then tune out . not worth the effort .
