this self important , confusing b w film watches like an infant on a very bad acid trip . you are dealing with something that reminds you of a piece of rotting lettuce that accidentally fell out of the back of a garbage truck : no one cares to touch it because it will probably be washed away on its own down the storm drain . there is no room for plot when you have got visceral imagery and subtle allegory . to me , it seems like the director tries to make the next great art movie while begging for intellectual accolades . i did not bring my beret either . watching this , i felt almost insulted since the film does such an effective job of distancing itself from you .
