the real problem with this story is that there is not much story to the story . there is hardly any plot to speak of . widower buys an electric grandma for his kids . one kid resists electric grandma . then finally accepts her . then the kids grow up and grandma leaves . really , very little happens . it is much more of a premise than a story . moreover , strip it of it is schmaltz , and you have a story that had already done before , and better : the lonely . same basic idea : person initially cannot accept the love of a robot , because it is just a machine , then eventually yields and comes to love the robot . the biggest difference is that the lonely is much more powerful , as both the protagonist and we , the audience , are shocked abruptly back to reality and forced to remember that in the end the robot really is just a mechanism . i also find the story highly flawed in that the electric grandmother is just too perfect . she is not only human , she is super human . she is wiser than a real person , she has no traces of mechanicalness to her at all , and she makes marbles appear out of thin air . it frankly really chafes at credulity to think that she is a machine .
