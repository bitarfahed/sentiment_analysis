heard some good remarks about this film as being very gory and frightning , but it is neither . obviously the screenwriter wanted to do a scary horror film but at the same time inject some teenage comedy for the young target audience . scares and comedy seldomly result in good films , the same goes for monster man . not really funny , nor scary or overly enjoyable on any other level . aproaching 39 years of age i have seen my share of horror movies . i have seen good ones and terrible ones , but the crop of films released these days are so frighteningly mediocre it bores me to watch em . the acceptance these days for bad films like this is what really annoys me . let is face it , they produced a lot of crap in the 70 is , 80 is and 90 is but they were regarded as such then as well . today they are regarded as good entertainment . bollocks !
