ok , i have now seen george zucco in at least four separate horror suspense films recently as i worked my way through various 50 pack collections , and i have to say , the guy had a limited range , but he was good at what he did . he was not karloff , but prc was lucky to have him . but the poor guy was stuck in a kind of back water ghetto of horror films , and he was not good enough to take them to the next level of interest . . . . not with the thread bare screenplays and direction and budgets he worked under . that is the case here . this movie is , well , slow , stodgy and unexciting for the most part . the heroine seems to be doomed to be a rent a center version of judy garland , the hero is bland as white rice , and the poor guy playing the monster does not even get a good transformation scene out of the deal . his make up effects are not scary at all he looks like a slightly more shaggy version of a farm hand , is all . it is not a total waste . zucco looks good on camera , he chews the scenery while managing to deliver some terribly affected and contrived set speeches without flinching or losing the flow . there are some moody b w shots here and there that do not completely suck . so all in all . . . this movie helped some c through z level actors pay their rent for another month , and it never sinks below a certain hacked out level of quality . watch it once if you like george zucco , or just feel the need to see every wolfman themed movie ever made .
