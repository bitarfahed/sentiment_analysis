i have seen about a half dozen of the low budget poverty row b westerns that ken maynard made in the 1930s , and i am consistently amazed at how poor an actor he was . how did he ever get to be a leading cowboy actor ? they say that he could ride pretty well back in the silents , but he does not do anything particularly impressive in these later sound films . still , maybe he got the leads because he was big and could ride . phantom rancher is not as bad as some of the other ken maynard films i have seen , but it still is not much . some of the other characters refer to him a couple of times as a young fella , where it appears to me that he is just as old as the other older actors . and if that is not silly enough , there is a rather significant script problem in this film . at one point , one of the characters makes a remark about how the phantom had prevented the poisoning of a well , something that had not happened yet . just a couple of minutes later , we then see that particular scene . no , it was not a flashback . at first i thought perhaps that when treeline films was doing the dvd transfer , they might have reversed two of the reels . but in those days film reels contained approximately 11 minutes of film , and the whole reversal only took about 3 or 4 minutes tops . everything else was in a logical order . so , it looks like that was a genuine continuity problem in the original film . maybe that is one reason why colony pictures did not last very long .
