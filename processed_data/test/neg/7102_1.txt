why lori petty was cast as tank girl , i will never know . her acting performance is lack luster . her voice is grating . it is almost impossible for me to put into words how bad this movie is . there are several modern pop references in the film , which i found to be very strange , given that the movie was supposed to take place far in the future . it would not have been hard to make this premise interesting either . some better writing would have helped loads . naomi watts makes an appearance in it as a mild mannered techno geek . i think they should have probably switched roles . i will never know why anyone would like this movie , unless they were a petty fan . try not to see this movie . total waste of time .
