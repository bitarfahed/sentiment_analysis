upon a recommendation from a friend and my admiration of philip baker hall i rented the first season disc of the loop . it is a typical tv comedy with all the clich s that the genre employs with the wacky scheming brother sully , ditzy blonde lizzy , token unrequited love interest piper , sarcastic asian helper darcy , which reminded me of arliss , as if anyone needed to be reminded of that show . the plot deals with various bad luck usually by sully that befalls sam that puts his job at the airplane in jeopardy , only to have him save the day , with hilarious hijinks ensuing in the middle . i did not descibe a certain episode . i described them all to a t . therein lies the problem as what seems like it might even be passable entertainment at first just gets uselessly stale when watching episodes in a row and growing bored beyond belief at the endless repetition . sully will do something wacky , mimi rogers will say something overtly sexual , russ will tell about his gay son , darcy will do her impersonation of sandra oh on arliss blah blah blah blah blah . the only positive is the lack of an annoying laugh track . but do not let that fool you into thinking it is any good . go watch a far better comedy . arrested development , always sunny in phillidelphia , two name two off the top of my head . surprised that this one is still on the air . oh that is right fox only cancels the good shows , i forgot . needless to say i do not trust my friend is taste in shows anymore . my grade : d
