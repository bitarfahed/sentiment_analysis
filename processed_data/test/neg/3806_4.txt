stodgy drama starring pat o'brien as a washed up reporter who turns up at his ex boss is house to ask for money to fund his son is operation , only to find him dead on the floor . since o'brien knows the identity of the culprit , he offers to take the rap in return for the money he needs . a decent premise is wasted on a film that pretends it has surprises , twists and turns , even though it really does not . performances are rotten across the board , the movie dresses itself up as a hard boiled american noir but the mix of dodgy accents does not work , and the story is hardly gripping . and it contains possibly the least attractive screen kiss of all time .
