while watching blackwater valley exorcism , i encountered scenarios and dialog so incredibly bad that i was convinced that this was supposed to be a comedy . a few choice bits of dialog worthy of a belly laugh : i ate a rabbit . i told you she was possessed ! are you telling me the devil is in my daughter ? ! there are many , many more , but you must discover these for yourself if you dare . the story goes off into all sorts of directions and things happen that probably should not and everyone seems to be a perv or psycho of some sort even the priest . and i have not even gotten to the bad acting . most notable in this area is the fellow playing isabelle is father . the director must have just told him to act like he is got a stick up his because that is the general impression one gets . i do not really want to steer anyone away from blackwater valley exorcism because there is entertainment value to be had . . . for all the wrong reasons , but if you are looking for a decent horror movie that makes sense and is actually scary . . . well , run do not walk .
