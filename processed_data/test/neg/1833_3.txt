okay , i had reasonably high expectations for this . the controversial subject matter was a good concept . as a horror fan i admit i was fascinated and very excited about this . it turns out they had a great idea , but it was terribly executed . let is see . this movie seems to run in 3 modes : happy , sex and dark . the problem is that the movie never decides what it wants to be . the happy parts i believe were meant to contrast with the dark parts , but it does not work . the soundtrack is one of the reasons . the movie transitions between these 3 modes very badly , i cannot even begin to say how much the directing and editing suck . there is sex in the most unappealing and unerotic way . i am not complaining but even for horror standards they were unnecessary and filler . the characters are all unlikeable with the exception of paula potente . her friend from munich is a slut and possibly one of the most annoying characters in movies i have come across recently . there is a bit of plot which i will not go into detail . . . it is not stupid and in more talented hands would make a good movie . there is even a nice twist and a cool conspiracy going on . do not try to understand everything because there are giant plot holes here . it is all so shoddily done that you do not care for the victims , the perpetrators , anyone . and to think this could have been great . i can say one good thing about it which is , the movie shed some light on today is unethical medical procedures . with genetics and controversial sciences advancing , this could have been a great philosophical film that raises and discusses these questions . but you will not find that here , just a series of scenes loosely pasted together with people and things that you do not care about . skip this and go watch flatliners instead , you are welcome . 3 10
