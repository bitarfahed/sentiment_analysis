i do not normally write reviews , but this film was special . i could not turn it off . i do not believe i have ever seen a worse movie , but there i sat , watching . it was like a horrible car wreck with blood flowing all over the highway . it was disgusting , but i could not turn away . where do i start ? the movie seems to think it is a sports thriller , but it is so utterly ridiculous , it can only be a comedy , but it is not funny , not even in a dumb silly way . it is like watching your cousins try to act out a skit on family get togethers . it is painful to watch , but at least it is only for a minute or two . second string went on for over an hour . whoever was involved with making this movie should not work again . the writing , directing , acting , and everything was just terrible . the problem is i cannot describe how bad it was ; you just had to see it . and i am sure this will never be shown again , so unless you saw it , you are out of luck . i mean it was almost worth seeing just for the fact that it gave me some appreciation for every other bad film i have seen over my lifetime . and for every film i see in the future that i cannot stand , i will think to myself , well , at least it was not bad as the second string .
