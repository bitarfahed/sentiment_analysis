that is pretty much all i can say about this flat and uninspired remake of the 1979 carol kane vehicle . camilla belle is not much of an actress , and she brings no energy and vitality to the role of jill johnson , the babysitter harassed by an anonymous phone caller . but if you are looking for some great home architecture and interior design ideas , this movie provides more inspiration than anything you will see on tlc or hgtv . jill spends nearly 90 minutes wandering through the house of the rich doctor and wife for whom she is providing her sitting services , searching for the origins of strange sounds and things that keep going bump in the night . as she lurks around corners and peers down hallways , we get to see a beautiful master bathroom with his and hers sinks that look like roman tubs , a huge kitchen with incredible back lit glass shelving , and the piece de resistance , a self contained aviary and coy pond that feature a self watering system . because the movie is not compelling enough to draw us into jill is fear , we are distracted by the grandeur of the house , which is not something you should be doing when you are watching a thriller . even as jill is pursued by the faceless maniac , we cringe because she is breaking valuables and messing up the coy pond , not because she is about to get murdered . the movie plods along as predictably as most teen slasher movies , and the ending is anything but original . by the time it was over , i just wanted to find out where the heck that house was and if it was real . never mind jill and the kids she was babysitting . 2 stars both for the house .
