good premise , mediocre execution . interesting story yet the film was a little dull . a lot of people are saying that this is one of the worst movies of the year and that is not true at all . it may be a disappointing film but it is an average film at best . i have never seen the cartoon version of the movie so i cannot compare the two . it is probably better because they have a chance to explain interesting characters in the film including sithandra , aeon is friend . the problem with aeon flux is that it takes itself too seriously . it carries the same serious tone throughout the entire film and that gets a little tiring . there is no humor and the film becomes a little boring at times . this is the same problem that elektra had . because the film is so serious , the dialog sounds cheesy and the serious scenes seem forced . the action scenes are pretty good but that is not what the film is really about so do not go in expecting just an action movie . the twist at the end is not mind blowing but it is still a nice ending and better than other thrillers that have come out this past year hide and seek . the costumes are little weird but still look nice and interesting . the visuals were are also done well so the film at least looks nice . so , the movie may be a case of style over substance . interesting to look at but may not hold your attention for a very long time . in the end , it is not the best film out there but it might for a decent rental . rating 4 10
