i do not really consider myself a conservative , so i was not personally offended by this film , but it was pretty clear that the plot and the characterization in this film were secondary to the message . and the message is that all conservatives are either evil or stupid or both . the characters are one dimensional either good , freedom loving americans , or brainless , greedy , evil conservatives . there is nothing clever or creative , just anti conservative . i do not really mind the political bias itself , but it should not be the only purpose behind the movie . and clearly it is . on the positive side , the cast is wonderful and chris cooper is impression of w is funny the first two or three times , but after that it is just the same old joke being told over and over again . so if you really hate the conservatives , you will probably enjoy this film , but if you are looking for something with realistic characters and a story that is less black and white , then you would be better off watching something else .
