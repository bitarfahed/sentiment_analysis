what can i say ? after having read herbert is books and loving lynch is movie version , i was extremely disappointed . i felt i was watching a reject version of buck rogers . the sets looked like left overs from a star wars tv special ! i felt the acting was a bit amateurish by most . the costumes were garish and over done which gave it a 60s flash gordon , pulp feel . the worms ! they are supposed to be sand worms and yet they appeared to big stalagtites with a mouth at the blunt end . the effects in general were pretty second rate . i will not even start about the disgraceful navigator effect . this so called frank herbert is dune was not even faithfull to his books ! it should have been called frank herbert is dune for dummies . key plot elements were left out , names were changed and the entire feel of the story was sanitised . i did not even recognise the harkonnens ! in fact most of the characters appeared nothing like herbert is descriptions had depicted them . i am starting to get upset just remembering what a tragedy it was . i am glad i could not stomach the second installment . . . .
