this movie is an obvious rip off of underworld usa starring cliff robertson . you owe it to yourself to invest a couple of hours and watch the original . i found this one to be disjointed and hard to follow , with a lot of scenes that did not make much sense . i am not opposed to violence in movies but this whole thing seemed to be nothing more than an excuse to blow away one person after another , many of whom were only marginally connected with the storyline . with everyone else getting killed , all through the movie i wondered why frank did not just blow johnny is brains out . he certainly could have and johnny sure did not act like someone that could be trusted . another thing that bothers me is the sex scenes ; why do these people have sex with their clothes on ? is that supposed to turn us on ? surely they did not think they had a shot at a pg rating . nothing in this movie seemed to play out naturally . as if one were watching people in real life , instead it was done in a heavy handed and shallow manner .
