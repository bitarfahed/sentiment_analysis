i saw this film in a cinema club in germany in 1970 . most of the attendees were from the local private school at which i was teaching . i had seen a few godard films previously while i lived in france , and , though they had not been my favorites , i could at least make sense of them . being able to speak french helped . the fairly innocuous beginning of le week end soon turned into a kaleidoscope of images , very well , maybe too well photographed , that soon made no sense to me . for me , films , like books , must have some clear sort of meaning . this did not for me . i suppose i am not into artsy films . images still haunt me from the film . to this day i refuse to eat rabbit meat , well loved by the french . those who have seen the film might realize why . yes , the theme of materialism came through , but the cannibalism , the car wrecks , and all the other scenes of destruction and horror only sickened me . i am not sure if i walked out before the end , but i certainly felt like it . i promised myself that i would never watch another godard film , and i have not and never knowingly will . my rating : nothing . it was the most disgusting film i have ever seen . others may have been more inept , silly or stupid . i have forgotten those while scenes from this one will haunt me forever .
