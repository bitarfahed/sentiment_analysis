i understand that people have different expectations of low budget , arthouse movies . i also know that john sayles has a sort of glow about him , that earthy , intellectual anti hollywood vibe , a la tim robbins , the coen brothers and atom egoyan , that makes him a darling with the critics from the get go . but this is not a good movie . i am sorry , it just is not . it meanders . it has too many characters . its tone is uneven , its point of view is muddled , the acting is all over the board , from naturalistic to over the top . it lingers for long moments with minor characters we do not care about and cuts away from tense scenes just when things are getting good . it misses the mark . the worst flaw in the movie is that the two closest things to a protagonist , edie falco is marly and angela bassett is desiree , are straight jacketed in characters that have no drive . marly is an apathetic drunk , steeped in her life is own inertia . desiree is a woman trapped in her own repressed pain . when your two main characters world views can be summed up with the phrases i do not care and i want to leave here , why should the audience give a rat is patootie ? i will be plain : sayles writes funny dialogue . he is very adept at crafting a scene . the problem is , these scenes do not go anywhere . there is no spine to the movie . no drive . the movie does not create rooting interest in any of the characters . in my opinion , he is also too preachy about big bad corporate america gobbling up the little guy . if you want to see a quality small movie , see david lynch is straight story . pass this one up .
