movies like this give independent films a bad name ! this simply a boring compilation of vingettes , with no structure whatsoever . i would not be surprised if the screenwriter was completely stoned . if you want to see a good stoner comedy , watch half baked . it is no award winner , but at least it made me laugh . the film was obviously made on a micro budget . every scene either takes place in someone is house , someone is apartment or some outdoor location . if the writing was good and the dialogue was interesting , i would have ignored the film is budget like in the case of edward burns films , but obviously that is not the case this time around . i quote robert deniro from a bronx tale when i say , there is nothing in the world worse than wasted talent . everybody in the cast is talented . luke wilson , alicia witt , brittany murphy , jeremy sisto all talented performers ! and they all have been in much better movies . the actors give it their all , but they could not go too far with such a lame script . the only scene i found interesting was jack black is cameo , where he sings a song about being in the woods . and of course , there was the brief strip club scene at the beginning , which i also found appealing . the characters are uninteresting and the story barely exists . many movies are awful , but at least you understand their intentions . what was bongwater is intention ? the world will never know . my score : 2 out of 10
