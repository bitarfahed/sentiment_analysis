the vhs cover for the evil below makes it look like a cool underwater horror flick . sadly it is closer to a boring adventure flick and has no real horror elements . it starts off with some divers who get attacked and we see a bit of blood , but that is the only blood in the entire film . the evil below is not the worst around , but for a horror film it is tediously boring . the acting is decent and to be honest the only thing that keeps you watching is the developing romance between the two leads . there is a few nightmare sequences where it just seems like the director did not know what to do . i manged to sit through it all just about but i would not recommend this film to anyone . horror fans might want a copy for the cool vhs cover , but it is best left at that .
