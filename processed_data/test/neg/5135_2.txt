yeah , i made it all the way to the end . it was pretty hard though . sometimes i was wondering if there was even a director on set . the acting . . . well , i do not even know what to say . i thought the girl who played emily tried her best with the boring , sometimes wacky and sometimes predictable dialog . i know that it was low budget but the hollow sound and the lighting unfortunately made it look and sound like a 100 minute long audition tape . i am not even going to talk about the ending . . . the best thing about the movie was the music . that being said , i do not recommend this movie to anyone . even for lesbians who do not have many movies to watch overall , this is nothing to waste your time on . if you are looking for a sad lesbian themed movie , check out the children is hour , lost and delirious or high art . those will all leave you depressed and gloomy but you will not have to suffer cinematic hell in the process .
