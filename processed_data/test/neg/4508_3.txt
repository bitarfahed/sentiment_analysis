victor buono as the devil ? surely somebody must have been drunk when that casting decision was made . that is not the worst part of this silly mish mash of sundry haunted house devices but it gets my vote for being the funniest part of it . while the film is by no means terrible it does not even approach other 1970 is haunted house flicks like amityville horror , the changeling and the legend of hell house . the evil can be entertaining in spots , but do not expect to be scared . it is better approached as what it is : a silly horror film made all the sillier by it is over serious approach .
