this movie is proof you cannot just go to a redbox and read descriptions of films and pick one and give it a try . while i will give em great credit for having produced a film with halfway decent special effects on such a low budget , and at least a halfway decent script and story line . . . unfortunately , it was only just that : halfway decent . if you like movies where things are not all neatly wrapped up , and do not mind low budget effects , you might like this film . honestly , it was not really my cup of tea . i should have just gone to bed rather than spend my time watching . for a better science fiction movie produced on an even lower budget ! have a look at primer .
