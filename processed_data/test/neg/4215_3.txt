margaret mitchell spins in her grave every time somebody watches this mess ! fine costuming and sets cannot even begin to overwhelm lackluster performances by joanne whalley as the title character and the ever bland timothy dalton as rhett . even worse than the acting and perhaps partially explaining it is the script , which is astoundingly cliched and predictable . add to that hellishly bad script a score that will have you cringing , and you have got a disaster i would not wish on any viewer . scarlett is just amazingly lousy , and i cannot imagine how it ever got made , much less made it to video .
