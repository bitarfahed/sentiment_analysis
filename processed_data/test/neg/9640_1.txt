i find it terribly ironic that left wing hollywood continues to hedge its bets , making these awful lukewarm movies that neither condemn the war on terror nor embrace it . if you are a sixties survivor and a committed pacifist , and you are hoping for an all out condemnation of war like born on the fourth of july or all quiet on the western front , this movie will really feel like a rip off . none of these soldiers actually question this war , or any war , or the idea of war . they just gripe about having to do another tour . on the other hand , if you are a patriotic american who wants to see a story of courage and honor , this movie will really feel like a rip off . none of these soldiers loves america , or even loves the service . the way they pout and sulk makes them come across more like suburban teenagers than blue collar tough guys . it is not we were soldiers , and it is not sands of iwo jima . it is not even mindless action , and the war scenes are less rambo and more buffy . ryan philippe so completely cannot carry this kind of movie . though he is devastatingly sexy , in a rough trade , men is room , bisexual sort of way , it is hard to picture him as a slow talking texas boy who wants to stand up and be counted . this is no sgt . croft in mailer is the naked and the dead . he is more like joel in truman capote is other voices , other rooms . he cannot sell you on the idea that he is been in combat and done his bit , or that he wants his woman and wants her right now . he fizzles on the battlefield and in the bedroom scenes , looking as if he would much prefer to bend over and take a good stiff attack from the rear .
