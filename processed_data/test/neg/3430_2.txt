other than john krasinski , this movie was absolutely terrible . the lacey chabert and andrew keegan love story was as clich d as possible , full of unbelievably bad lines about how her parents would not ever let them be together and super hammy longing looks . none of the emotion had any depth or reality whatsoever . the two accented characters dean edwards as rupert and whoever it was playing the gun expert . . . . once they saw how bad the accents were , could not they have decided to just drop them and rewrite a couple of lines to avoid giving the audience headaches ? apparently not . i do not even know where to start with the editing , particularly the sound editing . if you hate obvious over dubbing as much as i do , do not watch this . that being said , krasinski was great . off the bat i will admit that i am a huge office fan and that is why i rented this . but he is quite entertaining as the off the wall friend with crazy ideas . he is got a clich d role , but he still manages to make it as entertaining as possible . the ending was awful . just flat out terrible . the idea of the robbery gone awry had potential , but keegan floundering around after being shot , all the way to his studio to fall ontop of a painting of chabert which looks nothing like her is the most cringe worthy scene .
