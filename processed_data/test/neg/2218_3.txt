at the earth is core was on television yesterday . i was at my computer working and happened to glance over and see what must have been some of the worst action sequences ever made . i was instantly enthralled by the film is shoddy production values , appalling acting by all included even peter cushing and horrific , unintentionally hilarious action sequences and puppet monsters . the film is about a victorian scientist who takes a stereotypical buff american hero on a ground boring trip in the welsh countryside . little do they know that a great evil lurks at the center of the earth is core . . . . forget the fact that the title does not make sense . if they were really at the earth is core , they would be about 2700 kg m3 underground and burning alive in a sea of iron or whatever it is down there . forget that the puppets used in the production rival the beast master for being the fakest looking of all time . no , the real genius of at the earth is core is its naive stupidity a gung ho action spectacle without real action and without real spectacle . it is in essence just a gung ho movie and a stupid one at that . people who enjoy mst3k style stuff will love this it is appallingly bad , and indeed so bad it is almost enjoyable in a strange way .
