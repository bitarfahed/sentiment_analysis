when i first read about berlin am meer i did not expect much . but i thought with the right people , the right locations , the right music and fashion you could at least make a trivial movie about the hip berlin everyone seems to be talking about . but eissler failed , it is so ridiculously unauthentic . it is a complete misrepresentation of what it is going on in berlin is so called scene . of course it is not all about hippness , but you should expect more from a movie that is being sold as the definite berlin movie . and apart from all the credibility stuff , it really is a bad movie . mediocre acting and a rather boring plot . interestingly some of the actors have proved in other movies that they are actually quite talented . so it really must be poor directing skills . do not bother watching berlin am meer unless you are 17 , come from some small town in western germany and want to move to the big city after you finished school . then you might actually find it enjoyable and totally cool .
