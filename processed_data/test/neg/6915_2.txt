no one in this movie has very much to do . this is probably the longest 65 minutes i have ever spent watching a movie . the makeup effects on the pianist with macromeglia are pretty good , but that is the only thing that keeps this from being rate a 1 . the doctor is assistant goes through extreme mood swings from passivity to hysteria in seconds and then seems to forget where she was in the next scene . the director assembled a lot of the right ingredients for a mad doctor movie , but somehow forgot the skeleton of a story to hang them on . unless you know someone in the cast or crew , i would not recommend even sampling this one .
