you know those movies that are so unspeakably bad that you have to laugh ? half caste was not one of them . which sounds good , right ? but no , it is not . it is not a bad attempt at a horror movie that is fun to watch because it is lame , or not well acted , or has bad special effects or anything else like that . no , half caste is just plain boring . they do not even make an attempt to be scary until the last 20 minutes are so . it is just kids running around in the african bush country and getting high off of elephant dung for the first 75 of the movie , and it is not even funny . the last 20 minutes , though , are hilarious . i have no idea what happened , but it was really fun to watch that cgi leopard rip out the throats of all of those white guys i could not tell apart anyway . if you are in the mood for a bad horror movie , do not rent this one , because you will go to sleep before they get to the fun stuff . if you do accidentally rent this movie , i would recommend fast forwarding to the end , and skipping any scene that happens in daylight . you will not miss anything . you will not have any idea which character is which or exactly what is going on even if you do watch all of the back story .
