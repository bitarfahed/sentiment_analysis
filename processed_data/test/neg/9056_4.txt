personally , i did not really gain a whole lot from the act of seeing with one is own eyes . i have noticed a lot of really highly rated reviews on here for the film , and i am kinda surprised . maybe i missed something that other reviewers felt moved by but i found the film pretty tedious and basically pointless . the action of the film is a bunch of autopsy footage that is filmed in an art house style lots of extreme close ups , weird editing , etc . . . and with no sound or dialogue . i guess the act of seeing with one is own eyes could be considered a study of human anatomy , or maybe if you really wanna dig a little some sort of comment on the fragility of humanity or whatever but i personally found it to be a bunch of semi interesting but ultimately dull autopsy footage . if that is your thing , then this will be a winner for you . as for me i have seen more interesting er footage . not a bad film , as it is not really a film in any traditional sense i just found nothing really notable about it 4 10 .
