i tried watching this movie , but i did not make it past the first 15 minutes . it is a terrible disappointment , considering the cast , but i cannot look past the fact that the dialogue is in english and some of the actors pretending to be indian are not even close read : kristin kreuk . considering that india alone has 1 6th of the world is population and one of the biggest movie industries , i do not think it would have been hard for the film makers to have found an excellent indian actress to play the part . and i do not say so because of some blind patriotism , but because it is absolutely and totally absurd for a non indian to play the role of an indian pakistani . now some people say that as long as she is convincing who cares ? but my point is exactly that she is not convincing and never can be not due to her acting skills , but due to her ethnicity . for example , however good an actor tom hanks may be , he will never be able to play an australian aborigine ! but that is still minor to the biggest faux pas the film makers made : having the dialogue in english . it totally destroys the mood , as well as any semblance of authenticity . had the same movie been made in native languages hindi , urdu , punjabi with english subtitles , this may have been an excellent movie . unfortunately , as things stand , i would not recommend anyone seeing it , apart from film students who want to study what not to do in movies .
