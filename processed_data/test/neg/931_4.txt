first there was tsui hark is zu warriors 2001 , which is visually ground breaking , but much lacking in the acting and writing departments , now this movie , which is visually almost as good as zu though no longer ground breaking , but is even worse in the acting and writing departments . it is really sad that there seems to be an almost complete lack of acting and writing talents in the hk movie industry . i guess you need to understand cantonese to understand how bad and vulgar the dialogs in the movie really are . it is like some delinquent kids talking in the street , it is that bad . to make it worse , the actors and actresses themselves look like delinquent kids , and cannot act even if their life depend on it . i understand that this movie is supposed to be a comedy aimed at the younger generation in hk , but has hk youths really become so brain dead that they cannot appreciate anything but such juvenile and vulgar acting writing ? if that is the case , it makes me ashamed to be from hk . i wish hk movie makers will learn some lessons from directors like zhang yi mou or ang lee , and finally make a movie that is both visually stunning as well as competent and mature in the acting and writing departments . and stop using young singers idols heartthrobs as actors because they cannot act however many fans they may have in hk !
