a group of 7 gold prospectors head into a mine that was recently opened back up after an earthquake . of course , they do not pay attention to local legend that something is down there and killing people . this low budget 25 , 000 horror flick has a slight cult following and i am not exactly sure why unless it is because it is so obscure . i will admit the last half hour is pretty entertaining , but the hour getting there is pure torture . lots of walking and talking and our titular strangeness does not appear until 45 minutes in . even in the extras co writer chris huntley admits it commits the unforgivable sin of being boring . i would forgive them if they were strict amateurs , but this group graduated from usc so i would hope they know an exploitation film should be exploitive . anyway , like i said , the last half hour is cool as three survivors battle the stop motion monster and there is a cool john carpenter like score . i wanted to see more of the monster , but it is literally on screen for 45 seconds . even if the movie is not the best , code red dvd has given this great attention . you have interviews and an audio commentary by director melanie anne phillips , producer actor mark sawicki and co writer huntley . the tales about how the film was made are pretty fascinating and inspiring like a cave set being built in a backyard . even more interesting are sawicki and huntley is usc student shorts , which are actually all better than the feature production . huntley was a pretty talented artist and it is a shame he did not go on to anything else . sawicki has worked steadily in hollywood as a visual effects and camera guy . the film is vhs is kind of legendary for how dark it was and i am sure this is much better . however , you still get scenes where the only image are five helmet lights bouncing around in the blackness . safe to say , the original my bloody valentine is still horror film set in a mine champ .
