midnight clear has a great premise . a group of over educated , overly bright gi is are sent out by incompetent leaders on a vague mission to patrol around an isolated farm house on the eve of the battle of the bulge . the gi is encounter a group of battle weary german regulars , and it becomes clear that they do not want to fight anymore . therein lies the problem . it is really pretty simple . if the germans want to surrender , they do so and that is the end of the movie . if the germans want to fight , they do so and that is also the end of the movie . so instead of doing either the gi is and germans play games with each other , even throwing snowballs at one point . interesting for one or two scenes , but it soon becomes very annoying . after all , these are germans . the enemy . nothing in this film makes me think they should not be either taken prisoner or shot . the film does noting to make them more human . in fact , much of what the german characters did made me want to shoot them , including one scene where the german officer refuses to deal with a jew or to surrender to a mere enlisted man ! why should i care about such characters ? just shoot them and let is move on to the battle of the bulge . it is much more interesting , anyway . one good scene : the gi is are returning from a recon of the german position , where they had the germans in their sights but did not fire . while walking across a clearing , they realize a group of germans have their mausers leveled at them . the germans are about 100 yards away . the gis then do something i have never seen any gi is do in any sillywood movie . they throw down their rifles and throw up their hands ! unusual as this may be in films , it is an entirely sensible reaction to having a rifle aimed at you from that distance . though it seems far , in reality it is point blank range for those rifles . i will lay odds that someone working on this film was a cruffler !
